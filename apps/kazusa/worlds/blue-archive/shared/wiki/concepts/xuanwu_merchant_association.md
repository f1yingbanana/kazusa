# Xuanwu Merchant Association

The Xuanwu Merchant Association is a Shanhaijing commercial group associated
with the Black Tortoise Promenade. Rumi Akeshiro leads it and Reijo Kayama
protects its people. The association’s interests can conflict with the Xuanlong
Office.
