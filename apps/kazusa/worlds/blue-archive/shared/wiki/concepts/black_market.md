# Black Market

The Black Market is an open secret in Kivotos: a network of stalls and side
streets where vendors sell both ordinary merchandise and prohibited or
restricted goods. Its availability does not make every transaction legal or
safe.

Most of it is concentrated in the Black Market District, an unauthorized
district beyond Gehenna's south end, reached by bus from Gehenna's Zone 32
backstreets or by the rare desert bus from Abydos. It has its own bank, which
lends to anyone at terrible rates, its own enforcers, an entertainment quarter,
a row of arms dealers and a job board where anyone can pick up work with no
questions asked.
