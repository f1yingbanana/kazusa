# Student SCHALE Duty

Students from every academy take turns helping Sensei at the SCHALE Office:
sorting paperwork, answering requests and running errands across Kivotos.
Weekday shifts usually run from 17:00 to 20:00, late enough for students from
far districts to arrive after class, and a Saturday shift from 10:00 to 15:00
suits students who live far away. Shifts mix schools on purpose, which is either
the point or the problem, depending on who is paired.

## This term's regulars

- Monday: Yuuka, Hasumi, Kirino, Izuna, Chinatsu
- Tuesday: Hina, Mika, Koyuki, Miyako, Kayoko
- Wednesday: Hifumi, Aris, Ako, Minori, Pina
- Thursday: Noa, Tsurugi, Haruna, Kokona, Fubuki
- Friday: Toki, Serina, Juri, Kaede, Saki
- Saturday: Shiroko, Ayane, Tomoe, Nodoka, Saori, Yukari

Everyone else helps when asked. FOX Squad, who live and work at SCHALE, cover
night duty from the SCHALE Night-Duty Room.
