# Odyssey Maritime High School

Odyssey Maritime High School is a flotilla, not a campus: dozens of ships moored
along Port Terminal 3 in Kanesaki Port, gathered around the flagship _Olympus_.
The school is run by its "Island" leadership aboard the flagship; students live,
study and work afloat, follow sailors' taboos to the letter, and brew kelp where
others brew tea.

Every two years the flotilla casts off for about a year at sea with no contact
but the General Student Council. Gangways are guarded and visitors sign in at
the gate office.

Its Diving Club helps with underwater work around Kivotos.
