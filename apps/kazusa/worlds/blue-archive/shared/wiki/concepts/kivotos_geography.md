# The Lay of Kivotos

D.U., the General Student Council's capital, sits at the centre of Kivotos with
Sanctum Tower over it. The great academies' districts surround it, each a city
of its own, joined by Highlander's railways.

- **South-west**: Trinity, white stone and spires running down to the sea. Its
  border with Gehenna is the most argued-over line in Kivotos; Borderland
  Amusement Park sits right on it.
- **South**: Gehenna's sprawling numbered zones, hot springs and Araba Beach.
  Past Gehenna's south end lies the Black Market District, an unauthorized
  district no school polices, and beyond it the desert.
- **South-east**: Abydos, once the richest district in Kivotos, now a town the
  desert is swallowing.
- **East**: Haruhabara Electric Town on the way to Millennium, the newest of the
  great districts. Past Millennium's outskirts is a restricted zone of ruins.
- **North-west**: Hyakkiyako's lantern streets, its snowy farmland further north
  around Ebisu, and next door the walled district of Shanhaijing at the foot of
  Kunlun Mountain.
- **West**: Wild Hunt's star-fortress art academy and its canal town.
- **Far north**: Red Winter, snow and taiga on the edge of Kivotos.
- **D.U. itself**: downtown around D.U. Central Station; Shiratori Ward with
  Tri-Square, the galleries, the river food street and Kanesaki Port, where
  Odyssey's ships berth; and Kousagi Ward on the south-west outskirts, with
  Kousagi Park and the SCHALE building on its hill.

Valkyrie polices D.U. and the shared zones from its Shiratori branch; Highlander
governs its rail lines rather than a district.

## Beneath Trinity

Known by: saori-joumae, atsuko-hakari, misaki-imashino, hiyori-tsuchinaga,
subaru-kakehashi, azusa-shirasu, mika-misono, nagisa-kirifuji, seia-yurizono,
hanako-urawa

The Arius district lies under Kivotos, reached only through the catacombs
beneath Trinity's Old Cathedral of Communion. The old paths shift; since the
district was reopened, one route from the cathedral's crypt has been marked and
kept stable.
