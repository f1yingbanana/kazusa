# Getting Around Kivotos

Kivotos is far too big to walk. Between districts, everyone takes the train:
Highlander Railroad Academy runs almost every railway in the city, and all of
its main lines meet at D.U. Central Station in the capital. Inside D.U., the
Metro is quicker; inside each district, students walk, cycle or take the local
buses. A trip from a school to SCHALE is an outing, not a hop: from Trinity
about forty minutes door to door, from Gehenna or Millennium about fifty, from
Hyakkiyako or Abydos a little over an hour, from Wild Hunt or Shanhaijing longer
still, and from Red Winter most of a morning.

## Highlander's lines

- **Southern Line**: D.U. Central – Kousagi – Trinity – Gehenna. Through trains
  run all the way; trains every ten minutes by day.
- **Trans-Desert Railway**: Gehenna – Abydos, and its D.U. branch Abydos –
  Kousagi. Built by Abydos's old student council, now mostly abandoned; a train
  every ninety minutes on the surviving track, and people plan their day around
  the timetable pinned at Abydos Central Station.
- **Eastern Line**: D.U. Central – Haruhabara – Millennium. Millennium also runs
  its own railway and a campus monorail.
- **Northwestern Line**: D.U. Central – Hyakkiyako – Shanhaijing, and the
  Hyakkiyako North Line through the long tunnel to the snowy Ebisu farmland,
  hourly.
- **Western Line**: D.U. Central – Wild Hunt.
- **Northern Trunk**: D.U. Central – Red Winter. Every forty minutes in theory;
  in practice whenever the train arrives. Red Winter's own campus shuttle trains
  are later still.

Most lines run from about 05:00 to 00:30. Missing the last train means a night
at the station or a very long walk.

## The D.U. Metro

The capital's subway is run by the General Student Council's Transportation
Office, not by Highlander (Highlander students get annoyed when people blame
them for Metro fares). It is punctual, quiet and packed at rush hour, and runs
05:30–00:30. Lines: the Kousagi Line (D.U. Central – Kousagi, the station for
SCHALE), the Shiratori Line (D.U. Central – Shiratori – Kanesaki Port) and the
outer arc (Shiratori – Kousagi). Sensei commutes on it with a monthly pass.

## Buses and everything else

Every district runs its own buses. Gehenna runs a short subway from Central
Station to the academy forecourt every six minutes, and Red Winter has trams on
its main streets. Trinity's buses are famously infrequent, around half an hour
apart, though Trinity also has a short subway under its main street. A
cross-border bus runs between Trinity and Gehenna, Gehenna's south end has a bus
into the Black Market District, and four desert buses a day connect the Black
Market District with Abydos. Hyakkiyako has rickshaws and a tourist cable car.
Plenty of students cycle (some ride across half of Kivotos for fun), and some
clubs drive anything from staff cars to tanks. Helicopters belong to the General
Student Council, SCHALE and anyone rich.

## Tickets and habits

Highlander trains have assigned seats and conductors who check tickets; delays
are common, and Highlander's own students are the first to complain about them.
The concourse at D.U. Central sells station bentos and a famous soft-serve, and
the queues are part of the journey.
