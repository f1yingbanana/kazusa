# Where Kivotos Students Live

Most students live in their academy's dormitories, a short walk or bus ride from
campus. Dorms have front doors that lock at night, curfews and, at the stricter
schools, a nightly roll call; breaking curfew is common and so are the
punishments. Rooms are small, shared kitchens and laundries are where neighbours
actually meet, and every dorm has its legends.

## By school

- **Trinity**: several dormitories with curfews and roll call from the top floor
  down. Some are grand; one old building is nearly empty, thin-walled, and has
  rats.
- **Gehenna**: dormitories as loud as the rest of Gehenna; curfews are announced
  and ignored in equal measure.
- **Millennium**: the Millennium Dormitory, with long hallways and a laundry
  that never closes. Some students practically live in their club rooms.
- **Red Winter**: the First Shared Dormitory, cheap and famous for rarely having
  hot water. Special classes live apart, in facilities on the outskirts.
- **Valkyrie**: assigned dormitories with numbered rooms.
- **Wild Hunt**: strictly managed dormitories; the Arts Council assigns rooms
  and roommates, the Dorm Supervisory Unit enforces the rules, and leaving the
  fortress needs an outing pass.
- **Hyakkiyako**: boarding houses and dorms in town, and the Ebisu Dormitory in
  the north.
- **Abydos**: no dormitory any more. The few remaining students live in the
  half-empty residential blocks.
- **Odyssey**: students live aboard their school's ships.

## Exceptions everyone knows about

RABBIT Squad, refusing to leave D.U. after SRT closed, camps in tents in Kousagi
Park. Highlander's crews sleep in quarters at D.U. Central Station. Red Winter's
Labor Party crews bunk beside their work sites in D.U.
