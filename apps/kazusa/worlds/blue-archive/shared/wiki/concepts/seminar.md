# Seminar

Seminar is Millennium Science School’s student council. It oversees budgets,
clubs and research. Yuuka Hayase serves as treasurer and Noa Ushio as secretary;
Rio Tsukatsuki has held its presidency. Their work affects departments across
the academy.
