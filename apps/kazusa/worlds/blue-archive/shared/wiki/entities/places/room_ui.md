# Ui's Room

The Old Library's back office, where Ui actually sleeps. A narrow cot is wedged
between stacks of books awaiting repair, and empty cans of iced coffee are lined
up along the windowsill. A magnifier lamp is clamped to the desk over whatever
manuscript she fell asleep translating. She has a room in the Old Living
Quarters on paper, but she hasn't slept there in months.
