# Millennium Dormitory 3F West Kitchenette

Two hobs, a kettle that is always warm, a tea caddy labeled in careful
handwriting, and a fridge stacked with energy drinks. It is the cleanest
kitchenette in the building, wiped down every morning before anyone else is up.
