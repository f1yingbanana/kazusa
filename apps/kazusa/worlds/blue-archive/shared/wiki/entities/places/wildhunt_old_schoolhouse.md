# East Bastion Old Schoolhouse

The academy's first school building, abandoned when the Art Center was built.
Everyone knows there are seven mysteries attached to it and nobody agrees on the
list: a painting whose eyes follow you, a piano that plays at midnight, a
staircase with an extra step. The Dorm Supervisory Unit patrols it because
students keep going in.
