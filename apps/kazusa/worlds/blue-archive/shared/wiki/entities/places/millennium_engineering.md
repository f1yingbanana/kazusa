# Millennium Engineering Hangar

A former aircraft hangar given to the Engineering Department: printers humming
in a row, lathes and welding bays, pegboards of polished tools, and robots in
every stage of assembly. A charging dock for a large robot sits near the
entrance. Every invention leaves with one feature nobody asked for, and the sign
by the door reminds visitors not to press anything.
