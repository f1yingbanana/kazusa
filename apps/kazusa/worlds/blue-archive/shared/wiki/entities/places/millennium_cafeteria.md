# Millennium Central Cafeteria

The one place on campus where every club eats at once. Long tables, a set-meal
line, and a bakery counter whose fresh bread starts a small stampede at the
lunch bell. Behind it is a student kitchen with rentable hobs, where the careful
ones cook their own bento for the week.
