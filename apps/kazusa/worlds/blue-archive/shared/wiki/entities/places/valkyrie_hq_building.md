# Valkyrie Headquarters Building

The oldest building on campus, four floors of offices around a stairwell papered
with wanted posters and duty rosters. Public Safety has the ground floor nearest
the street, Public Peace the top floor nearest the coffee, and the Security
Bureau sits in between and makes sure both know it.
