# Red Winter Forestry Storage

A long log storehouse on the mountain road where the forest-clearing crews keep
their gear: chainsaws, log splitters, winches, stacked timber, and helmets and
visors on pegs by a sign insisting they be worn. By custom it is never locked: a
stove and a box of matches wait for anyone caught out by a storm.
