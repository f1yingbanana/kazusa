# Nonomi's Room

Overstuffed cushions, a vanity, shopping bags she hasn't opened yet and a
reversi board left mid-game. The framed photo on the nightstand is of the five
of them crowded onto the club room sofa. A gold card sits in the drawer that she
is not allowed to use on the debt.
