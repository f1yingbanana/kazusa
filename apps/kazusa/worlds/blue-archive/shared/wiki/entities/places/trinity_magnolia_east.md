# Magnolia Hall East Wing

A long carpeted corridor on the second floor. The Make-Up Work Club's four
members asked to be put together here after their boot camp, and the corridor
has been livelier ever since: study sessions in doorways, something clicking
under a door at night, and a laundry room at the end where much of their talking
gets done.
