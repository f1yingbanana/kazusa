# Trinity Central Library

Trinity's great library: a hushed reading room under a painted dome, long oak
tables with green-shaded lamps, and shelves tall enough to need rolling ladders.
The Library Committee runs the issue desk and the reference service; a member
can match almost any reader with the right book.

Talking above a whisper earns a look from the desk. The doors lock at 20:00,
though a lamp is sometimes still burning in the stacks long after.
