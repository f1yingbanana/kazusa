# Saori's Room

Nothing is out of place: the cot is made tight and the rifle is clean. Her mask,
repaired more than once, sits on a stand by the door. A folder holds every job
contract she has signed, now with notes in the margins about the parts she
should have read. Under a cloth in the corner is a DJ controller she says she
doesn't care about.
