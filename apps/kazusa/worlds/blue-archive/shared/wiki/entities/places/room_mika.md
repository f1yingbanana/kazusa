# Mika Misono's Room

A cramped attic room at the very end of the top floor, reached up the emergency
stairs. The ceiling slopes, the walls are thin and something scratches inside
them at night. She has covered every surface with accessories and jewellery,
filled a jar with seashells, and strung fairy lights over the damp patches. A
copy of her probation terms is pinned by the door.
