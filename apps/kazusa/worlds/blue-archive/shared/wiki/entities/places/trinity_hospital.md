# Saint Teresa General Hospital

A white hospital at the edge of the Residential Quarter with wards, an operating
floor and a children's ward on the third floor. The Remedial Knights volunteer
here alongside the regular staff, doing ward rounds, night shifts and charity
events. A canteen on the ground floor feeds staff and visitors until 20:00.

When a patient needs hiding as well as healing, the Knights have been known to
arrange that too.
