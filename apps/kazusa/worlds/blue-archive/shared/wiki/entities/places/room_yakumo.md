# Yakumo's Room

Rail maps cover the walls, every line pinned with timetables and her own notes
on how it could be run better. A shelf holds empty station bento boxes, washed
and kept as souvenirs: the octopus pot, the train-shaped one. Business proposal
binders are stacked under the bed. She locks up out of habit.
