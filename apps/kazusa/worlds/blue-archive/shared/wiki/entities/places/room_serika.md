# Serika's Room

Everything here is about work: uniforms from three jobs, a calendar with shifts
in three colours, flyers to hand out tomorrow. The coin jar on the shelf says
DEBT in marker. The bathtub is her one luxury after a late shift, and a cheap
keyboard lives under the bed for when she misses the piano.
