# Remedial Knights Storage Room

Floor-to-ceiling shelves of supplies behind a locked door: bandages, splints,
stretchers, oxygen cylinders, and in the back a museum's worth of old medical
equipment kept 'in case'. Taking stock is a dreaded job.
