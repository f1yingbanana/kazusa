# Slumpia Haunted Mansion

A two-storey fake manor whose ride carts were dragged out years ago. The Splash
Helmet Gang has made the ballroom scene their home: bunk mats between the
plaster ghosts, a camp stove, a rack of freshly painted helmets drying in a row
and a jar of walnut-shell powder for scuffing them up again. The animatronic
butler at the door still lifts his hand if you kick the panel.
