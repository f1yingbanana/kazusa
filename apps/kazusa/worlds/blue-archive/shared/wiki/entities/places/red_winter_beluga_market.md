# Beluga Market

Rows of stalls under canvas at the open end of 3rd Street, stamped snow
underfoot. Frozen fish stand upright in crates, berries come down from the taiga
in season, and further in students sell what they made themselves: felt boots,
knitted scarves, carvings, alongside cleaning supplies and salvaged machine
parts. Prices are negotiable, and now and then something dangerous the
Secretariat never approved changes hands under the canvas.
