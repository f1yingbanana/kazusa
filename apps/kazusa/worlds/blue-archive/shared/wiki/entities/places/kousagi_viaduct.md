# Kousagi Viaduct

Where the Southern Line leaves Kousagi Station it crosses the ward's river on a
long concrete viaduct. Underneath is a shaded riverside path with benches, a
pair of vending machines and years of graffiti; trains thunder overhead every
few minutes until late. Maintenance ladders climb some of the piers, behind
gates that are supposed to be locked.
