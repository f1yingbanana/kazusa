# Hanae's Room

One of the few rooms whose old fireplace still works, and she keeps it lit all
winter. Morning glories grow in pots on the sill, pom-poms hang from the
wardrobe, and a radio calisthenics chart is taped beside the bed she leaves at
05:30 sharp. A first-aid kit the size of a suitcase waits by the door.
