# Valkyrie Police School, Shiratori Branch

Kivotos's police academy keeps its D.U. campus in a leafy corner of Shiratori:
low brick buildings among old trees, a parade ground, and patrol bicycles racked
along every wall. Students here are trainees and serving officers at once,
sorted into bureaus: Public Safety takes civil complaints and walks the beats,
Public Peace investigates the serious cases, and the Security and Investigation
Bureaus keep their own floors (and their own grudges). The Corrections Bureau
runs the prison on downtown's edge. Officers come and go at all hours, so the
front desk never closes, but the campus cafeteria keeps short hours and everyone
complains about it.
