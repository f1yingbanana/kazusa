# RABBIT Camp Cookfire

A blue tarp stretched over an oil drum cut into a stove, a folding table and
four ammunition crates for chairs. Dinner is whatever the hill, the locals or
the late-night discount shelf at the station provided: wild greens, gifted
vegetables, near-expiry bento warmed in a pot. The whiteboard on the tarp pole
holds the duty roster and the day's operation name.
