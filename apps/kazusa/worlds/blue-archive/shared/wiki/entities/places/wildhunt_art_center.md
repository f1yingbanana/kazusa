# Wild Hunt Art Center

A long Italianate building with a clock tower, where nearly every class at Wild
Hunt is taught. Each wing smells of its medium: turpentine, sawdust, hot
electronics, machine oil. Practical exams at the end of each month fill the
corridors with nervous students carrying canvases.
