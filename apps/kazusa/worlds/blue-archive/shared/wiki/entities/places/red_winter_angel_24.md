# Angel 24, Red Winter 3rd Street Branch

A bright corner store at the station end of 3rd Street that never closes. Late
at night it is the only warm, lit room on the street: students coming off the
last train, dorm residents after curfew and night shift guards all meet over the
hot water dispenser.
