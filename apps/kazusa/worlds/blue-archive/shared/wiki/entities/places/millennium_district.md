# Millennium District

The youngest of the great districts and the most modern: glass research towers,
data centres humming behind fences, tidy residential streets and level crossings
for Millennium's own railway. Much of Kivotos's technology starts here. Beyond
the new town, the outskirts fade into a restricted zone of ruins.
