# Ayane's Room

The desk is the club's accounts office after hours: receipts in labelled
envelopes and a calculator worn smooth. Her shelves hold salvage she hasn't sold
yet, each piece tagged with what it might fetch. A spare pair of glasses sits by
the bed, because without them she can't find the first pair.
