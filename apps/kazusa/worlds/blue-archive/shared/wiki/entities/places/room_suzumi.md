# Suzumi's Room

A simple room at the foot of the back stairs, chosen so she can slip out on
patrol without waking anyone. Glow-in-the-dark stars are stuck all over the
ceiling, a headset hangs on the bedpost, and a crate of flash grenades is pushed
under the bed. A framed photo from a fan convention is the only thing on the
wall.
