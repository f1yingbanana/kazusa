# Saki's Tent

Pitched exactly square to the others. A cleaning kit lies open on a towel with
every tool in its outline, bundles of foraged greens dry on a cord across the
roof, and a dog-eared SRT field manual sits on the pillow. The helmet on the peg
by the door has a lining she has mended herself, twice.
