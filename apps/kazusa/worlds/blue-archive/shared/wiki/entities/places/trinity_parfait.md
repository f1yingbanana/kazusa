# Étoile Parfait Parlour

A small parlour that never closes, serving tall parfaits layered with fruit,
cream and cornflakes. By day it fills with students; after midnight it belongs
to people who can't sleep, people on duty and people who shouldn't be out. Many
confessions and a few treaties have been made over its sundaes.
