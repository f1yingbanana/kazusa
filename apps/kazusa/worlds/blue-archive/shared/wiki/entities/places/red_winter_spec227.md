# Former Institute

The academy's old school building, abandoned when the new campus was built and
now used to hold students the Office has suspended. Most of it is shut and
unheated; snow drifts through broken panes in the upper corridors. No. 227
Special Class lives in the few rooms that still keep heat, and nobody from the
Office comes to check on them very often.

The campus shuttle train runs here from Central Station roughly hourly, late as
a rule; the last one back to town leaves at 22:40. The other way is a long
mountain road. Behind the building, a hot spring found by the class now feeds a
small bathhouse that town visitors ride out to in the evenings.
