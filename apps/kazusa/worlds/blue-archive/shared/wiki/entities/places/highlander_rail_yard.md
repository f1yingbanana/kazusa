# Highlander Freight Yard

A wide fan of sidings east of D.U. Central Station, reached by a staff walkway
along the tracks. Freight trains are made up and broken down here at all hours,
shunters clank under floodlights, and the engine sheds smell of diesel and hot
metal. The Freight Logistics Department runs the yard, and once famously lost a
whole train from it.
