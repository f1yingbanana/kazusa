# Justice Task Force Headquarters

A grey stone block near the gymnasium, home of the Justice Task Force, Trinity's
discipline and security committee. Inside: a duty desk that never closes, a
briefing room with a map of the district, the chair's office, and racks of black
uniforms in short supply.

Reports are filed here, suspects are brought in through the side door, and the
vice-chair's office light is usually the last to go out.
