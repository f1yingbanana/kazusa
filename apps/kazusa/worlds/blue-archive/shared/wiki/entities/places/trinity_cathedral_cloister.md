# Trinity Cathedral Cloister

A square covered walk around a herb garden behind the cathedral, with a row of
small whitewashed cells where Sisters who serve the cathedral live. A bell wakes
them at 05:30, and the gate to the street is shut at 21:00. Visitors wait at the
gate.
