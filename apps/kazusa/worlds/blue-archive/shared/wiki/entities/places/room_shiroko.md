# Shiroko's Room

Spare and practical: the bike on the wall, running shoes by the door and a
whiteboard covered in cycling routes and suspiciously detailed floor plans of
banks. A robber's balaclava lies folded in a drawer. The scarf she was given the
winter she was found hangs on its own hook.
