# Dormitory No. 4, Room 404

Home and clubroom of the Occult Research Society: Eri, Tsumugi, Kanoe and Rena.
Eri's corner has a witch hat on a mannequin head over a tarot deck and a pile of
failed practical sheets. Kanoe's bunk by the window has a binaural microphone on
the sill, where a crow and a black cat wait that she insists aren't hers. Rena's
corner is a sewing machine, a dress form and a sticky note that says ghosts
aren't real; Tsumugi's guitar case doubles as her suitcase and her bunk is
papered with ticket stubs.
