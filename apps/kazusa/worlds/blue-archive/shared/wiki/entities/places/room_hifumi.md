# Hifumi's Room

A room that is, by any measure, a Peroro shrine: plushes on every shelf,
limited-edition bags hung like art, and a pinboard of upcoming Momo Friends
release dates with red circles. On the desk are tidy study notes copied out for
her friends. The key lives on a Peroro charm she would never lose.
