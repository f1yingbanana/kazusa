# Konoka's Room

Room 302, next door to her director. An enormous second-hand massage chair takes
up half the floor and is the room's real bed. Horoscope clippings are taped
round the mirror, a charm hangs from every handle, and the newspaper on the side
table is folded open to the crossword.
