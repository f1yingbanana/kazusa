# Marina's Room

Bare and scrubbed spotless: Marina is an expert cleaner, thanks to all the
punishments. A district map over the bed is stuck full of pins and arrows
correcting where she thought she was. A can of cat repellent stands by the door,
just in case. She locks the room and then has to hunt for the key. The Secretary
General sleeps across the corridor and notices every night the key goes missing.
