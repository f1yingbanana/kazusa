# First Shared Dormitory Fireplace Lounge

The only truly warm room in the building: a brick fireplace, a samovar kept
going all day, armchairs worn to the springs and a radio tuned to Red Bear.
Hockey games are listened to here in noisy groups, and residents toast bread on
forks at the fire when the cafeteria is closed.
