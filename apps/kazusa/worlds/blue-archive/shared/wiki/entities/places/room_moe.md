# Moe's Tent

Crates labelled DO NOT DROP form a low wall around this tent, and a power cable
snakes in from somewhere it probably shouldn't. Inside: a ruggedized laptop
covered in stickers, a sleeping bag buried in candy wrappers, and a framed
photograph of a very satisfying explosion hung where a window would be.
