# Meru's Room

Sketchbooks in drifts, screentone scraps stuck to the floor and convention
badges on the lampshade. On the wall a corkboard sits behind a curtain: under it
is a chart of real students paired off for stories, which no one is allowed to
see. The wall she shares with the Publishing Department's critic next door is
thin enough to hear a typewriter pass judgement at midnight. She locks the door
whenever she leaves.
