# Red Bear Editorial Room

Desks under a skylight, buried in galley proofs and business proposals. The
editor-in-chief's corner has rail timetables pinned over the desk; the critic's
corner has a stack of books waiting for verdicts. Writers who miss deadlines are
reminded firmly, sometimes with a pistol.
