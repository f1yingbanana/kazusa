# Tea Party Residence

A stone manor behind high walls on the outskirts, home to the members of the Tea
Party during their term. A guard post stands at the gate, a car waits for the
morning drive to the Tea Party Chamber, and the windows on the east side
overlook Songbird Park.

Since the assassination attempt, security is tighter and a Remedial Knight calls
daily on the convalescent upstairs. One of the three suites has stood empty
since its occupant lost her privileges.
