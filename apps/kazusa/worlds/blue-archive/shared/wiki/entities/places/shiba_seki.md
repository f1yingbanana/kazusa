# Shiba Seki Ramen

Master Shiba's ramen has fed Abydos students for years, often on credit. The old
shop was blown up and he carried on from a stall under an awning, with a
counter, six stools and the same broth. Students get the big portion for the
small price. A student part-timer works the evening rush, and the Foreclosure
Task Force eats here whenever there is money to.
