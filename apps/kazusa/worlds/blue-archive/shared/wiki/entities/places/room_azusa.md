# Azusa Shirasu's Room

Almost bare: a cot made with military corners, equipment laid out in rows, and a
tripwire she strings across the doorway every night out of habit. The single
exception is a shelf of Momo Friends plushes, Skullman in the middle, each one a
present. She doesn't lock the door; she traps it.
