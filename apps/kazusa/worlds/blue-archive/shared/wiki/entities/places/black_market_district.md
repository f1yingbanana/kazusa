# Black Market District

An unauthorized district the size of a small academy's, run by nobody's rules
but its own. Covered alleys of stalls sell everything from snacks to restricted
weapons; dropouts, students on leave and unregistered clubs live in its blocks.
It has its own bank and its own enforcers. People come for what they can't buy
anywhere else, and they keep their hands near their guns.
