# Meru's Workroom

The furthest room in the library, past the end of the closed stacks. A drawing
desk under a daylight lamp, a tablet on its charger, drifts of screentone
offcuts and a wall of reference photos. This is where Meru comes to start over
when drawing gets too hard; she calls it rehabilitation.
