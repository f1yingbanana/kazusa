# Live House Gargoyle

A low brick cellar with a small stage and a bar. Student bands, touring acts and
a monthly metal night that shakes the canal. Academy students crowd the early
sets and leave at the interval in a rush, because the gate shuts at 22:00
whether the encore has started or not.
