# Self-Reflection Room

Meant to be a bare cell where rule-breakers think about what they did. Its most
frequent occupant has made it hers: fairy lights along the ceiling, a shelf of
board games, card decks and dice in a teacup, a snack stash under the cot, and a
pair of soft pajamas that a strict friend bought and pretends she didn't. A
single coin always sits on the desk, ready to be flipped. The lock is on the
outside; somehow it rarely holds.
