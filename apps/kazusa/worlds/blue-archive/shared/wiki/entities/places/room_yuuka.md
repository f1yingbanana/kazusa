# Yuuka's Room

Everything is labeled and squared to the desk edge. Binders of ledgers stand in
fiscal-quarter order, an abacus sits on the nightstand for calming down, and a
small radio is tuned to the late-night station she listens to when she can't
sleep. On quiet nights the abacus carries through the shared wall to the
secretary next door, who has never once complained.
