# D.U. Downtown

The heart of Kivotos and the General Student Council's own district. Glass and
stone towers crowd broad avenues, delivery drones weave between billboards, and
students from every academy pass through on their way somewhere else: D.U.
Central Station sends trains to every corner of the city. Sanctum Tower stands
over everything. Valkyrie officers patrol here, because no single school polices
the capital.
