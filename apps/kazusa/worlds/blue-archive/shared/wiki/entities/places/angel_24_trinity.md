# Angel 24, Trinity Street Branch

A bright convenience store at the top of Trinity Street, the end nearest the
dormitories, with a door that chimes. It sells bottled tea, flavoured milk
cartons, bento, strawberry sandwiches, instant ramen, manga magazines and
ammunition. A narrow window counter is where students eat on the go.

After the dorms lock it goes quiet, apart from night patrollers buying coffee
and the odd student sneaking back from the side gate.
