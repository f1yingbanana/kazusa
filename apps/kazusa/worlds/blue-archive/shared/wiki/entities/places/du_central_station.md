# D.U. Central Station

A vaulted concourse of glass and steel where Highlander's thirty-two high-speed
lines meet the D.U. Metro. Departure boards flicker over a river of students;
conductors in Highlander caps check tickets at the gates. The concourse sells
station bentos and a famous soft-serve ice cream, and the queues for both are
long at lunch. The Metro platforms are a floor down: the General Student
Council's Transportation Office runs them, and they are known for being punctual
and quiet. Lost-and-found, lockers and an Angel 24 kiosk sit by the south exit.
