# Remedial Knights Central Ward

The Remedial Knights's ward on campus: a row of white beds behind curtains, a
treatment room that smells of antiseptic, and a front desk where a Knight is
always on duty. Students come in with sprains, bullet grazes and the occasional
imaginary illness.

Upstairs is the Knights' own club room: a sofa for whoever just came off night
duty, the rota board that splits them between the ward, the hospital and
patrols, and a good tea set; the captain hosts tea there most weekdays at 15:30.
The Knights are famously thorough. Some say too thorough: patients have been
known to leave with more bandages than injuries.
