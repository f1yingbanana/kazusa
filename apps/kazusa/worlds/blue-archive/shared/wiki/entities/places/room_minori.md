# Minori's Room

A narrow upstairs room in the prefab bunkhouse, the same size as every crew
member's. A hard hat hangs on the bedpost, rolled protest placards are stored
under the bunk, and a thick binder of union meeting minutes sits beside a stack
of dog-eared political novels full of her own margin notes.
