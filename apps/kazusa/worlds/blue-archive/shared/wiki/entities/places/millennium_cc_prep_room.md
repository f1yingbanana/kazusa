# Cleaning & Clearing Prep Room

To anyone passing, it's a janitor's room. Inside: a rack of maid uniforms
pressed and numbered, mops and buckets, a locked cabinet of charges and
ammunition, a mission board with a long unbroken column of successes, and a
small round table with a proper tea set. Someone's bomber jacket is always
thrown over the back of the one armchair.
