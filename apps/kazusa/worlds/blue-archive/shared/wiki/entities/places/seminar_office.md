# Seminar Office

Desks pushed into a square, a wall of binders labeled by quarter and club, two
calculators on every desk, and a small lounge behind a glass partition with a
sofa and a kettle. Budget requests, patent registrations and complaints all end
up here. The lights often stay on well after 20:00.
