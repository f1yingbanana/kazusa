# Red Winter Federal Library

A domed hall at the far corner of campus with more shelves than readers. The
Knowledge Liberation Front looks after it with almost no budget: one heater that
works, purchase requests that come back rejected, and a fierce rule against
dog-earing. Behind the reading hall the closed stacks run deep into the
building, and somewhere at the very back is a workroom only the Front uses.
Other students mostly come in to hide, nap or escape a coup.
