# Trinity Catacombs

Stone passages lined with niches, running for kilometres beneath Trinity with no
complete map. Around three hundred entrances are known, nearly all of them dead
ends, and the old true paths to Arius used to move. Since the Arius district was
reopened, one route from this crypt has been marked with paint and lamps. Off
that route, the tunnels still lose people.
