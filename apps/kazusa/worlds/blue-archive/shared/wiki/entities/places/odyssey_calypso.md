# Berthing Ship Calypso

A broad, slow former ferry converted to cabins, moored two berths down from the
flagship. Narrow corridors, bunks bolted to the walls, a small galley and a
radio room on the upper deck. A sailor stands gangway watch at night and the
lights go out at 22:30, sea or harbour.
