# Abydos Combined Classroom

A room built for forty, with five desks pulled together near the window and the
rest stacked at the back. The timetable on the chalkboard was written by the
students themselves; with no teaching staff left, lessons are textbooks,
correspondence coursework and each other. The desk in the sunniest spot is a
well-known place to doze off.
