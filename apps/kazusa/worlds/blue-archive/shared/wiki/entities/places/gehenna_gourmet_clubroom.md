# Gourmet Research Society Clubroom

A small room furnished like a private dining room: a white tablecloth, good
chairs, a tea set, a camping stove, and a wall map of restaurants marked with
stars or with crosses. The cupboard holds napkins, cutlery and explosives in
roughly equal amounts.
