# Momo Friends Automated Store

A Momo Friends shop with no staff at all: robot arms fetch plushes from glass
towers and a machine takes payment. A corner shelf sells second-hand limited
editions at eye-watering prices. On release mornings, the queue starts outside
the Galleria before it opens.
