# Hyakkiyako Great Garden

A walled stroll garden of raked gravel, pines and a wide carp pond, used as an
outdoor lesson ground. The largest carp, an ancient gold one the students call
"Master", comes to the bank whenever someone sits down. A path climbs the back
of the garden to the Tree Observatory.
