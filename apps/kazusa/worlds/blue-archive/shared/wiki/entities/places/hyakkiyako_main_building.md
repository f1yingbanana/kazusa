# Hyakkiyako Main Academy Building

A wide timber-and-plaster school building whose roof rises into a five-storey
castle keep, the symbol of the academy. The homerooms are on the lower floors
with sliding doors and polished floors; stairs to the keep itself are roped off
after hours.
