# Oasis View Apartments

Built when Abydos was rich and named for a view of the Great Oasis that dried up
long ago. Most doors are empty, the landlord collects rent by post, and several
Abydos students rent units here because it is cheap and a short walk from
school. There is a coin laundry in the basement and a water tank on the roof
that everybody climbs up to sooner or later.
