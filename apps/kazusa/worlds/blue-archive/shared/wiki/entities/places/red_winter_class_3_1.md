# Red Winter Class 3-1

The senior homeroom closest to the stairs, so runners from the Office Building
can reach it fastest. The front desks belong to the president and her Secretary
General; the Labor Party's leader and the head of the Publishing Department sit
further back. The loudspeaker over the door interrupts lessons with decrees.
