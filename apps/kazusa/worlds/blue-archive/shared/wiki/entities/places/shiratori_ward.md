# Shiratori Ward

A broad ward south of downtown, recently rebuilt after heavy damage. Office
towers give way to galleries on Plato Avenue, an electronics street, a river
lined with food stalls, a large park and, at the ward's edge, Kanesaki Port.
Tri-Square in the middle is where everyone arranges to meet.
