# Highlander Crew Quarters

Narrow rooms along the top floor of the station's east wing, assigned to crews
on the D.U. roster. A keycard door at the stairhead, a duty board showing who is
on which train, and a bell schedule of quiet hours that the first freight of the
morning ignores. The whole floor shivers when an express leaves.
