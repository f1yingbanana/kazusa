# Hanako's Room

A tidy, elegant room that looks like a top student's, which it is. A swimsuit
hangs on the wardrobe door like a trophy, and exam papers with perfect scores,
some of them third-year papers, are used as coasters. She leaves the door
unlocked to see who'll be scandalised by walking in.
