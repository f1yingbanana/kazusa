# Takane's Room

Neat to the point of severity. A typewriter for reviews sits on a desk beside a
camera with a long lens; every book on the shelf is annotated in red pencil. A
hockey pennant hangs over the bed, and a hot plate for bean-sprout stir-fries
hides in the wardrobe, away from the warden. Through the thin wall, the manga
artist next door can be heard scratching away at night, which Takane reviews
aloud.
