# Cherino's Room

A small, overheated bedroom behind the Presidential Office, reached through a
door behind the portrait. A canopy bed far too big for its owner, a rubber duck
by the washbasin, and a velvet stand holding a row of spare fake mustaches, one
for every mood. After a coup the door is the first thing she barricades, or the
first thing she flees through.
