# Millennium Dormitory 2F West Kitchenette

Two hobs, a microwave, a rice cooker left on warm for midnight noodles, and a
fridge half full of ice-cream tubs and energy drinks. The sink is never quite
empty, and the corridor argues about whose turn it is.
