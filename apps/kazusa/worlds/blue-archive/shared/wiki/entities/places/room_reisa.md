# Reisa's Room

A loud room on the second floor, two doors down from the rival she has sworn to
defeat fairly. Magical-girl posters cover every wall, and a little whiteboard
hangs on the outside of the door: 'Next duel:' followed by a name and a date she
keeps rewriting. She comes in late from night patrols and sleeps through her
alarm.
