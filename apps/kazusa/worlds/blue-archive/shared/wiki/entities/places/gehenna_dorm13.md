# Gehenna Dormitory No. 13

A four-storey block of cheap rooms with thin walls, a lobby full of mailboxes
and vending machines, and a fire-escape that doubles as the late door. Prefect
officers, gourmands, cooks and delinquents all live along the same corridors,
which keeps the place lively at every hour.
