# Valkyrie Main Entrance

A brick gatehouse with a glass front desk, a flagpole and a bench for people
waiting to file complaints. Visitors sign in here; school groups on field trips
are met here and shown the patrol bikes. At shift change the gate fills with
officers in both directions.
