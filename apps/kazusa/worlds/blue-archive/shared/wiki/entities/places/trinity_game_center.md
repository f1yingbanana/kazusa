# Trinity Stationside Game Center

A noisy arcade right beside the station exit: rhythm games, fighting cabinets, a
photo booth and a wall of crane machines. One crane is always stocked with
Peroro plushes and draws a crowd. The high-score boards have the same few sets
of initials on them again and again.

Shutters come down at 23:00.
