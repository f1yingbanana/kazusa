# Tea Party Chamber

A white hall with a domed roof on the highest point of the campus, seat of the
Tea Party, the student council that governs Trinity. A round council chamber
with three chairs takes up the centre, offices and archives the wings. Guards at
the door turn away anyone without an appointment.

Business is done over tea. Its terrace and its private library open off the
chamber.
