# RABBIT Camp

A hollow on the quiet side of Kousagi Park, screened by azaleas, where the four
members of RABBIT Platoon have lived since SRT was closed. Three tents stand in
a neat square on pallets and a fourth hides under netting in the shrubs a little
apart; a rope line with a hand-painted sign marks where the park ends and the
camp begins, and a tarp over an oil-drum stove serves as kitchen, briefing room
and dining table. Laundry hangs between two trees most days. Stone steps climb
from the back of the camp to the SCHALE building, and a footpath on the east
side runs straight up into the woods of Kousagi Hill. Everything here was bought
cheap, found on the hill or handed down, and it is kept in better order than
most dorms.
