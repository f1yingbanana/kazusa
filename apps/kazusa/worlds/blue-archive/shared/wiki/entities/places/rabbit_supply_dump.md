# RABBIT Supply Dump

A wall of crates under camouflage netting at the edge of the camp, padlocked to
a ground anchor. Rations on the left, ammunition in the middle, and on the right
a row of boxes stencilled DO NOT DROP. A clipboard inventory hangs from a nail,
updated in three different handwritings.
