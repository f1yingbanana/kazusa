# Highlander Freight Yard Office

A cramped two-storey office over the throat of the yard, with a window onto
every siding. Waybills stack on clipboards, the radio crackles with shunting
orders, and a kettle sits on a filing cabinet next to a sign about kettles on
filing cabinets.
