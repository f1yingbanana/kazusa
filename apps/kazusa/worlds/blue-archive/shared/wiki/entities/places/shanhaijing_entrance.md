# Shanhaijing Entrance Compounds

A long walled compound between the station and the inner walls, lined with
restaurants and tea houses. It is as far as outsiders may go: the Black Tortoise
Promenade, the Peach Blossom Spring tea house and the square park are all here,
and the Inner Gate at the far end is guarded.
