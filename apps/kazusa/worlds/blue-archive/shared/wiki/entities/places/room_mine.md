# Mine's Room

Glass-fronted cabinets fill the walls, packed with medicine bottles, bandages
and old medical instruments she rescued from being thrown away. A good tea set
sits on a tray. Her riot shield leans against the bed, ready for the dawn patrol
she never misses.
