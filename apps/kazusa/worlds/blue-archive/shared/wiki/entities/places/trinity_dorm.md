# Willow Hall

A peaceful three-storey Gothic dorm behind tall hedges and weeping willows,
small enough that everyone knows everyone. The ground floor is a lounge with
upholstered armchairs and a fireplace, which everyone coming or going passes
through; upstairs, carpeted corridors lead to the rooms. Its residents include
the After-School Sweets Club and two of the Vigilante Crew's night patrollers.

The dorm mother locks the front door at 22:00 and takes roll call from the top
floor down. The side gate is quicker for reaching the shops, and the bus stop at
the corner of the lane has a bus about every 30 minutes, which is why people
usually walk.
