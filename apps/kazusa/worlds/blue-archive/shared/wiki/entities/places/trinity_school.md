# Trinity General School Campus

White marble paths, clipped rose hedges and wrought-iron benches spread over the
hill above Trinity Street. Everything starts at Trinity Square: the main gate,
the Academic Hall, the Central Library and the dining hall face it. Further out
stand the Tea Party Chamber, Justice Task Force Headquarters, the Remedial
Knights's Central Ward, the gymnasium, the club building called the West Annex
and, in a forgotten corner, the Old Library.

The campus is big; a busy student can cross between ten buildings in a day.
Between periods the paths fill with pale uniforms, bookbags on one shoulder and
rifles on the other.
