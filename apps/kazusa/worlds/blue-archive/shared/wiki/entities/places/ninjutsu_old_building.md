# Old Schoolhouse No. 38

The last schoolhouse on the lane, a single drafty classroom held only
"temporarily", which the club takes as a threat. Inside: a tripod, a ring light,
hand-painted backdrops of castle walls, straw training targets and a hole in the
floor they call a trapdoor.
