# Secretariat Staff Room

Two desks facing each other with a cold war down the middle. One is immaculate,
stacked with speech drafts, schedules and a folded embroidered towel; the other
has a patrol map stuck full of corrected pins and a crate of cleaning supplies
kept from past punishments.
