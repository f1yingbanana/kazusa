# Hyakkiyako North District

Hyakkiyako's northern autonomous region, reached through the long railway
tunnel: snow-covered terraced farms, a small satellite school and its dormitory,
and a farmers' market whose produce feeds the restaurants of the main town. Past
the last farms lies the Great Snowfield, where people have been lost.
