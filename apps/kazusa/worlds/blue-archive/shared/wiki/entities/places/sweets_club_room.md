# Sweets Club Room

A sunlit room upstairs in the West Annex, claimed and redecorated by the
After-School Sweets Club. It smells of vanilla, toasted pastry and melted
chocolate. A long wooden table in the middle is surrounded by mismatched
pastel-cushioned chairs and covered in dessert magazines and tea sets.

In the corner stand a small refrigerator of butter, cream, preserves and milk
cartons, a countertop oven and a baking station. The club has never filled its
president's chair.
