# Trinity Cathedral

A vast white cathedral between Trinity Street and the campus, its spires visible
from anywhere in town. It is the headquarters of the Sisterhood, heir to the old
Justina Council of Saints. Anyone may come in to pray or sit in the nave, and
many students do.

Behind the altar are the Sisters' vestry and offices; behind the cathedral, the
cloister where Sisters live and a walled garden that only they may enter. Older
halls beyond the storerooms are closed to everyone.
