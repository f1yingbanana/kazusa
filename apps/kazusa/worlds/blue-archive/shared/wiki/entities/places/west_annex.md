# Trinity West Annex

An old brick building at the western edge of the campus, handed over to small
clubs. The stairs creak, the corridor is lined with hand-lettered signs, and
something sweet is usually baking upstairs.

After classes it fills with club members; at 20:00 the caretaker walks the
corridor rattling her keys and locks the front door.
