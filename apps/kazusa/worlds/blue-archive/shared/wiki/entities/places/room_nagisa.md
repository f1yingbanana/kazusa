# Nagisa's Room

An elegant suite whose real centrepiece is a wall of tea tins, each labelled by
estate and harvest. Paperwork from the Tea Party is piled on the writing desk,
hidden under a silver tray when guests come. Herbs grow in a window box. She
locks the door, checks it, and sometimes checks it again.
