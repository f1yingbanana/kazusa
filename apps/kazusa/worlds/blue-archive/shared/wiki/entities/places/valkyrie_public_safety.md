# Valkyrie Public Safety Bureau

A busy ground-floor room of desks and filing cabinets, a whiteboard of beat
assignments across D.U., and an answering machine that takes more complaints
than some officers do. Lost cats, noise, a stolen bicycle, a gang fight on the
next block: everything starts here.
