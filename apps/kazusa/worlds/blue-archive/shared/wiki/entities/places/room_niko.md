# Niko's Room

A bunk, a desk, and a shelf turned pantry: a rice cooker, a hot plate she is not
strictly allowed, jars of simmered tofu pouches and a label maker that has
tagged every jar, every drawer and her own pillow. Visitors rarely leave without
something wrapped in foil.
