# Wild Hunt Canal Town

Narrow streets of pastel houses between the moat and the station, with bridges
every hundred metres. It lives off the academy: galleries hang student work, the
shops sell everything the store inside won't, and students with outing passes
crowd the cafés on weekends. The north moat beyond the last bridge is reeds and
an old boathouse.
