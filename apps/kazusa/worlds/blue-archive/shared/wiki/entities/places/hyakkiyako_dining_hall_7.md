# Student Dining Hall No. 7

A long wooden dining hall with noren curtains at the counter and benches by the
windows. Lunch is a crush; in the afternoons the kitchen lets students book a
stove for cooking practice, which is how it ends up smelling of burnt
tamagoyaki.
