# Trinity Academy Prison

Stone cells in the basement under Justice Task Force Headquarters, where Trinity
holds students accused of serious offences until their trial. A guard sits at
the end of the corridor day and night. The cells were briefly famous when a
member of the Tea Party was held here after the Eden Treaty crisis.
