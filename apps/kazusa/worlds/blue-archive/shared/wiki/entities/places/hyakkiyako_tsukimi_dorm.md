# Tsukimi-sou Dormitory

A rambling two-storey wooden dorm with sliding doors and tatami rooms, kept by a
strict, kind landlady who cooks breakfast and dinner and counts heads at 22:30.
There is a small bath downstairs, and a veranda on the river side for moon
viewing.
