# Hasumi's Room

An orderly room with a shelf of good tea tins and a bathroom scale turned to
face the wall. A drawer holds an emergency supply of sweets, and a locked
notebook holds three years of Justice Task Force memories. Her lamp is often
still on at 02:00.
