# Abydos Residential Area

Once the richest district in Kivotos, now a town the desert is swallowing. Whole
blocks stand empty, sand drifts against shuttered shopfronts, and the centre has
no food shop still open: for groceries, ramen or a late snack, people walk out
to Suburban Town by the station. The surviving school sits on the north side, a
few apartment blocks still have tenants, and past the last streets the dunes
begin. Much of the land beyond them belongs to Kaiser now.

Trans-Desert Railway trains leave Abydos Central Station for Gehenna and for
Kousagi in D.U. every 90 minutes until 21:00. The desert bus to the Black Market
stops on the station forecourt four times a day, and a local bus runs out to the
border town every half hour.
