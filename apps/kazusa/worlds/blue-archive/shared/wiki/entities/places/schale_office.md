# SCHALE Office

The SCHALE building stands on a hill in Kousagi Ward: one half offices, the
other a residence hall for visiting students. The top-floor office has
floor-to-ceiling windows over the city, Sensei's desk buried in district
paperwork and club budget reports, a leather sofa for visitors, an espresso
machine, and a side table that keeps accumulating pastry boxes brought by
students. Students are welcome during the day; at night the building is locked
unless Sensei is working late.
