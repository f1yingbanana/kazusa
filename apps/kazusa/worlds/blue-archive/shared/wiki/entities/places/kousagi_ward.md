# Kousagi Ward

The outskirts of D.U., where the towers give way to low apartment blocks, small
shops and green hills. Kousagi Station is the southern gateway out of the
capital, the street outside it has everything a student needs, and the SCHALE
building stands on the hill above Kousagi Park. Kaiser Construction once planned
to flatten the area into a "Kousagi Town" development; the plan stalled.
