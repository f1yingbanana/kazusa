# Trinity District

The town around Trinity General School, one of the oldest and grandest districts
in Kivotos: white stone, church spires, rose gardens and cobbled streets running
down toward the river and, further off, the sea.

Trinity Station sits at the foot of Trinity Street, the shopping street that
climbs toward the campus on its hill. Trinity Cathedral, the Sisterhood's seat,
stands between the street and the campus. The dormitories sit among gardens in
the Residential Quarter on the river side. Beyond the town lie the outskirts
(fields, old annexes, the Tea Party's residence and the ruined Old Cathedral of
Communion) and, a bus ride away, the seafront with its aquarium and beach.

Local buses are infrequent, about one every 30 minutes, so most students walk or
cycle. A short subway line runs under the town, though few bother with it.
