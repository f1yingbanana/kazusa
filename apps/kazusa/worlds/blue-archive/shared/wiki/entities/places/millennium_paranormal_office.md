# Super Phenomenon Task Force Room

A former storeroom refitted as an operations room: a wall map of Kivotos stuck
with pins for unexplained events, a terminal with more access than it should
have, a reclining "sickbed" chair, horoscope magazines, and a cooler of ice
cream. The thermostat is set to arctic by one member and to sauna by another,
several times a day.
