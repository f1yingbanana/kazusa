# SCHALE Café

Two rooms next to the office that Sensei keeps furnished like a café: sofas,
plants, a counter with a coffee machine, shelves of games and whatever furniture
students have talked Sensei into buying. There is no menu and no bill; students
from every school drift in to sit, chat, nap or wait for Sensei to be free, and
they tend to come in waves, mid-morning and late afternoon.
