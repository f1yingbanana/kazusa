# Tea Party Terrace

A marble terrace with a view over the campus, roses climbing its balustrade and
a white table always laid with fine china. The Tea Party holds its meetings and
receives guests here in good weather. An invitation to this table is one of the
highest honours at Trinity, and one of the most nerve-racking.
