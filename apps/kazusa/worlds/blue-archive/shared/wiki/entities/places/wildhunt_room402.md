# Dormitory No. 5, Room 402

Home and headquarters of the Free Trade Cartel: Miyo, Fuyu and Ritsu. Fuyu's
side is a tangle of modded televisions under a catapult blueprint; Miyo's desk
holds a row of vintage typewriters, campus maps criss-crossed with routes, and a
resignation letter she has never handed in. Under Ritsu's bed are chisels and
rock samples, and a half-carved stone sits on the windowsill.
