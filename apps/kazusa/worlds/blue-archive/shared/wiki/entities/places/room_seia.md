# Seia's Room

A warm, over-comfortable room where she is still treated as an invalid. Books
are stacked in towers around the bed, a jigsaw lies half finished on the
blanket, and a feeder hangs outside the window so the small birds of the park
come right up to the glass. She sleeps a great deal, and dreams more than she
would like.
