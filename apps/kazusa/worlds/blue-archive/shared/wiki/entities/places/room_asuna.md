# Asuna's Room

Maid uniforms drying on a rack, snack wrappers, a dartboard that has clearly
been attacked, and a bowl of lucky charms and trinkets picked up wherever she
wandered that day. She forgets to lock the door, and the neighbours have learned
to live with music at 02:00.
