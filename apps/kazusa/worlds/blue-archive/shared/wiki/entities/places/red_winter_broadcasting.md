# Red Bear Broadcasting Station

A brick tower topped with an aerial and a red star. It carries "Red Bear", the
academy's only broadcast, to every loudspeaker on campus: news on the hour,
marching music, weather (snow), the president's addresses and a great deal of
praise for the president. Because whoever holds the microphone holds the
academy, the tower is the first building every coup takes, and its door has been
replaced several times.
