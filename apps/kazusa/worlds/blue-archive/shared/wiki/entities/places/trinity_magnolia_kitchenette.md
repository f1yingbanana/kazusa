# Magnolia Hall West Kitchenette

A narrow kitchenette with a kettle, a toaster, a shelf of everyone's tea tins
and a snack drawer with a lock that doesn't work. At two in the morning there is
often someone at its little table who couldn't sleep, and sometimes two.
