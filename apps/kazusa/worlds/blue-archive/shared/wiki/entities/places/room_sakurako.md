# Sakurako's Room

A plain whitewashed cell at the end of the cloister nearest the vestry; she
seldom leaves the cathedral. The confession and event schedule for every Sister
is pinned above the desk. Beside it lies a fashion magazine annotated in the
margins as carefully as scripture. Something sparkly is folded away at the back
of the wardrobe.
