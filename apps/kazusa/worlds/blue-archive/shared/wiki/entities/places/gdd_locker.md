# Yuzu's Locker

Big enough for exactly one person sitting with her knees up. Yuzu has padded the
walls, run a cable in for a small monitor and a headset, and clipped a fan to
the vent slits. Through the gap she can hear the whole club room without anyone
looking at her. Knocking is allowed; opening it is not.
