# Kousagi Park Washhouse

A low concrete block near the park's centre with restrooms, a row of outdoor
sinks and a tap the groundskeeper never turns off. Joggers fill bottles here;
the campers from the hollow wash faces, socks and mess tins here every morning.
A notice about "no overnight camping" has been politely covered by another
notice.
