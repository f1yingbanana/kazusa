# Millennium Campus Convenience Store

A bright little store that never closes, stocked for people who work strange
hours: shelves of energy drinks, onigiri, instant noodles, solder and USB cables
next to the gum. After midnight the customers are the same faces in pajamas and
lab coats, pretending not to see each other.
