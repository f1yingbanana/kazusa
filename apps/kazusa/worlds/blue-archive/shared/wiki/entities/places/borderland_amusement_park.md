# Borderland Amusement Park

An amusement park built right across the Trinity–Gehenna border. The main
entrance and the Ferris wheel are on the Trinity side; the rear gate and half
the rides are in Gehenna. Students from both schools come on weekends, and so do
their quarrels.

Jurisdiction stops at the border line painted through the middle of the park,
which makes chasing anyone a headache for the Justice Task Force and the Prefect
Team alike. After closing, the park is dark and silent, and the Ferris wheel is
a strange, peaceful place to be at night.
