# Federal Library Closed Stacks

Rows of shelves that go back further than anyone has catalogued: first editions,
banned pamphlets, fanzines in protective sleeves. One bay near the end has been
turned into a napping nest of blankets and cushions, guarded by a sign that says
reading only.
