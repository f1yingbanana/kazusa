# Millennium Tower

The tallest building on campus and the seat of Seminar, Millennium's student
council. The lobby is open to anyone with business, usually a club head with a
budget request clutched to her chest. Above it are Seminar's offices and
records, the conference room near the top, and a floor of locked rooms where the
guards at the stairwell take their job seriously. A scorch mark on one upper
window frame is from a rocket that was not supposed to go that way.
