# Otogi's Room

A bed made in a hurry, a desk covered in a radio she is repairing for nobody in
particular, and a box of small gifts strangers have pressed on her: keychains,
candy, a hand-knitted cup sleeve. The window latch has been oiled, which says
more than anything else in the room.
