# Labor Party Bunkhouse Canteen

Long tables, a serving hatch, an urn of tea and a pudding ration chart on the
wall that is watched very carefully. Crews eat breakfast here before their shift
and argue over dinner afterwards. The back door leads straight through the fence
onto Shiratori Reconstruction Area 5.
