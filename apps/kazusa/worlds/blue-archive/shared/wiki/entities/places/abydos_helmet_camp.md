# Kata-Kata Helmet Gang Camp

The Kata-Kata Helmet Gang's base: tents, scrap-metal shacks and a bonfire ring
among half-buried buildings. The gang used to harass the school with someone
else's money behind them, and they still raid the town when they are bored.
