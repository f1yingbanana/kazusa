# Kokoro's Cabin

Her diving gear hangs along the bulkhead in exactly the order she will put it
on, and the pre-dive checklist taped beside it has been laminated twice. A small
frying pan lives under the bunk and a waterproof songbook under the pillow,
where nobody needs to know about it.
