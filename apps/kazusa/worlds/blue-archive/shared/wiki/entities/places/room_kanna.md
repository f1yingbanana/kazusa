# Kanna Ogata's Room

Room 301, at the quiet end of the third floor. The bed is barely used and the
kitchenette not at all; the wall above the desk is a corkboard of photos and red
string from cases open and closed. Two shelves hold detective novels and DVDs of
old crime films, alphabetized.
