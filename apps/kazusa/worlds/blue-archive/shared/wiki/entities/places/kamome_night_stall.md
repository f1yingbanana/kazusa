# Menyu Suzu

A wooden food cart with a red lantern and a little bell over the counter, parked
by the terminal fence every night. The owner grills chicken skewers over
charcoal and pours oolong tea, and remembers orders rather than faces. Its
regulars are dockworkers, sailors off the night watch and the occasional
off-duty officer who wants to eat where nobody knows her.
