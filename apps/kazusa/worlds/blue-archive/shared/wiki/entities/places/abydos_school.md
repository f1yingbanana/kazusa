# Abydos High School Campus

Abydos was once big enough to get lost in. What is left is a walled campus on
the north side of town: the Main Building, which the students sweep clean every
morning, a mostly closed annex, a gymnasium, a cracked pool, a field the sand is
slowly burying and a condemned wooden schoolhouse at the far edge. The gate
posts carry old bullet scars from the Helmet gangs' raids. Delivery trucks and
the occasional visitor from SCHALE are the only traffic.
