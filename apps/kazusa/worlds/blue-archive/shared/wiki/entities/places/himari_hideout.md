# Clubhouse Annex Room 13

Himari's secret hideaway, reserved by quietly editing the school's room
registry. Inside: a plush recliner with a blanket, a tarot deck laid out on a
side table, a record player stacked with enka, horoscope magazines, and a
terminal patched into the campus systems, including the broadcast speakers. It's
the one room on campus that "Big Sister" was never allowed to see.
