# Haruhabara Electric Town

Neon signs stacked six storeys high, speakers blaring anime themes, and
sidewalks crowded with students hunting parts, games and figures. Millennium
students come here straight after school. The Electric Street runs from the
station past the Multimedia Center, the Video Hall and dozens of narrow shops.
