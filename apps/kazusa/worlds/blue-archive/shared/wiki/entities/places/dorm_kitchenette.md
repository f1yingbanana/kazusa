# Willow Hall Kitchenette

A small shared kitchen off the lounge with a kettle, a two-burner stove and a
fridge crammed with labelled snacks and milk cartons. Residents grab breakfast
here, brew tea in the evening and now and then attempt baking, with mixed
results for the smoke alarm.
