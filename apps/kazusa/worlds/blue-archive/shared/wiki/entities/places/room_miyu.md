# Miyu's Tent

The fourth tent sits a little apart, draped in netting and leaves so well that
delivery drivers walk past it. Inside are a thermos of sweet tea, a rifle
wrapped in a blanket like something asleep, and rows of plain pebbles in an egg
carton, each compartment labelled with a mood.
