# Tomoe's Room

Framed portraits of the president on every wall, speech drafts in labelled
folders and a megaphone on the windowsill. On the nightstand lies a hand-copied
songbook of lullabies. Tomoe is usually at the Office Building until late and
comes back only to sleep. The door across the corridor belongs to the Security
Committee chair, which makes coup weeks awkward on the second floor.
