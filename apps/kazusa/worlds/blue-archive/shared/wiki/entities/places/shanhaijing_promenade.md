# Black Tortoise Promenade

Once a middling shopping street, now the best eating in Kivotos: lanterns,
steaming baskets in every window and hawkers calling out the day's dishes. The
merchant association runs it from the main restaurant, and the Genryumon
inspectors are never welcome.
