# Red Winter Main Building

A stepped tower with a star on its spire, far larger than the student body
needs: whole floors are shut and unheated. Homerooms are on the lower floors
near the boilers. The two lifts are slow and the staircases are cold, so the
cloakroom on the ground floor is jammed with students shedding coats and boots
just before the 08:30 bell.
