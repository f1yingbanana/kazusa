# Trinity Market

An iron-and-glass market hall just off Trinity Street. Stalls sell fruit and
vegetables, loose tea from every school in Kivotos, cut flowers and seed
packets, cheese, and hot snacks. Saturday mornings are crowded, and the
Vigilante Crew patrol it because pickpockets love a crowd.
