# Third-Floor Storeroom, Abydos Main Building

Behind a wall of stacked desks at the back of an unused third-floor storeroom,
someone has built a napping fort: cushions, a duck-down quilt and an alarm clock
with the bell taped silent. Hardly anyone knows it is here.
