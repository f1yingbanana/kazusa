# Valkyrie Correctional Facility

The Corrections Bureau's prison, ringed by high walls and searchlights on the
edge of downtown. Holding cells, a visiting room with glass partitions and a
yard that inmates see for an hour a day. Its record is famous for the wrong
reasons: several notorious prisoners have walked out of it.
