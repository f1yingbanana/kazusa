# Utaha's Room

Half bedroom, half workshop: a pegboard of tools she would not let anyone else
touch, bins of robot parts, a lab coat with proud grease stains, and a second
charging dock in case the big robot visits. The bed is often unslept in; the
hangar keeps her late.
