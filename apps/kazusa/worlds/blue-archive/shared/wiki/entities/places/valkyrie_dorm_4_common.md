# Valkyrie Dorm 4 Common Room

Sofas facing a television, a kitchenette with a kettle, a microwave and a fridge
full of labelled lunches, and a rack of the day's papers. Officers coming off
late shifts eat here when the cafeteria has already closed, which is most
nights.
