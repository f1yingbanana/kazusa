# Aoba's Room

The room furthest down the corridor from the twins, on purpose. A workbench
corner holds a toaster, a radio and a fan in various states of repair, and the
mug on the desk has been glued back together three times. Earplugs and
motion-sickness pills sit by the bed, both well used.
