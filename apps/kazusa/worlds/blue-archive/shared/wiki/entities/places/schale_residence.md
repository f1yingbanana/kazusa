# SCHALE Residence Hall

The other half of the SCHALE building: a residence wing where students from any
school can rest, study, cook and stay over when work runs late. It has a resting
room, a bookable self-study room, a cafeteria with practice kitchens, a small
arcade of retro machines, an Angel 24 and a vegetable garden on the roof.
