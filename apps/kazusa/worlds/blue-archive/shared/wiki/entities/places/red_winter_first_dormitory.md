# First Shared Dormitory

A five-storey prefab block between the campus and 3rd Street, the cheapest
housing the academy offers and so where most students live. Rooms are small and
rent is almost nothing; the radiators knock all night, and hot water is a rumour
that comes true only in the early morning. The front door is open 06:00–23:00
and a dorm warden takes roll call at 22:30 in the fireplace lounge. Residents
eat at the Third Communal Cafeteria or make tea and toast at the lounge samovar.
