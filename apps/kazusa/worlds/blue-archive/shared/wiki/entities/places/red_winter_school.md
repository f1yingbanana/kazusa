# Red Winter Federal Academy Campus

A walled campus of heavy stone buildings under a permanent crust of snow. The
Main Building's spire, where classes are held, can be seen from anywhere in the
district; in front of it the Office Building faces Red Mustache Revolution
Plaza. Around them sit the Federal Library, the Third Communal Cafeteria, the
Labor Party's converted boiler house, the Red Bear radio tower and the parade
ground.

State Guard sentries stand at the main gate from 06:30 until it is chained at
22:00. Shovelled paths connect the buildings; everything else is knee-deep.
