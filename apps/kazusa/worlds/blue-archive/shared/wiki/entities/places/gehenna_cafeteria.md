# Gehenna Dining Hall

A cavernous hall of long tables under iron chandeliers, feeding thousands from a
tiny club. The menu is whatever the School Lunch Club could make in time; the
queue is the most dangerous place on campus at noon, and sometimes the Gourmet
Research Society takes the whole kitchen hostage.
