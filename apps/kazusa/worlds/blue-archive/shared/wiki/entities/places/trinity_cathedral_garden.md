# Trinity Cathedral Garden

A walled park behind the cathedral with lawns, old trees, a fish pond and a
gravel clearing where Sisters train. It is kept spotless by one tireless Sister,
who can usually be found raking leaves. A bench under the cedar is the best
place in Trinity to be left alone.
