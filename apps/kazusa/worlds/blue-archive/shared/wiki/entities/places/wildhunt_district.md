# Wild Hunt Integrated Arts District

A star fortress of an art academy, its bastions and Italianate towers surrounded
by moats and canals. Inside the walls, every student lives in a dormitory the
Arts Council assigns and the Dorm Supervisory Unit polices. There is one gate,
and the inspection there is famous: bags are searched, unapproved pigments and
even mugs are confiscated, and nobody leaves without an outing pass.

Outside the walls, a small canal town of galleries, cafés and craft shops lives
off the academy. The Western Line terminus at the end of the quay has trains to
D.U. Central every 15 minutes. Things the academy store won't sell have a way of
coming over the walls anyway.
