# Flagship Olympus

The largest ship in the flotilla and the closest thing Odyssey has to a main
building: the Island leadership works from its bridge, lessons run on its middle
decks and the whole school eats on its mess deck. Its long weather deck is the
school's parade ground. Divers swim beside its hull at night when nobody is
supposed to be swimming.
