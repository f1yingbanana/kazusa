# Samovar Used Books

Three narrow rooms of secondhand books stacked to the ceiling, with a samovar on
the counter for customers who stay long, which is most of them. The back room
has boxes of old fanzines and Red Bear back issues. Library volunteers and
critics argue here over who saw a rare edition first.
