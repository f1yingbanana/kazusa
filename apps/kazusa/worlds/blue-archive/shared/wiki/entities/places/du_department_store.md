# Kivotos Central Department Store

The city's flagship department store. Cosmetics and accessories on the ground
floor, clothes and school-uniform tailoring above, a large bookshop and a
stationery floor, then toys, where the Momo Friends corner has a ball pool and a
limited-edition shelf that sells out on release mornings. The rooftop food court
is a favourite meeting spot for students from different schools.
