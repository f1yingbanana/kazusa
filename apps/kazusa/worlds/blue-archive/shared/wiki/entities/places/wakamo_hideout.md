# Storehouse Loft on Kousagi Hill

The loft of an old shrine storehouse no one has visited in years, reached by a
ladder. Fox masks hang from the rafters, a parasol leans by the round window,
and that window looks straight across the park at the SCHALE office. Wakamo
sleeps here between appearances.
