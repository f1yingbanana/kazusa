# Old Schoolhouse Lane

A cracked path at the back of the grounds lined with numbered wooden
schoolhouses from before the academy was rebuilt. Clubs too small or too
argumentative for the main buildings take them over one by one, and the
Hyakkaryouran keeps its clubhouse at the far end.
