# Red Winter Parade Ground

A wide field with a reviewing stand at one end. Mornings it is drill and
inspections; after classes it becomes the academy's battlefield for snowball
fights, which the president considers good for discipline. In the coldest weeks
it is flooded and freezes into a rough skating rink.
