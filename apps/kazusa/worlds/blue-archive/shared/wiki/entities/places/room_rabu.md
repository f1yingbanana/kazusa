# Rabu's Room

The ride operator's booth above the ballroom, with a window over the gang's bunk
mats so she can see them all. A cot, a rack of helmets buffed to a shine and a
jar of coins fished from under vending machines. A rubber jar-opening grip is
hidden in the desk drawer, where none of her girls will ever find it.
