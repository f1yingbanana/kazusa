# Abydos Desert

Most of Abydos lies under sand. Streets run into dunes, rooftops poke out of
them, and old rail lines vanish under drifts. Much of the land was sold to
Kaiser to pay the school's debts and is fenced and patrolled. Anyone going far
takes water, a map and someone who knows the way back.
