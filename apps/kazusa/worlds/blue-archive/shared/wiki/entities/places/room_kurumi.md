# Kurumi Takakura's Room

The cleanest room in the building, and she would like that noticed. A cleaning
caddy stands by the door, the mini-fridge holds strawberry, banana and melon
milk in rows, and a sticker album lives under the pillow where nobody will see
it. Her shield leans on its own rack by the bed.
