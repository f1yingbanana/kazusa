# Red Winter District

A vast, snowbound district on the far northern edge of Kivotos. Stern apartment
blocks and onion-domed halls stand among the taiga, and snow-plough trams keep
the main streets open between Central Station, the 3rd Street shops, the academy
campus and the dormitory. Beyond the last tram stop the road climbs into the
mountains: the Former Institute on the outskirts, and further up, forestry
depots, security posts and the remote No. 183 facility.

The academy's government changes often enough that statues are built to be
swapped out, and the radio station is the first building every coup seizes.
Warmth of any kind, a working radiator, a hot bath, a thaw, is treated as a
small festival.
