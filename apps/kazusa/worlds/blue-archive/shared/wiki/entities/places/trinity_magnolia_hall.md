# Magnolia Hall

The largest dormitory at Trinity: a four-storey stone building wrapped around a
magnolia garden, closest to the campus gate. Hundreds of students live here. The
ground floor has the entrance hall, the dorm mother's office and a dining room;
the residents' corridors go by wings.

Roll call is at 22:30, floor by floor. Notable corridors: the second-floor East
Wing, where the Make-Up Work Club lives side by side, and the third-floor West
Wing, home to several Justice Task Force officers.
