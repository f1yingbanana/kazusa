# Kotone's Cabin

A ham-radio set fills the desk, with logbooks of call signs from listeners on
land stacked beside it. Large-print books line the shelf, a kelp-tea kettle sits
on the floor, and every drawer has a spare pair of thick glasses in it, just in
case.
