# Shanhaijing District

A walled district of tiled roofs, pavilions and gardens at the foot of Kunlun
Mountain. Shanhaijing keeps a closed-door policy: visitors may enter the
entrance compounds by the station, a long strip of restaurants, tea houses and
the Black Tortoise Promenade, but going through the Inner Gate needs a pass.
Inside are the Central Special Zone, the Genryumon's old palace and the student
courtyards.
