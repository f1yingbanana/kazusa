# Abydos Central Station

Once the grand gateway to the richest district in Kivotos, now mostly sealed off
by sand. One platform still runs, with a timetable pinned by the gate:
Trans-Desert Railway trains to Gehenna and to Kousagi in D.U., a handful each
day. Someone sells snacks out front most days.
