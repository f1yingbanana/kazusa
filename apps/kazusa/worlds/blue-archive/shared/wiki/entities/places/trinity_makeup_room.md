# Make-Up Work Club Annex

An old two-storey school annex on the edge of Trinity, where the Make-Up Work
Club was sent for its boot camp. The members cleaned it from top to bottom,
scrubbed the outdoor pool and lived in its bedrooms until they passed. The study
hall still has their desks pushed together and their mock exams pinned up, and
the laundry line in the yard still has their pegs on it.

The club kept the key. They come out after class to study, and on weekends to
swim, drill or just be together, then walk back to the dorm before roll call.
