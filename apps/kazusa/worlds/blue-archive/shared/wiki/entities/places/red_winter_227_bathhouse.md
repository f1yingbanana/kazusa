# No. 227 Bathhouse

All that is left of a grand hot spring resort that was built on the class's
spring and then blown up: one stone tub, renovated, under a wooden roof, with
changing rooms and staff quarters behind. Evening visitors come out on the
shuttle train, soak, and sometimes miss the last one back.
