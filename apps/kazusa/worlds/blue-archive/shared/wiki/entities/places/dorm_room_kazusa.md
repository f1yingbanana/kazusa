# Kazusa's Room

Kazusa's room on the second floor: tidy and a little dim, curtains half drawn. A
bass guitar rests on its stand beside the desk, and a drawer is reserved for
sweets. It is hers alone; she locks the door whenever she leaves, and does not
open it for just anyone.
