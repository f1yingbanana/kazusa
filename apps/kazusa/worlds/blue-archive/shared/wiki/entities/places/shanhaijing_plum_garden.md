# Plum Blossom Garden Main Building

A low schoolhouse in a garden of plum trees, with a painted entrance gate, small
classrooms with tiny chairs, and a kitchen that makes snacks at three. The
instructors are students themselves, and everyone in Shanhaijing respects them.
