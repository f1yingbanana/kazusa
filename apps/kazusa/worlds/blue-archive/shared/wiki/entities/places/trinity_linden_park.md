# Linden Park

A green park in the middle of the Residential Quarter: avenues of lime trees, a
duck pond, a playground and benches facing west for the sunset. Early risers do
their morning exercises on the lawn, children from the neighbourhood fill the
playground after school, and every Sunday a flea market of folding tables takes
over the main avenue.
