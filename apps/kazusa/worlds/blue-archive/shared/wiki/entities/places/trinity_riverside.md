# Trinity Riverside Path

A paved path following the river along the bottom of town, with willows, iron
benches and lamps that stay lit until 23:00. Runners use it at dawn, families
picnic on its grassy banks at weekends, and in the evening it is quiet enough to
think. A gelato stand opens on fine days.
