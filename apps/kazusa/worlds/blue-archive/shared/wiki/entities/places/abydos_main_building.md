# Abydos Main Building

A three-storey concrete building with sandbags at the entrance and most of its
rooms closed. The handful of students who remain keep one corridor of it alive:
the Foreclosure Task Force's room, a single classroom, the library and a small
store, with a generator and the water pump in the basement. Upstairs, whole
floors of empty classrooms collect dust and the odd napper.
