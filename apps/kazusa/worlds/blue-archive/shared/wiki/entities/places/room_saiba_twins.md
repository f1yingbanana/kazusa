# Momoi and Midori's Room

A line of tape on the floor divides it. One half is a console tower, tangled
controllers, event posters, scenario notebooks and cod-roe cracker bags; the
other is a drawing tablet, art books and claw-machine plushies. On the tidy
side's shelf sits an old handheld that no longer turns on, kept on purpose.
