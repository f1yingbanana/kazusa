# Arius Squad Shelter Kitchen

A scrubbed table, four mismatched chairs and a two-burner stove. Supply crates
from Trinity are stacked by the door and the parole terms are taped up where
everyone can see them. On weekdays the lunches for the walk to Trinity are
wrapped here before anyone leaves.
