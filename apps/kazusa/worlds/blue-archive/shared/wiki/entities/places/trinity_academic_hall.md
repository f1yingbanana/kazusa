# Trinity Academic Hall

The grand teaching building: white colonnades, vaulted ceilings, stained glass
and polished floors. First-year homerooms are on the ground floor, second-years
on the first floor and third-years on the top floor, with lecture halls in the
east wing.

Homeroom is at 08:30 and lessons run until 15:20. Between periods the corridors
are loud with talk of exams and the clink of slung rifles. A narrow door on the
top floor leads up into the bell tower.
