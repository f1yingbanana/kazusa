# Ichika's Room

A room full of things she meant to get into: a guitar gathering dust in the
corner, beginner manuals for knitting and photography, and boxes of flea-market
finds she hasn't unpacked. It looks relaxed, as she does, until you notice
everything is exactly where she left it.
