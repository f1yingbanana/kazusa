# 12th Millennium Stadium

An all-weather stadium with retractable seating and a big screen, used for
athletic festivals and inter-school games. On game days part-time sellers climb
the stands with drink tanks on their backs. The baseball grounds, the Fitness
Center and the infirmary cluster behind it.
