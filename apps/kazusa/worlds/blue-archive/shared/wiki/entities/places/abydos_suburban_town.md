# Abydos Suburban Town

When the centre emptied, the shops that survived moved out here, near the
station and the cheaper rents. It is a few streets of low buildings with awnings
against the sun: the covered market, a ramen stall, the district's only 24-hour
store, a junk-and-antiques dealer and a small park. Flyers for part-time jobs
paper every lamppost. The local bus to the border town and the desert bus to the
Black Market both leave from the station forecourt.
