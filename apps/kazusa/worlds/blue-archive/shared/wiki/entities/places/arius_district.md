# Arius District

A sunless district of broken stone streets and Gothic ruins under Kivotos, split
by a civil war a decade ago and ruled for years by an adult who taught its
children that everything is in vain. Since Arius was put on parole, Trinity and
Valkyrie repair crews have patched roofs, strung work lamps along the streets
and reopened the school grounds. The remaining students live in the old
barracks; the paroled former squad keeps a shelter of its own nearby.

The only way in or out is the marked route through Trinity's catacombs, about
fifty minutes on foot. On weekdays a column of Arius students walks it each
morning to exam classes at the Trinity Old School Building and back each
evening. The Basilica where the old rites were held stands roped off at the
district's far end.
