# Trinity Street

Trinity's shopping street, sometimes still called Church Street: cobblestones,
striped awnings and flower boxes, running uphill from Trinity Station toward the
campus. At the station end are the game center and the Trinity Galleria mall;
halfway up are Cafe Mille-Feuille, a bakery, a 24-hour parfait parlour and the
covered Trinity Market; at the top, the dorm end, the Angel 24.

Students flood it after class and on weekends, and the Vigilante Crew walk it at
night. Most shops close at 20:00, but some, including the Angel 24 and the
parfait parlour, stay open all night.
