# Facility No. 183

An old research station on a ridge high in the snowy mountains, used to hold the
science and engineering students the Office has sentenced. Its residents cannot
leave without permission and do commissions for the Office in exchange for fuel
and food, which a supply truck brings up twice a week. The building is cold and
full of salvaged machinery; the garage holds a tank under construction.
