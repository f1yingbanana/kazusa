# No. 227 Bathhouse Milk Bar

A wooden counter by the exit, a chilled cabinet of glass bottles and a
chalkboard menu: fruit milk, coffee milk, homemade kompot and mors. It is
staffed by a member of the special class in the evenings, and the drinks are
only sometimes strictly non-alcoholic.
