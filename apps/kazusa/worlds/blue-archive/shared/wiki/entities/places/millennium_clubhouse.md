# Millennium Clubhouse

Three storeys of club rooms along one long corridor per floor, every door
plastered with posters, warnings and recruitment flyers. The Game Development
Department, Veritas and the Paranormal Affairs Department all live here, along
with a students' lounge and, at the back, an older annex of rooms nobody uses.
Noise complaints and power-draw warnings are a weekly event.
