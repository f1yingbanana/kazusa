# Kanesaki Port

The capital's harbour, where cargo ships unload into long rows of warehouses and
merchant guilds keep their offices. The Carpaccio Family handles security on the
piers. A small beach curves along one end of the port, not the kind anyone
expects, and Odyssey Maritime School's ships berth at Port Terminal 3.
