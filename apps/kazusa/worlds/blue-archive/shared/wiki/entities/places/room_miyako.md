# Miyako's Tent

The platoon leader's tent, pitched at the head of the square. Her bedroll is
folded to SRT standard and a laminated card of the academy's ideals is clipped
to the ridge pole. The hutch by the entrance belongs to Bun Bun, who gets the
dry corner when it rains.
