# Momiji's Room

Manga and fanzines in protective sleeves stacked in careful towers to the
ceiling, sorted by a system only she understands. A binder on the desk holds
every rejected book-purchase request she has filed, neatly dated. She often
sleeps in the library instead and keeps the door double-padlocked.
