# Terminal 3 Dive Pier

A low concrete pier at the seaward end of the terminal where workboats tie up
and divers go in. A steel ladder drops into the water, an air compressor
grumbles under a lean-to, and the Diving Club keeps its gear shed at the root of
the pier. At dusk it is the emptiest spot in the port.
