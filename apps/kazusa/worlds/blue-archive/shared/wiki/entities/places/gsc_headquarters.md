# Sanctum Tower

The tallest structure in Kivotos and the seat of the General Student Council,
the city-wide authority over the academies. The lower floors are public counters
for permits, transfers and complaints, always with a queue. Security gates guard
the lifts to the council's offices; scaffolding still wraps the floors damaged
in the recent crises, and work crews come and go.
