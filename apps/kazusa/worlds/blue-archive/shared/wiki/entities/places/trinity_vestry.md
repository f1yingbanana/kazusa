# Trinity Cathedral Vestry

The working heart of the Sisterhood: a robing room, offices where Sisters keep
schedules and records, and two confessional booths where students come to talk.
Past the offices, a corridor of storerooms leads to older halls kept locked; the
keys hang on one heavy ring.
