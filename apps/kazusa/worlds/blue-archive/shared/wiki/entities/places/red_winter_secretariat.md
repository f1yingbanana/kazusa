# Red Winter Office Building

A columned building with a balcony over Revolution Plaza, from which decrees are
read. Mustache sculptures, the symbol of the president's authority, stand on
every landing and over every door. Inside are the Presidential Office, the staff
room of the Secretary General and the Security Committee, the State Guard's duty
room and the registry where every student's record lives on punch cards.
Petitions, complaints and purchase requests are dropped in a brass box by the
door and are mostly rejected. The State Guard holds the door around the clock;
in a coup, it is usually the second building taken, after the radio tower.
