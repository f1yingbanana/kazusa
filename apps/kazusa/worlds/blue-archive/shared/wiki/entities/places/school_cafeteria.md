# Trinity Dining Hall

A high-ceilinged hall with long tables, arched windows and a serving line with a
set meal of the day. Nearly every student on campus eats lunch here on weekdays,
so it is loud and crowded at noon.

The dessert counter by the entrance sells cream puffs and pudding that run out
within minutes. Breakfast and dinner are quieter, mostly students from dorms
without a kitchen.
