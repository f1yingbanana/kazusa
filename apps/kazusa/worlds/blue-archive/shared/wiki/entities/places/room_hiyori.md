# Hiyori's Room

Magazine towers line every wall, fished out of recycling bins and sorted by
issue. She still sleeps in the sleeping bag rather than the bed frame, and the
huge backpack by the door is always packed, just in case. A portable battery
someone gave her keeps her phone alive.
