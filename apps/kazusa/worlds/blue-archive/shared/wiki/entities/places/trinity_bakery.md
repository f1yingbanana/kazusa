# Boulangerie Lumière

A narrow bakery that smells of butter from halfway down the street. The bread is
so good that the shelves empty at every mealtime, and regulars know to come
early or go hungry. The owner also bakes cakes to order for anyone who asks a
day ahead; it is the best-kept secret on the street.
