# Abydos Old Schoolhouse 3

The third of the old wooden school buildings, the only one still standing. It
was condemned long ago; the windows are boarded and some floors give underfoot.
Students dare each other to go in after dark, and forgotten things from Abydos's
better days still turn up in its cupboards.
