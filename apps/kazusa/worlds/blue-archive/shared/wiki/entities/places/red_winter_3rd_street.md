# Red Winter 3rd Street Shopping District

The district's main shopping street, roofed over with glass arcades so the snow
stays outside. It runs from the Central Station square up toward the campus: a
sweets shop the president visits on foot, a used bookshop the library crowd
haunts, the Red Bear printing house and its newsstand, a warm café, a pharmacy,
an Angel 24 that never closes, and at the far end the open-air Beluga Market.
After classes it is where everyone ends up.
