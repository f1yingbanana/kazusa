# Yatoura Fishing Village

A cluster of tiled houses around a small harbour down the coast from Kanesaki
Port, reached by a winding coast road and an hourly bus. Shrimp boats go out
before dawn and the catch is auctioned on the quay by seven. The villagers have
their legends about the sea, and still talk about the summer the shrimp went
missing and a squad of student soldiers came down the coast to find out why.
