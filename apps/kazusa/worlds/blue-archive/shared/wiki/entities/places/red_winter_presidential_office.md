# Red Winter Presidential Office

A long, red-carpeted room on the top floor. The desk stands on a riser so its
occupant looks down at visitors; the chair has three cushions on it. A fridge
reserved for pudding hums in the corner, medals for every occasion wait in glass
cases, and an oil portrait of the president hangs behind the desk. A door behind
the portrait leads to the president's private room.
