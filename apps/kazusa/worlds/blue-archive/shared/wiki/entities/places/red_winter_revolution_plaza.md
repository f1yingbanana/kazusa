# Red Mustache Revolution Plaza

The academy's ceremonial square, paved under the snow and fronted by the
columned Office Building on one side and the Red Bear radio tower on the other,
around a giant statue of the president wearing an outsized mustache, floodlit
day and night. The statue's bolts are kept loose because it changes after every
coup and changes back after every counter-coup. Speeches are given from a wooden
podium most lunchtimes; the Red Winter Book Festival fills the square with
stalls once a year, and in between it is where every crowd of students crossing
campus meets.
