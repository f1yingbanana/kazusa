# Trinity Gymnasium

A big sports hall with a sprung floor, a weights room, and an equipment store
with a door that sticks shut. Behind it is a 25-metre outdoor pool, drained in
winter and fenced off at night.

Sports clubs take over after 16:00; early mornings belong to the few who train
alone.
