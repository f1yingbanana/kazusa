# Odyssey Fleet Berth, Port Terminal 3

Odyssey Maritime School is a flotilla, not a campus: dozens of ships moored
along Port Terminal 3, gathered around the flagship _Olympus_. Its only land is
this terminal, rented from the port. The school is run by its "Island"
leadership aboard the flagship; students live, study and work afloat, follow
sailors' taboos to the letter, and brew kelp where others brew tea. Every two
years the flotilla casts off for about a year at sea with no contact but the
General Student Council. Gangways are guarded and visitors sign in at the gate
office.
