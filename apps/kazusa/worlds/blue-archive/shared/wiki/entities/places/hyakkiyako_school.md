# Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy Grounds

The academy is an alliance of clubs rather than a centrally run school, and its
grounds show it: a main building crowned by the castle keep, club buildings each
flying their own banners, a great walled garden, and a lane of old numbered
schoolhouses that clubs squat in. Students come in through Castle Square, where
the tour groups stop.
