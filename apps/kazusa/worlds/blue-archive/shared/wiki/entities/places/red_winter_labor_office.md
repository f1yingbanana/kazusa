# Labor Party Workforce Office

The old campus boiler house, still warm from the pipes, taken over by the Public
Works Department. A job board lists construction crews across Kivotos and who is
short of hands; placards are stacked by the door and megaphones charge on a
shelf. Students from bankrupt schools come here looking for work, and anyone who
needs urgent hands sent somewhere asks here first. Behind a curtain at the back,
a row of camp cots by the warm pipes is where organisers sleep when they are
home between contracts. Thursday nights it fills for strike meetings and
arguments about pudding.
