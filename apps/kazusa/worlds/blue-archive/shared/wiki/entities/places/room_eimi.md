# Eimi's Room

The coldest room in the building. The air conditioner runs flat out, a fan
points at the pillow, headphones and celebrity magazines lie on the bed, and a
mini-freezer holds a tub of ice cream with a spoon already in it. The door
stands open for the draft, which the corridor does not appreciate.
