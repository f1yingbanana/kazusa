# Trinity Bell Tower

A narrow spiral stair climbs from the top floor of the Academic Hall to an open
gallery beside the bells. From up here you can see the whole of Trinity, from
the station to the outskirts, and anyone crossing the square below.

The bells ring at 08:30, 12:00 and 15:20. Students aren't supposed to come up,
which is exactly why some do.
