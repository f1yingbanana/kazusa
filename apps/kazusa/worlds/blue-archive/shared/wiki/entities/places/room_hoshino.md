# Hoshino's Room

Hardly any furniture: a thin futon on a hard floor, a low table, a whale plush.
When the light goes out, the glow-in-the-dark fish stickers she put up on the
ceiling swim across it. In the table drawer is an old notebook that belonged to
the late council president, which she shows nobody.
