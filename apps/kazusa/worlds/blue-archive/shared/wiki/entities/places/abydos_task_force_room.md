# Abydos Foreclosure Task Force Room

The Task Force's base and the real heart of the school. The whiteboard tracks
the monthly interest payment and this month's earnings; a district map on the
wall shows Kaiser's holdings in red. A cabinet holds antiques and old books
waiting to be appraised, the snack shelf is restocked from someone's allowance,
and ammunition is counted on the long table. Meetings, money-making schemes and
long arguments about them all happen here.
