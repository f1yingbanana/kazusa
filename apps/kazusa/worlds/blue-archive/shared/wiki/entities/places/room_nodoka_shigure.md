# Nodoka and Shigure's Room

An old classroom with the desks pushed to the walls, chosen because its stove
still draws. Two cots with patched blankets sit close to it. A big telescope
stands at the window on a tripod, with star charts taped over cracked plaster
and a jar of picked-up coins beside it; along the other wall, shelves of jars of
fermenting fruit and kompot and a flask on a hook. They bar the door at night
against draughts and bears.
