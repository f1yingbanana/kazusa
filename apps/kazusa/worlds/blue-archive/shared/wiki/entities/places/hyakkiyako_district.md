# Hyakkiyako District

A tourist town of wooden shopfronts, lanterns, shrines and cherry trees,
gathered around the academy's castle keep. Rickshaws and tour buses carry
visitors from the station through the Shopping District, Movie Village and the
Central Plaza, and the riverside quarter behind the castle is where most
students live. The railway runs north from here through a long tunnel to the
snowy farmland of the North District.
