# Kousagi Station Street

A short, lively street running from Kousagi Station toward the hill: an Angel 24
on the corner, a game center, a karaoke box, a sweets shop with a blue bird on
its sign, a small station mall and a police box in front of the station.
Students heading to SCHALE pass through it, and many stop.
