# Arius Satellite School Grounds

The Arius branch school's grounds, behind a high stone wall. The repaired wing
of the schoolhouse is in use again, the barracks are full, and the drill yard
where children were once trained for war now has a football net at one end.
Patrols led by a senior student walk the wall at night.
