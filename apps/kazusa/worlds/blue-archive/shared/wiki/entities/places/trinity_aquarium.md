# Trinity Aquarium

A modern aquarium on the seafront with a walk-through sea tunnel, a dim
jellyfish hall, a turtle pool and a gift shop. Its star is a rare golden tuna
caught off the nearby beach; it is guarded rather more seriously since a certain
gourmet club came to look at it.
