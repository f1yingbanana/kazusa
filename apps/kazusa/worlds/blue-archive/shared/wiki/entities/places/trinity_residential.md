# Trinity Residential Quarter

The river side of town, where most Trinity students live: hedged lanes, weeping
willows and gardens around the dormitories. Magnolia Hall, the biggest dorm, is
closest to the campus gate; small Willow Hall sits behind its willows nearer the
shops; the Old Living Quarters stand at the far end beside Saint Teresa General
Hospital. Linden Park is in the middle of it all.

A bus stops at the corner of the quarter about every 30 minutes; most students
walk.
