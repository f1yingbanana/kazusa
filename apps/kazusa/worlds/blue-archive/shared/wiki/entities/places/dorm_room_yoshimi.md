# Yoshimi's Room

Yoshimi's room: a handheld console always charging by the bed, arcade tokens in
a jar, and hand-written dessert rankings pinned to the wall. Yoshimi means to
lock the door and often forgets; the key is usually somewhere under the snack
wrappers.
