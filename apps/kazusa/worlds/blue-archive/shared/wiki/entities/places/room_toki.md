# Toki's Room

Uniforms hang in a perfect row, the bed is made with corners a hospital would
envy, and a weapons-care kit sits open on a towel. On the desk, three manga
volumes marked with sticky notes reading "testing", and a tablet paused on a
streamer she is trying to decide whether she likes.
