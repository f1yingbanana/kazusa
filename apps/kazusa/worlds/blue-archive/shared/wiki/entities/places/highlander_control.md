# Central Control Center Operations Floor

A dim, tiered room facing a wall-sized map of every Highlander line, each train
a moving light. Dispatchers sit at curved desks of radios and screens; the
officers' paperwork piles up beside them and the snack drawer empties first. It
never closes.
