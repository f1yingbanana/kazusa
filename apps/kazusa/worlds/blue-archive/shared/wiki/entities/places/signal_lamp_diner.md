# Signal Lamp Diner

A narrow counter diner just outside the yard gate, an old red signal lamp over
the door. Set meals come fast and cheap, the owner knows every crew's order, and
the counter seats eight elbow to elbow. Nobody eats alone here, whether they
like it or not.
