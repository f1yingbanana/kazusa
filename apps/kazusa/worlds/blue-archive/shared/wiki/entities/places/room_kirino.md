# Kirino's Room

Room 204 on the first-years' floor. Recruiting posters cover the walls, a
pegboard of paper targets shows more holes around the rings than in them, and a
pair of handcuffs hangs by the door so she never forgets them. Takeout wrappers
from street stalls fill the bin.
