# Subaru's Room

It used to be the duty sergeant's room, so it opens onto the corridor where she
can hear anyone up at night. Sheet music is pinned above the bed, three
harmonicas sit in a case, and a tin of snacks labelled FOR THE KIDS is restocked
from her café wages. A patrol map of Arius is tacked inside the door.
