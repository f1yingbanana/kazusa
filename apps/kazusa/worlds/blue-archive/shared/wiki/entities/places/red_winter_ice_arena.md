# Red Winter Ice Arena

A barrel-roofed arena near the station with steep wooden stands. The academy's
hockey team plays Friday evenings and Sunday afternoons to crowds that take
results very seriously; more than one coup has started in these stands after a
bad loss. On Saturdays it opens for public skating.
