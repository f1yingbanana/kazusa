# Millennium Square

The middle of campus. A fountain that runs light shows on festival nights,
benches under young trees, screens with Seminar notices, and a row of food
stalls and carts at lunchtime selling yakisoba, crepes and skewers. If two
Millennium students agree to meet somewhere, it's here.
