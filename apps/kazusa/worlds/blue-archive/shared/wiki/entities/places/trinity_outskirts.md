# Trinity Outskirts

Where the town thins into fields, hedges and old estates. The walled Tea Party
Residence looks over Songbird Park; down the lane, the Make-Up Work Club keeps
its annex and the Arius students take lessons in the Trinity Old School
Building. Glebe Field, the school's own farmland, is where students sentenced to
community service pull weeds. The road ends at the ruined Old Cathedral of
Communion.

Buses barely come out here; most people walk or cycle.
