# Trinity Seafront

Where the Trinity district meets the sea: a promenade of palms and ice-cream
kiosks, the Trinity Aquarium, and a long white beach that once belonged to the
Tea Party. An uninhabited island with old ruins can be seen offshore on clear
days.
