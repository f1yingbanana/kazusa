# Main Gate

Tall wrought-iron gates crowned with Trinity's crest, flanked by stone pillars and a guard booth that is usually empty. It is the natural meeting point before and after school; a few students always linger here waiting for friends, and Church Street begins at the bottom of the slope outside.
