# Trinity Main Gate

Tall wrought-iron gates crowned with Trinity's crest, between stone pillars and
a guard booth. Paths lead from here down the hill to Trinity Street and across
the gardens to the dormitories.

It is the natural meeting point before and after school. In the morning a
Justice Task Force officer with a clipboard takes the names of latecomers.
