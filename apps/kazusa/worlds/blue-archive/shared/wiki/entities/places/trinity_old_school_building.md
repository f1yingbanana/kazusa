# Trinity Old School Building

An old three-storey school building on Trinity's outskirts, emptied years ago
and reopened for Arius students preparing for Trinity's entrance exam. The
classrooms have mismatched desks, fresh paint over old graffiti and a timetable
taped to the door. Trinity volunteers teach some lessons.
