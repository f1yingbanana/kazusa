# Game Development Department Club Room

Every surface is covered: retro consoles, dev PCs, tangled controllers, snack
bags and design notebooks. The red sofa is where the club argues about
scenarios. In one corner, under an enormous railgun leaning against the wall, is
a nest of blankets where the club's youngest member sleeps beside a CRT that
loops an old RPG, a "hero's inventory" shelf and a bag of cat treats. A tall
locker with a name sticker stands against the back wall. The club owes Seminar
money, and everyone knows it.
