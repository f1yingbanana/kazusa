# Gehenna Academy Infirmary

A long white ward smelling of disinfectant, with curtained beds, a treatment
room and a nurse's desk. Prefect officers come here after raids, delinquents
come here after the Prefect officers, and the Emergency Medicine Club drops off
whoever it scraped off the street.
