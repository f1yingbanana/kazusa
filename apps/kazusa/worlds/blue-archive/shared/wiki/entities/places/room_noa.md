# Noa's Room

White walls, grey bedding, black desk, nothing in colour. Shelves of identical
notebooks hold her records, dated to the second. The only thing that doesn't fit
is a single air freshener on the windowsill, a gift she kept. Through the wall
she can hear an abacus clicking late at night.
