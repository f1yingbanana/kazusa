# Trinity Square

A wide paved square at the heart of the campus, with a three-tiered fountain in
the middle and the school's notice boards along one side. The main gate opens
onto it, and the Academic Hall, the Central Library and the dining hall face it,
so nearly everyone crosses it several times a day.

Announcements, club recruitment drives and festival stalls happen here, and when
a rumour spreads, this is where the crowd gathers.
