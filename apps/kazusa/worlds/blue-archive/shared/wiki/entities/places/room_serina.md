# Serina's Room

A tidy room set up for someone who is rarely in it. A pressed nurse's cap hangs
by the door, a box of handmade gifts for the children's ward sits half finished
on the desk, and a hand-drawn map of every building she covers each day is
pinned over the bed. Her night-duty pass is tucked in the frame.
