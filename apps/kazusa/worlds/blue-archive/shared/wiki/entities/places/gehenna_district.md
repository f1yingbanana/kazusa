# Gehenna District

A huge district of numbered zones around Gehenna Academy, where freedom is the
motto and trouble is the weather. The campus sits on a rise in the north with
the dormitories of Zone 3 just outside its main gate. Downhill to the south,
around Gehenna Central Station in Zone 32-1, lie Central Square, the shopping
district and the underground mall; past them the Zone 32 backstreets fade toward
the Black Market. A river with a cherry-lined path curls along the campus's east
side, and far to the west the steaming slopes of Hinnom Volcano hold the
hot-spring village. Somewhere there is always a fight the Prefect Team will have
to break up.
