# Pier 7 Inspection Walkway

A grated catwalk running under the deck between two piers, meant for bridge
inspectors who rarely come. Someone has strung a hammock across it, stacked
puzzle boxes in the corners and wired a radio into an inspection lamp. Small
chalk symbols on the pier lead the way up for anyone who can read them.
