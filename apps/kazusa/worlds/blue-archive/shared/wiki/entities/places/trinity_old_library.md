# Old Library

A crumbling stone building among overgrown hedges, so stuffed with old and rare
books that people call it the Antiquarian Bookstore. Stacks lean against the
windows, ancient manuscripts wait to be translated, and the air smells of dust
and paper.

Visitors are rare and not encouraged: they must sign the log, keep their voices
down and keep their drinks far from the books. The Library Committee chair keeps
it, and all but lives here.
