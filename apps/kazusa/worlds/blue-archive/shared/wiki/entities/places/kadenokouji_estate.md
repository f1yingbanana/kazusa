# Kadenokouji Estate

A walled compound on the hill above town: an old main house, a moss garden, a
family shrine and servants' quarters, with a doctor living on the grounds. The
Kadenokouji are a venerable miko lineage, and the household runs on ritual,
schedules and polite silence.
