# Magnolia Hall West Wing

A quiet third-floor corridor where several Justice Task Force officers live,
along with a Library Committee member who reads all night. Boots are lined up
outside doors, a duty roster is taped by the stairs, and the kitchenette at the
end sees more late-night tea than it should.
