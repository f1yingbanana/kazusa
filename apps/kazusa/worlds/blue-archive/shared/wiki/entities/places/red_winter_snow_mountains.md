# Red Winter Snow Mountains

Forested mountains above the district, cut by a few ploughed roads and the
Northern Trunk line. A mountain road climbs from the Former Institute past the
forestry depot and District 8's security post, and further up a single track
leads to the No. 183 facility. Snowstorms come in fast, bears are real, and
people who leave the road get lost.
