# Magnolia Hall Dining Room

A long room off the entrance hall with tables for sixty, a serving counter and a
tea urn that never goes cold. Breakfast is a scramble, dinner a long chatty
affair; it is where the dorm's gossip, and sometimes its feuds, get served up
alongside the stew.
