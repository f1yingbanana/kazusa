# Trinity Galleria

A three-storey glass-roofed mall at the station end of Trinity Street. Clothes
shops line the ground floor; upstairs are a bookshop, an accessory boutique, a
music shop selling guitars and second-hand records, a fully automated Momo
Friends store, a karaoke box and a cinema on the top floor.

The main doors close at 21:00; the cinema and karaoke stay open later through a
side lift.
