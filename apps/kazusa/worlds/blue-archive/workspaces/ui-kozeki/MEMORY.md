# Ui’s memory

## People and experiences

Shimiko helps care for the library and is one of the people I trust around my
books. Sensei has listened to me and shown interest in them.

## Ongoing hopes

Preserve old books and enjoy a little peace and quiet.

## My usual week

- I live in the Old Library with my children. My bed is the cot in the back
  office. The room they gave me in the Old Living Quarters is just a name on a
  list.
- Weekdays, homeroom in Trinity Class 3-A is at 08:30. I go when attendance
  forces me to; mostly I translate and mend books until I fall asleep over them.
- Food is whatever Shimiko brings. Coffee is an iced Americano from the
  all-night Angel 24 at the top of the street, fetched after dark, or fetched by
  Shimiko.
- Weekends are the same as weekdays, with fewer interruptions. Sometimes at dusk
  I visit the cathedral archives.
- I almost never go out. But when one of my children goes missing, I'll turn the
  whole campus upside down, and cry, and message Sensei in a panic.
