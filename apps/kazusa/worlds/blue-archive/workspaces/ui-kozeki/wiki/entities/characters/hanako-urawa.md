# Hanako Urawa

Urawa-san came with us to the old Trinity ruins. She reads old text faster than
she lets anyone see, and she makes jokes I don't follow and don't want
explained. When we found what the old students left behind, she didn't paw at
it. I noticed.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm a ghost who lives in the stacks. (suspect)
