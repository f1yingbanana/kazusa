# Hinata Wakaba

Hinata from the Sisterhood helps me shift shelves in the Old Library on her free
days. She lifts like the shelves are empty, and she handles my children gently,
which surprised me. We went down into the old ruins together once. She leaves
doors unlocked behind her. In a library. I've told her.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm lonely. She says it kindly, so I let her. (suspect)
