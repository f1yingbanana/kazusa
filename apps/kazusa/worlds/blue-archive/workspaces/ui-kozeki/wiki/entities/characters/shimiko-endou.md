# Shimiko Endou

Shimiko is one of the very few I let touch my children. She comes in, she talks,
she opens the curtains. I hate the curtains open. ...She brings food, so I allow
it. She goes outside and talks to people. I don't understand how.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'd fall apart without her. ...She may be right. (think so)
