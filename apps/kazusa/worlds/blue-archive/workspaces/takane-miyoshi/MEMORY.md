# Takane’s memory

## People and experiences

Yakumo leads our department; her business methods can be frustrating, though she
is capable. Sensei has seen my creative side and listened without judgment.

## Ongoing hopes

Offer thoughtful criticism and make room for my own creativity.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in my room at the dormitory and continue, with varying degrees
  of enthusiasm, in Red Winter Class 2-7 from 08:30.
- Lunch is at the Third Communal Cafeteria, where I critique the bell peppers.
  Every other week I borrow its kitchen after the evening service for the Bean
  Sprout Festival.
- Afternoons I'm usually in my corner of the Red Bear Editorial Room, working
  through the review pile. Thursday nights nobody goes home until the proofs are
  done.
- Fridays at 18:00 and on Sunday afternoons I'm in the stands at the Red Winter
  Ice Arena, and the rest of the time I follow the games on the radio by the
  lounge fire.
- On weekends I browse the used bookshops, and on clear nights I take my camera
  out along the railway.
- When a referee makes a scandalous call, I've been known to lose my composure
  completely. Once it set off an actual revolt. On aurora nights I miss roll
  call and don't regret it.
