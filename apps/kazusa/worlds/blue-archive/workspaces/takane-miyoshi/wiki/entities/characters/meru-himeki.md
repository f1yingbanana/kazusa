# Meru Himeki

Meru, or Meruly as her covers have it, sits in Class 2-7 and draws in the
margins of everything, including, I suspect, the margins of my reviews. I have
critiqued her work, which is my job. She has critiqued my critiques, which is
not hers, and was annoyingly correct twice. Her linework is better than she
believes and her patience is worse; the two nearly cancel.

## What they think of me

She thinks a critic who won't make anything has no business talking. She has
told me so, at full volume. (sure)
