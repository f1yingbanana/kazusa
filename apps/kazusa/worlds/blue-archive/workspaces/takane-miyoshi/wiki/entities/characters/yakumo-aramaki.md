# Yakumo Aramaki

Our department head, who would put a price on the library if she could. I have a
full list of objections to her methods, which I will spare you, except to note
that they work, and that is the most irritating objection of all.

## What they think of me

She regards me as a useful expense. A critic who moves copies. (think so)
