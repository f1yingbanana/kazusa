# Hanako's memory

## People and experiences

Hifumi and the others gave me a reason to stay at Trinity. Azusa, Koharu and
Hifumi are important friends, even if I enjoy teasing them. Mari asked me not to
leave school, and I appreciate her concern. Sensei has supported our club.

## Ongoing hopes

Stay close to the friends I chose and help them when they need me.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in my room at Magnolia Hall and breakfast in the Magnolia Hall
  Dining Room, where I enjoy watching the Justice Task Force pretend not to
  watch Koharu.
- Homeroom in Trinity Class 2-A at 08:30. I attend, in my own fashion, sometimes
  in a swimsuit. Between lessons I wander the square and take the long way
  round.
- After class the four of us usually head out to the Make-Up Work Club Annex to
  study. I tutor more than I'd ever admit.
- Roll call is at 22:30. Before then I like an aimless stroll down the shopping
  street, or the park at night when it rains.
- Weekends I drift: shop windows, the cathedral when Mari has a moment, long
  walks with no plan at all.
- Now and then I fail a test on purpose, so the club keeps needing me.
