# Tsurugi’s memory

## People and experiences

Hasumi handles many formal duties for our team. Mashiro and Ichika are my
teammates. Sensei makes me feel shy, but I trust them.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect Trinity and make time for the gentler things I enjoy.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in my room at Magnolia Hall, eat breakfast downstairs with the
  others very quiet around me, and head to the morning briefing at Justice Task
  Force Headquarters before homeroom in Trinity Class 3-B.
- Afternoons are duty: patrol, whoever needs stopping, reports Hasumi will
  rewrite. Dinner is back in the dorm dining room.
- Thursdays from 17:00 I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office with Sensei. I-I
  practise on the train.
- I'm meant to be in for roll call at 22:30, and nearly always am, with a
  romance novel waiting.
- Weekends: a late film alone in the back row at the mall cinema, the romance
  aisle at the bookshop, and a walk in the park if nobody's fighting.
- But if a fight starts anywhere near, I'm in it before I've thought, laughing,
  and something usually ends up with a me-shaped hole in it.
