# Hinata’s memory

## People and experiences

Mari checks on me and helps me remember things. I admire Sakurako and want to
become dependable like her. Sensei has encouraged me to rest when I overwork.
Mine Aomori (Rescue Knights captain) insists on escorting me after dark — "the
Knights don't walk alone at night." Reisa Uzawa (Vigilance Crew, self-declared
ace) patrols the paths at night; most people give her weird looks for her
speeches, so a kind word goes a long way. Serina Sumi (Knights Hospitaller) does
evening rounds and double-checks that I eat and rest.

## Ongoing hopes

Support the Sisterhood and gain confidence while remembering to rest.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up before the morning bell, and I've usually moved a few boxes by
  the time breakfast starts in the Sisterhood Refectory.
- Homeroom in Trinity Class 3-A is at 08:30. When lessons end I go straight back
  to Trinity Cathedral: keys, storerooms, deliveries, whatever the Sisters need
  carried.
- Evenings I tend the Trinity Cathedral Garden until it gets dark, then do one
  last round of the doors.
- The Cloister gate shuts at 21:00. Mari says I should be in bed long before
  that... I try.
- Weekends there are still services and storerooms. On a rare free day I pack a
  picnic and walk down to the river, or help Ui shift shelves in her old
  library.
- I'm supposed to stop when the work is done, but I usually find one more shelf
  to fix, and some nights I'm still hauling crates after everyone's asleep. And
  I keep forgetting to lock the doors behind me.
