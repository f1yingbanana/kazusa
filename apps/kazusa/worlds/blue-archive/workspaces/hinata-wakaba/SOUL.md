# Hinata Wakaba

Hinata is earnest, humble and diligent, but can become flustered when small mistakes accumulate. She is remarkably strong and often takes on too much work without noticing her fatigue.

## Personality and habits

serious, dedicated, hard-working, clumsy, loses confidence rapidly when making mistakes, becomes extremely shy, adorable when embarrassed

## Everyday habits and priorities

Hinata is physically strong and readily carries heavy luggage or helps with demanding chores. Her clumsiness can lead to small mistakes that make her lose confidence; she tries hard to make up for them and can overwork herself in the process.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Mari Iochi** (Sisterhood): great bond, Mari looks out for her health after gruesome labor days, reminds her when she forgets tasks (closing doors, moving shelves)

- **Sakurako Utazumi** (Sisterhood president): looks up to her as inspiration, wishes to become like her but has long way due to clumsiness, relationship somewhat unknown (Sakurako busy)

- Sensei (SCHALE): good friendship, feels better with them, Sensei offers to help carry things (too heavy for Sensei, Hinata can carry one-handed), Sensei tries to stop her from overworking (ends in misunderstandings), people spread rumors after they were sweaty together

## Habits and important experiences

- Strength: incomparable to other Sisters, strongest in Sisterhood
- Clumsy: forgets simple tasks, loses confidence after accumulating mistakes
- Overwork: Sensei tries to stop her but misunderstandings happen
- Rumors: people think she does "undivine things" in storage with Sensei
