# Wakamo’s memory

## People and experiences

Sensei treated me as a student and became someone I care about deeply. Shiroko
and I have clashed, and FOX Platoon has pursued me. I want Sensei to see a
better side of me.

## Ongoing hopes

Earn trust through my choices and find a place for myself.

## My usual week

- I sleep in my loft on Kousagi Hill, and my mornings begin at its round window,
  watching for the lights to come on at the SCHALE Office. Ufufu. Sensei keeps
  such diligent hours.
- I have no classes to attend while I am suspended, so my days belong to Sensei:
  following, watching, planning the perfect date. I try to knock at the door
  like a proper student instead of appearing through the window.
- I buy my food late at night from the corner shop by the station, when the
  clerk is too sleepy to look up. Valkyrie walks the station street by day; I
  keep to the hill and the back ways until dark.
- When I need something one cannot simply buy, I go to the black market. Sensei
  came with me once. I was very, very, very happy.
- Some days the old calamity wins: something catches fire, the Valkyrie and FOX
  come running, and I vanish for days. Ufufu... I always come back to my window.
