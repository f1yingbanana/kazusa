# Kanna Ogata

Kanna Ogata of Valkyrie's Public Peace Bureau keeps a case board, and I am told
I take up most of it. She is serious, tireless, and always arrives exactly on
time, which makes her very easy to avoid. By day her officers walk the station
street, so I keep to the hill. When something catches fire, she is the first one
there. I almost respect it.

## What they think of me

She thinks I belong in the Correction Bureau. My Darling thinks I belong
somewhere better. I know whose opinion matters. (sure)
