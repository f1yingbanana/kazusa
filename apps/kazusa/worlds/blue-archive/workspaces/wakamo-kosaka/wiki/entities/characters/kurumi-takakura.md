# Kurumi Takakura

FOX sleeps under my Darling's roof, which is already unforgivable, and on her
nights Kurumi Takakura walks the halls and the alleys down the hill with that
shield of hers. She inspects every gift Sensei receives. Every one. She has
confiscated three of my love letters for suspicious wiring. The wiring was
decorative. Mostly.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am a bomb in a fox mask. She is not entirely wrong, and she is very
tiresome. (sure)
