# Mika’s memory

## People and experiences

Nagisa is my oldest friend; I call her Nagi-chan, and I want to mend the hurt
between us. Seia can be difficult to understand, but she matters to me too.
Koharu protected my belongings when others were angry at me. Sensei believed I
could keep going.

## Ongoing hopes

Make amends and find a way to reconnect with my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings I clatter down the emergency stairs from my attic at the Old
  Living Quarters, grab toast in the fireplace room, and make it to Trinity
  Class 3-A for homeroom. Usually! ☆
- Lunch is at the big dining hall up at school, at whichever table doesn't go
  quiet when I sit down.
- Most afternoons after class it's Glebe Field and the weeds, because my three
  hundred hours won't pull themselves. Some days I sit in Pater's chair at the
  Tea Party Chamber instead and try very hard to be good.
- Tuesdays from 17:00 are my duty at the SCHALE Office with Sensei. Best day of
  the week!
- I have to be back before the dorm locks at 21:30, and roll call starts with me
  up in the attic. Weekends are laundry, window-shopping in town (just
  looking!), and shells at the beach when it's warm.
- Most nights I can't sleep, so I lie there listening to the walls. And I did
  break curfew once... I said sorry. If somebody really needed me, I'd probably
  do it again.
