# Momoi’s memory

## People and experiences

Midori is my twin and fellow club member. I loved Yuzu’s game and brought Midori
to the club. We found Aris in the ruins and brought her into our group. Sensei
supports our game-making adventures.

## Ongoing hopes

Make an amazing game with Midori, Yuzu and Aris.

## My usual week

- Weekdays: Midori drags me out of Momoi and Midori's Room, I grab crackers on
  the way, and I speedrun to Millennium Class 1-C by 08:30. Clear rate: decent!
- Lunch is the bread-counter race at the Millennium Central Cafeteria. If I
  lose, it's cod roe crackers from my bag.
- After the bell it's the Game Development Department Club Room until way past
  dark: scenario writing, playtesting, hiding from Seminar's budget audits.
- Weekends are for events: release-day lines, stamp rallies in the electronics
  town, the challenge menu at the family restaurant, and dragging Sensei along
  for "research".
- The dorm door locks at 23:30 and anything later gets logged for the Cruel
  Arithmetician, so I try. Ish.
- Real talk: if a game's good, I play it all night and sleep through first
  period. Midori never lets me forget it.
