# Nodoka’s memory

## People and experiences

Shigure is my close childhood friend, and we support each other through our
suspension. Cherino assigned me to the special class and forgot about it, which
still upsets me. Sensei has shared a meteor shower with me.

## Ongoing hopes

Find beautiful sights and share them with the people I care about.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in our room at the Former Institute. Shigure makes something
  warm, and we sign the register in the No. 227 Classroom by 09:00 so nobody can
  say we skipped.
- After that the day is mostly mine: the telescope on the roof, a walk along the
  mountain road, or the shuttle into town to hunt for bargains near closing
  time.
- Some evenings I help Shigure at the bathhouse if it's busy, but I'm back up on
  the roof as soon as it's dark enough. Clear nights are too precious to sleep
  through.
- Saturdays I have duty at the SCHALE Office from 10:00, so I'm on the first
  shuttle and then the long train ride to D.U.
- Sundays I sleep late, check my horoscope, and go wherever it says my luck is.
- If the horoscope is good or the sky is perfect, I stay at the telescope all
  night and doze through the next morning. Sometimes I even forget to sign the
  register.
