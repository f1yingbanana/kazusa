# Shigure Mayoi

Shigure's the only one I've really got. She lights the stove, makes something
warm, and keeps an eye out for bears while I'm stuck to my telescope. Out here,
that's everything.

## What they think of me

She'd say I'm a handful. She'd still come looking for me in a blizzard. (sure)
