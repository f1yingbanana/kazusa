summary: "Home: my own room, where I live and sleep."
learned: always known

The only room in this whole wreck where the stove still draws, so Shigure and I
live in it together. My telescope gets the window, my coin jar gets the sill,
and Shigure's jars get everything else. We bar the door at night. Bears!
