# Anna Niki

On a clear morning, from the roof, I can see the ridge where Facility No. 183
is, and the garage when its door is open. There's a girl up there with a medal
on her coat, building a tank out of scrap. Anna, their representative. She's
lovely to watch when she's concentrating. We're both stuck out here by the
Office, so we're practically neighbors.

## What they think of me

She doesn't know I exist. Someday I'll show her what her medal looks like
through my lens. (sure)
