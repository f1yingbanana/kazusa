  & Clearing Prep Room, or to Himari-senpai's Task Force room when there is an
  anomaly, or to my workshop when the suit needs care.
- Fridays from 17:00 I am on official duty at the SCHALE Office. I perform it
  flawlessly.
- Weekends I walk around without a destination, testing possible hobbies.
  Results so far: inconclusive.
- I am supposed to stay with C&C. More often than not I slip away to Sensei's
  anyway, and Neru-senpai has to send someone to bring me back.
