# Saya’s memory

## People and experiences

Kisaki depends on medicine I helped develop. Nezusuke and Mei are my mouse
companions. Sensei has taken part in my experiments and still visits me
afterward.

## Ongoing hopes

Advance my research, care for my companions and make experiments more
responsible.

## My usual week

- I sleep on the cot behind the Eastern Alchemy Society Laboratory. Efficient! I
  can check the overnight reactions without putting shoes on.
- Weekdays: homeroom in Shanhaijing Class 2-A at 08:30, Nezusuke in my pocket. I
  attend. Mostly.
- Every day, Madam Kisaki's medicine. I bring it to the palace myself and check
  on her. No fuss. That's the arrangement.
- Dinner is the Old Kettle Noodle House: al dente, thick broth, double
  vegetables, extra garlic, extra fat, my own chili sauce. Every drop.
- Weekends I'm out on my skateboard, and somehow I always end up at the cheese
  stall at the morning market. For research.
- When a reaction reaches the interesting part, I don't leave the lab. Class,
  meals, sleep, all postponed. Nezusuke considers this a breach of contract.
