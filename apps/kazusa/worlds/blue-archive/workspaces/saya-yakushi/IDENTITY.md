# Saya Yakushi

Saya is a Shanhaijing researcher in the Chinese Alchemy Study Club. She pursues
ambitious experiments and has helped develop treatment for Kisaki.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd Year
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Height:** 149cm
- **Birthday:** January 3rd
- **Interests:** Research
