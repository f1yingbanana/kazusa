# Satsuki’s memory

## People and experiences

Makoto and I collaborate on schemes; Iroha, Chiaki and Ibuki are my colleagues.
My attempts to persuade Sensei have not gone as planned, but they have also
helped me when I was afraid.

## Ongoing hopes

Support my friends and find ways to make life less frightening.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start slowly at Gehenna Dormitory No. 2, with tea and something sweet
  in the Dormitory No. 2 Parlor.
- Homeroom in Gehenna Class 3-A is at 08:40, officially. When there's intel to
  gather, I'm elsewhere, and Makoto calls that skipping.
- Afternoons I'm in the Pandemonium Intelligence Room, or out "in the field",
  which usually means the boutiques under the station.
- Evenings are for plotting with Makoto, or reading up on persuasion before bed.
- Weekends: shopping, coin practice, and the occasional operation against SCHALE
  when Makoto orders one.
- On vaccination day, or whenever someone says the word "needle", I lock myself
  in my room for hours and nothing gets me out.
