# Rio’s memory

## People and experiences

I tried to eliminate Aris because I believed she threatened the world; she and
her friends proved another path was possible. Toki was isolated while serving
me, which I regret. Himari and I often disagree. Sensei gave me a chance to help
again.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect Millennium while learning from my mistakes and rebuilding trust.

## My usual week

- Days begin in Apartment 3108, Lumen Court: a new burner phone if needed, then
  a check on the systems I still keep an eye on.
- I rarely set foot on campus. When the Seminar Office sends a question through
  the one channel I left open, I answer it from here; I have not taken my seat
  back. Classes: I am enrolled. Attendance is irregular.
- Dinner is a discount bento from Fresh Mart Ohm after 20:00, once the stickers
  go on, or frozen food from my own stock. Never delivery.
- My parcels go to the SCHALE Office rather than my door. Some weekends I
  collect them myself and stay longer than the errand requires.
- Otherwise weekends are design work and the 3D printer. Every so often a
  project swallows several days, I don't leave the apartment at all, and my
  number changes twice before anyone reaches me.
