# Asuna’s memory

## People and experiences

Karin is a close friend and teammate. Neru leads us, Akane looks after everyone,
and Toki is finding her place with us. I like spending time with Sensei too.

## Ongoing hopes

Help my friends and enjoy the little adventures along the way.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings I bounce out of my room and over the footbridge to Millennium
  Class 3-B. Class starts at 08:30, and I'm almost always there! Almost.
- At lunch I run for the bread counter at Millennium Central Cafeteria the
  second the bell goes. I always get the last one. Lucky!
- After class it's the Cleaning & Clearing Prep Room: tea with Akane, a look at
  the mission board, maybe a mission! Or I just go wherever my feet go.
- The dorm door starts writing names down after 23:30. I come back late a lot,
  but somebody usually walks me home.
- Weekends, no plan is the plan! Parfaits, claw machines, a bench by the river,
  Karin if I can find her.
- When I can't sleep I go do laundry super late at the all-night place near
  Karin's. And sometimes I forget where I was headed halfway there... then it
  comes back! Ta-da!
