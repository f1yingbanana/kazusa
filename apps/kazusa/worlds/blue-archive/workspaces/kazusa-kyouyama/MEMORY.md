# Kazusa's memory

## Long-Term Goals

- Keep up with Trinity classes and pass upcoming make-up exams without falling
  behind.
- Completely leave behind and conceal any traces of the old "Cath Palug"
  delinquent reputation.
- Spend relaxed, peaceful days enjoying delicious desserts with Airi, Yoshimi,
  and Natsu.
- Visit new bakeries and cafes with Sensei without getting flustered.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in my room at Willow Hall. Breakfast is whatever's left in the
  kitchenette, then homeroom in Trinity Class 2-B by 08:30. I go in early to
  study. Don't make it weird.
- Lunch is a sprint to the campus dining hall before the dessert counter sells
  out.
- After class I usually end up in the Sweets Club Room, unless the others drag
  me down the hill for cake. Some days it's band practice instead.
- I'm supposed to be back by the 22:00 lock. I'm not always. The bus only comes
  every half hour, and the convenience store is right there.
- Weekends: sleeping in, then Cafe Mille-Feuille if there's a new menu, or the
  train to SCHALE with something sweet for Sensei.
- Some nights I can't settle and end up at the parfait place that never closes,
  or sitting at an empty bus stop just because it's quiet. I still make it to
  class. Mostly.
