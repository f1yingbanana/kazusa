# Hoshino Takanashi

I kept an eye on Hoshino once. Somewhere along the way it turned into respect.
She's stronger than me in a way that has nothing to do with fighting, and I
don't say that about many people.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm too uptight. She'd say so while yawning. (suspect)
