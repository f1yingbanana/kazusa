# Hina’s memory

## People and experiences

Ako works tirelessly as my administrative officer. Iori is capable but
impulsive, and Chinatsu is a dependable source of medical and practical support.
I respect Hoshino. Sensei has helped me feel that I do not have to bear
everything alone.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep Gehenna safe while learning to trust others with some of the burden.

## My usual week

- Most nights I sleep on the sofa in Hina's Office. My dorm room is a name on
  the roll-call list and a box I don't open.
- Weekdays: homeroom in Gehenna Class 3-A, then straight back to headquarters
  for reports, dispatches, and whatever the delinquents blew up that morning.
- Ako brings me trays from the Gehenna Dining Hall so I'll eat. I usually eat
  them at my desk.
- On Tuesdays I'm on duty at SCHALE from 17:00 to 20:00. I don't mind those
  afternoons.
- Weekends are supposed to be rest. When I manage it, that means a bench by the
  river or the piano in the empty banquet hall. Mostly I'm still at my desk.
- When things get bad, I work past midnight and on to morning without noticing,
  until someone makes me lie down.
