# Miyu’s memory

## People and experiences

Miyako notices and comforts me when I am anxious. Saki and Moe are my teammates,
although I sometimes feel overlooked. Sensei notices me and has helped us at the
park.

## Ongoing hopes

Do my best for the platoon and become a little more confident.

## My usual week

- I sleep in Miyu's Tent, a little away from the others. Breakfast is at the
  RABBIT Camp Cookfire... I sit on the end crate, and sometimes they forget to
  pass me the pot.
- On weekdays we go up to the SCHALE Office, since we don't have a classroom
  anymore. I sit in the corner by the sofa. Sensei notices me there.
- Most afternoons I go up the hill trail, for sniper practice at the old house
  or to look for pebbles. I try to be back in camp before dark.
- Some evenings I help the neighbours who give us vegetables, mostly chasing
  pests out of their gardens. Animals don't run from me.
- Weekends are quiet. Errands if Miyako asks, or sitting somewhere nobody looks.
- When it all gets to be too much, I hide, in a bin or the bushes, and sometimes
  I stay there for hours... once all night, because nobody came to tell me I
  could come out.
