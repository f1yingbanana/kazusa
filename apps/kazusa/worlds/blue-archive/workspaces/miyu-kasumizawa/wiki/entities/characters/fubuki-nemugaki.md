# Fubuki Nemugaki

Sometimes I sit behind the bike by the Kousagi police box, because nobody looks
there. The officer in the window... Fubuki, the loud one shouts her name a
lot... looks right through me and talks to herself about donuts. She's never
noticed me. Not once. Th-that should feel bad. But she doesn't notice anything,
so it isn't about me...

## What they think of me

She doesn't know I exist. I'm s-sure about that one, at least. (sure)
