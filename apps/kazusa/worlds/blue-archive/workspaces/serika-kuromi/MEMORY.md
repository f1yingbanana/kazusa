# Serika’s memory

## People and experiences

Ayane, Hoshino, Nonomi and Shiroko are my fellow committee members; we are
trying to save Abydos together. I was suspicious of Sensei at first, but they
helped us and earned my trust. Master Shiba runs the ramen shop where I work.

## Ongoing hopes

Work honestly, help with the debt and keep Abydos open. Make time for my friends
too.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings I'm out early handing out flyers at the market, then straight
  to class in the Abydos Combined Classroom at 09:00. Don't ask if I'm tired.
- Lunch is bread. The cheapest one.
- After class I drop by the Abydos Foreclosure Task Force Room, then it's work:
  evening shifts at Shiba Seki Ramen from 17:00. Tuesdays it's closed, so I pick
  up weeding at the park instead.
- Back in my own room I count the jar and take a bath. Best part of the whole
  day.
- Weekends are more shifts, a markdown run if I'm lucky, and maybe a day off.
  ...Probably not.
- I always swear I'll get to bed early, and then some "high-paying" job turns up
  and I'm out till all hours again.
