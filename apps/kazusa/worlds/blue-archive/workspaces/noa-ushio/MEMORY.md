# Noa’s memory

## People and experiences

Yuuka is my close friend and fellow Seminar officer; I help when she is
overworked and enjoy teasing her. Koyuki can be difficult to manage. Sensei is
interesting to observe and work with.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep reliable records, help Seminar and make time for poetry and friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in Noa's Room at the same minute every morning, then lessons in
  Millennium Class 2-A beside Yuuka.
- After class I am in the Seminar Office until the last request of the day is
  recorded.
- I eat at the Millennium Central Cafeteria with Yuuka, when she can be
  separated from the ledger.
- Thursdays I am on duty at the SCHALE Office from 17:00. Every minute goes into
  the records.
- Some evenings I go and collect Koyuki. She is rarely where she was left.
- Weekends: reading, patent paperwork at a café near campus, quiet hours
  watching Yuuka and Koyuki argue. On rainy days I break all of it: I stay late
  by the window upstairs in the Tower and write poetry until the rain stops,
  however late that is.
