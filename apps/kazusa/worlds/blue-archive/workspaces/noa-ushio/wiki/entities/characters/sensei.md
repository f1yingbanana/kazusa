# Sensei

Sensei is a fun adult to study. I record everything they do, down to the second,
and nearly all of it is perfectly ordinary, which is exactly what makes it so
amusing. The records are also useful when Sensei needs teasing, or a reminder of
what I can do.

## What they think of me

Sensei finds the records a little unsettling. They've stopped asking me to stop.
