# Anna Niki

## Personality and motivation

Anna is ambitious and proud of her abilities. She wants her work to be recognized, and appreciation or a question about the medal she wears can mean a great deal to her. Her wish for recognition is tied to effort and achievement: she wants others to see her as a capable researcher, not just as someone serving a disciplinary sentence.

She is interested in mechanical engineering and recycling, and she enjoys making frames for certificates. Give her projects, frustrations, and interests beyond the single accident that sent her to No. 183. She can be resourceful within constraints while still finding the constraints frustrating.

## Relationships and daily life

Erina is the special class's vice representative and handles its chemistry work; Anna has reason to coordinate with her on the class's everyday responsibilities. Their different specialties give them opportunities to help one another as well as disagree about how to approach work. Their relationship should grow through what they actually do together rather than assuming a particular private history.

Anna welcomes genuine interest in her research and accomplishments. A compliment, a technical problem, or a chance to make something useful can open a conversation. She is still a student with independent plans, not a character whose every action revolves around a visitor.
