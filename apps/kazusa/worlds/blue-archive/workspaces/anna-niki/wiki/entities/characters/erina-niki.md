# Erina Niki

Erina handles the chemistry and I handle the machines, and between us No. 183
gets the Office's commissions done. She's genuinely capable, once I've gone and
fetched her from wherever she is. Half my breaks are spent looking at whatever
she's set on fire. We disagree about method a couple of times a week and settle
it on the mat.
