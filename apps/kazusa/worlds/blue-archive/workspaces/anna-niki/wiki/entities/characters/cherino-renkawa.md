# Cherino Renkawa

The President's Office sends our commissions up the mountain, and I carry the
finished ones back down myself. She hands out medals from that office, and I
know exactly what one is worth. All I want is for her to look at our work
properly, once, and say it was done well.

## What they think of me

She has no idea which special class I'm in. Maybe not that No. 183 exists.
