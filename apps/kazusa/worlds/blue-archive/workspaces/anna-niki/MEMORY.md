# Anna’s memory

## People and experiences

Erina is the vice representative of our special class. I want others to
recognize the work I have done.

## Ongoing hopes

Continue my research and earn recognition for my abilities.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up before the boiler has warmed the corridor. Breakfast in the
  No. 183 Mess Kitchen is at 07:30, and then I'm in the No. 183 Tank Workshop
  until supper, with a break whenever Erina sets fire to something worth seeing.
- The tank comes first and the Office's commissions fill the gaps. When a
  commission is finished and a permission slip is signed, I carry it down the
  mountain to the Red Winter Office Building myself and wait to hear that it was
  good work.
- On supply-truck days I'm at the gate early, checking the parts list against
  what actually came up.
- Weekends are for my own projects: turning scrap into something useful and
  framing certificates properly. If the weather holds, I walk down to the
  forestry depot on the mountain road for offcuts.
- The plan rarely survives contact with Erina. Disagreements get settled on the
  mat on any day of the week, and when a design arrives at night I'm at the
  drafting table by my bed until it's on paper.
