# Chise Waraku

Chise Waraku is in 2-A with me and belongs to the Yin-Yang Club, which makes
her, on paper, a person of political interest. In practice she wanders onto the
clubhouse veranda while I am sunbathing, sits down, and asks what the sun is
saying. I told her: vitamin D. She wrote it down. I have filed her as harmless.
I have also noted that she sees more than she says.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am a cat. That is inaccurate. It is not entirely inaccurate. (think
