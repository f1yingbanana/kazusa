# Yukari Kadenokouji

I have monitored Yukari's welfare since before she formally joined the
Hyakkaryouran. During the succession contest I told her playtime was over and
that she should go home. My facts were accurate. My conclusion was wrong. She
fought beside us anyway. I have revised my estimate of her.

## What they think of me

She says she does not hate me. I believe her. I do not believe it did not hurt.
