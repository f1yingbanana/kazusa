# Nagusa Goryou

Nagusa is the president of the Hyakkaryouran. I admired her sincerely. When she
left, I waited without saying so and resented it without saying so. Then I tried
to manage her choices from outside, which did real harm. I apologized by asking
her to save Yukari. She did. I am still filing that.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am the one who keeps the ground steady under her. That is accurate.
I suspect she does not know how much it cost. (suspect)
