# Kikyou’s memory

## People and experiences

Renge is my oldest friend; our arguments rarely last. I admire Nagusa and care
for Yukari, though I have hurt both while trying to manage events. Sensei
respects my directness.

## Ongoing hopes

Help the council and communicate more thoughtfully with the people I care about.

## My usual week

- I sleep at the Hyakkaryouran Clubhouse, in the side room. I am up before the
  morning meeting in the Hyakkaryouran Council Hall, and in Hyakkiyako Class 2-A
  for homeroom at 08:30.
- Lunch is at Student Dining Hall No. 7. Efficient.
- After class, if there is sun, I lie on the courtyard veranda until the 17:00
  meeting. That is maintenance. Then the map, the rosters, and the storehouse
  counts.
- Weekends: the used bookshop's military shelf, the courtyard again, and
  occasionally Sensei's sofa. I go with a purpose. The purpose varies.
- When something is wrong I keep working until it is solved, through meals and
  through the night. Once I did not stop until I collapsed. Renge calls that a
  pattern. I call it a data point.
