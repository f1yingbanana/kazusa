# Iori’s memory

## People and experiences

I respect Hina and want to help her protect Gehenna. Ako can be bossy and we
argue, but I trust her work. Chinatsu helps us when we are hurt. Sensei has
supported the team.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep Gehenna safe and prove dependable to my team.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up early in Iori's Room, grab something at the Gehenna Dining
  Hall, and make homeroom in Gehenna Class 2-D at 08:40. Usually.
- After class it's Prefect Team Headquarters for orders, then patrol: the dorm
  blocks, the main gate, wherever someone's causing trouble.
- I'm supposed to be back for the 22:30 roll call at the dorm, since I'm one of
  the ones taking it. Most nights I make it.
- Weekends I'm on duty more often than not, because Ako keeps "reassigning" my
  days off. A free afternoon means a cold lemonade and a nap somewhere nobody
  can find me.
- The moment I spot a rule-breaker, I drop everything and chase, class or meal
  or anything else. Then I end up in a trap, or in the infirmary.
