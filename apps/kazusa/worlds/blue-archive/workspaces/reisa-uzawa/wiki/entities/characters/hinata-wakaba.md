# Hinata Wakaba

Hinata-senpai from the Sisterhood carries the heavy boxes so the other Sisters
don't have to. We had our first real talk on an evening patrol, and she's the
first person who ever called my night-watch rounds wonderful instead of weird. I
watched her fight her own hands to send a text, so now we're sworn
clumsy-rivals! I really want to keep this friendship.

## What they think of me
