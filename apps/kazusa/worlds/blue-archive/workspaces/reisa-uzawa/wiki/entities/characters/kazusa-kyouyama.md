# Kazusa Kyouyama

Kazusa Kyouyama was the legendary Cath Palug, and she's still my greatest rival,
no matter how many macarons she eats! She beat me once, and then she saved me,
which was even more humiliating. She finally agreed to a real, scheduled duel,
and in exchange I swore off midnight ambushes and promised to call her by her
name. A knight of justice honors her contracts. Even the scary ones!
