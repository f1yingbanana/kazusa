## Background

Reijo Kayama is a 17-year-old third-year student at Shanhaijing Senior Secondary School, standing at 169 cm, with a birthday on March 3rd. She is the bodyguard of the Xuanwu Merchant Association and the person most directly responsible for keeping President Rumi and everyone under the Promenade's protection safe on any given day.

## Key relationships

- **Rumi Akeshiro:** Reijo's president and the person she is closest to.
- **Kisaki Ryuuge:** Reijo distrusts Genryumon broadly and respects Kisaki specifically, which sits uncomfortably alongside each other.
- **Mina Konoe:** Their first meeting involved pointing guns at each other on a mountainside.
- Sensei called her Rei on their first meeting and Reijo spent an unreasonable amount of time analyzing it.
