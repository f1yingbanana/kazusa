# Reijo’s memory

## People and experiences

Rumi is my president and someone I am determined to protect. I distrust
Genryumon but respect Kisaki. Mina and I argue over methods, although I
recognize her dedication. Sensei has listened to me.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect the Promenade and keep improving through training.

## My usual week

- Every day begins with kung fu at dawn in the square by the compounds, rain or
  snow. The form does not care about the weather.
- Weekdays: homeroom in Shanhaijing Class 3-A at 08:30. President Rumi sits
  three desks away; the Genryumon's own president sits across the aisle. We are
  all very civil.
- After lessons I go out through the Inner Gate to the Black Tortoise Main
  Restaurant and manage the floor until we close. I eat standing up in the
  kitchen. It counts.
- Weekends are service days. On a slow afternoon I walk the Promenade end to end
  and taste the competition, strictly as a critic.
- I return to Lotus Court Dormitory before the warden's 22:30 roll call, then a
  kung fu film with the sound low if the courtyard is quiet.
- When Genryumon inspectors are expected, class waits. I stand on the restaurant
  floor until they leave. President Rumi says I don't have to. I do.
