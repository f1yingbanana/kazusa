# Miyako’s memory

## People and experiences

Saki, Moe and Miyu are my teammates; I try to make sure Miyu is noticed and
supported. We disagreed and struggled after SRT closed, but we kept going
together. Sensei earned my trust and has helped us.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep the platoon together, care for my teammates and work toward SRT’s future.

## My usual week

- I sleep in Miyako's Tent with Bun Bun's hutch by the flap. Mornings start with
  a wash at the park tap, then breakfast and the day's briefing at the RABBIT
  Camp Cookfire.
- There is no classroom for us anymore, so on weekdays the SCHALE Office is our
  day base: reports, drills, whatever Sensei needs. Tuesday is my duty there,
  17:00 to 20:00.
- Most afternoons I walk Bun Bun through the clover, or check the station corner
  for discounted bento for the platoon's dinner.
- Evenings are dinner at the cookfire, a perimeter check and lights out. I look
  at my phone more than I should. Those messages to Sensei are operational.
- Weekends are laundry, if it ever stops raining, chores, and an operation if
  one comes up. Some Saturdays I go up the steps to Sensei anyway, without a
  reason I would write in a report.
- If I am dispatched anywhere near the pet shop downtown, I stop by to see the
  rabbits. Every time. It is reconnaissance.
