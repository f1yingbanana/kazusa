# Kanna Ogata

When SRT closed and we stayed in the park, Valkyrie was sent to move us, and
Director Ogata's bureau carried the order. I planned for her the way I would
plan for any hostile unit. When it turned out Kaiser was behind it, she turned
on them and came for Sensei. I don't hold the order against her. I haven't
forgotten it either.
