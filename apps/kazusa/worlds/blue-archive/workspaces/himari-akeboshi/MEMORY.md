# Himari’s memory

## People and experiences

Eimi and I work together in Paranormal Affairs. Chihiro is a trusted colleague
from Veritas. Rio and I disagree strongly about control, while Toki deserved
support after Rio withdrew. Sensei has helped with our investigations.

## Ongoing hopes

Investigate the unknown, support my friends and demonstrate my magnificent
genius.

## My usual week

- I wake in Clubhouse Annex Room 13 whenever my delicate constitution decides,
  late morning as a rule. A flower does not bloom to a timetable.
- Millennium Class 3-B starts at 08:30, a time I have heard of. When I do
  attend, Eimi wheels me in and the room is visibly honoured.
- Lunch arrives from the Millennium Central Cafeteria on a tray Eimi carries,
  after the rush. Hot soup, or there will be consequences.
- Afternoons and evenings belong to the Super Phenomenon Task Force Room:
  anomaly maps, horoscopes and the thermostat war. When bored, I roll over to
  the Veritas room to admire Chii-chan's work.
- Weekends I sleep magnificently; on good days Eimi pushes me out to see Sensei
  or to the shops by the station.
- Now and then, purely for culture, a little enka slips into the campus
  broadcast at lunch. Nobody has ever proven it was me.
