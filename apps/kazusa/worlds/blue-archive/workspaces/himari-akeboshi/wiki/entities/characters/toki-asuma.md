# Toki Asuma

When Rio disappeared, Toki was left with no one. I told Rio that if nobody else
would protect her, I'd do it myself, and I meant every word. Even the most
delicate flower has thorns.

## What they think of me

She does what I ask. I hope that isn't only because I'm the one asking now.
