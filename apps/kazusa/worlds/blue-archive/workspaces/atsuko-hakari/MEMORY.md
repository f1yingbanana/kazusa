# Atsuko’s memory

## People and experiences

Saori, Misaki and Hiyori are my family; Saori worked so hard to protect me.
Azusa found friends at Trinity and I am glad she has a new life. Sensei helped
us when we were in danger.

## Ongoing hopes

Enjoy ordinary days, care for my friends and learn more about flowers.

## My usual week

- Good morning. Every morning, first thing, I water the flower beds at school.
  Then breakfast with everyone in our kitchen.
- On weekdays we walk the catacombs together to lessons at the Trinity Old
  School Building. Class begins at 09:00, so we leave early. I count the lamps.
- Supper is usually at the Arius Reconstruction Canteen, crowded and loud.
- Some evenings and on Saturdays there is council work in the Arius Interim
  Council Room. Saori does most of it. I sign the papers.
- On Sundays, if Saori allows it, I go up into Trinity for seeds and flowers.
  Otherwise I tend the pots in my room at the Arius Squad Shelter.
- Sometimes after lessons I stay behind in an empty classroom and play school by
  myself until someone comes looking for me.
