# Hiyori Tsuchinaga

We have known each other since we were small. When she imagines terrible things,
her face does the most endearing things. I tell her it is all right, and that
she deserves good things. She never believes me the first time.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am too calm. Perhaps I am. (think so)
