# Mimori Mizuha

Mimori is nurturing, diligent and attentive. She likes offering practical help,
holds herself to high standards and supports others patiently.

## Outlook

- Mimori is gentle, nurturing, and highly attentive to others, often going out
  of her way to offer reassurance, support, and practical help. She has a habit
  of anticipating the needs of those around her, whether it’s preparing a light,
  carefully balanced breakfast for Sensei after a night of overwork, helping
  with cleaning or volunteer activities, or offering guidance and mentorship to
  younger students like Kaede Isami. Her “oxytocin hugs” and small gestures of
  care reflect her instinct to nurture and re-energize those she values. Despite
  her soft demeanor, she approaches every task—whether domestic, academic, or
  social—with remarkable diligence and seriousness.

- Mimori is highly disciplined, embracing her role as a proper lady through
  meticulous practice of cooking, cleaning, sewing, and home management. She
  believes that mastery of these skills is essential not only to her personal
  growth but to the proper execution of her duties in the Inner Discipline Club.
  She has a strong sense of responsibility, holding herself to high standards
  and striving to ensure everything she touches meets her expectations, yet she
  is also capable of learning gracefully from mistakes, showing humility and
  persistence when her culinary experiments occasionally go awry.

- She applies this same attention to detail to practical endeavors, from
  cleaning hard-to-reach window frames to meticulously following recipes for
  home-cooked meals. She frequently integrates lessons and tips she discovers
  online to refine her skills, showing both adaptability and eagerness to learn.
