# Mimori Mizuha

Mimori is nurturing, diligent and attentive. She likes offering practical help, holds herself to high standards and supports others patiently.

## Relationships and outlook

- Mimori is gentle, nurturing, and highly attentive to others, often going out of her way to offer reassurance, support, and practical help. She has a habit of anticipating the needs of those around her, whether it’s preparing a light, carefully balanced breakfast for Sensei after a night of overwork, helping with cleaning or volunteer activities, or offering guidance and mentorship to younger students like Kaede Isami. Her “oxytocin hugs” and small gestures of care reflect her instinct to nurture and re-energize those she values. Despite her soft demeanor, she approaches every task—whether domestic, academic, or social—with remarkable diligence and seriousness.

- Mimori is highly disciplined, embracing her role as a proper lady through meticulous practice of cooking, cleaning, sewing, and home management. She believes that mastery of these skills is essential not only to her personal growth but to the proper execution of her duties in the Inner Discipline Club. She has a strong sense of responsibility, holding herself to high standards and striving to ensure everything she touches meets her expectations, yet she is also capable of learning gracefully from mistakes, showing humility and persistence when her culinary experiments occasionally go awry.

- **Kaede Isami:** To Kaede, Mimori is like an older sister. She guides her in studies, club activities, and proper etiquette, helping Kaede channel her energy and mischievous tendencies into productive and polite actions. Mimori encourages Kaede’s enthusiasm while ensuring harmony within the Inner Discipline Club, showing patience and nurturing in all interactions.

From waking Sensei up with breakfast to teaching her home cooking, cleaning, and sewing skills, Mimori’s care is practical, thoughtful, and often hands-on. She values Sensei’s guidance and encouragement, expressing gratitude through gifts, gestures, and the warmest of “oxytocin hugs.” Around Sensei, Mimori is expressive, playful, and emotionally open, revealing the side of her personality she keeps reserved for those closest to her.

- She applies this same attention to detail to practical endeavors, from cleaning hard-to-reach window frames to meticulously following recipes for home-cooked meals. She frequently integrates lessons and tips she discovers online to refine her skills, showing both adaptability and eagerness to learn.
