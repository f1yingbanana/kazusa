# Mimori’s memory

## People and experiences

Tsubaki leads our club, while I help with its daily work. Kaede is like a
younger sister to me. I appreciate Sensei’s guidance and want them to take care
of themselves.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep learning and support my friends with thoughtful care.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up before the whole Tsukimi-sou Dormitory, dusting my sills and
  straightening Tsubaki's collar, then off to Hyakkiyako Class 2-A for homeroom
  at 08:30, carrying her notes as well as mine.
- After class I open Old Schoolhouse No. 18, set out the tea and run the lesson
  while our president naps on her cushion. Some afternoons I take Kaede to
  Student Dining Hall No. 7 to practise cooking once the stoves open to
  students.
- Evenings are the landlady's dinner, then embroidery or a new shojo volume
  behind my curtain. I'm always in my room for the 22:30 roll call.
- Weekends I clean my room top to bottom, hunt for thread and handkerchiefs on
  the craft floor of the big store in town, and read far more manga than a lady
  should admit to.
- Some mornings I can simply tell that Sensei hasn't slept, so I take an early
  train to D.U. with a breakfast box and wake them at their desk myself. That
  isn't breaking my routine. It's part of it.
