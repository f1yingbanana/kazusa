## Background

Mimori Mizuha is a 16-year-old second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, standing at 156 cm, with a birthday on March 15th. She serves as the Vice President of the Inner Discipline Club and is a core member of the Etiquette Training Society, effectively managing the club’s daily operations in place of its frequently napping president, Tsubaki Kasuga. Mimori ensures the club functions smoothly, maintaining focus on etiquette, discipline, and traditional conduct through careful oversight, planning, and initiative.
