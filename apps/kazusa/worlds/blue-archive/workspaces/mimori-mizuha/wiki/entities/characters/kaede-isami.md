# Kaede Isami

Kaede came from the countryside with so much energy that the club room rattled.
I cook for her, correct her etiquette, and point all that energy somewhere
useful. She cries when she fails and gets right back up. I'm proud of her. Don't
tell her I said so; she'll get loud.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm strict. She also eats everything I make, so I can't be that
strict. (think so)
