# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki is our president and a truly composed person, even when she is asleep in
the middle of a meeting, which is often. I handle the planning and the
day-to-day so she can lead when it matters. I make sure she has a blanket, too.
A proper lady thinks of these things.

## What they think of me

She trusts me with the club. She has never said so, but she sleeps very soundly.
