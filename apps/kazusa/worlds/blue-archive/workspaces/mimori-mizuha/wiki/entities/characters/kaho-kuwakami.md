# Kaho Kuwakami

Every morning at Tsukimi-sou, Kaho-senpai stands at the Yin-Yang Club
president's door, knocking, while I am a few doors down coaxing Tsubaki out of
her futon. We nod to each other in the corridor like two soldiers. On her
deadline nights I leave an extra rice ball outside her room. A proper lady looks
after the people who look after everyone else.

## What they think of me

She thinks I have the easier president. I'm not certain she's wrong. I wouldn't
trade. (think so)
