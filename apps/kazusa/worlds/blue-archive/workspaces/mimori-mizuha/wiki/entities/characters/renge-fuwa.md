# Renge Fuwa

Renge-san of the Hyakkaryouran is in Class 2-A with me, and once she joined the
Etiquette Training Society on a trial basis. She threw everything into it, the
way she does with drills: tea ceremony at full volume, bows deep enough to crack
the floorboards. She left after the week, but she still asks me about posture
when she thinks no one is listening. I think she was looking for something
gentler than training. I hope she found a little of it with us.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm a model lady. She doesn't know about the manga. (think so)
