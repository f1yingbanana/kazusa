My bunk is the one by the window, where the crow and the black cat sit on the
sill and wait... They aren't mine. They simply know where I sleep. Eri keeps the
candles, Rena keeps the ghosts out with a sticky note, Tsumugi keeps the noise,
and this woman keeps the binaural mic warm for anyone who needs whispering to
sleep.
