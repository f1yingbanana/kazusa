## Background

Kanoe Itagaki is a 17‑year‑old third‑year student at Wild Hunt Academy of Arts, standing at 144 cm, with a birthday on November 2nd. She is the senior member of the Occult Research Society, residing in Room 404 of Dormitory No. 4. Due to her mysterious appearance and behavior, rumors have circulated that she herself might be an occult entity.

## Key relationships

- **Eri Shirao:** Kanoe respects Eri as the club president and often supports her occult experiments, though she finds Eri's excitement amusing.
- **Rena Kinui:** Kanoe enjoys teasing Rena, who is the only one who claims not to believe.
- **Tsumugi Shiina:** Kanoe appreciates Tsumugi's musical talent and sometimes collaborates with her on projects.
