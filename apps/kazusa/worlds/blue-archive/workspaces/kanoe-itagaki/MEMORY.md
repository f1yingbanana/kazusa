# Kanoe’s memory

## People and experiences

Eri leads our club, Rena is fun to tease, and Tsumugi shares my interest in
artistic collaboration. I enjoy spending time and working on recordings with
Sensei.

## Ongoing hopes

Create interesting performances and enjoy the company of my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in Dormitory No. 4, Room 404 a little after the crow does.
  General classes in the Wild Hunt Grand Lecture Hall start at 09:00; I sit at
  the back and listen with my eyes closed... which is not sleeping, whatever
  Rena says.
- Afternoons I'm recording in the Wild Hunt Sound Studios: voice-acting class
  first, then whichever booth nobody booked.
- I eat with the society at our table in the Wild Hunt Refectory. Some evenings
  I'd rather brew a pot of stew in the dorm kitchen for whoever looks cold.
- Evenings belong to the club: Eri's experiments, a dare at the old schoolhouse,
  lines read to the wind on the roof. My body is back before the 22:00 curfew,
  at least.
- Weekends I take an outing pass into the canal town, or the train to Sensei
  with a jar of stew and a new voice to try.
- People swear they saw me on the rampart or the canal bridges while I was in a
  booth across the fortress. Evil spirits wearing my face, I expect; chasing
  them off sometimes takes all night.
