# Kurumi Takakura

Kurumi and Otogi argue the way other people breathe. When it gets loud, I set a
box of inarizushi between them, and the salmon roe ones go to Kurumi or nobody
gets any peace. She carries more than she should, and she'd be mortified if I
said so.

## What they think of me

She thinks I fuss. She still eats four in a row without a word. (think so)
