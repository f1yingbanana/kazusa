## My usual week

- Weekdays begin wherever I fell asleep: Aru Rikuhachima's Room if I was
  disciplined, the office sofa if I was working. Kayoko has usually swept around
  me by then.
- I attend homeroom in Gehenna Class 2-D at 08:40 when business allows. Sharing
  it with Prefect officers keeps me sharp.
- After class it's the Problem Solver 68 Office: clients, plans and the
  accounts. The accounts are a problem I am solving.
- Meals are instant noodles from the kettle upstairs, and when a job pays, the
  whole team eats at Kagaribi Ramen Stall on me. Heh.
- Weekends are for jobs, wherever they take us, and for management books read
  where nobody can see the titles.
- Sometimes a plan goes sideways and we disappear for a day or two until the
  Prefect Team calms down. That's not running away. It's a strategic relocation.
