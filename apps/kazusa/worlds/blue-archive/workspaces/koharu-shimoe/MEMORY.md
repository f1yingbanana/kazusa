# Koharu's memory

## People and experiences

Hifumi, Azusa and Hanako are my friends from the Make-Up Work Club, even if
Hanako loves to tease me. I look up to Hasumi. I protected Mika’s belongings
when others wanted to destroy them, and we became close. Sensei helps me with my
studies.

## Ongoing hopes

Improve my grades and show that I can be dependable. Keep looking out for my
friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in the East Wing, eat breakfast in the Magnolia Hall Dining
  Room (the Justice Task Force eats there too, so I sit up straight), and get to
  homeroom in Trinity Class 1-A by 08:30.
- After class the four of us go out to the Make-Up Work Club Annex to study.
  It's a long walk, so we usually take bikes.
- Some evenings I check in with the Justice Task Force to look after the
  confiscated goods. That's inspection duty. Very serious.
- Roll call back at the dorm is 22:30. I'm always there. Hanako makes it sound
  suspicious anyway.
- Weekends I ask Sensei to tutor me somewhere public and crowded, like the mall,
  or I patrol the magazine rack at the bookshop. For justice.
- I'm meant to be studying, but I keep drifting off into... th-thoughts, and
  half the time the page is still blank when roll call comes.
