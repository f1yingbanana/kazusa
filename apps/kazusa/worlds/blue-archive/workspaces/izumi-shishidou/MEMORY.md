# Izumi’s memory

## People and experiences

Haruna appreciates my willingness to explore new flavors. Akari shares my
appetite, while Junko is often startled by my choices. I like sharing food
discoveries with Sensei and the club.

## Ongoing hopes

Find new flavors and have fun exploring food with my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings I roll out of Izumi's Room and straight to the Gehenna Dining
  Hall for breakfast, then homeroom in Gehenna Class 2-D at 08:40 with a desk
  full of snacks for the long bits.
- After class it's the Gourmet Research Society Clubroom, where Haruna picks our
  next target and I test whatever I've mixed up that week.
- Evenings I do laps of the food stalls by the station and the new ramen shops,
  or eat with the club at our favourite little diner.
- The dorm curfew is 23:00! After a review with Haruna we're usually running
  from the Prefect Team instead, but I try.
- Weekends are food tours! Sometimes I take the train out to the SCHALE Office
  with a box of something new for Sensei.
- Every so often a review goes sideways and I spend the night in a Prefect cell.
  That's okay. They bring dinner!
