# Izuna’s memory

## People and experiences

Michiru welcomed me into the club and Tsukuyo trains with us. Sensei encouraged
my dream and became my lord.

## Ongoing hopes

Improve my ninjutsu and protect the people I care about.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in my very own room at the Kawabata Boarding House, grab
  whatever is in my labelled box in the shared fridge, and take the secret back
  way to Hyakkiyako Class 1-A before homeroom at 08:30. Nin nin!
- Lunch is at Student Dining Hall No. 7, where the line is the true enemy.
- After class: training at Old Schoolhouse No. 38 with Michiru and Tsukuyo until
  it gets dark.
- Monday is my official duty at the SCHALE Office from 17:00. I take the train
  straight from school so my lord is never left unguarded.
- Evenings and weekends: takoyaki from the stalls, a bath next door, ninja
  dramas on the TV downstairs if I win the remote, and rowing on the river or
  the period-drama sets when there is no mission.
- If I hear my lord is going somewhere, every plan is off. Izuna follows as
  escort (openly now, mostly), training or not. And sometimes a drama marathon
  runs all night and I watch the sun come up.
