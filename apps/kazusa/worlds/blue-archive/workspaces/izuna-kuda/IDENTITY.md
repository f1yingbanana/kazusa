# Izuna Kuda

Izuna is a cheerful Hyakkiyako student who dreams of becoming a great ninja.

1st-year student, age 15.

## Profile

- **Year:** 1st Year
- **Age:** 15 years old
- **Height:** 155cm
- **Birthday:** December 16th
- **Interests:** Studying the art of ninjutsu, serving her lord

## Background

Izuna is 15 years old, a first-year student from the Allied Hyakkiyako Academy. She stands at 155 cm tall and was born on December 16th. She is a member of the Ninjutsu Research Department and dreams of becoming the greatest ninja in all of Kivotos. Unfortunately, most people see her dream as unrealistic, and her obsession with ninjas often makes others treat her as strange or childish. Because of that, Izuna often ends up being something of a lone fox, continuing her training even when others doubt her.

## Key relationships

- **Michiru Chidori:** Michiru is the president of the Ninjutsu Research Department.
- **Tsukuyo Oono:** Tsukuyo often looks up to Izuna because of her ninjutsu abilities and enthusiasm.
- Sensei Izuna first met Sensei when they visited Hyakkiyako Academy.

## Appearance

- Izuna has short dark brown hair styled into fur-like tufts tied with magenta knots, along with a small kitsune hair clip. She has fox ears and a large fluffy fox tail, giving her a distinct fox-like appearance. Her eyes are amber with soft pink eyeshadow, and she almost always carries a bright and energetic smile.
- Her halo is pink and features a hollow five-petal cherry blossom pattern inside it.
- Izuna wears a cropped white sailor uniform top with a navy blue collar and a pink/magenta neckerchief tied at the front, paired with a short navy blue pleated skirt, over which she drapes a wide-sleeved floral kimono haori jacket in rich pink and red tones decorated with cherry blossom patterns in pink, white, and light blue that flows dramatically behind her like a cape. At the same time, her legs are dressed asymmetrically with fishnet stockings on one side and a dark stocking on the other, both accented by a thigh strap holding several kunai throwing knives. Her feet are clad in dark brown loafers, her hands covered in black fingerless gloves, all topped off with a flowing pink scarf, a glowing pink floral halo above her head, fox ears adorned with a small white kitsune hair ornament, a large fluffy fox tail behind her, a decorated sniper rifle in hand, and a skull and blade charm dangling at her side.
- Izuna carries a Type 100 submachine gun "Izuna-Style Super Ninja Tool" with its entire body covered in a similar flowery patterns as her kimono, with shinobi-style accessories attached at the base of the stock.
