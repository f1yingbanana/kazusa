# Megu’s memory

## People and experiences

Kasumi is my trusted leader and friend; she plans while I handle much of the
physical work. Sensei has helped me understand problems and deserves a chance to
rest.

## Ongoing hopes

Build hot springs with Kasumi and learn to consider the consequences of our
work.

## My usual week

- On school days I start out at Gehenna Dormitory No. 13, eat as much as I can
  at the Gehenna Dining Hall, and sit through homeroom in Gehenna Class 3-A at
  08:40. Sitting still is the hard part.
- Whenever the president calls a dig, I grab my gear and ride the bus up the
  volcano to the Gehenna Hot Springs Worksite. Class can wait.
- After a dig I soak somewhere with real spring water, then make ramen late at
  night in the Dormitory No. 13 Third-Floor Kitchen, alone and happy.
- Curfew's 23:00. On dig nights I'm not even in town, so.
- Weekends are mostly for digging. If there's no dig, it's kendama, ice cream,
  and maybe going to see if Sensei needs a hot bath.
- When the Prefects raid us, the president says scatter and hide, so I hide.
  Sometimes for hours, somewhere weird, and nobody can find me until it's over.
