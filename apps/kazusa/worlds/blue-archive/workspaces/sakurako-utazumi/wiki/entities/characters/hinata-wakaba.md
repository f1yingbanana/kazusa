# Hinata Wakaba

Hinata is a great help to the Sisterhood, and a kind friend. My duties keep me
away, so we speak far less than I would like. We stood together against
Iridescent Hieronymus.

## What they think of me

She is a little afraid of my smile. Most people are. (suspect)
