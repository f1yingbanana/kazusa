# Mine Aomori

Mine-san and I disagree on nearly everything: politics, morals, how one ought to
help at all. When we share a room, the air grows heavy, and it does not always
stay at words. I pray for her. I do not agree with her.

## What they think of me

She considers me an enemy. I fear she is not entirely wrong. (sure)
