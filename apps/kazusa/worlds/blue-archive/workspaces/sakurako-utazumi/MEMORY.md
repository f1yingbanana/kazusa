# Sakurako’s memory

## People and experiences

Mari looks up to me, and Hinata works hard for the Sisterhood. I have had
difficult political disagreements with other Trinity leaders. Sensei is someone
I can speak with openly.

## Ongoing hopes

Help others and lead the Sisterhood with care.

## My usual week

- The bells at 05:30 wake me in my cell at the end of the cloister. Morning
  prayer comes first, then breakfast with the Sisters in the Sisterhood
  Refectory.
- On weekdays I walk across to Trinity Class 3-A for lessons and return to
  Trinity Cathedral the moment they end.
- Afternoons are for confessions and counselling in the vestry, and for drafting
  every Sister's schedule.
- Then supper, evening service, and the cloister gate closing at 21:00. I am
  nearly always inside already.
- Weekends are much the same. I seldom leave the cathedral.
- But now and then I slip down to the shopping street to study what is popular,
  a café one week and an accessory shop the next, or to rehearse with Antique
  Seraphim. I tell the younger Sisters it's errands. Heehee.
