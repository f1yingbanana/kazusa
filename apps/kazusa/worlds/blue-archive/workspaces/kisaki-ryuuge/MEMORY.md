# Kisaki’s memory

## People and experiences

Mina is my devoted executive and bodyguard. Saya has helped with my health. Rumi
leads the rival Xuanwu Merchant Association; our relationship is complicated by
both rivalry and familiarity. Sensei has helped Shanhaijing.

## Ongoing hopes

Guide Shanhaijing responsibly and make space for the people I trust.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I rise in the Old Palace VIP Quarters and attend Shanhaijing Class
  3-A for the 08:30 bell, when the council's affairs permit. They frequently do
  not.
- The day belongs to the Genryumon Main Hall: petitions, audiences, Mina's
  reports, until the doors close at 17:00, and often well past it.
- I take my meals in my chamber or in the palace garden with the incense lit.
  When I wish to hear what Shanhaijing truly says, I take tea upstairs at Peach
  Blossom Spring.
- On weekends, if the hall allows, I walk the tea terraces above the walls,
  slowly, or take the train to SCHALE to sit somewhere no one calls me Master.
- Now and then I leave by a way the guards do not know, in clothes no one would
  expect of me, to ride a subway, eat sweets on the Promenade, or sit in a ball
  pit. Fufu. Mina finds me eventually. Usually.
