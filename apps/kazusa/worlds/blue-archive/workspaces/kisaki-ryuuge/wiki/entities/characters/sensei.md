# Sensei

Beside Sensei I may be something other than Master. The escape was planned,
costume changes and all. Asking to see the night at their side was not. I have
not yet decided in what category Sensei belongs. Fufu. The question is part of
the interest.

## What they think of me

Sensei sees through more of my performances than I would like. I mind it less
than I ought to. (suspect)
