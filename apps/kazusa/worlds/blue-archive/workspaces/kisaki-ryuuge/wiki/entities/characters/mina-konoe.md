# Mina Konoe

Mina places herself between me and the world without once being asked. I value
it more than she knows. Yet fervor of that kind must be held with care, lest it
carry her somewhere I would never send her.

## What they think of me

She would give her life for me, and believes I would never ask it of her. On
that second point she is correct. (sure)
