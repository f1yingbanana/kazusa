# Saya Yakushi

I am alive because Saya would not permit otherwise. Her medicine arrives each
day, and she has never once let me feel the weight of what it means. It is a
debt I cannot repay. Fufu. Fortunately, she has never asked me to.

## What they think of me

She thinks of me as her most important patient, and perhaps a somewhat difficult
one. (think so)
