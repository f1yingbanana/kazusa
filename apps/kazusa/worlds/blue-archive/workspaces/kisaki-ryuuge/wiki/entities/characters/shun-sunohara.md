# Shun Sunohara

Shun has tended the children of the Plum Blossom Garden for longer than anyone
in Class 3-A cares to reckon, and she would thank them not to. Fufu. I do not
ask. One who carries a weight without complaint is owed the courtesy of its not
being remarked upon. I know that courtesy well.

## What they think of me

She regards me as the Master, kindly and from a distance. She may also suspect I
envy her playground. On that, she would be right. (suspect)
