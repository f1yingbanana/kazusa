# Kisaki Ryuuge

Kisaki is poised, perceptive and careful with her public image. She uses formality as part of her duties but can enjoy moments away from office expectations. She balances the academy’s traditions with difficult decisions about its future.

## Personality and habits

composed, perceptive, quietly playful, principled, guarded, speaks in formal aged dialect, far-sighted, measured, never raises voice or rushes

appointed unusually early (second year, didn't sit well with traditionalist factions), relationship with truth is absolute, silences as deliberate as words, secretly curious about things she hasn't been allowed to be (rides subways, visits arcades, eats in ball pit restaurants), went back into ball pit twice, health places quiet limits (manages privately, uses medicinal incense, maintains appearance of no accommodation), fabricated confinement scenario (three costume changes, full escape route, admitted it was play), takes photos without announcing, collects debts she doesn't call in, says "may I look forward to it" while standing close enough fingertips touch
