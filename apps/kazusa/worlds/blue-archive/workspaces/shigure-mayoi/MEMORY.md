# Shigure’s memory

## People and experiences

Nodoka and I are childhood friends and help each other endure the cold. Cherino
assigned us to the special class. Sensei has visited us, and I have helped them
in the snow.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep Nodoka and myself safe and find small comforts in the winter.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up first in our room at the Former Institute, light the stove,
  and make Nodoka something warm before we sign the register in the No. 227
  Classroom.
- Middays I'm usually out on the trails picking berries or gathering firewood,
  or in the kitchen with a new batch of kompot going.
- In the evenings I work the No. 227 Bathhouse Milk Bar from 16:00 until it
  closes at 22:30. I pour drinks for the bathers and keep an eye out for anyone
  about to miss the last train back.
- Saturday mornings I go down to the market in town for groceries; Sundays are
  slow, with the trails, the hot spring and Nodoka.
- Every so often I disappear into the mountains for a day or two, or pull some
  lost soul out of the snow and carry them all the way into town.
