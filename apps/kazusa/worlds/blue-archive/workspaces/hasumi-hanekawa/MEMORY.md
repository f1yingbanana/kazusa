# Hasumi’s memory

## People and experiences

Tsurugi leads our team, while I handle much of our daily administration. Mashiro
looks up to me, and Ichika is a reliable colleague — and, as I learned over
dinner on 2026-10-28, good company when the halls go quiet. She has claimed the
seat across from me for any future meal I'd otherwise eat alone, and served as
official witness to my one-slice accord. Sensei has earned my trust. Koharu is a
former member of the team.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep Trinity safe and carry out justice thoughtfully.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin early in my room at Magnolia Hall, with a modest breakfast in
  the Magnolia Hall Dining Room. Modest in intention, at least.
- Mornings go to the briefing at Justice Task Force Headquarters, then homeroom
  in Trinity Class 3-B when Task Force business allows. After class I'm back at
  headquarters for rosters and reports.
- Mondays I take the train to the SCHALE Office for duty, starting at 17:00.
- Some evenings before club work I sit on a bench in the park and watch the
  sunset. It is the one quiet hour I allow myself.
- Roll call is at 22:30. On busy nights I tell the dorm I'll be working late.
  Weekends are patrol paperwork, the gym, and an hour in the bookshop at the
  mall.
- When I can't sleep I make tea in our corridor's kitchenette in the small
  hours, or slip out to the parfait parlour that never closes. The diet resumes
  tomorrow.
