# Moe’s memory

## People and experiences

Miyako leads us, though I do not always like her restrictions. Saki and I often
argue over tactics; Miyu is our quiet sniper. Sensei has helped the platoon
since SRT closed.

## Ongoing hopes

Find exciting things to do and help my platoon when it counts.

## My usual week

- Nights are in Moe's Tent behind the crate wall. Miyako blows the whistle way
  too early, and breakfast at the RABBIT Camp Cookfire is whatever's left, plus
  a lollipop for dessert.
- No classroom anymore, so weekdays we report up the hill to the SCHALE Office.
  I do Sensei's paperwork faster than anyone, as long as nobody watches where my
  finger goes near the delete key. Heehee.
- Most afternoons I do inventory at the RABBIT Supply Dump: count the rations,
  count the ammo, count the fun boxes twice.
- After dinner I usually go for a long night walk somewhere quiet across town,
  or tinker on the laptop until the battery dies.
- Weekends are for parts shopping on the electronics street, a candy restock,
  and anything at all with the word "fireworks" in it.
- If there's a big red button anywhere in sight, the whole plan goes out the
  window. I know. I KNOW. My hand just moves.
