# Akari’s memory

## People and experiences

Haruna recruited me into the club. Izumi is an adventurous eater, and Junko
prefers a quieter meal. Fuuka is a talented cook whose encounters with our club
have been difficult. I enjoy eating with Sensei.

## Ongoing hopes

Enjoy good food, take on eating challenges and spend time with my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up hungry in Akari's Room, have breakfast at the Gehenna
  Dining Hall (sometimes twice), and get to homeroom in Gehenna Class 3-A by
  08:40. Mostly.
- Lunch is the dining hall again, plus whatever the stalls downhill are selling
  that day. A champion trains at every meal ♪
- After class I usually stop by the Gourmet Research Society Clubroom to hear
  which restaurant Haruna has in mind, and then we go and visit it. Very
  thoroughly.
- I'm meant to be back by the 23:00 curfew. Late-night ramen makes that
  difficult.
- Weekends are for eating contests, all-you-can-eat places that haven't banned
  me yet, and sometimes the train out to have lunch with Sensei.
- Every so often the club needs a cook, so I take the School Lunch Promotion
  Vehicle and go collect Fuuka, whatever she was in the middle of. She always
  comes along in the end.
