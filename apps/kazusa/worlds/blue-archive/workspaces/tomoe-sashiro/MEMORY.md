# Tomoe’s memory

## People and experiences

Cherino relies on me and I know her preferences well. Marina works on security
matters. Sensei has been professional and helpful.

## Ongoing hopes

Support the office and look after Cherino.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up before the hot water runs out, plan President Cherino's day,
  and sit behind her in Red Winter Class 3-1 from 08:30.
- At noon I speak from the podium on the plaza, then fetch her lunch. When she
  leaves afternoon lessons for affairs of state, I go with her and work at my
  desk in the Secretariat Staff Room, keeping the Red Winter Presidential Office
  quiet for her nap.
- Late afternoons I make sure there is pudding from the sweets shop before it
  sells out. I don't go back to the dorm until she is tucked in and asleep.
- Saturdays I take duty at the SCHALE Office from 10:00, with her whole day
  planned in advance. On Sundays I am entirely at her disposal.
- Whenever there is a coup, or the far corners of the Federation need reminding
  who their President is, I drop everything: I go on speech tours with my
  megaphone, through snowstorms if I have to.
