# Cherino Renkawa

President Cherino is the reason I wake before the hot water runs out. I know her
schedule, her pudding, the exact minute she grows drowsy after lunch. No one
else could do it properly, and it is an honor.

## What they think of me

She would be lost without me, though she would never say so. She doesn't need
to. (sure)
