## My usual week

- School days I wake up in my room at Gehenna Dormitory No. 13, do my makeup
  (non-negotiable!), and slide into Gehenna Class 2-D with Eri right before
  08:40 homeroom.
- Lunch is usually the Gehenna Dining Hall. We go a bit late so the line's less
  of a war zone, and chat the whole way through.
- After class Eri and I hang out in Gehenna Old Classroom 7, our totally
  unofficial club room, then wander down the hill for parfaits, selfies and new
  besties.
- Curfew's 23:00 and I'm, like, mostly back for it.
- Weekends are sleeping in, then the shops or the boutiques under the station
  with Eri. If I've tanked a test, I'm on the train to SCHALE begging Teach for
  tutoring.
- When a make-up test is coming, I go full serious mode: I hole up in the old
  classroom and cram for real, phone face-down, no posting at all. It's kind of
  a whole thing.
