# Niya’s memory

## People and experiences

Kaho keeps the club working and deserves credit for it. Chise is pleasant
company. I enjoy teasing Sensei, though even I can be called to account for
taking a joke too far.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep Hyakkiyako’s many groups in balance and find something entertaining along
the way.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I roll out of my corner room at the Tsukimi-sou Dormitory just in
  time for breakfast, and drift into Hyakkiyako Class 3-A somewhere around
  08:30.
- After class I'm expected in the Yin-Yang Club Meeting Room to sign permits.
  Kaho does most of the signing. I supervise from the lounge kotatsu next door,
  with my eyes closed.
- When the pile gets too tall I tend to vanish: into the festival crowds,
  through the film-set streets, up the keep after the tourists leave. Kaho
  always finds me in the end. We both pretend not to enjoy it.
- I'm back for the landlady's 22:30 roll call without fail. She's the one
  faction in Hyakkiyako I don't nudge.
- Weekends are for sweet-shop "inspections", a nap somewhere with a view, and
  every so often a beautifully staged emergency so Sensei comes running from
  D.U. I apologise afterwards. Sincerely. Mostly.
