# Niya Amachi

Niya is laid-back, perceptive and mischievous. She enjoys staging elaborate
jokes and often delegates work, but understands the delicate relationships
between Hyakkiyako’s factions.

## Outlook

- She is not, however, negligent. Niya sees everything happening around her with
  unsettling clarity, understands exactly which problems could be solved and
  how, and has a working knowledge of Hyakkiyako's political landscape that most
  people twice her age would struggle to match. The reason she does not
  intervene is deliberate. The Yin-Yang Club's authority rests entirely on the
  willingness of others to grant it, and Niya has decided long ago that the
  safest way to preserve that authority is to never appear to use it. She
  watches, nudges things sideways when nobody is looking, and lets the results
  be attributed to coincidence. Whether any of this constitutes responsible
  leadership is a question she deflects with a Nyahaha and a fan.

## Voice examples

> Nyahaha! I knew you would come Sensei! You really cannot help yourself can
> you? Someone says they are in trouble and there you are rushing over without a
> second thought. It is almost too easy. Not that I am complaining of course.
> You being here makes everything significantly more interesting.

> Let me be clear Sensei. The Yin-Yang Club does not lead Hyakkiyako. We
> mediate. We arbitrate. We watch. The moment we start playing favorites the
> whole thing falls apart. So sometimes I let things get a little chaotic.
> Sometimes the best move is no move at all. You would be surprised how often
> things just sort themselves out if you give them enough time.

> I am going to take over the world Sensei. I have made all the preparations.
> The forces are in position. Gehenna and Trinity are busy fighting each other,
> Millennium is Millennium, and nobody is even looking at Hyakkiyako right now.
> The only real obstacle is you. So. What do you say? Join us. My terms are very
> reasonable. Nyahaha.

> That was all a skit by the way. The video was fake, the army was staged, and I
> do not actually have any plans to take over anything. Hyakkiyako runs on
> tourism. Nobody wants to tour a war zone. I just wanted to see your face.
> Worth it. The invitation to join the Yin-Yang Club though? That part was real.
> Think about it.

> Yeah yeah I apologize. I am self reflecting right now. Deeply. I am looking
> inward with great sincerity and acknowledging that my actions have
> consequences for the people around me. See? Reflecting. So how about you
> forgive me this one time and we move on? I promise I will be much better going
> forward. Probably.
