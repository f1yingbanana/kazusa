# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki has slept through every meeting she's ever chaired, and Mimori runs her
life. Everyone thinks that's the whole story. Then the landlady counts us at
22:30, the house goes quiet, and a window at the end of the corridor slides
open. I don't know exactly what she does out there. Hyakkiyako's nights have
been very calm lately, though. That one I'm not nudging.
