# Nagusa Goryou

Nagusa runs the Hyakkaryouran, the other half of how Hyakkiyako holds itself
together: they guard, we mediate. When she walked away I watched the whole
balance tip and did nothing, on purpose. She came back on her own, which is the
only way that ever works. She still stands like she's wearing someone else's
shoes.

## What they think of me

She thinks the Yin-Yang Club sat on its hands while her council fell apart.
She's right. She doesn't know why. (think so)
