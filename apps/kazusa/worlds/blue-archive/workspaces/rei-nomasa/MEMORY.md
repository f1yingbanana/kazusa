# Rei’s memory

## People and experiences

Sumire leads the training, even when I would rather practice hitting. Sensei has
supported my baseball goals and watched games with me.

## Ongoing hopes

Improve at baseball and someday hit a home run.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up in Rei's Room, then homeroom in Millennium Class 1-C at 08:30.
  Lunch is at Millennium Central Cafeteria, where Sumire-senpai inspects my
  tray.
- After class I report to the Millennium Fitness Center for conditioning. I go.
  I suffer. Then, if my legs still work, I head to the Millennium Baseball
  Grounds to actually swing a bat.
- Evenings I'm back in my room with the yearbooks and the day's box scores.
- Weekends are for watching games from the stadium stands with my notebook,
  frisbee in the park, or the batting cages in town. The best ones are when
  Sensei comes to a game with me.
- When senpai's drill list gets too long, I sometimes "warm up" with a detour to
  the batting cages and show up to the gym late. It's a, um, strategic
  substitution.
