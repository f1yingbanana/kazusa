# Momiji’s memory

## People and experiences

Meru is my fellow Knowledge Liberation Front member; we share a love of
creation, though her blunt critiques can sting. I have had to ask Cherino to
leave the library when she naps there. Sensei shares my interest in books.

## Ongoing hopes

Preserve important books and keep the library going.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in my room in the dorm, grab breakfast at the Third
  Communal Cafeteria if there's time, and get to Red Winter Class 1-4 for 08:30.
- After class I go straight to the Red Winter Federal Library and stay at the
  circulation desk until closing, mending spines and shushing people. On Mondays
  it's closed, so I go browsing for secondhand books on 3rd Street instead.
- Some days I take another purchase request to the Red Winter Office Building.
  They usually say no.
- I'm supposed to be back in the dorm for roll call at 22:30.
- Weekends are for book hunting: the used bookshops, the plaza whenever there's
  a festival, and once in a while a long trip to wherever a rare edition has
  turned up.
- When I'm on the trail of something like the legendary fanzine, I forget all of
  this. I miss meals and class, I go out digging in the snow with my dowsing
  rods, and sometimes I just fall asleep in my nest in the stacks instead of
  going home.
