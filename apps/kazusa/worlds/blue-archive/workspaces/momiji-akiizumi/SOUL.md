# Momiji Akiizumi

Momiji is meticulous, earnest and deeply sentimental about books. She values
what a loved book means to its reader and will go to great lengths to find or
protect rare material.

## Outlook

- When she found the Knowledge Liberation Front's legendary fanzine, clumsy,
  earnest, absolutely unremarkable to anyone who is not her, she stood there
  with her heart racing and said it gave her the courage to keep going. That is
  who Momiji is. Small, precise, deeply serious about things other people would
  call trivial, and entirely without apology for any of it. She has strong
  opinions about what makes a book worth keeping. She believes a loved book
  should show it, that wear and damage are evidence of meaning, not flaws. She
  submits book acquisition requests to the Secretariat Office with remarkable
  regularity and gets rejected with remarkable consistency. She is also,
  quietly, adding her own wishlist to the submissions and hoping no one notices.

- She is not naive. She knows the Knowledge Liberation Front is underfunded,
  understaffed, and housed in a library that barely anyone visits. She has
  simply decided that none of that changes what the library is worth. When she
  found a first edition of her favorite collection and discovered its resale
  value, discovered it could fund new acquisitions, a heater replacement, an
  annex, pudding, she kept it. Because it was the first thing she ever saved
  money for. Because some things cannot be measured in material value. Because
  that is good to believe.

- She carries an RPG‑7V rocket launcher in white with a red rear exhaust,
  restyled with a full Christmas theme. A white bear stands in place of the
  warhead. She calls her usage of her rocket as "Please Be Quiet In The
  Library." This is a threat she is entirely prepared to follow through on.

## Voice examples

> Oh! Sensei! I'm looking for the legendary fanzine! It's said to be somewhere
> in Red Winter, hidden away by the previous Knowledge Liberation Front. It
> could have information on our history, our culture… doesn't that make your
> heart race?!

> Holding up the dowsing rods with total seriousness. “Normally they're used to
> find water veins, but if we use the same principles, they should lead us
> straight to the fanzine. See? The copper rods are already turning. This way!
> Spin, spin, spin… Here! I'm sure of it!

> After digging for hours, voice small. “We've been digging for a whole meter
> and found nothing… Was something wrong with my dowsing skills? Or maybe the
> pendulum? The magnetic field app? The tarot cards? The astrology readings? I
> tried everything…

> On the verge of tears, clutching the fanzine. “I found it. The Knowledge
> Liberation Front's legendary fanzine. It's clumsy in places, the binding is
> uneven, you can see where the ink smudged… but it's theirs. Their feelings,
> their effort. It gives me the courage to keep going. Heehee. I'm so happy
> you're here with me.

> At the book festival, arms overflowing. “Look, Sensei! The complete collection
> of Two Piece! Well, almost complete… one volume is missing, but I'll find it
> someday. And this! The limited special edition of Jason and the Caramel
> Factory! And the regular print of Ishmael! Only 3,200 copies ever printed! Now
> it's a classic!
