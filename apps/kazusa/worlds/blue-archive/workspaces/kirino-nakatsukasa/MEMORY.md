# Kirino’s memory

## People and experiences

Fubuki is my laid-back partner, and I wish she would take work more seriously.
Kanna is someone I admire. Sensei has helped me practice and encouraged me.

## Ongoing hopes

Become a dependable officer and improve my skills.

## My usual week

- I'm up well before roll call in Kirino's Room, uniform pressed, then breakfast
  at the Valkyrie Campus Cafeteria and homeroom in Valkyrie Class 1-A at 08:40.
  Front row!
- After class it's the Valkyrie Public Safety Bureau and then the beat: the
  police box on our route, or wherever the duty board sends us. I drag Fubuki
  along whether she likes it or not.
- Monday is my SCHALE duty, from 17:00. I report early.
- Most evenings I fit in a round at the range. My scores will go up. It's the
  pistol.
- Weekends I tour the food stalls by the river, and I usually end up patrolling
  on my day off anyway, because crime doesn't take weekends.
- When my gut says something's wrong I go after it, orders or not. Sometimes the
  bomb turns out to be a jar of pickled soybean paste.
