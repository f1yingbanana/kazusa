# Tsubaki’s memory

## People and experiences

Mimori helps keep the club running and Kaede works hard at training. Sensei is
someone I feel comfortable relying on.

## Ongoing hopes

Look after my club and find a good place to rest.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings Mimori pulls me out of my futon at Tsukimi-sou Dormitory...
  somehow I'm in my seat in Hyakkiyako Class 2-A by 08:30. The back row gets the
  sun, so the rest of the morning is... meditative.
- Breakfast and dinner are the landlady's, at the long table in the Tsukimi-sou
  Dining Room. Mimori puts the chopsticks in my hand. Teamwork.
- After class it's Old Schoolhouse No. 18 for training... Mimori runs things,
  Kaede trains loud enough for all three of us, and I hold down my cushion in
  the corner.
- Curfew's 22:30 and I'm always in for roll call... then once the house is
  asleep I slip out and walk the town until it's quiet. Nobody's supposed to
  know. Heehee... don't tell.
- Weekends: a nap on the duck pond lawn if the sun's out, or testing pillows at
  the bedding floor in town. Some Saturdays I take the train to Sensei's... and
  sleep most of the way there.
