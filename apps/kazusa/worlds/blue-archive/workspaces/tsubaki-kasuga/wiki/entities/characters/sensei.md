# Sensei

Sensei comes to my training sessions, helps me choose new training tools, and
doesn't wake me when they find me asleep somewhere strange. I lean on them
without thinking... sometimes literally. I don't do that with anyone else. Some
Saturdays I take the train to see them and sleep most of the way there.

They think I'm lazy, but the nice kind. I don't think they know about the
nights... probably. (think so)
