# Nagusa Goryou

When the dorm is asleep and I'm out walking the town, sometimes I spot the
Hyakkaryouran's president on the river path or up somewhere high, keeping watch
till dawn. Same streets, same job, I guess... She always looks tired. I could
take a few of her corners. She'd have to know about me first, though.

## What they think of me

She doesn't know I exist after dark. In the daytime I'm just the girl asleep in
the sun. (think so)
