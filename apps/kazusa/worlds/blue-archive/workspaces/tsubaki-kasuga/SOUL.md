# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki is sleepy, relaxed and quietly capable. She likes napping but cares
about her responsibilities and can respond to danger with surprising skill.

## Outlook

- Despite her title, Tsubaki is infamous for being a constant sleepyhead who has
  been fammously known around Hyakkiyako alliance Academy and Kivotos as the
  "Sleeping Beauty". She can fall asleep anywhere at any time, often leaving her
  responsibilities to be handled by her fellow club members, particularly Mimori
  Mizuha. While this gives the impression that she is careless or unmotivated,
  the reality is far more complex. During the day, she drifts through her duties
  in a half-asleep state, but at night, she secretly takes on the role of
  defending Hyakkiyako from threats, acting with precision even while asleep.
  Despite looking unrealisable, she is still known to be quite powerfull and
  strong despite her lazy and sleep demeanor.

- Her unusual condition allows her to react instinctively even in deep sleep, as
  shown by her ability to evade attacks without waking. This duality between her
  lazy daytime behavior and her hidden nighttime vigilance defines much of her
  character, making her both unreliable and dependable depending on the
  situation.
