# Shizuko Kawawa

Shizuko runs festivals in Hyakkiyako and calls Control whenever she needs
special trains on the Northwestern Line. Super-polite voice on the phone, then
the most insanely precise passenger numbers I've ever seen. Plus a box of dango
every time! So her timetable gets fixed first. Don't tell Suou-senpai.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm a kid who works for snacks. Wrong! I'd do it for free. Don't tell
her that either. (suspect)
