# Nozomi’s memory

## People and experiences

Hikari is my twin and partner in mischief. Suou guides us and expects us to be
responsible. I like teasing Sensei and inviting them along.

## Ongoing hopes

Find fun adventures and do my part for the railway.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I roll out of Nozomi's Room at the last possible second, bang on
  Hikari's wall, and make it to the Highlander Lecture Wing by 09:00. Usually.
  Pahyahya.
- Afternoons are shifts on the Central Control Center Operations Floor:
  dispatch, driving runs, and reports I put off until Suou-senpai notices.
- I eat at the Highlander Crew Mess with Hikari, or we squeeze in next to Aoba
  at her diner by the yard gate so she can't eat alone.
- Quiet hours start at 23:00, and I'm asleep in my uniform before anyone can
  check.
- Weekends: queueing for limited merch in the department store over the station,
  a free run if the roster's empty, or dropping in on noob Sensei at the SCHALE
  Office.
- After a big overtime shift I sometimes skip my own bed completely and crash on
  Sensei's sofa instead.
