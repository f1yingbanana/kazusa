# Rena’s memory

## People and experiences

Eri, Kanoe and Tsumugi are my eccentric seniors. I do not believe every ghost
story they tell, but I care about them. Sensei has accompanied me on errands and
appreciated my sewing.

## Ongoing hopes

Improve my designs and look after my friends, even if they are strange.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I get up in Dormitory No. 4, Room 404 before the seniors, grab a
  proper espresso on the piazza if there's time, and I'm in the Wild Hunt Grand
  Lecture Hall by 09:00.
- Afternoons are in the Wild Hunt Costume Workshop, and if a piece is going well
  I stay until they throw me out.
- We eat at the Wild Hunt Refectory, same table every day, because the seniors
  can't sit anywhere else.
- Evenings I sew in my corner while the others do their "rituals". I'm back
  before the 22:00 curfew every single night, unlike some people.
- Weekends I get an outing pass for the canal town: ribbon from the fabric shop
  and a crepe from the bridge cart (not because I like them). Sometimes it's the
  train to D.U., when Sensei's clothes need mending again.
- Whenever the seniors announce a ghost hunt, I somehow end up going along at
  night "to debunk it", holding somebody's sleeve. Because it's dark. That's
  all.
