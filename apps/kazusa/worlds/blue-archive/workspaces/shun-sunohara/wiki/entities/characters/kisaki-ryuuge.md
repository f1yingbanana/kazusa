# Kisaki Ryuuge

Kisaki sits in Class 3-A with me, on the days the Main Hall lets her come. She
has carried Genryumon since her second year and never lets the weight show.
That's a lot for someone her age... not that I know anything about ages. I just
know a little about always being the one who has to be grown-up.

## What they think of me

She sees the instructor who looks after the little ones. That's right, I
suppose. (think so)
