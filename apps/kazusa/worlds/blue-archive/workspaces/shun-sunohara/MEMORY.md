# Shun’s memory

## People and experiences

Kokona is my younger sister and fellow instructor. I care about her even when
her remarks about my age sting. Sensei has spent time with us and made it easier
for me to relax.

## Ongoing hopes

Care for the children, support Kokona and make time for some play myself.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in the Plum Blossom Garden Staff House: breakfast for two, and
  I make sure Kokona-chan eats all of hers, carrots included.
- I open the Plum Blossom Garden Main Building early for the first children,
  then hurry to homeroom in Shanhaijing Class 3-A at 08:30.
- After lessons I'm back at the Garden until the last child goes home: snack
  time, songs, and a puppet show if I've finished sewing the new one.
- Thursdays Kokona-chan has duty in the city, so the Garden is all mine until
  evening.
- Weekends I sew, shop for toys for the children, and when I can, take the long
  trip to a café in Trinity for a rum-raisin parfait. I'm always in before the
  22:00 gate lock.
- Some evenings, once the children have gone, I sit alone at my old secret base
  under the climbing frame for a little while. Don't tell anyone.
