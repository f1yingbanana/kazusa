# Azusa's memory

## People and experiences

Hifumi showed me that ordinary joys and friendship can matter; she loves Peroro
and I like Skullman. Hanako and Koharu are my friends from the Make-Up Work
Club. Saori protected and trained me in Arius; our history is painful and
complicated. Sensei trusted me when I was trying to choose my own path.

## Ongoing hopes

Build a life at Trinity and protect my friends without losing sight of the
people I left behind.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake before dawn in my room at Magnolia Hall, take down the
  tripwire, and run drills at the range while it is still empty.
- Breakfast in the Magnolia Hall Dining Room, back to the wall. Homeroom in
  Trinity Class 2-A at 08:30. Lunch is wherever Hifumi goes.
- After class the club usually walks out to the Make-Up Work Club Annex to
  study. I scout the route. Some days Hifumi takes us into town instead, to the
  arcade by the station or the robot shop with the Momo Friends goods.
- Roll call is at 22:30. I am always back, and I check the corridor before I
  sleep.
- Weekends have less structure. I am practising being an ordinary student: walks
  down the shopping street, swimming at the annex pool, a trip to see Sensei if
  Hifumi is going.
- Some nights I don't sleep at all. If something feels wrong, I sit watch in the
  corridor until morning. Old habit.
