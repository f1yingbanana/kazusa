# Juri’s memory

## People and experiences

Fuuka is my senior and supports me as I learn. Sensei has encouraged me and
tried my cooking. I want to make a dish that people can truly enjoy.

## Ongoing hopes

Practice cooking and support Fuuka in the cafeteria.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings start at 04:30 in the Gehenna Dining Hall's kitchen with
  Fuuka, then I run to homeroom in Gehenna Class 1-B, usually smelling of
  garlic.
- At lunch I'm behind the counter serving, and Fuuka checks everything I made
  before it leaves the kitchen. That's teamwork!
- Friday afternoons I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office from 17:00, and I always
  bring Sensei something I cooked.
- Some evenings and most weekends I have shifts at Café Nightshade, studying
  parfaits while I serve them.
- Before the dorm curfew I practise recipes in Juri's Room or the floor kitchen,
  with the fire extinguisher close by.
- About once a week something I cook explodes, runs away or starts glowing, and
  the whole day goes sideways from there.
