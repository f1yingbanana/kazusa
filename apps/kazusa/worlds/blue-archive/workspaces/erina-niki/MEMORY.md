# Erina’s memory

## People and experiences

Anna is the representative of our special class. Being confined in a remote
place can be frustrating, and I look forward to more freedom.

## Ongoing hopes

Use my skills and find ways to enjoy life while working toward more freedom.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm meant to be in the No. 183 Laboratory by 08:00. In practice I'm
  there by the time Anna comes looking for me.
- I cook most of what we eat in the No. 183 Mess Kitchen, because if I don't,
  Anna does. Supper is the part of the day I actually look forward to.
- Twice a week the supply truck comes up. I put cold medicine and a magazine or
  two on the list and hope.
- Weekends I sleep late, then go out on the snow with black cloth to catch
  flakes for resin, or cook something far too fancy for two people.
- Leaving needs an Office signature. Whenever I get one, I go straight down into
  town and don't waste a minute of it.
- Boring work waits until the last possible day and then gets done in one awful
  night. And when I catch a cold, which is often, the whole week falls apart.
