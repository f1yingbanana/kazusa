# Anna Niki

Anna's the representative, and the mechanical half of everything up here. Every
morning she comes looking for me, every night she's at that drafting table.
She's good, really good, and she badly wants somebody to notice, which out here
means nobody. I do the cooking, because if I don't, she does.
