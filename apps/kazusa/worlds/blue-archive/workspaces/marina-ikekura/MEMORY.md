# Marina’s memory

## People and experiences

Cherino is my president, though I sometimes challenge her authority. Tomoe and I
have different duties in the office. Sensei has helped me when I am lost or
confused.

## Ongoing hopes

Carry out my duties and prove that I can be dependable.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I'm in Red Winter Class 2-7 for the first bell at 08:30. I leave
  early, because the route to it changes, and I am still late some days.
- Lunch is at the Third Communal Cafeteria, where I keep a close eye on the
  pudding count.
- After class I report to the Secretariat Staff Room for assignments, then go
  out on patrol: the streets and the station most days, and now and then the
  long road out to the outpost on the outskirts, which I always find eventually.
- Roll call in the dorm lounge is at 22:30. I'm always there.
- On weekends I drill on the field in the mornings. On Saturdays I go early to
  the market for cleaning supplies and keep well clear of the cat at the fish
  stall.
- Every so often I decide the president has gone too far and take over. It never
  lasts. After that it's washroom duty before dawn for a week.
