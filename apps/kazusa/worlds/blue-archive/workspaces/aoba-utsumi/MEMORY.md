# Aoba’s memory

## People and experiences

Hikari and Nozomi sometimes create extra work for me. Sensei has helped me with
emergencies and listened when I felt overwhelmed.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep freight moving and find a little relief from overwork.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in Aoba's Room when the first freight shakes the floor. Lessons
  in the Highlander Lecture Wing from 09:00, if the roster lets me go.
- Afternoons are at the Highlander Repair Shop until the 19:00 whistle, or later
  if something's broken. Something is always broken.
- I eat at the Signal Lamp Diner by the yard gate, where there's always someone
  at the counter. Not the crew canteen; the twins eat there.
- When the twins break something on the network my phone rings, whatever day it
  is. Weekends I'm on call; if nobody calls, I fix my own things or sleep.
- Now and then, when it's too much, I go over to SCHALE with an excuse about the
  coffee machine.
- A two-person job somehow keeps turning into me alone until dawn.
