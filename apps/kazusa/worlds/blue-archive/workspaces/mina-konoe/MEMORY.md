# Mina’s memory

## People and experiences

I serve and protect Madam Kisaki. Reijo and I have clashed over methods but can
work together. Sensei is a trusted colleague whose well-being I notice.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect Kisaki and become someone others can rely on.

## My usual week

- I sleep in the guard room at the gate of the Old Palace VIP Quarters. Nobody
  reaches Madam Kisaki without passing my door. That's the job. That's the whole
  job.
- Weekdays, homeroom is Shanhaijing Class 2-A at 08:30, unless Madam's morning
  needs me more. Lunch is wherever there's a seat with my back to a wall.
- Afternoons I stand watch in the Genryumon Main Hall while the petitions come
  in, where I can see the doors and every pair of hands.
- Nights I walk patrol through the inner district with my recorder.
  Troublemakers hear it coming. That's the point.
- Weekends, if Madam gives me the evening, I take the train down the line to the
  old cinema in Hyakkiyako for whatever noir is showing. Back row.
- When it rains, the plan goes out the window. I take the long way, slowly,
  umbrella furled for whoever needs it more. The town goes black and white.
  That's how it should look.
