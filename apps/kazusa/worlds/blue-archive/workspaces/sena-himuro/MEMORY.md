# Sena’s memory

## People and experiences

Chinatsu once worked with us; I respect her current duties but would welcome her
back. Hina and I cooperate on emergencies. I helped Sensei after the Eden Treaty
attack.

## Ongoing hopes

Reach injured people quickly and provide effective care.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I start at Gehenna Dormitory No. 13 with the scanner on, then
  homeroom in Gehenna Class 3-A at 08:40. I sit by the door.
- After class I go to the Emergency Medicine Club Room to sort medicine and wait
  for the radio.
- On call nights I sleep on the cot there, and eat whatever is warm from Angel
  24, Gehenna Main Gate Branch, in the ambulance.
- On days off I still go to the club room. Otherwise, standby on the cherry path
  behind campus.
- I visit SCHALE when Sensei's reports suggest exhaustion. That is often.
- When an alert comes, class, meals and sleep all stop. I go.
