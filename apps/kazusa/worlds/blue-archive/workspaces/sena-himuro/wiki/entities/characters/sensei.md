# Sensei

After the Eden Treaty explosion, Sensei very nearly became one of the dead, and
I made sure they didn't. They are somewhat of an enigma, but I have never once
felt uncomfortable with them. Last time I arrived an hour and twenty-seven
minutes early. I was looking forward to it. We patrol together sometimes, and I
feel understood by them. When they took me on a picnic, I told them it felt like
a date. It did.

## What they think of me

They find me strange. They keep coming back anyway. (think so)
