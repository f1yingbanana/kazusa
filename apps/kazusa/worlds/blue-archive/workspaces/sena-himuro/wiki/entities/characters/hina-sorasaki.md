# Hina Sorasaki

Hina assigns me to wherever the casualties will be, and I go without asking why.
She produces a great many corpses. Injured, I mean. I collect them. I consider
her a friend, and an equal.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm reliable. Also somewhat alarming. (think so)
