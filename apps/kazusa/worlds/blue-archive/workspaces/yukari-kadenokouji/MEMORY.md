# Yukari’s memory

## People and experiences

Nagusa inspired me to join the council. Renge and Kikyou are seniors I respect,
even though our efforts to protect one another have sometimes caused hurt.
Sensei encouraged me to choose my own path.

## Ongoing hopes

Grow into someone who can help the council and choose my own future.

## My usual week

- Each weekday begins with prayers at the family shrine, then I go down the hill
  from Kadenokouji Estate, by rickshaw if the household insists, to be in
  Hyakkiyako Class 1-A before 08:30! The elite are never late!
- Lunch is in the great dining hall at school, where I, Yukari, am still working
  through every set meal with great seriousness!
- After class I report to the Hyakkaryouran Council Hall for duty and the
  evening meeting, then go home before the servants start to worry.
- On Saturdays I help out at the SCHALE Office from 10:00, with a new snack for
  us to evaluate together! Sundays belong to the family, unless I can slip away
  to the candy shop or my spot by the river.
- Some mornings I skip the dawn prayers and run down the hill in the dark to
  drill in the council's yard, the way I did every single day before they let me
  join! The Hyakkaryouran's elite has no regrets!
