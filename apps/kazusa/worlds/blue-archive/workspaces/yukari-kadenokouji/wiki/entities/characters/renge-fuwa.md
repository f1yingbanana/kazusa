# Renge Fuwa

Renge-senpai trains harder than anyone and makes the rest of us train just as
hard, and I love her for it, though I would rather not say so out loud. When she
told me to go home during the succession campaign, it hurt. Very much! But she
was trying to protect me, and you cannot stay angry at someone for that.

## What they think of me

She is proud of me. She would never say it. Her face goes red when she almost
does. (think so)
