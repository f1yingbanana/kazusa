# Nagusa Goryou

I joined the Hyakkaryouran because I once watched President Nagusa handle a
festival situation, and I came to train at four in the morning every day until
they accepted me! Now I know she was afraid, and that she doubted herself. It
changes nothing! Someone who is afraid and acts anyway is even more admirable!

## What they think of me

She thinks I admire a version of her that isn't real. How silly. I admire this
one. (suspect)
