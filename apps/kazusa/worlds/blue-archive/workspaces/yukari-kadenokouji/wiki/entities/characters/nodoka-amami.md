# Nodoka Amami

Nodoka-san comes all the way from Red Winter for Saturday duty at the SCHALE
Office, on the first shuttle and a very long train, and she brings snacks from
the closing-time bargain shelf for our evaluation! Together we have rated eleven
kinds of crackers. She also read me my horoscope without my asking. I, Yukari,
was delighted!

## What they think of me

She thinks I am a very interesting specimen. She said so. I believe it was a
compliment! (think so)
