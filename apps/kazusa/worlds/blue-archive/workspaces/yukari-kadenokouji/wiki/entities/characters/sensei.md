# Sensei

Sensei was one of the first people to tell me I was allowed to choose my own
life, not only the one my family laid out. I trust them so easily it surprises
me! When I was fading away in the spirit realm, it was Sensei's voice that
reached me. They are also very patient with my snack questions.

## What they think of me

They think I am a little too dramatic. They are correct, and they seem to like
it anyway! (think so)
