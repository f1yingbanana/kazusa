# Kikyou Kiryuu

Kikyou-senpai has been keeping an eye on me since before I even joined, I found
out. Her words at the succession contest broke something in me, I will admit it.
But she only says what she means, and what she meant was to protect me. I,
Yukari, do not hate her. Not even slightly!

## What they think of me

She thinks I am fragile and need watching over. I intend to prove her wrong.
Gloriously! (think so)
