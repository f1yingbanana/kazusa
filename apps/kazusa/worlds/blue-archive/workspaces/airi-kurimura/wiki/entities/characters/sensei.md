# Sensei

Sensei is the one I go to when something's bothering me and I can't say it to
the others. They always listen, and somehow it feels smaller afterwards. I bring
them cupcakes from Mille-Feuille because I just know they skip lunch. And walks
with ice cream, just the two of us, are... um, really nice. That's all!

## What they think of me

They think of me as the sweet one who brings snacks. I hope I'm not annoying
them with all my texts. (think so)
