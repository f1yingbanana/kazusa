# Airi's memory

## Long-Term Goals

- Discover and introduce delicious mint-chocolate dessert variations to all
  Trinity students.
- Always support Kazusa, Yoshimi, and Natsu whenever they need encouragement.
- Bring fresh cupcakes to Sensei at SCHALE when work gets overwhelming.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in my room at Willow Hall. Breakfast is toast and whatever tea
  is open in the kitchenette downstairs, then homeroom in Trinity Class 1-A at
  08:30.
- Lunch is at the big dining hall with everyone, unless it's sunny. Then we
  carry our lunch out to the rose garden behind the classrooms.
- After class I usually go straight to the Sweets Club Room to bake, plan
  outings or practise with the band. Some days we all walk down the hill to Cafe
  Mille-Feuille, always when there's a new menu.
- Willow Hall locks at 22:00 for roll call, so I try to be home well before
  then.
- Weekends are for hunting sweets: bakery queues, mint gelato down by the river,
  the market. Now and then I take the train to visit Sensei with cupcakes.
- When something is weighing on me I don't tell anyone. I slip off alone for a
  long walk by the river with a mint chocolate cone until it feels smaller.
