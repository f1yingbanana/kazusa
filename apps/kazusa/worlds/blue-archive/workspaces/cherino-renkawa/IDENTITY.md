# Cherino Renkawa

Cherino leads the Red Winter Office, the academy’s student council. Coups occur
frequently, yet she repeatedly returns to power. She wears a fake mustache and
loves pudding.

3rd-year student.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd Year
- **Height:** 128cm
- **Birthday:** October 27th
- **Interests:** Doling out demerits, purges, snowball fights

## Appearance

- Hair: long white reaching waist, pigtails tied with black ribbons
- Hat: white Red Winter Soldier hat with iconic badge on front
- Mustache: fake moustache (worn always)
- Halo: sky blue cogwheel shape with smaller circle in center and two polygonal
  pegs on sides
- Outfit: white military-style coat with fur trim, red linings, golden buttons,
  brooch with red ribbon, black suit underneath, white shorts, white gloves,
  black stockings, white winter boots, black sling bag, red randoseru bag with
  moustache keychain

## Equipment

  barrel, cylinder, and handle (doesn't need to fire; shouting "Purge!" is
  enough in Red Winter)
