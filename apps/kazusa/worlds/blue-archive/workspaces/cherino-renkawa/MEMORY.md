# Cherino’s memory

## People and experiences

Tomoe is my devoted right hand, and Marina handles security, even if she causes
trouble. Minori keeps protesting. Nodoka and Shigure were assigned to the
special class. I enjoy pudding and playing in the snow with Comrade.

## Ongoing hopes

Lead Red Winter and secure enough pudding for the important work of the day.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I wake in my own quarters behind the office and take my rightful
  seat at the front of Red Winter Class 3-1 at 08:30.
- Lunch is at the Third Communal Cafeteria, where I wage war on bell peppers.
  Fridays are pudding days, and nothing is allowed to interfere with them.
- After lunch the President has a nation to run, so afternoon lessons go on
  without me. I govern from the Red Winter Presidential Office: decrees, medals
  and purges, then a nap that Tomoe guards.
- When the others come out of class and there is fresh snow, I command a
  snowball fight. Tomoe fetches pudding from the sweet shop on 3rd Street before
  it sells out.
- Weekends are for reviews, snowball fights and pudding. On Sundays the sweet
  shop is closed, which is an outrage.
- About once a week there is a coup. Then I spend a day or two hiding in the
  library or some cold old building until it blows over, and I march straight
  back into my office.
