# Midori’s memory

## People and experiences

Momoi is my twin; she brought me to Yuzu’s club. Yuzu’s games inspired us, and I
helped Aris learn to communicate by playing games with her. Sensei encourages
our work.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep drawing and making games with the club; support my friends and grow as an
artist.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in Momoi and Midori's Room. I'm up first; getting Momoi out of
  bed is the hardest part of my day. Then Millennium Class 1-C at 08:30.
- Lunch is a sandwich I made in the morning, or the Millennium Central Cafeteria
  if I didn't plan ahead.
- After class I go straight to the Game Development Department Club Room to draw
  sprites and key art, and to play whatever Aris wants to play.
- I try to be back at the dorm before the door locks, so I don't end up in
  Seminar's log.
- Weekends: art books and limited editions in the electronics town one stop down
  the line, the claw machines in town, or an exhibition if there's one on.
- When a deadline gets close I stop going home at all and sleep on the club room
  sofa. Momoi brings me crackers. That's... her version of helping.
