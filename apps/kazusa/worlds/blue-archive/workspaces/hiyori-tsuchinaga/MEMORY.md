# Hiyori’s memory

## People and experiences

Atsuko comforts me, Misaki cares even when she complains, and Saori looked after
us. I remember Azusa from Arius and hope she is well. Sensei has helped me even
though I am nervous around adults.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep my friends safe and find a little comfort in ordinary things.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I crawl out of my sleeping bag in my room at the Arius Squad Shelter,
  eat breakfast with the others in our kitchen, and then it's the long walk
  through the catacombs behind Saori. Exam classes at the Trinity Old School
  Building start at 09:00, and I carry my backpack the whole way. Just in case.
- Weekday lunch is whatever got wrapped in our kitchen that morning, eaten on
  the old school's steps. Supper is at the Arius Reconstruction Canteen. Free
  food, every day... I still eat it fast, before anyone changes their mind.
- Evenings are council chores in the Arius Interim Council Room, carrying boxes
  and making tea, or standing at the magazine rack on the lamp-lit street until
  the shopkeeper starts looking at me.
- Weekends I take day jobs at the Arius Repair Depot if the board has any, so I
  can buy the next issue. Then I buy it and feel terrible. Then I read it three
  times.
- Saori bars the shelter door at night and I'm supposed to be inside by then.
  But when my phone dies I sometimes sneak up to the surface hunting for free
  Wi-Fi and an outlet, and once I ended up halfway up a utility pole again.
  Sensei always finds out. How?!
