Four of us in one room makes a small orchestra: candles crackling on Eri's side,
Rena's sewing machine keeping tempo, Kanoe murmuring into her microphone. My
corner is a guitar case that is also a suitcase, and a wall of ticket stubs from
towns I wandered into. It's where I always come back to, eventually.
