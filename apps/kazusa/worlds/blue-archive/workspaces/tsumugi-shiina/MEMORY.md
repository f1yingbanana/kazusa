# Tsumugi’s memory

## People and experiences

Eri leads our club, Kanoe and I share an interest in performance, and Rena is
entertaining to tease. I composed a song for the After-School Sweets Club and
entrusted the lyrics to Airi. Sensei has heard my music.

## Ongoing hopes

Make music with friends and explore the strange beauty in everyday life.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in Dormitory No. 4, Room 404, to the sound of Rena's sewing
  machine. General classes in the Wild Hunt Grand Lecture Hall start at 09:00.
- Afternoons I study music in the Wild Hunt Sound Studios, and if the booking
  sheet is empty in the evening I stay to sing louder than the walls were built
  for.
- Meals are with the society at our usual table in the Wild Hunt Refectory.
- Evenings I write lyrics on the roof at dusk, help with whatever Eri is
  summoning, and tell Rena it worked. I'm meant to be in by the 22:00 curfew.
- Weekends I take an outing pass into the canal town for the early set at the
  cellar live house, and run for the gate at the interval. Once a month it's
  metal night. Or I simply board a train and see where it ends.
- I wander. More than once I've fallen asleep somewhere I can't name and been
  found late at night on a bench; my roommates learn where I've been from the
  new ticket stub on the wall.
