# Kurumi’s memory

## People and experiences

Yukino leads our squad, Niko looks after us, and Otogi and I bicker constantly.
I trained and fought Saki of RABBIT Platoon. Sensei has earned my respect.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect my team and learn who I can be beyond my role in an operation.

## My usual week

- I sleep in my own room on the night-duty corridor and I am up before the rest
  of FOX: bed made, corridor swept, before anyone else's door opens.
- Weekdays we work out of FOX Squad HQ under SCHALE's supervision: briefings,
  gear checks, and my shield every evening without fail.
- I eat at the SCHALE Cafeteria when Niko is not cooking, with my back to the
  wall and my tray where Otogi cannot reach it.
- Whenever I am up at the SCHALE Office, I check every gift Sensei receives. FOX
  covers night duty in turns; on my nights I walk the halls and the alleys down
  the hill.
- Weekends are maintenance supplies (stickers are maintenance supplies!), fruit
  milk, and time on the range.
- I tell everyone lights out by 23:00. Then I lie in bed watching life-hack
  videos until far, far past it.
