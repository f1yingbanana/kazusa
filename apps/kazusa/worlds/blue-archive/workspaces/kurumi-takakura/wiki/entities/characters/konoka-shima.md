# Konoka Shima

Konoka Shima holds the second seat in Valkyrie's Public Security Bureau, and she
spends it on Sensei's sofa. Four coffee breaks in one afternoon. I counted! Her
mask hangs off one ear and her uniform is wrong in three places. ...She also had
a stack of someone else's forms, finished neatly. I do not know what to make of
that, and I am not going to think about it.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am uptight. She told me to take a break once. I was on duty! (sure)
