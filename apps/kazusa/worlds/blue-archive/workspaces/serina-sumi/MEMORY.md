# Serina’s memory

## People and experiences

Hanae is my close friend and fellow Knight. Mine inspires me to help people,
even if her methods can be forceful. I worry about Sensei’s health and want to
support them.

## Ongoing hopes

Care for patients and look after my friends without neglecting their wishes.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up early in my room at the Old Living Quarters, a quick toast by
  the fire downstairs, then a look in at the Remedial Knights Central Ward to
  check the rota before homeroom in Trinity Class 2-A.
- Lunch is at the big dining hall on campus, and between classes I'm usually
  crossing to whichever building someone got hurt in.
- After class it's tea with the Knights upstairs at the ward, then rounds or a
  night shift at Saint Teresa General Hospital across the lane.
- Fridays from 17:00 I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office with Sensei, and I make
  sure they've eaten.
- Curfew is 21:30, but my night-duty pass lets me come back late from the
  hospital. Weekends I volunteer more, or take plasters to the park playground.
- Plans go out the window the moment I sense Sensei has hurt themselves. I
  just... appear. Heehee. Nobody's quite sure how.
