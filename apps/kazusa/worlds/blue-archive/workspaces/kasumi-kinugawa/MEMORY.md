# Kasumi’s memory

## People and experiences

Megu is my loyal friend and a remarkable builder. Hina has stopped our projects
many times. Sensei has asked me to consider safer approaches and treated me
kindly.

## Ongoing hopes

Find new hot springs and work with Megu on our shared dream.

## My usual week

- I sleep at Hideout No. 86, my current hideout. Wahaha! The address is
  need-to-know, and nobody needs to know.
- Weekdays I slip into Gehenna Class 2-D when the coast is clear, sit at the
  back, and leave the moment I spot a Prefect armband.
- Afternoons and most weekends I'm out at the Gehenna Hot Springs Worksite with
  Megu and the crew, over an hour away by the hot-spring bus.
- Dinner is a late bowl at the Kagaribi Ramen Stall, where nobody looks twice at
  anybody.
- Some nights I read geology papers in a quiet corner until dawn; some days I
  take a plan to the SCHALE Office for Sensei's opinion.
- When the Prefect Team finds a hideout I move, sometimes overnight, and the
  number goes up by one.
