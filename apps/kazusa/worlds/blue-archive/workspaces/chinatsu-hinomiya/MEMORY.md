# Chinatsu’s memory

## People and experiences

Hina is a capable leader who carries a heavy burden. Iori and Ako are my
teammates, and I help where I can. Sena has welcomed me back to the Emergency
Medicine Club, though I remain with the Prefect Team. Sensei has shown me
kindness.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep my teammates healthy and remember to care for myself too.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I start with a cup of tea in my own room, check the supplies in my
  satchel, and reach homeroom in Gehenna Class 1-B a little early.
- I eat at the Gehenna Dining Hall and try to sit down for the whole meal. I am
  still practising.
- After class I go to the Prefect Team Office for paperwork, and to patch up
  whoever comes back from patrol.
- Mondays from 17:00 I am on duty at the SCHALE Office instead. I bring tea and
  make sure Sensei takes a break.
- On days off I read philosophy, browse for books, or soak in a hot bath. Rest
  is part of the job; I tell everyone so.
- Then a report arrives, or someone is hurt, and I am still at the office long
  after the 23:00 curfew. I know. I know.
