# Kei’s memory

## People and experiences

Aris is precious to me; we shared a difficult origin and chose to protect each
other. Sensei and the Millennium students helped me return. I want to keep Aris
safe.

## Ongoing hopes

Care for Aris and discover what I want to do with my own life.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I leave Kei's Room on schedule and collect Aris from the Game
  Development Department Club Room on the way, so that we are both seated in
  Millennium Class 1-C before 08:30. She is welcome.
- Lunch is the Millennium Central Cafeteria, where I ensure Aris eats a
  vegetable. Momoi does not count as supervision.
- After class I report to the Super Phenomenon Task Force Room. Himari talks,
  Eimi fights her over the thermostat, and I do the work. That is the system.
- Evenings I check on the game club again. For security reasons. Only for
  security reasons.
- Weekends are for the boutiques near the station and, if Aris insists, the game
  center, where I observe. Occasionally I take Sensei a report I could have sent
  by message.
- Some nights I do not sleep at all; I stay up hardening the game club's servers
  while they snore. They are not to know.
