# Rio Tsukatsuki

Rio provided my first AMAS body. It was uncomfortable, but I acknowledge it was
made with genuine effort, and she understands the Nameless Gods' technology as
few others do. The moment she starts treating people as acceptable losses again,
I'll be the first to say so.

## What they think of me

She sees me as a problem she partly caused and partly solved. (suspect)
