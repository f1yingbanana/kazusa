# Hibiki Nekozuka

Hibiki understands that every machine is better with one more function nobody
asked for! We could talk about that forever. Well, I could. She tends to drift
off partway through, but she always comes back.

## What they think of me

She likes working with me. I think she wishes I'd talk to crowds a bit less, so
she wouldn't get pulled up there too. (think so)
