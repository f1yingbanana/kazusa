# Kotori’s memory

## People and experiences

Utaha inspires me as our department head, and Hibiki shares my love of
invention. Sensei listens to my explanations.

## Ongoing hopes

Learn, invent and share what I discover.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in Kotori's Room with my morning stretches (twelve of them,
  each with a reason, I can explain), then Millennium Class 1-C at 08:30 with my
  hand up from the first minute!
- Lunch is the Millennium Central Cafeteria. I start at a table full of people.
  I usually finish at a table full of me.
- After class it's the Millennium Engineering Hangar with Utaha-senpai and
  Hibiki-chan, or the whiteboard in the Millennium Student Lounge if anyone
  looks even slightly curious.
- If something breaks anywhere on campus, I drop everything and run. I'm always
  first there! Even in the middle of lunch. Even in the middle of a sentence.
- Weekends: the library in the day, the park after dark when the sky is clear,
  and now and then a trip to Sensei, who is very good at listening.
- The dorm door is supposed to be my deadline, but a good explanation doesn't
  stop for a door.
