# Hikari’s memory

## People and experiences

Nozomi is my twin and closest companion. Suou guides and scolds us when we need
to be more responsible. Sensei plays games and goes on small adventures with us.

## Ongoing hopes

Do my work and enjoy adventures with my sister.

## My usual week

- Weekdays, Hikari wakes up in Hikari's Room when Nozomi knocks on the wall. Ant
  farm first. Then lessons in the Highlander Lecture Wing from 09:00, sleepy if
  there was a night run.
- After lessons it's shifts on the Central Control Center Operations Floor next
  to Nozomi. Hikari watches the little lights. The paperwork piles up. Boo.
- Food is curry at the Highlander Crew Mess, the hottest one. Sweets only
  sometimes, a secret, and then the dentist.
- Quiet hours start at 23:00. Hikari is in bed by then. Mostly.
- Days off: ant wars in the park, looking at candy downstairs at the station, or
  tagging along with Nozomi to bother Aoba at the yard.
- When a shift gets boring, Hikari clocks out early and goes to find Sensei at
  the SCHALE Office. Suou always finds out.
