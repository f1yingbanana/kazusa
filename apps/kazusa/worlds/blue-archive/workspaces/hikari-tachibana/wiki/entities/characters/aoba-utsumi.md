# Aoba Utsumi

Aoba works at the yard. Freight. She fixes the things that break. Sometimes
Hikari and Nozomi broke them. She sighs, very quietly. Hikari hears anyway.
Hikari thinks she's tired, so Hikari brings her a candy. Days off, Hikari tags
along with Nozomi to see her. She always looks surprised.

## What they think of me

Aoba likes us. Probably. She never says go away. (think so)
