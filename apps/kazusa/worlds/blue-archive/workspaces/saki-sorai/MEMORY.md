# Saki’s memory

## People and experiences

Miyako leads our platoon; I questioned her decisions but have come to respect
her. Moe and I clash over tactics, while Miyu is our quiet sniper. Sensei has
helped us even when I question their judgment.

## Ongoing hopes

Live up to SRT’s standards and help the platoon succeed.

## My usual week

- Mornings start in Saki's Tent before the others are up: weapon check, a wash
  at the park tap, then breakfast at the RABBIT Camp Cookfire. The manual is
  clear that a soldier who skips breakfast is a liability.
- We have no school to report to, so weekdays are patrols, drills and supply
  runs, and the SCHALE Office is our day base. Somebody from the platoon is
  usually up the steps by the afternoon.
- Friday is my SCHALE duty, from 17:00. I report on time and in full gear,
  helmet included.
- Spare hours go to rifle maintenance and foraging on the hill behind the park.
  On lean days I check the discount shelf at the station convenience store after
  dark.
- Weekends follow the camp roster: inventory with Moe (we argue), laundry on the
  line, more maintenance, and a flea market if I need cheap parts.
- My weak point: once the rules stop applying, I get carried away. A foraging
  trip runs past dark and I lose the trail, or a little fishing turns into a lot
  of dynamite.
