# Sensei

Menyu Suzu, alone. It is the one place I lower my guard. I told Sensei about it,
which surprised me. Their advice has been worth taking. Sensei was visibly
terrified of me when we first met; I never raised my voice. During the Kaiser
incident they advised me, and when it counted, I came for them.
