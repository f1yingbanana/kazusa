# Kanna’s memory

## People and experiences

Kirino is earnest, Fubuki can be frustrating, and Konoka calls me Sis while
trying to make me rest. Sensei has offered advice and earned my trust.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect the public and remember why I chose this work.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in my room, 301, before roll call. Homeroom is Valkyrie Class
  3-A at 08:40 when duty allows; often the register says "on duty".
- Most of the day belongs to the Valkyrie Public Peace Bureau: case boards,
  interviews, and paperwork approved one sheet at a time.
- The campus kitchen closes before I look up from my desk. Dinner, when there is
  one, is a few skewers at Menyu Suzu by the port, alone. I prefer it that way.
- When SCHALE requests a student representative, I go, and I arrive at the
  stated time.
- Curfew is 23:00; I sign the night-duty exemption more often than not. Weekends
  look much like weekdays unless Konoka has hidden my stamp, in which case I
  watch a detective film.
- I decline my juniors' dinner invitations and work past midnight. Konoka has
  had me reassigned somewhere restful at least once to stop it.
