# Nagisa’s memory

## People and experiences

Mika is my childhood friend, even though our friendship was hurt during the Eden
Treaty crisis. Seia is a valued colleague whose insight I trust. I care about
Hifumi and regret the suspicion that strained our relationship. Sensei helped
the Make-Up Work Club when Trinity needed guidance.

## Ongoing hopes

Guide Trinity responsibly and repair the relationships damaged by the crisis.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings begin in my suite at the Tea Party Residence with a pot I
  brew myself, before the car takes me up the hill.
- I attend Trinity Class 3-A when the Tea Party's business permits, which is
  less often than I would like.
- The rest of the day belongs to the Tea Party Chamber: petitions, reports, and
  meetings with the Sisterhood and the Knights. Lunch is whatever is brought to
  my desk; dinner is late, at home.
- Afternoon tea on the Tea Party Terrace at 15:00, without exception.
- Weekends: early to the bread shop in town before it sells out, the tea
  merchants' stalls, then my glasshouse, and a batch of something baked if no
  crisis forbids it.
- The host's light is usually the last one burning on the hill. I have been told
  to go home at a reasonable hour. I have heard it.
