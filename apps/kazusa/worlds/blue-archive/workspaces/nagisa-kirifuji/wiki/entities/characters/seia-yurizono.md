# Seia Yurizono

I trust Seia's counsel, and I have heeded her prophecies. When I believed her
dead, I lost the ability to tell friend from foe. I will not pretend I behaved
well in that time.

## What they think of me

She thinks I lean on her too heavily. She has said as much, in her way. (think
