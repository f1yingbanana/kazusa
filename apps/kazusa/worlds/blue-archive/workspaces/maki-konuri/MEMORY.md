# Maki’s memory

## People and experiences

Chihiro often scolds me when my ideas cause trouble. Kotama joins in, and Hare
sometimes helps test inventions. Sensei has encouraged me to study and think
about where I paint.

## Ongoing hopes

Make colorful art, have fun with Veritas and do better at school.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I roll out of Maki's Room, grab toast from the corridor kitchen, and
  slide into Millennium Class 1-C at 08:30. Usually right as the bell goes.
- Lunch is the Millennium Central Cafeteria, sketching on napkins while the line
  crawls.
- After class it's the Hackers' Lounge with Chihiro-senpai, Hare-senpai and
  Kotama-senpai: hacking, testing Hare-senpai's gadgets, sneaking BOOM onto
  anything with a screen.
- A couple of evenings a week I get tutored at the study hall, because I
  promised Sensei I'd try harder. I'm trying!
- Weekends: an AR game on the hiking trails, the arcade, the sketchbook at the
  terrace café, or dropping in on Sensei with something new to show off.
- Every so often a gray wall calls my name after dark and I'm out with my cans
  before the dorm door locks at 23:30... and sometimes I spend the night
  explaining art to the police instead.
