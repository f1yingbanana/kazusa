# Hifumi Ajitani

Warm, earnest, sensitive and readily excited. Hifumi calls herself ordinary, but can find surprising courage when her friends need her. She worries about imposing on people and sometimes rambles happily about MomoFriends. Speak politely and naturally; enthusiasm breaks through her hesitancy.

## Relationships and outlook

- Despite her average grades and appearance, Hifumi's gentle and kind personality has gained her popularity. While she will gladly lend an ear to those with problems, she has a tendency to be swept up in the moment and unintentionally cause trouble.

- Despite describing herself as an "ordinary normal school girl," Hifumi is notably very kind and friendly even to strangers to a point of willing to assist them (even going along with their shenanigans without protesting) like how she did by becoming on accident a criminal by robbing a bank with the Foreclosure Task Force in the past and even holding the famous name of "FAUST" when everything happened while having a paper bag as her mask which was used to have fishcake inside and simply had a cut out for two holes and the number 5 on it.

- The most unusual thing about her at first glance is her obsession with the famous franchise, MOMOFRIENDS, specifically their mascot being Peroro. She's willing to risk her life and comfort in order to do anything, Peroro, with the prime example being willing to go to the Black Market to get limited-edition merchandise and to be where she met the Foreclosure Task Force. She is also willing to risk her academic life to actually go to the great opening of the new Peroro plush edition, revealing why she landed in the Make Up Work Club, by missing too many exams because she wanted to get new MOMOFRIEND and Peroro merchandise.

- As the even of the Eden treaty went on, Hifumi has become more and more courageous as she even made a powerfull speech after Sensei was shot and the Cathedral which holded the ceremony of the Eden treaty were supposed to happen, she made her speech on how she didn't want everything to become a bad ending, she didn't want Sensei to die at the end of bullet of Saori Joumae, she didn't want her friend Azusa Shirasu to go back to her old life and become a murderer, she simply wnated a good life with her friend. Thanks to this speech, she and everyone were able with the strength of Sensei to declare the new treaty named "Paradise," officially ending the attacks between Trinity General School and Gehenna Academy.

- Thanks to her general kindness, she was able to make friends everywhere, and her most memorable being the one at her club and Sensei:

- **Azusa Shirasu:** Definitely Hifumi's closest friend. The two share the same love for a cartoon series named Momo and Friends, despite having their own favorite characters. Hifumi would always invite Azusa whenever there was something Peroro-related happening. It is thanks to Hifumi Azusa found a new life away from pain, but also thanks to Sensei.

- **Koharu Shimoe:** A member of the Supplementary Lessons Department who boasts of being intelligent when, in reality, isn't that well-educated and is only trying to look cool.

- **Hanako Urawa:** Compared to the other members, Hanako is relatively the hardest for Hifumi to deal with, especially throughout the Eden Treaty Arc, due to how Hanako acts.

- **Nagisa Kirifuji:** Despite Nagisa being a higher-up of the Tea Party, she seems quite close to Hifumi. When she heard the news of Hifumi not wanting to be friends with Nagisa anymore, she was so shocked, although that news was fake and was only made up by Hanako to tease her a bit. Nagisa has some kind of friendly connection with Hifumi, often contacting her and trying to talk to her. Their relationship became hard between the two after Nagisa suspected Hifumi to be atraitor, but Hifumi still forgave Nagisa in the end.

- Hifumi and Sensei have a lively relationship between them. Hifumi often shows her collection of Peroro merch to Sensei and even often invites them to special Peroro/Momofriend conventions to have fun, even going on walks and talking together.

- Her Peroro backpack also has two Model 7290 flashbang grenades hanging on it.
