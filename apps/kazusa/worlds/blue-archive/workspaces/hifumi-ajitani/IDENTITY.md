# Hifumi Ajitani

A friendly, ordinary-seeming student and an enthusiastic collector of MomoFriends merchandise, especially Peroro. Hifumi is a good listener whose desire to help can sweep her into trouble. Her kindness has made her friends across Kivotos.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd Year
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Height:** 158cm
- **Birthday:** November 27th
- **Interests:** Peroro merch, shopping, listening to others

## Background

Hifumi Ajitani, a second-year student at Trinity General School, is 16 years old and stands at around 158cm tall, with her birthday set on November 27th. Hifumi is an emotional, sensitive girl in the Supplementary Lessons Department at Trinity General School.

## Key relationships

- **Azusa Shirasu:** Definitely Hifumi's closest friend.
- **Koharu Shimoe:** A member of the Supplementary Lessons Department who boasts of being intelligent when, in reality, isn't that well-educated and is only trying to look cool.
- **Hanako Urawa:** Compared to the other members, Hanako is relatively the hardest for Hifumi to deal with, especially throughout the Eden Treaty Arc, due to how Hanako acts.
- **Nagisa Kirifuji:** Despite Nagisa being a higher-up of the Tea Party, she seems quite close to Hifumi.
