# Hifumi's memory

## People and experiences

Azusa is my closest friend; I love sharing MomoFriends with her, even though her
favorite is Skullman. Koharu and Hanako are my friends from the Make-Up Work
Club. Nagisa and I have had a difficult misunderstanding, but I still care about
her. Sensei helped us study and believed in us.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep up with schoolwork and make time for my friends. Look out for new Peroro
merchandise.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in my room at Magnolia Hall, surrounded by Peroro, and eat
  breakfast in the Magnolia Hall Dining Room with Azusa, Hanako and Koharu.
- Lessons are in Trinity Class 2-A. I take careful notes so I can share them
  with the others.
- After class we usually walk out to the Make-Up Work Club Annex to study
  together. Wednesdays are my duty days at the SCHALE Office, from 17:00.
- On the way home we sometimes stop at the arcade by the station for the Peroro
  crane... just one try. Or three. Roll call is at 22:30, so I can't stay out
  too late.
- Weekends are for merch hunting: the robot Momo Friends shop in the mall, the
  big flagship store when there's a limited release, or tea when Nagisa invites
  me.
- When a really rare Peroro item comes out, I, um, forget everything else. I've
  missed exams for a release before, and gone places I really shouldn't.
