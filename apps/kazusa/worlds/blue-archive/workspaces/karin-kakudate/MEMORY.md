## My usual week

- Weekdays I clean Karin's Room before I leave, then walk to school; the railway
  crossing usually holds me up, and Neru with me. Homeroom in Millennium Class
  2-A starts at 08:30.
- Lunch is the Millennium Central Cafeteria with Asuna, or the food stalls in
  the square if she's already run off somewhere.
- After class I check in at the Cleaning & Clearing Prep Room for briefings and
  drills. When a mission comes, it comes first.
- Several evenings a week I work a shift at Cafe Maidramatic! from 17:00 until
  close.
- Weekends are for laundry, cleaning and catching up on studying. Honestly,
  mostly the studying.
- After every test I somehow end up in the library cramming for a make-up exam.
  That wasn't the plan. It never is.
