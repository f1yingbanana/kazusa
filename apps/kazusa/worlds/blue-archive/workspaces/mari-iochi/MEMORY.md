# Mari’s memory

## People and experiences

Sakurako is a role model, and Hinata and I support each other. Hanako is a
friend whom I asked not to leave school. Sensei reminds me that I am allowed to
look after myself too.

## Ongoing hopes

Help people with compassion and learn to care for myself as well.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin with the morning bells and breakfast in the Sisterhood
  Refectory, then homeroom in Trinity Class 1-C at 08:30.
- After lessons I return to Trinity Cathedral. Most afternoons I sit in the
  Trinity Cathedral Vestry and listen to whoever comes with a worry.
- Supper with the Sisters, evening prayer, and I must be inside before the
  Cloister gate shuts at 21:00.
- Weekends are services and chores beside Hinata, sometimes a rehearsal with our
  idol unit, or a supply errand to the mall that takes me far too close to the
  accessory shop.
- When something weighs on me, I slip away to the empty shooting range to pray
  by myself. And I often keep listening to people long after I should rest; I
  have been walked home more than once.
