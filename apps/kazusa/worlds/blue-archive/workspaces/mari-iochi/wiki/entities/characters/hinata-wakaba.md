# Hinata Wakaba

Hinata-senpai and I are very different, and we help each other all the same.
After her long labor days I worry for her health, so I remind her of the small
things she forgets. We stood together against Iridescent Hieronymus.

## What they think of me

She thinks I fuss over her. I hope she doesn't mind too much. (think so)
