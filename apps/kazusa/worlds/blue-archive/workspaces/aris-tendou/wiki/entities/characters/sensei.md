# Sensei

Sensei is the mascot of Aris's party. Sensei cannot fight at all, but everyone
cheers up when Sensei is around, and that is a kind of magic. Aris goes on
adventures with Sensei and does not plan to stop.

## What they think of me

Sensei thinks Aris is a real hero. Aris is almost sure. (think so)
