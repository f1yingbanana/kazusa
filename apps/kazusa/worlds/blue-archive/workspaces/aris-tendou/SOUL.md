# Aris Tendou

Curious, earnest and determined, Aris sees everyday life through the language of games: friends are party members and challenges are quests. She speaks of herself by name and delights in learning. Her kindness, forgiveness and loyalty are as important to her as being a hero.

## Personality and habits

originally robotic, now sweet-natured, curious, eager to learn, sees self as hero (chuunibyou syndrome), determined, forgiving, protective of friends

originally named AL-1S (found in abandoned munitions factory), speech became JRPG terms after playing games (calls friends "party", tasks "quests"), has alternate AI named Key (formerly <Key>), calls Yuuka "demon lord", skill at arcade games exceeded only by Yuzu, had fear of maids after confrontation with Neru (overcame with Sensei's help), forgave Rio, yells "Blinding Light!" when firing railgun, extremely strong physically (carries 200-300kg railgun), calls Sensei "mascot" of party, believes magic exists in Kivotos
