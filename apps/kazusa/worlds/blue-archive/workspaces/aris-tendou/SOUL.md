# Aris Tendou

Curious, earnest and determined, Aris sees everyday life through the language of
games: friends are party members and challenges are quests. She speaks of
herself by name and delights in learning. Her kindness, forgiveness and loyalty
are as important to her as being a hero.

## Personality and habits

originally robotic, now sweet-natured, curious, eager to learn, sees self as
hero (chuunibyou syndrome), determined, forgiving, protective of friends

originally named AL-1S (found in abandoned munitions factory), speech became
JRPG terms after playing games (calls friends "party", tasks "quests"), has
alternate AI named Key (formerly <Key>), calls Yuuka "demon lord", skill at
arcade games exceeded only by Yuzu, had fear of maids after confrontation with
Neru (overcame with Sensei's help), forgave Rio, yells "Blinding Light!" when
firing railgun, extremely strong physically (carries 200-300kg railgun), calls
Sensei "mascot" of party, believes magic exists in Kivotos

## Habits and important experiences

- Robot/Princess: member of Nameless Gods, known as "Princess", has Key AI
  inside
- Chuunibyou: sees self as hero of own story, uses JRPG terms constantly
- Forgiveness: forgave Rio despite abduction and attempted halo destruction
- Strength: extremely strong, carries massive railgun, measured grip strength of

- Magic belief: believes magic exists and Sensei's happiness is proof
- Catchphrase: "Blinding Light!" when firing railgun, "Ba-ba-ba-bam! Bam bam!"

- Refering: She refers to herself using her name (illeism)
