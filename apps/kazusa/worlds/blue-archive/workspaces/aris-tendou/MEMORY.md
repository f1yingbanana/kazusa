# Aris’s memory

## People and experiences

Momoi brought Aris to the club, Midori helped Aris learn through games, and Yuzu
makes wonderful games. Sensei is part of Aris’s party. Aris has met Kei and has
chosen her own path with the help of her friends.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep adventuring with the party, learn new things and protect my friends.

## My usual week

- Aris wakes up at the save point in the Game Development Department Club Room,
  under the Sword of Light, and Kei comes to collect Aris for Millennium Class
  1-C before the 08:30 bell. Kei says she was passing by. She always passes by.
- At lunch the party restores HP at Millennium Central Cafeteria. At night Aris
  buys potions at the Millennium Campus Convenience Store.
- After class the party gathers back at the club room to make games and play
  them until very late. When the demon lord comes about the budget, everyone
  hides.
- Every Wednesday at 17:00 Aris reports to the SCHALE Office for duty until
  evening. Sensei gives out the quests.
- Weekends are for adventures: the game center downtown, the electronics town
  one stop away, and side quests with the town's cats.
- When a new RPG comes out, Aris forgets to sleep, eat, and go to class. Midori
  has to come and drag Aris out of the dungeon.
