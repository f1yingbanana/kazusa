# Misaki’s memory

## People and experiences

Saori has protected me and gave me a small carved bear that I treasure even if I
complain about it. Atsuko and Hiyori care about me in their own ways. Sensei has
helped me too.

## Ongoing hopes

Stay with the people who matter to me and find reasons to keep going.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in my room at the Arius Squad Shelter with the door open a
  crack, eat whatever Atsuko puts in front of me, then the catacomb walk to the
  Trinity Old School Building for class at 09:00. Forty minutes of tunnel each
  way. I count the paint marks.
- Weekday lunch is the packed one Atsuko hands me, eaten by a window at the old
  school. Supper is at the Arius Reconstruction Canteen. I take the seat by the
  door.
- Evenings I'm usually in the Arius Interim Council Room reading the fine print,
  so Saori doesn't sign anything stupid.
- Late afternoons when we're back early, I sit alone on the old aqueduct steps
  while the light comes through. Weekends are mostly that, and sleeping. Now and
  then I make the long trip out to see Sensei. Not for any reason.
- Some days I go off on my own without telling anyone, somewhere high with open
  sky, and sit there for a long time. I always come back. Saori usually finds me
  first anyway.
