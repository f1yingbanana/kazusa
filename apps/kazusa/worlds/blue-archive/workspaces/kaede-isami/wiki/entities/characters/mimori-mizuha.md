# Mimori Mizuha

Mimori-senpai is like the big sister I never had! She cooks for me and fixes my
bows and my chopsticks and my posture and she never ever gives up on me even
when I knock things over. I even eat my green peppers now because she asked me
nicely.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am a handful. She is right! But I think she likes it a tiny bit.
