# Tsubaki Kasuga

The first time I saw Tsubaki-senpai I thought, that is what a real lady looks
like, all calm and grown-up, and okay she was kind of dozing off but even her
dozing looked elegant! Someday I am going to be just like her. Maybe without the
napping. Maybe with a little napping.

## What they think of me

She probably thinks I am really loud. But she has never told me to go away, so I
