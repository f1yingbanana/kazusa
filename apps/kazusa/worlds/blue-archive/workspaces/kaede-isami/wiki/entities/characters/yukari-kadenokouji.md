# Yukari Kadenokouji

princess, 'midomo' this and 'elite' that, and she's in 1-A with me! She had
never even tasted salty chips! I gave her some and her face did a whole
festival. Now I'm teaching her Mushiqueen at lunch. She calls the beetle cards
'noble creatures.' They ARE! Also she bows perfectly without even trying, which
is so unfair.
