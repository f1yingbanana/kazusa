# Kaede’s memory

## People and experiences

I admire Tsubaki and Mimori; Mimori helps me practice and treats me kindly.
Sensei supports me when I doubt myself and enjoys my discoveries.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep practicing at my own pace and have fun with my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in my room at Tsukimi-sou Dormitory, eat breakfast with
  Tsubaki and Mimori, and walk to Hyakkiyako Class 1-A trying to walk like a
  lady the whole way, homeroom is 08:30.
- Lunch is at Student Dining Hall No. 7, and some afternoons Mimori borrows a
  stove there and teaches me to cook.
- After class I go to Old Schoolhouse No. 18 for etiquette practice and
  cleaning, and I am not allowed to touch Tsubaki's cushion.
- Fridays are my duty at the SCHALE Office from 17:00, I take the train from
  school and try very hard not to break the coffee machine.
- I get back before the landlady counts us at curfew. Weekends are hero shows,
  the card counter on release days, candy, and the grove by the duck pond.
- But if someone says there is a giant beetle somewhere or a new Mushiqueen pack
  is out, I forget everything and run, even before school, even in my nice
  sandals.
