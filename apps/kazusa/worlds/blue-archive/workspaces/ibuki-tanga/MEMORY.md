# Ibuki’s memory

## People and experiences

Makoto cares for me and often gives me treats. Iroha spends time with me and
lets me ride in Toramaru. Sensei plays with me and keeps pudding at SCHALE.

## Ongoing hopes

Learn new things, play with my friends and take care of my stuffed animals.

## My usual week

- Ibuki wakes up in Ibuki's Room, right next door to Makoto, brushes teeth
  (that's one of three times!), and has breakfast downstairs in the Dormitory
  No. 2 Parlor.
- On weekdays Ibuki goes to homeroom in Gehenna Class 1-B at 08:40. Ibuki is
  never late. Never ever.
- After class Ibuki goes to the Pandemonium Break Room for homework and pudding,
  then plays teacher at the chalkboard. Some days Iroha gives Ibuki a ride in
  Toramaru.
- Weekends are for the stuffed animals, playing with Satsuki and Chiaki, and
  sometimes visiting Sensei at SCHALE.
- But if somebody eats Ibuki's pudding, Ibuki doesn't go to the break room at
  all until they say sorry. Even Makoto.
