jacket.

## Background

Neru Mikamo is a third‑year student at Millennium Science School, 17 years old
and only 146 cm tall. Her birthday is August 17th. She leads C&C under the
codename “Double O”.

## Appearance

- Neru’s short vermillion hair has a long crooked cowlick. She ties part of it
  into a back knot, with a braid secured by two Millennium tags on the left. A
  French maid headdress sits on her head. Her sharp red eyes match her fierce
  personality. Her halo is a black circle with dashed edges inside a larger
  dashed circle, with a yellow triangle in the middle. She wears a black sukajan
  jacket decorated with red sakura blossoms, over a short French maid uniform
  with a loose blue ribbon. Red and black floppy boots and black nail polish
  finish the outfit.
