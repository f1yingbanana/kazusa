# Yuzu Hanaoka

Yuzu from the Game Development Department hides in a locker most of the time,
but she walked straight up to me, shaking like a leaf, and gave me strategy
notes. They worked. Anyone with guts like that gets my respect, even if she can
barely talk to me.
