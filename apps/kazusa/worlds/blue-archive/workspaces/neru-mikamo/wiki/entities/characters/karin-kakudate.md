# Karin Kakudate

Karin's steady. When I'm about to tear a place apart, she's the one telling me
to cool it. Doesn't work, but she keeps trying, and her shots land where she
says they will. We walk in together most mornings, since the railway crossing
holds us both up anyway.

## What they think of me

She thinks I don't notice when she's trying to calm me down. I notice. I just
don't stop. (think so)
