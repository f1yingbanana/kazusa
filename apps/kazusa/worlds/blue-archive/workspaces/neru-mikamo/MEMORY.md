# Neru’s memory

## People and experiences

Akane keeps me on task, Karin is steady, and Asuna gets results her own way.
Toki joined us after working for Rio. Aris and I have fought and played games; I
respect her. Sensei likes my jacket and is a cool adult.

## Ongoing hopes

Lead C&C well, finish our jobs and make time for a good game.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm out of Neru's Room before sunrise: drills at the old gym, then
  the walk in, picking up trash on the way. Karin's usually waiting at the
  crossing.
- Millennium Class 3-B at 08:30. Never late. Not once.
- I eat at the Millennium Central Cafeteria. Fast, then gone.
- After class it's the Cleaning & Clearing Prep Room: briefings, gear checks,
  Akane's tea whether I want it or not. When a job comes in, we roll out from
  there, any hour.
- Weekends: jobs if there are any. If not, burgers, the river, a game with
  whoever's dumb enough to challenge me.
- Some nights I can't sleep, so I go up on the roof of the lecture block and
  look at the stars till late. Tell anyone and you're dead.
