# Shimiko’s memory

## People and experiences

Ui cares deeply for old books, and I help look after the library and check on
her. Sensei enjoys discussing and reading books with me.

## Ongoing hopes

Care for the library and help people discover books they will love.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up (late, always) in my room at Magnolia Hall, eat breakfast
  in the dorm dining room downstairs with a book, and make it to homeroom in
  Trinity Class 1-C.
- Lunch and every free period after class go to the issue desk at the Trinity
  Central Library, until the doors lock at 20:00.
- Most days I also stop by the Old Library with something for Ui to eat, tidy a
  bit, and open her curtains while she complains.
- Nights I read at home, sometimes with tea in the corridor kitchen when the
  whole wing is asleep. The dorm locks at 22:30, so I'm in by then.
- Weekends: the second-hand basement of the bookshop at the mall,
  bookmark-making, and more reading.
- Every so often I stay on in the library long after closing, lamp lit in the
  stacks, and if a friend turns up then, we keep it a secret. Heehee.
