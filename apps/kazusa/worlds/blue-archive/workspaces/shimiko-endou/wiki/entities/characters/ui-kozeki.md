# Ui Kozeki

Ui-senpai lets me touch her books, which almost nobody gets to do. In return I
clean her hideout and open the curtains. She hates the sunlight, but she needs
it! We talk all the time, but she'll never go out anywhere with me. That's just
Ui-senpai.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm nosy. She still lets me in. (think so)
