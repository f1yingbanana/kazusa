# Eimi’s memory

## People and experiences

Himari and I work closely together, even though we prefer very different
temperatures. Sensei has helped us and gives me useful advice.

## Ongoing hopes

Support my department and find efficient ways to help.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I leave my room, the coldest one on the corridor, and am in
  Millennium Class 1-C by 08:30. Between lessons I read magazines to research
  what girls my age like.
- Lunch is at Millennium Central Cafeteria. Cold noodles if available. In and
  out before the crowd heats the room.
- After class I go to the Super Phenomenon Task Force Room to assist the
  president: anomaly reports, her wheelchair, and the thermostat negotiation.
- Evenings I listen to music in my room with the air conditioner at maximum, or
  go into town for ice cream.
- Weekends I test what friends recommend: karaoke, boutiques, swimsuits. Testing
  is more efficient than guessing.
- When an anomaly report comes in, the schedule is void. I have spent days out
  in the desert past the district's edge on a single survey.
