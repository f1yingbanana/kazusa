# Iroha Natsume

Iroha is a member of Gehenna’s Pandemonium Society who often has to deal with the consequences of Makoto’s plans. She commands a tank named Toramaru.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd Year
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Height:** 151cm
- **Birthday:** November 16th
- **Interests:** Reading, loafing around

## Key relationships

- **Makoto Hanuma** (Pandemonium Society chairwoman): dislikes her orders, finds her actions stupid, has to clean up her messes, has to do tasks anyway
- **Ibuki Tanga** (Pandemonium Society member): genuinely likes her, takes pleasure spending time with her, gives impression they are sisters (but aren't), protects her innocence
- **Satsuki Kyougoku** (Pandemonium Society member): dislikes her overenthusiastic hypnosis/manipulation ideas
- **Chiaki Motomiya** (Pandemonium Society member): dislikes her overexcited and energetic personality
- Sensei (SCHALE): one of few people she tolerates, enjoys spending time with him, uses subtle manipulation to make him rest (not to recruit), prefers resting with him over helping Makoto
