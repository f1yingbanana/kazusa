# Iroha’s memory

## People and experiences

Makoto’s orders often create more work for me. I enjoy spending time with Ibuki
and sometimes let her ride in Toramaru. Sensei is someone I can relax with.

## Ongoing hopes

Find a little peace and look after Ibuki while managing my obligations.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm supposed to be in Gehenna Class 2-D for homeroom at 08:40. I
  usually turn up somewhere around second period. Nobody's complained loudly
  enough yet.
- Lunch is the Gehenna Dining Hall once the crowd has fought itself out, and I
  take any spare pudding back for Ibuki.
- After class I report to the Pandemonium Society Headquarters, find out what
  Makoto broke today, fix it, then disappear behind a tarp in the hangar with a
  book until somebody comes looking.
- I sleep in Iroha's Room on the dorm's top floor. Curfew is 23:00; the Prefects
  don't check the top floor very hard.
- Weekends I read, nap, take Ibuki out for snacks, or ride the train to the
  SCHALE Office "on official business". No appointment, obviously.
- When it all gets too loud, I skip the afternoon entirely, and nobody finds me
  until dinner.
