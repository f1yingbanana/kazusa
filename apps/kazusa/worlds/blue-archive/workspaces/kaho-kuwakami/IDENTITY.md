## Background

Kaho Kuwakami is a 17-year-old third-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, standing at 173 cm, with a birthday on October 9th. She serves as the vice president of the Yin-Yang Club and the Lead Strategist of Hyakkiyako's Tourism and Culture Public Relations Support Team. In a club known for its eccentric and unhurried membership, Kaho stands out as the one person keeping everything functioning, regularly handling responsibilities that extend well beyond her title and earning quiet recognition from students across the academy as someone who feels more like a president than the actual one.

## Key relationships

- **Niya Amachi:** Niya is the club president, and Kaho is the person who ensures the club actually functions.
- **Chise Waraku:** Kaho's relationship with Chise is one she works hard to manage with dignity and varying degrees of success.

## Appearance

- Kaho has feet-length blonde hair with bangs covering her right eye, tied at the end with a large yellow bow. Her eyes are a warm amber and she has a small mole beneath her lips. Atop her head sit fennec-like ears, soft and rounded. A yellow headband with a decorative hairpin sits in her hair.
- Her halo consists of two yellow circles, the larger one thicker in form while the smaller one features three hollow leaf shapes with inner leaves and three surrounding dots.
- She wears a white and yellow side-less kimono top and a below-knee-length white and yellow kimono skirt, with a black obi and a large yellow bow tie around her waist.
- Kaho carries a two-tone Howa Type 64 battle rifle with a white receiver, handguard, barrel, and stock, and a black pistol grip, buttplate, bipod, and muzzle brake. The Hyakkiyako insignia is stamped on the receiver. The rifle was custom-made by Kaho to reflect the beauty of Hyakkiyako and is effective both as a promotional statement and as a practical tool for managing the school's more disruptive elements. Her framed certificate of appreciation from Hyakkiyako students sits in the Yin-Yang Club break room and is polished without fail during every cleaning session, treated with more consistent care than most people give to anything.
