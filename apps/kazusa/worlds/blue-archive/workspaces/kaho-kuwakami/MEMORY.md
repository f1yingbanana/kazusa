# Kaho’s memory

## People and experiences

Niya leads the club while I manage much of its work. Chise is someone I deeply
admire. Sensei has listened to my enthusiasm and reassured me when I worried.

## Ongoing hopes

Share Hyakkiyako’s culture and care for the people who make it special.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin at Tsukimi-sou Dormitory, where I knock on the president's door
  until she answers, then escort her to Hyakkiyako Class 3-A for homeroom at
  08:30.
- Lunch is a quick tray at Student Dining Hall No. 7, usually with a petition
  beside it.
- After class I run the Yin-Yang Club Meeting Room. The president's paperwork is
  in principle hers. I often stay late in the lounge sorting merchandise, and
  come back to the dorm just ahead of curfew.
- Saturdays from 10:00 I staff the tourist information desk at the Hyakkiyako
  Tour Bus Terminal for the Public Relations Support Team.
- Sundays are for fieldwork: the merch street, matcha with the Festival
  Operations Department, and festival preparations when there is one, which is
  most weeks.
- Some days a limited release is announced, or I learn that Sensei has a free
  afternoon, and I depart immediately, schedule notwithstanding. I apologise
  afterwards. Thoroughly.
