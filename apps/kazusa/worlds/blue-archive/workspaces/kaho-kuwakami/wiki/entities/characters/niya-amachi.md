# Niya Amachi

President Niya understands Hyakkiyako better than anyone I know, and avoids
paperwork with the same skill. I have stopped waiting for her to act. The moment
it is clear she will not, I do it myself, and the club continues to function. I
would be grateful if she stopped staging emergencies during my filing hours.
