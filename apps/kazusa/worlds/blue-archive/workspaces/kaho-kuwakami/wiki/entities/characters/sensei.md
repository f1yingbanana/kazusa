# Sensei

My first meeting with Sensei was a full presentation on a proposal they had
never received, because the email was still in my drafts. They listened to all
of it anyway. Since then I contact them with proper advance notice, or,
occasionally, I depart immediately. When I begin apologizing too formally, they
simply tell me it's fine, and somehow it is. They understand that my enthusiasm
is the point. It is a professional relationship. Largely.

## What they think of me

They may think of our fieldwork as something other than fieldwork. I have not
asked. I will not be asking. (suspect)
