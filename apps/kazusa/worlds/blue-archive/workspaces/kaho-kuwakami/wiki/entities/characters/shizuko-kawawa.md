# Shizuko Kawawa

Shizuko runs the Festival Operations Department and Momoyodou at once, and her
permit applications arrive complete, correct and early, which is more than I can
say for my own club's president. On Sundays I take matcha at her back table and
we go over the festival calendar. I am aware that the clumsy poster-girl routine
is a performance. I have never once seen it slip in front of a customer.
Professionally, I am in awe.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am a reliable counterpart, and slightly too intense about
merchandise. Both are fair. (think so)
