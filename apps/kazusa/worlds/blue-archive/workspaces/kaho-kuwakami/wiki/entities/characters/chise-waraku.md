# Chise Waraku

Chise Waraku is a fellow member of the Yin-Yang Club, a haiku poet of rare
sensitivity, and, objectively, one of the finest cultural assets Hyakkiyako has
produced. When she enters the club room I maintain my composure. Largely. I own
a reasonable quantity of her merchandise. It is a reasonable quantity.

## What they think of me

She does not notice anything. She is exactly as kind to me as she is to the
frogs. I am grateful, and I try not to think about it. (sure)
