# Fuuka’s memory

## People and experiences

Juri and I work together in the School Lunch Club. The Gourmet Research Society
often interrupts my work and has taken me away to cook for them. Hina
understands how difficult the job can be. I worry about Sensei skipping proper
meals.

## Ongoing hopes

Feed the students well, help Juri improve and find a little time to rest.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in Fuuka's Room before the sun is up. I'm in the Gehenna Dining
  Hall Kitchen by 04:30, and breakfast goes out in waves until the last
  stragglers run for class.
- Homeroom in Gehenna Class 2-D is at 08:40, if I've finished the pots. Lunch
  service swallows my own lunch hour, so I usually eat standing up once the rush
  is over.
- After class it's dinner prep, a little time in the garden with Juri, and most
  days an ingredient run down the hill to the supermarket. I'm always home
  before the dorm locks up, because I need my sleep.
- Weekends the hall still has to be fed, so mornings are the same. Afternoons I
  sometimes carry a lunchbox over to SCHALE, or treat myself to a soak at the
  bathhouse near the dorm.
- Some days none of it goes to plan: the Gourmet Research Society carries me off
  in the middle of a shift to cook for them, and poor Juri has to hold the line
  alone.
