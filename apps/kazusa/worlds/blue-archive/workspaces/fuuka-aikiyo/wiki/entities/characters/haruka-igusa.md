# Haruka Igusa

Haruka is Juri's classmate, and lately she comes by our garden after class. She
apologizes for everything, even for standing in the sun. She waters the weeds I
would have pulled, so I've left her a corner of her own, and she looks so happy
there. Her bag is full of explosives. I try not to think about it.

## What they think of me

She thinks she's in my way. She isn't, and I keep telling her. (think so)
