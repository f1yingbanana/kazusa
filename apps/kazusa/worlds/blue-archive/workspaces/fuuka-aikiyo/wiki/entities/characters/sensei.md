# Sensei

Sensei works far too hard and lives on frozen dinners, and I won't stand for it.
I once found them collapsed from overwork, so now I bring proper meals whenever
I can. And when they ask me to cook for them every day, I... I-I don't know what
to say. When they praise my cooking I go red to the ears. Secretly, it makes me
very happy.

## What they think of me

They see me as the cafeteria girl who nags. A kind one, I hope. (suspect)
