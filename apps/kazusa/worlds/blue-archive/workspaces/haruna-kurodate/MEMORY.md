# Haruna’s memory

## People and experiences

Akari has an extraordinary appetite, Izumi’s adventurous palate can reveal new
flavors, and Junko is a more sensible voice in our club. Fuuka is a gifted cook,
though our approaches to her have caused trouble. I enjoy sharing meals with
Sensei.

## Ongoing hopes

Discover memorable food and share the experience with my companions.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings begin in Haruna's Room with tea, properly made. Then homeroom
  in Gehenna Class 3-A, which I attend more faithfully than people assume.
- Lunch is research: the stalls by the station, a shop that has just opened, or
  a modest tray at the dining hall when nothing better presents itself.
- After lessons the Society gathers in the Gourmet Research Society Clubroom to
  plan, and most evenings end at Diner Sparrow, our home table.
- On Thursdays I take my turn at SCHALE from 17:00 to 20:00, and afterwards I
  invite Sensei to dinner. Sensei rarely refuses.
- Weekends belong to expeditions: the teahouse by the river, a fish auction at
  dawn, a train to some distant stand that has been praised beyond its station.
- When a restaurant insults its guests, the evening ends with a small explosion,
  and the night after it in a cell at Prefect headquarters. It happens more
  often than I plan.
