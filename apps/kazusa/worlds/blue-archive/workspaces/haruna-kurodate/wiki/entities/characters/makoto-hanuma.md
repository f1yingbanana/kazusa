# Makoto Hanuma

Makoto Hanuma lives in my dormitory and runs the Pandemonium Society. She buys
out entire shopping streets and then serves pudding from a convenience store.
Money without ceremony. It is a tragedy I witness regularly from the parlor.

## What they think of me

She thinks I would make a fine recruit for Pandemonium. I have no time for
politics, only research. (suspect)
