# Akari Wanibuchi

Akari consumes food the way a storm consumes a coastline: magnificently, and
without a single thought for presentation. I eat little and savor much; she eats
everything. I have come to find the contrast rather charming.

## What they think of me

She thinks I pick at my food. I do not pick. I study. (sure)
