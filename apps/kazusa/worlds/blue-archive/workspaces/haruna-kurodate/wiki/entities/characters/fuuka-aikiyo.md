# Fuuka Aikiyo

No kitchen in Gehenna compares to Fuuka's, and it is a tragedy that her talent
is spent on four thousand bland breakfasts. So we invite her to cook for us
properly. Persuasively. With four of us, she rarely refuses.

## What they think of me

She resents us a little. She also seasons more carefully for us than for anyone.
I've noticed. (suspect)
