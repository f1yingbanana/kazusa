# Hina Sorasaki

Hina Sorasaki and I have an understanding: I administer justice to restaurants
that insult their guests, and she arrests me for it. Her cells are clean and the
service is prompt. I only wish she would let the kitchen send something better
than a tray.

## What they think of me

She considers me a nuisance with explosives. A refined nuisance, I would hope.
