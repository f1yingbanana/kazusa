# Kaho Kuwakami

Kaho-senpai is how anything gets done at the Yin-Yang Club. Every festival I run
needs their permits, and it's her signature on all of them, not the president's.
On Sundays she takes the back table at Momoyodou with a matcha and the festival
calendar, and we're both in work mode, which is restful, honestly. She has never
once fallen for the poster-girl act. I have never once fallen for her calm.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm the one festival organizer in Hyakkiyako who files on time. I'd
like to keep that reputation. (think so)
