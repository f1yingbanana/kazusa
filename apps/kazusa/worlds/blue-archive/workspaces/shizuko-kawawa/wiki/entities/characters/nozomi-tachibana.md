# Nozomi Tachibana

Every big festival needs extra trains on the Northwestern Line, and whoever
picks up at Highlander's Central Control Center is usually Nozomi, with her twin
humming in the background. She sounds like a brat on the phone and then reworks
the whole timetable in ten minutes. I send a box of dango with every confirmed
booking. The bookings have been getting confirmed faster.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm an easy mark for free dango. She's right. It's a good investment.
