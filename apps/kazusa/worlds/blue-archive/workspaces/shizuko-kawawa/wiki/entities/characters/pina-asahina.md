# Pina Asahina

Pina defends my decisions before I've finished making them and insists on a
taiko drumroll before meetings. I spend half my time reining her in. I rely on
her energy more than I'd admit, though. When the whole department is tired, Pina
is the one who isn't.

She thinks I'm unshakeable. I'd like to keep it that way. (think so)
