# Shizuko’s memory

## People and experiences

Pina brings energy and loyalty; Umika contributes research and ideas. Sensei has
helped me with festivals and my work at Momoyodou.

## Ongoing hopes

Create joyful festivals and look after my customers and team.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I'm up early above Momoyodou making the day's dango with the staff,
  then I run to Hyakkiyako Class 2-A for homeroom at 08:30.
- Lunch is a quick bowl in Student Dining Hall No. 7 between supplier calls, and
  after class I'm behind the counter until we close at 19:00. Then the
  department takes over the back table to plan the next festival.
- Wednesdays the shop is closed, which means contracts, permits and errands all
  over town, and sometimes the train to Sensei in D.U.
- Weekends are the busy days: queues out the door and my best smile on all day.
  Now and then a Sunday starts on the first train north to the farmers' market,
  and most nights end with a long soak at the bathhouse.
- I'm supposed to stop once the ledgers balance. I usually don't. More than one
  morning I've woken up face-down on the accounts with the lamp still on.
