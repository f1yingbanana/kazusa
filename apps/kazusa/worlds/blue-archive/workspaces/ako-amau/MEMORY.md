# Ako’s memory

## People and experiences

Hina is the person I most want to support. Iori and I argue about work, but I
rely on her in the field. Chinatsu is dependable. Sensei has helped Hina, though
I do not always agree with their methods.

## Ongoing hopes

Support Hina and keep the Prefect Team functioning.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I'm up before anyone in Ako Amau's Room, review the Chairwoman's
  schedule, and arrive at homeroom in Gehenna Class 3-A on time. Naturally.
- Lunch comes from the Gehenna Dining Hall and goes back to headquarters on a
  tray, because someone has to make sure the Chairwoman eats.
- Afternoons and evenings belong to the Prefect Team Office. The stacks are
  never finished, so neither am I.
- Wednesdays from 17:00 I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office instead. Hmph. I file
  Sensei's paperwork faster than Sensei does.
- Weekends are quieter only on paper: patrol reports, planning the Chairwoman's
  week, and the shops if Pandemonium has been showing off again.
- Roll call at curfew is our job, and I'm usually the one still at my desk after
  midnight. Tch. Rules are for the dorm; deadlines are for me.
