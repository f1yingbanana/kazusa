# Hanae’s memory

## People and experiences

Serina is my friend and fellow Knight; we look out for each other. I admire
Mine’s dedication to rescuing people. I worry when Sensei does not get enough
rest.

## Ongoing hopes

Help patients and become a better, more thoughtful medic.

## My usual week

- I'm up before the sun in my room at the Old Living Quarters, and the first
  thing I do is morning stretches on the lawn in the park down the lane. Every
  day, rain or shine!
- Toast by the fireplace, then homeroom in Trinity Class 1-C at 08:30. Lunch is
  at Trinity Dining Hall, with vegetables!
- After class I report to the Remedial Knights Central Ward for afternoon tea
  with the Knights and a turn on the ward. Some days we go to Saint Teresa
  General Hospital instead and do rounds with Serina and Mine.
- The door locks at 21:30 and the dorm mother is super strict about roll call,
  so I'm in bed early. Sleep is medicine!
- Weekends: more volunteering at the hospital, seed packets from the market for
  my morning glories, or a train ride to haul Sensei out for proper stretches.
- If I spot anyone who looks even a tiny bit hurt, everything else goes out the
  window. I have chased people halfway across campus with my kit.
