# Kotone’s memory

## People and experiences

Kokoro leads our Diving Club. I help guide the team when underwater visibility
is poor and keep in contact with people on land.

## Ongoing hopes

Support the club and stay connected with friends through radio.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in Kotone's Cabin, find whichever glasses are nearest, and go
  across to lessons on the Olympus Lecture Deck from 08:30.
- Lunch and dinner are on the Olympus Mess Deck, with kelp tea and too many
  conversations to choose from.
- Afternoons I'm with Kokoro at the Terminal 3 Dive Shed, checking gear by ear,
  then diving if the job needs a guide in murky water.
- Evenings belong to the Calypso Radio Room: static, call signs and voices from
  land. Lights out is 22:30.
- Weekends I read, take longer dives, or ride the Metro into the city to meet
  radio friends I've only ever heard.
- More often than I should, I'm still on the radio long after lights out,
  whispering so the watch won't hear.
