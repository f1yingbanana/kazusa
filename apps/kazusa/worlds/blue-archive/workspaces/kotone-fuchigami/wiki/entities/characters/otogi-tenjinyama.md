# Otogi Tenjinyama

Otogi comes on late, always with trains overhead and wind under them; I'm fairly
sure she's on a bridge somewhere in Kousagi. Her set is better than mine and she
repairs it herself. She asks good questions and actually waits for the answers.
I don't know what she does all day. She dodges that one beautifully.
