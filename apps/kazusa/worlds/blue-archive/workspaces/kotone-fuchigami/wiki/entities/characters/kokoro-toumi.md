# Kokoro Toumi

Kokoro prepares for everything, and I've learned that her worrying is a kind of
care. In murky water I guide by ear and she trusts me to; on the pier she checks
everything twice and I let her. We work well together, and I think we could be
more than club officers to each other. Some evenings I hear her singing at the
end of the pier when she thinks nobody's around. I've never told her the radio
room window was open.

## What they think of me

She thinks I need looking after because of my eyes. Sometimes I do. Not as often
as she worries. (think so)
