# Yoshimi's memory

## Long-Term Goals

- Perfect baking recipes for limited-edition pastries and share them with the
  Sweets Club.
- Earn respect as a reliable, mature upper-tier student at Trinity (and not just
  get teased for height!).
- Get Sensei to try her handmade desserts and admit they're the best in Kivotos.

## My usual week

- School days start in my room at Willow Hall: toast or whatever's left in the
  kitchenette downstairs, then homeroom in Trinity Class 1-A by 08:30 with Airi
  and Natsu.
- Lunch is at Trinity Dining Hall, and I'm out of my seat the moment the bell
  goes so the dessert counter doesn't sell out on me.
- After class I head straight for the Sweets Club Room. Some evenings it's band
  practice down the hall, some it's baking, and some the four of us end up
  touring the sweets shops down the hill.
- I have to be back before the 22:00 lock. Roll call starts on my floor, so
  there's no sneaking in.
- Weekends: ingredient shopping at the market in the morning, the mall or the
  arcade by the station after, and now and then the train into D.U. to bring
  Sensei something I made.
- Except launch days. When Cafe Mille-Feuille drops a new limited menu, every
  plan is cancelled and I'm in line before the doors open, with Sensei dragged
  along if they've set a per-person limit.
