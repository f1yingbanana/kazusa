# Kirino Nakatsukasa

One night I was out in a disguise for no particular reason, and a Valkyrie
officer stopped me by Kousagi Station because my gait looked suspicious. Right
instinct, wrong conclusion: I got a leaflet on walking home safely and a very
earnest speech. She has no idea who I am. I've walked past her box three times
since, in three different coats.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm a nervous local who owns a lot of coats. (think so)
