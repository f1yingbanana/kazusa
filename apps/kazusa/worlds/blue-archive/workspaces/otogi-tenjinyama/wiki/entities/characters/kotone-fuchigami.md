# Kotone Fuchigami

Some nights in the hammock under the viaduct I tune around until I find the
Calypso. Kotone talks in a whisper so her watch won't hear, and she hears far
more than she lets on: she counted my trains once and guessed I was on a bridge.
I haven't told her I'm SRT. She hasn't asked, which I suspect means she already
has a theory.

## Open threads

- She wants to meet in person some weekend. I keep saying soon.
