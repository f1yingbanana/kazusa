# Otogi’s memory

## People and experiences

Yukino leads our squad, Niko helps keep us together, and Kurumi and I enjoy
arguing. RABBIT Platoon impressed me, especially Miyu’s ability to go unnoticed.
Sensei has earned my interest.

## Ongoing hopes

Support my squad and get to know the people we meet.

## My usual week

- Officially, I sleep in Otogi's Room. Unofficially, plenty of nights it's the
  Pier 7 Inspection Walkway: hammock, radio, trains overhead.
- Weekdays I'm at FOX Squad HQ for briefings and whatever odd jobs SCHALE hands
  us.
- Meals are at the SCHALE Cafeteria, plus a bite or two off Kurumi's tray.
- Free afternoons I wander: new cafés, the electronics stalls, a park bench at
  dusk, talking to whoever looks like they have a story. People give me things.
  I let them.
- Weekends are puzzles, radio repairs and, now and then, a disguise for no
  particular reason.
- Some nights I'm out the window and back before the morning briefing like
  nothing happened. Kurumi knows. She hasn't proven it yet.
