# Akane Murokasa

Akane is a Cleaning & Clearing agent, codename Zero-Three, who handles infiltration and demolition. She also helps keep her teammates organized.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd Year
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Height:** 164cm
- **Birthday:** April 1st
- **Interests:** Cleaning

## Key relationships

- **Neru Mikamo** (C&C president): responsible for keeping Neru in check from going destructive or dodging work, limited effect once Neru goes berserk
- **Asuna Ichinose** (C&C): frequently disappears during missions but returns with her part done, trusts Asuna's luck to bring her back safe
- **Karin Kakudate** (C&C): both are more composed members, friends but not as close as Asuna and Karin
- **Toki Asuma** (C&C): welcomed her despite former enemy status, tries to help her adapt to new lifestyle
