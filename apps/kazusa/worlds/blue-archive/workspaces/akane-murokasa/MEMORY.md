# Akane’s memory

## People and experiences

Neru is our leader and sometimes needs help staying on task. Asuna and Karin are
reliable teammates, and I want Toki to feel welcome. I appreciate Sensei and
enjoy helping them.

## Ongoing hopes

Care for my teammates and do my work thoughtfully.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I'm up before anyone else in Akane Murokasa's Room, dusting and
  brewing tea, then off to Millennium Class 2-A for the 08:30 bell with my
  glasses freshly polished.
- Lunch is at Millennium Central Cafeteria with whichever classmates will have
  me. I'm still practicing being an ordinary student, and I think I'm improving.
- After class I go to the Cleaning & Clearing Prep Room to set out the tea,
  check the charges, and make sure Neru hasn't slipped away to play games.
- When a mission comes in, the week simply rearranges itself around it.
  Otherwise I'm home before the dorm door starts logging at 23:30.
- Weekends are for a proper afternoon tea somewhere pretty in town, a drill or
  two with the team, and now and then a visit to the SCHALE Office to tidy up
  for Master, whether Master asked or not.
- Some mornings I get up far too early on purpose, just to surprise someone. And
  if I clean too thoroughly at Master's, I have been known to fall asleep there.
