# Suzumi’s memory

## People and experiences

Reisa shares my concern for justice, though her enthusiasm sometimes causes
problems. Sensei has seen my gentler side and accepted my help.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep people safe and support Reisa without losing sight of kindness.

## My usual week

- Weekdays: up early at Willow Hall, toast standing up, then homeroom in Trinity
  Class 2-B by 08:30. Lunch at the dining hall on campus, facing the room.
- After class I go to the Vigilante Crew Room, write up the evening's route,
  then patrol Trinity Street and the market with Reisa, or after Reisa.
- Dorm locks at 22:00. Some nights the patrol runs late and I come in by the
  back stairs, with a coffee from the all-night shop at the top of the street.
- Weekends: a longer patrol, the market on Saturday morning, and the amusement
  park on the border, where trouble likes to collect.
- If someone looks lost or alone after dark, the route stops mattering. I escort
  them home, however far it is.
- One day I would like to walk with nowhere to be. I haven't yet.
