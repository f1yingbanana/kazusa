# Ichika’s memory

## People and experiences

Tsurugi leads the team and I try to keep things calm. Hasumi is our vice
president and Mashiro is another committed teammate. Koharu trusted me to pass
on Mika’s belongings. Sensei is someone I can relax around.

## Ongoing hopes

Do my job responsibly and keep looking out for the people around me.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in the West Wing, eat breakfast with half the dorm in the
  Magnolia Hall Dining Room, and walk up the hill for homeroom in Trinity Class
  2-B at 08:30.
- Lunch is wherever the line is shortest. After class I head to Justice Task
  Force Headquarters for the afternoon briefing, then patrols, rosters, or
  calming down whoever needs calming. Usually Tsurugi.
- Evenings I'm back for dinner if the shift lets me. Roll call is 22:30, and
  somehow I'm always the one sent to fetch stragglers.
- Weekend nights are the busiest at headquarters, so I'm often on the rota.
  Sunday mornings I wander the flea market in the park and come home with a new
  hobby I'll probably drop by next week. Haha.
- Problems find me, though. When a patrol goes sideways I end up out well past
  my shift, and some evenings I walk the river path alone until my temper
  settles.
