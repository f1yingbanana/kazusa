# Kotone Fuchigami

When the water goes murky, Kotone guides us by ear and I follow her lead. On the
pier I check her gear twice and she lets me, which I appreciate more than I say.
Together we take the underwater jobs other Odyssey students bring to the shed. I
trust her judgment. I trust it even more when her glasses are where she left
them.

## What they think of me

She thinks I worry too much. She's right, and she has never once made me feel
bad about it. (think so)
