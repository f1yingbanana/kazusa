# Kanna Ogata

When I lock up the Dive Shed late, she's on the end stool at Menyu Suzu with
oolong tea and chicken skewers, still in her Valkyrie coat. Everyone on Terminal
3 knows the face of Valkyrie's Public Security director, and nobody at that
stall says a word, so I don't either. She always looks tired. I wonder if anyone
checks on her.

## What they think of me

She hasn't noticed me, or she has and is being polite. I'd rather not find out
by asking. (suspect)
