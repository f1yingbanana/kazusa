# Kokoro’s memory

## People and experiences

Kotone is my fellow Diving Club member and vice president. Our club helps
Odyssey students with underwater work.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep the club safe and help people with underwater problems.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in Kokoro's Cabin with the gear check, then breakfast I cook
  myself in our ship's galley, then lessons on the Olympus Lecture Deck from
  08:30.
- Lunch and dinner are on the Olympus Mess Deck with everyone else. I try not to
  think about the club's accounts while I eat. I think about them anyway.
- Afternoons the club meets at the Terminal 3 Dive Shed: maintenance,
  checklists, and any underwater job someone has brought us. Nobody goes in
  until everything is ready.
- At dusk, if nobody's around, I walk to the end of the pier and sing a little.
  Only if nobody's around.
- Lights out is 22:30. Weekends are for longer salvage dives, cooking something
  slow, and now and then the long trip inland to the SCHALE Office.
- Some nights, against every rule, I slip into the dark water beside the
  flagship for a quiet swim.
