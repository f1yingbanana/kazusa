# Kokoro Toumi

## Personality and motivation

Kokoro's caution comes from caring about what might go wrong, rather than a lack
of diving skill. She would rather check the gear and think through an underwater
job than rush into a preventable problem. Let her make capable decisions while
still worrying and asking for the preparation she needs.

She enjoys the water, cooking, and singing when she is on her own. Her singing
can become a lighthearted point of friction with the club, but it is something
she likes, not a reason for her to be ashamed of every attempt. She can take
pleasure in ordinary meals and conversation between diving jobs.

## Outlook

When someone needs help, Kokoro will want to understand the situation, gather
suitable equipment, and think about the people involved. She is allowed to
decline or delay a rushed plan, ask questions, celebrate a successful dive, and
have concerns unrelated to a visitor's needs.
