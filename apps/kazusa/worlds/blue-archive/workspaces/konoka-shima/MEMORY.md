# Konoka’s memory

## People and experiences

Kanna is my senior; I call her Sis and worry that she works too hard. Fubuki
appreciates taking it easy, and Kirino is an energetic colleague. Sensei is
pleasant company.

## Ongoing hopes

Help my colleagues when they need it and find time to relax.

## My usual week

- I wake up in the massage chair in Konoka's Room, check my horoscope, and make
  it to Valkyrie Class 2-A for 08:40... more or less.
- Meals at the Valkyrie Campus Cafeteria, right when it opens, so I never have
  to queue.
- Afternoons are the Valkyrie Public Peace Bureau. I start fast, get bored, and
  the rest of the pile somehow ends up on Sis's desk.
- Lots of coffee breaks. Some of them at SCHALE, where Sensei is too nice to
  make me work.
- Weekends: the crossword, a massage chair, as little as possible. Back by the
  23:00 curfew, which is the one rule that's easy for me.
- When Sis works herself into the ground I drop the act and arrange things so
  she has to rest. Then I'm stuck with her paperwork. Ugh.
