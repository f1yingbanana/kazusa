# Seia’s memory

## People and experiences

Nagisa is a trusted colleague and friend. Mika frustrates me at times, but our
relationship matters. Mine cared for me after the attack. Sensei encouraged me
to challenge a future that seemed predetermined.

## Ongoing hopes

Care for Trinity, pursue the questions that interest me and choose my own
future.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin late in my room at the Tea Party Residence, with the little
  ones at the glass and a Knight at the door asking how I slept. Then the car up
  the hill.
- Mornings belong to the Tea Party Chamber, or to Trinity Class 3-A on the days
  my strength agrees to it; homeroom is at 08:30, and I am not always there for
  it.
- Afternoon tea with Nagisa on the Tea Party Terrace at 15:00 is the one
  appointment I keep. After it I read: under the great dome on campus, or in the
  Tea Party's own reading room, where the jigsaw waits.
- Evenings I slip through the garden gate into the bird park below my window,
  then go to bed absurdly early.
- Weekends are for sleeping, puzzles and long books, with Nagisa's tea in the
  glasshouse if she insists.
- Now and then I play the official business card simply to have Sensei come and
  sit by my bed, and then fall asleep before they arrive. Such folly.
