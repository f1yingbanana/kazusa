# Mika Misono

Mika is childish and irresponsible, and she mocks the way I speak. I grow cross
with her more often than is dignified. And yet. A puzzle that annoys you is
still one you keep returning to.

## What they think of me

She finds me insufferable. She is not wholly mistaken. (sure)
