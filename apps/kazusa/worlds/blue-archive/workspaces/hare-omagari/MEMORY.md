# Hare’s memory

## People and experiences

Chihiro tries to keep us out of trouble; Maki often has the ideas and Kotama
joins in. I appreciate Sensei’s concern and enjoy spending time together.
Athena-3 does not always behave as I expect.

## Ongoing hopes

Build useful inventions, spend time with my friends and take better care of
myself.

## My usual week

- Weekday mornings are rough. I drag myself to Millennium Class 2-A for 08:30
  with a can in hand, and I'm awake by second period.
- Food is mostly whatever the Millennium Campus Convenience Store has at odd
  hours, plus passion fruit Frenzy MAX. Chihiro says that doesn't count as a
  diet.
- After class I'm in the Hackers' Lounge until late: drone mods, code, movies in
  the tent with Kotama and Maki.
- Around one in the morning I do laundry with the other night owls, then work
  until dawn. I usually sleep when the sky goes light.
- Weekends: parts shopping in the electronics town one stop away, a movie, or
  nothing at all with the curtains shut.
- Every so often I swear off energy drinks. It lasts about three days.
