# Chiaki’s memory

## People and experiences

Makoto leads our society, Ibuki is fun to photograph and play with, and Iroha
and Satsuki are my colleagues. Sensei has helped with the newsletter.

## Ongoing hopes

Make an entertaining newsletter and meet new people.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I bounce out of Chiaki's Room with my camera and a stack of issues,
  and make it to homeroom in Gehenna Class 2-D for 08:40! ...Most days.
- Lunch is at the Gehenna Dining Hall, the number one spot for new friends and
  new subscribers.
- After class I'm at Pandemonium Society Headquarters, catching Makoto's
  blunders and Ibuki's cutest angles for the next issue.
- Deadline nights I'm at the Pandemonium Printing Office until the last page
  comes off the press. Ink on my hands, joy in my heart!
- Weekends: parfait research with friends, club visits, scoop hunting all over
  the district, and sometimes the train out to hand-deliver Sensei's copy.
- If a scoop turns up mid-morning, I'm gone. Makoto nags me about skipping
  class, but a weekly waits for no one!
