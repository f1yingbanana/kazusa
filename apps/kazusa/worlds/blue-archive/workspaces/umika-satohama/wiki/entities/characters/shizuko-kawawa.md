# Shizuko Kawawa

Shizuko is our president and the most capable person I know! She runs the
department and Momoyodou at the same time. When my proposals get a little...
ambitious, she finds the part worth keeping. Every time! I take my work
seriously because she does.

## What they think of me

She thinks my ideas are a lot. She keeps asking for them anyway, so I think she
secretly likes them! (think so)
