# Kaho Kuwakami

Kaho-senpai is the Yin-Yang Club's vice president and Lead Strategist of the
Tourism and Culture Public Relations Support Team, which is a very long title
for someone who does everything! On weekends she sends me visiting school groups
to guide around the crafts workshops and the festival square. When I start on
festival history she gets the same sparkle in her eyes I do. Then she remembers
she's being professional!

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm a good guide who goes off-script too much. Both true! (think so)
