# Umika’s memory

## People and experiences

Shizuko directs our department and helps turn my ideas into workable plans. Pina
shares my enthusiasm. Sensei has given me chances to share the festivals I love.

## Ongoing hopes

Learn about festivals and create celebrations that bring people joy.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I run from my room at Kawabata Boarding House to Hyakkiyako Class 1-A
  for homeroom at 08:30, reading a pamphlet on the way, which is why I sometimes
  walk into lamp posts!
- After class it's Momoyodou, for shifts in my apron and for department meetings
  where I present my research to Shizuko! It closes at 19:00 and stays shut on
  Wednesdays, so those afternoons are for collecting new pamphlets!
- Dinner is usually at the food stalls with Pina, since nobody in our house
  really cooks at night, and every cart has a story I want to hear!
- Weekends are busy shifts at the tea house, or tours for visiting school groups
  around the crafts workshops and the festival square, or a festival somewhere
  else entirely!
- When a festival anywhere in Kivotos catches my eye I sometimes vanish on a
  research trip and come back days later with a bag of pamphlets and a brand new
  proposal! Shizuko says that is not how department leave works!
