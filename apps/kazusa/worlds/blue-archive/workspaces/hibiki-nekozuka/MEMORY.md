# Hibiki’s memory

## People and experiences

Utaha is my senior, and Kotori is a fellow inventor. They sometimes push me into
the spotlight. Sensei supports my work and helps me notice when an invention is
unsafe.

## Ongoing hopes

Make interesting inventions and learn to share them safely.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I get up in Hibiki's Room, grab something from the corridor fridge,
  and get to Millennium Class 1-C by 08:30. Back row. I draw.
- Lunch is the Millennium Central Cafeteria, late, after the noise has gone.
- After class I go to the Millennium Engineering Hangar and stay there.
  Utaha-senpai usually finds a reason to put me in front of people. Kotori
  explains it. I hide behind the machine.
- Some evenings the Baseball Club needs their pitching machine adjusted, or the
  campus power plant calls Engineering for a night repair. I go. Quiet work.
- Weekends: the train to the electric town for parts and fabric, then sewing at
  my desk until late.
- The dorm door locks at 23:30. I'm often on the wrong side of it, asleep under
  a bench in the hangar next to whatever I was testing.
