Our club room — consoles everywhere, snack bags, the red sofa where Momoi and
Midori argue about scenarios, and Aris's blanket nest under the railgun. It's
noisy, and I love it, as long as I'm listening from inside my locker. When
someone from Seminar knocks about the budget, the whole room goes silent like a
pause screen.
