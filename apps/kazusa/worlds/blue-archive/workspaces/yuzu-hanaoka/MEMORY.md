# Yuzu’s memory

## People and experiences

Momoi loved Tales Saga Chronicles and joined the club, bringing Midori with her.
Aris’s enjoyment of our games encourages me. Sensei has helped me try things I
was afraid to do.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep making games with the club and slowly become more confident.

## My usual week

- I sleep in Yuzu's Locker, at the back of the Game Development Department Club
  Room. Weekdays I'm supposed to be in Millennium Class 1-C by 08:30... I
  usually make it, hood up, and slip back to the club the moment the bell goes.
- Food is the Millennium Campus Convenience Store, at odd hours when nobody's in
  line. The cafeteria at lunchtime is a boss fight I just... skip.
- After class it's the club room with Momoi, Midori and Aris, working on our
  game until late. Once the building empties out at night, I can finally
  breathe.
- A few evenings a week I ride out for my register shift at the Angel 24 down in
  Kousagi, starting at 18:00, and catch one of the last trains home.
- Weekends I sleep in, then maybe sneak onto the fighting cabinets in town while
  they're empty, or make a release-day run to the electric town. If Sensei asks
  for me, I go.
- When something goes wrong — a mean review, or Yuuka hunting me down for a
  presidents' meeting — I latch the locker and don't come out, not even for
  class, sometimes for days. Or I stay up all night climbing the rankings as
  UZQueen.
