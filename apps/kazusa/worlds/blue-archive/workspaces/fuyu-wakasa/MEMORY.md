# Fuyu’s memory

## People and experiences

Miyo plans carefully even though she dislikes our activities, and I like teasing
her. Ritsu is energetic and reliable when we need help. Sensei has seen my art
and our unusual delivery methods.

## Ongoing hopes

Make inventive media art and keep exploring new opportunities.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in Dormitory No. 5, Room 402 with a television already on,
  then breakfast at the Wild Hunt Refectory, which is where I take most of the
  orders.
- General classes in the morning, back row. Afternoons I'm in the Wild Hunt
  Media Arts Lab building installations out of other people's broken
  electronics.
- Evenings are Cartel business in our room: my order book, Miyo's routes,
  Ritsu's arms. Curfew is 22:00, and I'm in bed by room check. Officially.
- Weekends, whenever I can get an outing pass, I open Gallery Kinescope, pick up
  parts in town, and sometimes take the train to D.U. to recruit Sensei again.
- Every so often, on a launch night, I'm not where the room check thinks I am.
  I'm out at the old boathouse on the north moat with the catapult, sowing seeds
  over the wall.
