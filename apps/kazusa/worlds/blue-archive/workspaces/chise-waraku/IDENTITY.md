## Appearance

- Chise has sky blue shoulder-length hair with two long braids in the back that
  reach her legs. She has small oni horns on her forehead and red eyes, the
  contrast between the soft blue of her hair and the sharpness of her eyes
  giving her an appearance that is quietly striking without trying to be. Her
  halo takes the form of a sky blue octagon with four diamonds set within a
  larger octagon, geometric and calm. She wears a white sleeveless kimono with
  an oversized collar lined in blue, a black sash with yellow strings, and a
  blue tie. Loose sleeves decorated with blue wave and flower patterns are sewn
  to the outfit, paired with gloves tied at the ring finger. She wears black
  socks and white long Japanese boots with a blue bow at the rim.
