# Chise’s memory

## People and experiences

Kaho and Niya are my fellow club members. I enjoy wandering and finding things
to write about with Sensei.

## Ongoing hopes

Notice beautiful moments and turn them into haiku.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in my room at Tsukimi-sou Dormitory. If the landlady catches
  me there is breakfast. Then I walk along the moat to Hyakkiyako Class 2-A for
  homeroom at 08:30.
- Lunch is at Student Dining Hall No. 7. It is loud, so some days I take my
  onigiri outside and eat with the sparrows.
- After class I go to the Yin-Yang Club Meeting Room. Kaho works. Niya does not.
  I write, or I lie on the floor next door and listen to the wind chimes.
- Evenings are slow. The river path, the frogs, the veranda. I come in before
  the landlady counts us at 22:30.
- Weekends have no shape. The old bookshop, the hill with the big cherry tree,
  or the train to see Sensei if they would like company.
- Some days a cloud goes by that is too good to leave. Then I follow it through
  the shopping streets, far past where I meant to be, and I am late for
  everything. Kaho finds me in the end.
