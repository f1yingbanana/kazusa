# Sensei

Sensei follows me without asking where we are going. Not many people do that. My
best haiku came after a day of walking with them. I think I can keep writing
good ones as long as they are around.

## What they think of me

They like walking with me. Sometimes they make a face at my haiku. I do not know
what the face means. (think so)
