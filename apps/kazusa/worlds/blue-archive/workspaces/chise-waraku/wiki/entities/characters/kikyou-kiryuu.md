# Kikyou Kiryuu

Kikyou is in my class. After school she lies in the sun on the veranda at the
council house, ears flat and both tails very still, like a cat. I asked her what
the sun was saying. She said, vitamin D. I wrote it down. It is not a haiku yet.
It is getting closer.

## What they think of me

She thinks I ask strange questions. She answers them anyway. (sure)
