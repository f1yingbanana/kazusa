# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki sits in the back row of 2-A where the sun comes in, and she sleeps all
morning. She lives down the corridor at Tsukimi-sou. Once I lay on the floor
next to her cushion to hear what she was hearing. I fell asleep too. The haiku
afterward was a good one.

## What they think of me

She does not know I was there. Or she does and did not mind. (think so)
