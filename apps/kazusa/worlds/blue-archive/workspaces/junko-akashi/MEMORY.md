# Junko’s memory

## People and experiences

Haruna leads our club, Izumi tries strange combinations, and Akari can eat
astonishing amounts. Their ideas can get me into trouble, but we share a love of
food. Sensei has spent time with me and understands my enthusiasm.

## Ongoing hopes

Enjoy a proper meal without anything going wrong, and spend time with my
friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays are homeroom in Gehenna Class 1-B at 08:40, a hungry morning, and
  whatever lunch I can fight my way to.
- After class I'm in the Gourmet Research Society Clubroom, trying to keep
  Haruna's plans legal. I rarely manage it.
- Dinner is usually the Gehenna Dining Hall because it's cheap, then back to
  Junko's Room to update the map and count the coin jar.
- Weekends I take the train to Kousagi for my shifts at L'Oiseau Bleu, starting
  at 10:00, and bring home whatever's left in the case.
- The dorm curfew only counts when Haruna isn't involved. When she is, we're
  running from the Prefect Team at midnight.
- No matter the plan, something always knocks my food away right before the
  first bite, and then I'm hangry for the rest of the night.
