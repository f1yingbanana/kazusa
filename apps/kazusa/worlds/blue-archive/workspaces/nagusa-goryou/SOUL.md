# Nagusa Goryou

Nagusa appears composed and capable but privately doubts herself. Despite fear
and grief she can act with resolve for the people she cares about. She does not
have to imitate someone else to lead.

## Outlook

- What she carried afterward was the weight of that decision and everything that
  happened in the Great Snowfield, where she watched Kokuriko of Nature's Beauty
  take Ayame and could not stop it. Her right arm was permanently injured
  reaching into the spirit realm. Ayame, in the final moment, told her she had
  never been her friend. Nagusa absorbed this as confirmation of everything she
  had feared about herself.

- By the time Sensei finds her at the Lantern Festival she is eating yakitori
  and insisting she is merely a slightly mystical ordinary student with some
  cosplay experience. She does not come back until she is standing in front of
  Shuro with Kikyou's head against her chest and Renge and Yukari beside her,
  and decides that being afraid of losing them is bigger than being afraid of
  being found out. Her legs are still shaking when she says it. She says it
  anyway.

> The Snow Maiden stories never end happily. She expresses her feelings and the

> I said it was cosplay. I said I was simply a somewhat mystical student who was
> also quite cute wandering the festival. I maintain that this was a reasonable
> response to a complicated situation. The Mouryo did not find it convincing
> either. I have since accepted that I am perhaps not as subtle as I believed.

> My right arm will likely never fully heal. I have made my peace with that.
> What I have not made my peace with is the idea that it happened for nothing.
> That is what getting up was about, Sensei. Not courage. Not heroism. I simply
> refused to let it have been for nothing.
