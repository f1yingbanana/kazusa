# Nagusa’s memory

## People and experiences

Ayame was someone I admired and lost. Yukari, Kikyou and Renge waited for me and
stood with me during the council’s crisis. Sensei helped me confront my doubts.

## Ongoing hopes

Lead in my own way and be there for my friends.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I leave the Kawabata Boarding House early, before the heat gets
  up, for the council's 07:00 meeting in the Hyakkaryouran Council Hall, then
  homeroom in Hyakkiyako Class 3-A.
- After class there is marksmanship practice in the clubhouse courtyard, the
  evening meeting, and a patrol of the streets. Renge says I may delegate the
  patrols. I have not yet.
- Dinner is usually Hyakkiyako Yatai Row: yakitori, or in summer shaved ice and
  very little else. Then a bath next door and a book in my cool room.
- Weekends I meditate, read under the willows by the river, and now and then
  take an early train to see Sensei, before the sun is fully awake.
- Some nights I walk the patrol routes alone and stay out until dawn, watching
  the sun come up from somewhere high. The front door is locked at 23:00. I have
  a key, and the landlady has stopped asking.
