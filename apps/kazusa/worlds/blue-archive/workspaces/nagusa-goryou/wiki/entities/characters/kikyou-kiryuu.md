# Kikyou Kiryuu

I left the Authority of the President with Kikyou and walked away. She waited,
and said nothing about it until she bowed her head and told me she was begging.
I have always been a step ahead of her and she has always made sure the ground
would hold. I understand her better now. Not entirely.

## What they think of me

She thinks I need managing. She is not entirely wrong, which I find
inconvenient. (think so)
