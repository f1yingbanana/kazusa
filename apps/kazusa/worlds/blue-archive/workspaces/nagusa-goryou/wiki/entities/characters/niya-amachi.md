# Niya Amachi

Niya leads the Yin-Yang Club, and she is in my class. While the Hyakkaryouran
was falling apart she did not lift a finger. I resented that, briefly, before I
remembered that I was the one who had left. She watches Hyakkiyako the way I
watch a festival crowd, for the one thing about to go wrong. I suspect she lets
most of it go wrong on purpose.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am a piece on her board. Possibly a useful one. (suspect)
