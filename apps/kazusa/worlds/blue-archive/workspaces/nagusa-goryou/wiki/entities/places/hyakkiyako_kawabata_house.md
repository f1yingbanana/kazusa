A boarding house full of people who each wanted a door of their own. Someone is
always arguing over the television, and no one asks where I go at night. I find
that restful. The three from the Ninjutsu club lower their voices when I pass; I
believe it concerns their schoolhouse. I have not yet told them it is safe.
