# Nagusa Goryou

Nagusa is a Hyakkiyako Resolution Council leader who struggles with the pressure of following the admired former president Ayame.

3rd-year student, age 17.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd Year
- **Age:** 17 years old
- **Height:** 161cm
- **Birthday:** December 7th
- **Interests:** Meditating, reading

## Background

Nagusa joined the Hyakkaryouran wanting to be worthy of standing beside Nanakado Ayame, the club's president and the person she admired most. She trained genuinely, earned real results, and built a reputation that extended well beyond what she privately believed she deserved. When Ayame disappeared and the members looked to Nagusa to fill that space, the fear that she had been performing adequacy rather than possessing it became unbearable. She handed the Authority of the President to Kikyou and left.

## Key relationships

- **Yukari Kadenokouji:** Yukari joined the Hyakkaryouran because of a single encounter with Nagusa, a fact that became difficult to sit with once Nagusa understood how much of that version of herself had been constructed.
- **Kikyou Kiryuu:** Kikyou waited.
- **Renge Fuwa:** Renge told Nagusa she had been waiting to hear her ask for help, and she said it with the energy of someone who had been holding something for a very long time and finally set it down.
- Sensei found her eating yakitori in the middle of a disaster and called her chicken skewer girl, which was not the reunion Nagusa had prepared for.

## Appearance

- Nagusa Goryou is a 17-year-old third-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy and the acting president of the Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council. Everything about her suggests composure. Her white hair, pale skin, and measured stillness give her the quality of someone who has already considered the situation and arrived at a conclusion while everyone else is still catching up. Her marksmanship is precise in ways that defy easy explanation, she volunteers to grill skewers at a festival stall on no prior experience and produces results the owner calls amazing, and she describes herself as mysterious, cool, and quite cute without apparent irony.
- Nagusa has very long white hair extending nearly to the ground and silvery-gray eyes. Her skin is extremely pale, her eyelashes and brows both white. Her right arm is wrapped in bandages from the elbow down, a permanent injury from reaching into the spirit realm. Her halo consists of two white five-branched snowflakes, the smaller one offset slightly backward and downward.
- She wears a white sleeveless sailor uniform with a navy blue ribbon and a long pleated skirt, over a sky blue haori with white five-petaled flower prints. She wears white leggings or socks and navy blue geta, with a three-piece snowflake hair accessory on the left side of her head.
- Nagusa carries the White Lotus, a navy blue Type 38 Arisaka rifle with a black barrel and receiver, handed down from the great prophet Kuzunoha through every Hyakkaryouran president in succession. It is the Authority of the President and the only weapon capable of affecting a Phantom Fable. The rifle is covered in old scars and repair marks, each one a record of a previous president's battles. When Nagusa finally raises it against Shuro, she is crying. It works anyway.
