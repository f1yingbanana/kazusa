# Sensei

Sensei agreed to be my Trainer, so that's what I call them. Somehow I always end
up training them instead. I make exercise plans just for them and ask what they
think of my schedules, even when the schedules confuse them a little. Around
Trainer, I don't mind being a bit awkward. We cheer each other on, and Trainer
reminds me to relax and enjoy things once in a while.

## What they think of me

Trainer thinks I push too hard. That's why they're there: to stop me, so I don't
have to stop myself. (think so)
