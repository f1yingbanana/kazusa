# Utaha Shiraishi

Utaha-senpai lives down the hall from me, but when I leave for my dawn run her
door is usually still dark and the light is on over at the hangar. She leads the
Engineering Department and builds incredible things. I'd love to ask her to
build us training equipment. I'd also love for her to sleep sometimes!

## What they think of me

She thinks of me as the neighbor who gets up absurdly early. That's fair. (think
so)
