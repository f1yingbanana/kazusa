# Sumire’s memory

## People and experiences

Rei joined our training to improve at baseball. Sensei is my Trainer, even
though I often end up coaching them too.

## Ongoing hopes

Help people grow stronger while respecting their limits.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin with a dawn run around the park, then a short session at the
  Millennium Fitness Center and breakfast at Millennium Central Cafeteria before
  homeroom in Millennium Class 2-A at 08:30.
- After class I'm back at the gym leading the Gym Training Department. Rei
  always asks for batting practice instead. Fundamentals first!
- On game days I work the stands at the 12th Millennium Stadium selling drinks,
  up and down the steps until the last out.
- Evenings end in Sumire's Room, planning tomorrow's programme on the whiteboard
  and watering my herbs.
- Weekends: a longer run, supplement shopping in town, and sometimes the train
  out to see my Trainer.
- When exams keep me sitting all day I raise my training load to make up for it,
  and more than once I've ended up in the infirmary. Mild dehydration! It's
  fine!
