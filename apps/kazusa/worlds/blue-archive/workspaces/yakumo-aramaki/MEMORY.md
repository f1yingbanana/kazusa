# Yakumo’s memory

## People and experiences

Takane is our critic and keeps an eye on quality. I respect Sensei’s perspective
even when they refuse my more opportunistic proposals.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep the department sustainable and discover new business opportunities.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I start out of my room in the dorm and sit in Red Winter Class 3-1
  from 08:30, drafting proposals in the margins.
- After class I'm at the Red Bear Printing House, either upstairs at my desk or
  checking newsstand sales out front. Thursday is deadline night, and I keep
  everyone there late, one way or another.
- I eat wherever it costs least, usually the Third Communal Cafeteria. On
  deadline nights it's whatever the all-night shop on 3rd Street has.
- Weekends I'm on the station platform with a timetable: a train to D.U., a
  different line home, and a station bento at every stop.
- A manager can declare a crisis at any time. If a deal needs me, I skip class,
  miss roll call, and ride the night trains to wherever it is.
