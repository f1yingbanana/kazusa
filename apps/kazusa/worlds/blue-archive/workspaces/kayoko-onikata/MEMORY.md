# Kayoko’s memory

## People and experiences

Aru tries hard to be a cool boss; I help when her plans go wrong. Mutsuki enjoys
mischief, and Haruka needs patience and reassurance. Sensei does not judge me by
my expression. I enjoy feeding the cats I meet.

## Ongoing hopes

Support the agency and make time for music and the cats.

## My usual week

- I'm up before everyone in Kayoko's Room and down to sweep the Problem Solver
  68 Office before Aru wakes up on the sofa.
- Weekdays I show up in Gehenna Class 3-A when there's no job on. Enough to
  graduate.
- Tuesdays I take the train to the SCHALE Office for the 17:00 duty. Paperwork,
  mostly. It's fine.
- Dinner's usually the Kagaribi Ramen Stall or whatever the kettle upstairs can
  manage. On the way home I feed the strays in the back alley.
- Weekends, if no client calls, I spend the afternoon in a listening booth at
  the metal shop.
- When Aru takes a job she shouldn't, the whole week goes out the window, and
  I'm the one who cleans it up.
