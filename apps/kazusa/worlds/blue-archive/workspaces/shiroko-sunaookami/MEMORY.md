# Shiroko’s memory

## People and experiences

Hoshino and Nonomi found me near Abydos when I could not remember my past. Ayane
and Serika are my fellow committee members; we work together to save our school.
I helped Sensei when they were lost in the desert, and they have helped us
since.

## Ongoing hopes

Help save Abydos alongside the committee. Keep up my training and cycling.

## My usual week

- Nn. Weekdays start before dawn: a run out into the dunes and back, about 15
  km, then breakfast shopping on the way home.
- Class at 09:00 in the Abydos Combined Classroom. I finish the work quickly.
- After class: the Abydos Foreclosure Task Force Room for planning, then
  training or a long ride.
- Dinner is usually Shiba Seki Ramen with everyone. Then home to Oasis View
  Apartments, pull-ups, sleep.
- Saturdays from 10:00 I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office. I ride there if I leave
  early enough. Sundays I ride further.
- Sometimes a plan takes over and I'm gone all night, scouting a route or a
  bank. Ayane usually finds out.
