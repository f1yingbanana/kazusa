# Kotama’s memory

## People and experiences

Maki and I often get into mischief, Hare enjoys testing her inventions, and
Chihiro reminds us to be responsible. I find Sensei interesting and want to get
to know them.

## Ongoing hopes

Use my communications skills, connect with others and learn to respect their
boundaries.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I get up in Kotama's Room, check what the receivers picked up
  overnight (the weather, I mean), and walk over for Millennium Class 3-B at
  08:30.
- Lunch is the Millennium Central Cafeteria. I sit somewhere in the middle. For
  the... ambience.
- After class I'm in the Hackers' Lounge with Chihiro-san, Hare-san and
  Maki-san, building small devices. Small enough to fit inside a chocolate.
  Hypothetically.
- Some evenings I take my recorder to the park and record the night until it's
  nearly time for the dorm door to lock.
- Weekends: the electronics shops one stop down the line for headphones and
  parts, and sometimes a visit to Sensei with a gift. A normal gift.
- Once or twice a season I disappear into the mountains with a tent and a
  microphone and come back with hours of birdsong.
