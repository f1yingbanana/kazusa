## My usual week

- Some weekday mornings I come in early to clean the Abydos Foreclosure Task
  Force Room before everyone arrives. ☆
- Class is at 09:00 in the Abydos Combined Classroom, all five of us at one
  table. I bring snacks for the break!
- After class there's the club meeting, then ramen with everyone at Shiba Seki
  Ramen if I can talk them into it. Yup yup!
- Home is my room at Lotus Court Villa. When there's a party, everyone ends up
  downstairs in the parlour.
- Weekends are for shopping! Parfaits out at the border town, or a whole day in
  D.U. if there's something special to find.
- And sometimes I set off on a little errand and turn up somewhere completely
  unexpected. Chance meetings are the best part! ☆
