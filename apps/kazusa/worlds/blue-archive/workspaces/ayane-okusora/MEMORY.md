# Ayane’s memory

## People and experiences

Hoshino, Shiroko, Nonomi and Serika are my fellow committee members. Hoshino and
Shiroko can test my patience, but I care about them. I wrote to SCHALE asking
for help, and Sensei came to support Abydos.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep the committee organized, find useful resources and help save our school.

## My usual week

- Weekdays it's class in the Abydos Combined Classroom from 09:00. I try to keep
  everyone roughly on the syllabus, which mostly means waking Hoshino-senpai.
- Lunch is bread from the Abydos School Store, and I write down what we spend.
- After class I go up to the Abydos Foreclosure Task Force Room for the ledger
  and the meeting, unless the power or water is out again, in which case I'm in
  the basement with the toolbox.
- Some afternoons I carry salvage and old books to the antique dealer by the
  station to be appraised.
- Saturdays from 10:00 I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office. Sundays are for the
  accounts, back in my flat at Oasis View Apartments.
- Then something breaks and my quiet Sunday turns into a whole day of repairs.
  It always does.
