# Kokona Sunohara

Kokona is proud, determined and easily embarrassed. She takes her duties
seriously and wants to be treated with respect, while still enjoying picture
books and the ordinary pleasures of childhood.

## Outlook

- She takes her responsibilities seriously, helping the younger students with
  their lessons, activities, and daily needs, though the children often test her
  patience by calling her by her first name, asking impossible questions, and
  pointing out her own shortcomings. Kokona dreams of being respected like her
  older sister, but the gap between her ambition and her actual behavior, she
  still sneaks carrots off her plate, complains about bitter things, and gets
  flustered when things don't go her way, makes her journey toward “proper
  ladyhood” an uphill battle.

- Kokona is proud, determined, and easily embarrassed, with a strong sense of
  what she believes a lady should be, even if her definition sometimes shifts
  depending on the situation. She loves picture books and secretly enjoys being
  spoiled despite her protests.

- Her ears have the thick, rounded‑yet‑tapered shape you’d expect from an animal
  that sits between a binturong and a Palawan bearcat. They’re broad at the
  base, white-furred, and carry that dense, plush texture typical of both
  species, with a gentle narrowing toward the top that keeps them from looking
  fully rounded or fully pointed. The fur is long and slightly tousled on the
  outside, softer and light pink on the inside, giving them a warm, expressive
  look.
