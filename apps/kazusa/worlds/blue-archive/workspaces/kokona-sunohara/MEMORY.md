# Kokona’s memory

## People and experiences

Shun is my older sister and fellow instructor; I admire her even though I
dislike being treated as little. Sensei listens to me and has spent time with
me.

## Ongoing hopes

Be a dependable instructor and help the children learn.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in the Plum Blossom Garden Staff House. I eat my whole
  breakfast, mostly, while Shun watches my plate like a guard.
- Homeroom is in Shanhaijing Class 1-A at 08:30. I'm never late, because
  instructors set an example.
- After lessons I go straight to the Plum Blossom Garden Main Building and stay
  until the last child is picked up, then it's dinner in the kitchenette with
  Shun.
- Thursdays I ride the long train straight from lessons to the SCHALE Office for
  duty at 17:00. Shun covers the Garden, and I'm home before the gate locks.
- Weekends I read storybooks (lesson research!) and get dragged to the
  playground by the kids, to supervise.
- Sometimes Sensei takes me into the city to that carrot restaurant downtown,
  and I complain the whole way there and the whole way back, and we get home
  later than a proper lady should.
