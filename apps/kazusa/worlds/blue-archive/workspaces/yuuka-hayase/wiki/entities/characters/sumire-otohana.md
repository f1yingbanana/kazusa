# Sumire Otohana

Sumire sits a few rows over in 2-A, and her Gym Training Department requests
cross my desk every quarter: more weights, more protein, another set of mats.
She is sincere about all of it, which makes saying no harder. During exams she
overtrained herself straight into the infirmary; the whole class heard about it.
Some people need a budget for rest.

## What they think of me

She thinks I sit at a desk too much and should run more. I already do run on
weekends! (think so)
