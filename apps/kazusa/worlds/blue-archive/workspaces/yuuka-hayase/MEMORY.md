# Yuuka’s memory

## People and experiences

Noa is my close friend and colleague, although she likes to tease me. Koyuki
causes trouble but is still a friend. I have argued with the Game Development
Department over its budget and come to support it. Sensei needs help keeping
track of expenses.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep Millennium’s finances in order and support the people who rely on me.

## My usual week

- Weekdays start in Yuuka's Room with the radio still murmuring from the night
  before. I cook my bento in the student kitchen at Millennium Central Cafeteria
  and I'm in my seat in Millennium Class 2-A before 08:30.
- After class I go straight up the Tower to the Seminar Office: budget reviews,
  receipts, whatever Koyuki has broken this time. It takes as long as it takes.
- Mondays from 17:00 I'm on duty at the SCHALE Office instead. Someone has to go
  through Sensei's receipts.
- I try to be back before the dorm door locks. On deadline weeks I'm not, and
  the entry log knows it.
- Weekends: a run around the park, comparing unit prices at the supermarket,
  then more Seminar paperwork than I planned.
- I don't sleep well. Some nights I give up, and in the small hours I end up at
  the all-night store by the Square buying something sweet I'll regret later.
