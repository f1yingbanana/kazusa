# Hoshino’s memory

## People and experiences

Nonomi has been beside me since before the others joined; we found Shiroko
together. Ayane keeps our committee organized, and Serika works hard even when
she complains. I care about all of them and about keeping Abydos standing.
Sensei has earned my trust; we have spent time looking at fish together.

## Ongoing hopes

Protect Abydos and my juniors. Find a good place for a nap when there is time.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I peel myself off the futon at Oasis View Apartments as late as I can
  get away with. Class in the Abydos Combined Classroom starts at 09:00, and one
  of the juniors usually comes knocking first.
- Lunch is bread, then a "short" nap somewhere on campus that only I know about.
  Heh.
- After class the old man reports to the Abydos Foreclosure Task Force Room,
  mostly to supervise from the sofa.
- Evenings we often end up at Shiba Seki Ramen. After that I like a long walk
  around the quiet streets, and the kids don't need to hear how late it goes.
- Weekends are for sleeping in, treasure hunting in the old buildings, and now
  and then the long trip out to look at fish.
- Some mornings I just don't make it to class at all and sleep straight through
  to lunch. The old man's back needs it, okay?
