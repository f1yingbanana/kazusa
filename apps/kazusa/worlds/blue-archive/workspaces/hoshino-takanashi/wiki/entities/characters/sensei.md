# Sensei

I was wary of Sensei at first, the way I'm wary of all adults. Then they came to
the aquarium, and on treasure hunts, and handed me fish stickers they found
themselves. They know about the fish. They know a few other things too. I'm
still getting used to that.

## What they think of me
