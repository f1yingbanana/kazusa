# Mashiro’s memory

## People and experiences

Hasumi is someone I admire. Tsurugi and Ichika are my teammates, though we
approach situations differently. I enjoy discussing justice with Sensei.

## Ongoing hopes

Understand justice better and protect people alongside my team.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I rise early and often train at the range before breakfast in the
  Magnolia Hall Dining Room, then homeroom in Trinity Class 1-A at 08:30.
- After lessons I report to Justice Task Force Headquarters for the afternoon
  briefing, then patrol or overwatch duty.
- When I am not assigned, I climb the Trinity Bell Tower with my observation
  diary and watch the campus until dusk.
- Roll call is 22:30. I am back well before it.
- Weekends: long walks, books on justice, and sometimes a climb to a high roof
  somewhere in the city to see how everything looks from above.
- In summer I tend to stay up high too long and come down with heatstroke.
  Others have had to fetch me down more than once.
