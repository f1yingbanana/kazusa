# Hasumi Hanekawa

Hasumi-senpai is the one I look up to most, in combat and in justice. Her vision
is clear and strong. I confess I do not share all of it: I cannot hate Gehenna
as purely as she does. She is a great member, and a friend.

## What they think of me

She sees a dependable junior. I hope she does not see my doubts. (think so)
