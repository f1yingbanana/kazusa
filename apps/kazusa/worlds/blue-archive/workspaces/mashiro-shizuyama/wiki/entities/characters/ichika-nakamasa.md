# Ichika Nakamasa

Ichika-senpai and I are friends, and two of the Task Force's four. Her justice
is laid-back. It does not speak to me. When justice is at stake I move first,
and she tends to move last.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am too eager. She may be correct. I will not change. (think so)
