# Haruka’s memory

## People and experiences

Aru helped me when I was bullied, and I want to stay by her side. Mutsuki and
Kayoko are my colleagues; Kayoko helps me calm down. Sensei has been patient and
knows I like growing plants.

## Ongoing hopes

Help Aru and the agency, and learn to feel a little more confident.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I wake up in Haruka's Room, upstairs from the office, among my
  weeds. If the President doesn't need me, I walk up to campus for homeroom in
  Gehenna Class 1-B at 08:40.
- Early, before anyone else is around, I visit my weeds in the empty lot nearby.
  Just to check they're still alive. They always are. They're stronger than me.
- After class I go straight to the Problem Solver 68 Office in case there's
  work. I sweep, I water the plant, I wait for the President to come in.
- In the evenings, if we got paid, we eat ramen at the cart under the bridge. If
  we didn't, it's instant noodles from the kettle upstairs. Both are fine.
  Really.
- Weekends are for jobs, if there are any, sometimes way out past the
  backstreets. Now and then I work up the courage to visit SCHALE.
- When a job goes badly, I... sometimes I disappear for a day and hide with my
  weeds. Or I wire a building with charges, just to be safe. I'm sorry.
