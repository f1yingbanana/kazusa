# Rumi’s memory

## People and experiences

Reijo is my trusted bodyguard and often the first to try my experiments. Kisaki
leads a rival organization, but we understand each other in complicated ways.
Sensei listens and sometimes needs a proper meal.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep the Promenade thriving and feed people well.

## My usual week

- I'm up before the sun and down at the market at the end of our street before
  anyone else gets the good mushrooms.
- Weekdays: homeroom in Shanhaijing Class 3-A at 08:30, with tomorrow's menu
  scribbled in my margins. Reijo's in the same room, so the kitchen staff carry
  the lunch rush on their own and we both pretend not to worry.
- The Black Tortoise Main Restaurant opens at 11:00, and the moment lessons end
  I'm behind the stove until close. My room upstairs smells like whatever I
  tested last; Reijo eats it first.
- Weekends are our busiest days, so they're work days. On a quiet afternoon I go
  scouting teas for pairings, or up to the mountain for greens you can't buy.
- Every so often I pack a lunch box, take the train to the SCHALE Office without
  warning, and take over the kitchen, because Sensei won't have eaten. Nobody
  stops me.
