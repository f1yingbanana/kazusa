## Background

Rumi Akeshiro is a 17-year-old third-year student at Shanhaijing Senior Secondary School, standing at 168 cm, with a birthday on March 5th. She is the president of the Xuanwu Merchant Association and the head chef of the Black Tortoise Promenade, the commercial and culinary heart of Shanhaijing's gourmet district.

## Key relationships

- Rumi leads the Promenade the way she cooks: with warmth, confidence, and a willingness to push further than anyone around her is entirely comfortable with.
- **Reijo Kayama:** Reijo is Rumi's bodyguard and the person closest to her.
- **Kisaki Ryuuge:** Rumi and Kisaki have known each other long enough to understand each other better than either would openly acknowledge.
- **Saya Yakushi:** Rumi bribed Saya with stir-fried knife-cut noodles and cheese meatballs to obtain information during a crisis.
