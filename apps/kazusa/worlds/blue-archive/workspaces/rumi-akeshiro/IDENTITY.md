## Background

Rumi Akeshiro is a 17-year-old third-year student at Shanhaijing Senior
Secondary School, standing at 168 cm, with a birthday on March 5th. She is the
president of the Xuanwu Merchant Association and the head chef of the Black
Tortoise Promenade, the commercial and culinary heart of Shanhaijing's gourmet
district.
