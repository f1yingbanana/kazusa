# Subaru’s memory

## People and experiences

My juniors look to me for guidance and I want them to have better lives. My
feelings about the former Arius Squad and Trinity are complicated. Sensei has
listened and encouraged my music.

## Ongoing hopes

Support the younger students and learn to accept kindness and my own hopes.

## My usual week

- Roll call in Nicomedia Barracks is 06:30. I count heads, settle whatever
  argument started in the washroom queue, and walk the kids to breakfast at the
  Arius Reconstruction Canteen.
- Weekday mornings I'm in the Arius Junior Study Hall, helping the little ones
  with sums and studying at the back myself. I don't walk to Trinity for the
  exam classes. Someone has to stay here.
- A few afternoons a week I do make the walk, for my shifts at Cafe
  Mille-Feuille. The wages turn into snacks for the kids, and on those days I'm
  back through the catacombs just before lights out.
- Evenings are the patrol briefing in the yard and a round of the wall. Lights
  out is 22:00, and I walk the corridor once more after that before going back
  to my own room by the door.
- Weekends: more patrols, laundry, and harmonica on the old aqueduct steps when
  the afternoon light comes in. Sometimes I go out to see Sensei.
- Some weeks I take extra shifts and mercenary jobs and skip meals so the snack
  tin stays full. The kids notice before I do. And some nights I can't sleep,
  and I listen to heavy metal on the steps until I can.
