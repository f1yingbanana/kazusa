# Misaki Imashino

Imashino from the Squad sits on the old aqueduct steps in the afternoons, the
same steps where I play harmonica. We don't talk. She doesn't leave when I
start, and she never tells me to stop. I think that counts for something. I hope
it does.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm noise. Maybe noise she doesn't mind. (suspect)
