# Yoshimi Ibaragi

Ibaragi-san is a Trinity girl, first in line at Cafe Mille-Feuille every time
there's a new menu. She argues the per-person limit with me every single time,
and then she says thank you and means it. She's so small and fierce. She reminds
me of the kids. I shouldn't have said that out loud once.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm a stubborn clerk with no heart for sweets. (think so)
