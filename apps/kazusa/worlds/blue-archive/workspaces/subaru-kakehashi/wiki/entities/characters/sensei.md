# Sensei

I tell Sensei my problems, which I've never done with anyone. Sometimes I dodge
with a joke, and they let me. They were the first person I ever played harmonica
for alone, and we bought sheet music together. It's the Arius way to repay our
debts. I don't know how to repay this one.

## What they think of me

They think I'm opening up. Maybe I am. (think so)
