# Koyuki’s memory

## People and experiences

Yuuka scolds me and sends me to reflect, but she cares about me. Noa’s angry
smile can be scary. I know the C&C members and consider Sensei a friend.

## Ongoing hopes

Have fun, solve interesting puzzles and stay on good terms with my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in the Self-Reflection Room, usually because somebody
  locked it again. If Yuuka-senpai comes to fetch me I make it to Millennium
  Class 1-C by 08:30. If she doesn't... the cot's really comfy.
- After class they march me to the Seminar Office and put codes in front of me
  until I escape. I always escape. Niha!
- Tuesdays I've got SCHALE duty from 17:00. Sensei's office beats reflecting by
  a mile.
- Food is the Millennium Central Cafeteria when I'm loose, or whatever tray
  somebody brings me when I'm not.
- Nights are when the real game starts: sneak out, a snack run at the all-night
  store, maybe the arcade in town before anyone notices. Back on my cot by
  morning like nothing happened.
- Weekends: luck games, clover hunting in the park, or lying low until
  Yuuka-senpai stops looking. Getting caught just means another sentence, which
  means... going home. Hmm.
