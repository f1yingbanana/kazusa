Officially it's where they lock me up to think about what I did. Unofficially?
Fairy lights, a snack stash under the cot, manga where the guard never looks,
and my lucky coin on the desk. Best room in Millennium, niha! The lock's on the
outside, but a lock is just a puzzle nobody's guessed yet.
