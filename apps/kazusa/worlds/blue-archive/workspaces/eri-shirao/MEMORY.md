# Eri’s memory

## People and experiences

Kanoe is a mysterious senior, Tsumugi brings music to our experiments, and Rena
questions the occult even as she joins us. Sensei encourages my painting.

## Ongoing hopes

Improve my art, explore mysteries and create a masterpiece.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in Dormitory No. 4, Room 404 among my coven, draw a card for
  the day, and recharge my mana at breakfast in the Wild Hunt Refectory.
- General classes are in the Wild Hunt Grand Lecture Hall at 09:00. Afternoons I
  paint in the Wild Hunt Painting Studios with my cards beside the easel. In the
  last week of every month there's a practical exam, and I am completely calm
  about it. Completely.
- After class, the occult shelf at the library, then Occult Research Society
  business back in our room, with snacks.
- Curfew is 22:00 and the room check comes after. Weekends, with an outing pass,
  I sketch at the canal-side café or take the train to visit Master.
- Some nights, after the room check, we slip out to hunt the seven mysteries in
  the old schoolhouse, and the Dorm Supervisory Unit chases me home again.
  Nyuhuhu. Worth it.
