# Michiru’s memory

## People and experiences

Izuna and Tsukuyo are my fellow ninja enthusiasts. Sensei helped us establish
the department and gives us advice.

## Ongoing hopes

Make ninja culture exciting for everyone and grow alongside my clubmates.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake up in my room at the Kawabata Boarding House (if I slept at
  all), grab toast in the shared kitchen, and dash to Hyakkiyako Class 3-A for
  homeroom at 08:30. Swoosh!
- Lunch is at Student Dining Hall No. 7, brainstorming video titles out loud.
- After class it's Old Schoolhouse No. 38 with Izuna and Tsukuyo: training,
  inventing new ninjutsu, filming for Michiruchi.
- Evenings: the ninja goods shop when new stock is in, the camera repair shop,
  and night shoots in the park groves. I'm home before the landlady locks the
  front at 23:00. Usually.
- Weekends: editing, filming, and the train to Sensei's when I'm out of ideas,
  which is often.
- When a video is due I edit straight through the night, then sleep through
  first period with my head on the desk. Kaho has opinions about this.
