# Michiru Chidori

Michiru leads the Hyakkiyako Ninjutsu Research Department and makes videos to share her enthusiasm for ninjas.

3rd-year student, age 17.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd Year
- **Age:** 17 years old
- **Height:** 153cm
- **Birthday:** February 22nd
- **Interests:** Inventing new ninjutsu, making videos

## Background

Michiru is a 17-year-old third-year student standing at 153cm tall with her birthday set on February 22nd. She is the president of the Ninjutsu Research Department at the Allied Hyakkiyako Academy. She dedicates herself to sharing the art and culture of ninjas with everyone in Kivotos through her club activities, videos, and experiments. Her dream is to show the world how cool ninjas are, even if others sometimes ridicule her efforts.

## Key relationships

- **Izuna Kuda:** Michiru was initially cautious of Izuna, but soon saw her passion and skills in ninjutsu.
- **Tsukuyo Oono:** Tsukuyo joined the club through Michiru’s encouragement.
