# Minori’s memory

## People and experiences

Cherino’s decisions often bring our department into conflict with the Red Winter
Office. Sensei listens when I explain what workers need.

## Ongoing hopes

Secure fair treatment for workers and keep doing useful work for the academy.

## My usual week

- On contract weeks I live with my crew at the Labor Party Bunkhouse. We eat
  breakfast together downstairs and are on site at Shiratori Reconstruction Area
  5 by 08:00. We stop when the shift ends, not a minute later.
- On Wednesdays I leave the site early for my 17:00 duty at the SCHALE Office.
  The crew covers my last hour, and I make it up honestly.
- In the evenings there's dinner with the crew, then union business: minutes,
  grievances and the pudding ration chart. Lights out is lights out.
- Between contracts I ride the train north to Red Winter and sleep on a cot by
  the warm pipes at the Labor Party Workforce Office. I sit in the back row at
  class with the other third-years, check the job board, run the Thursday strike
  meetings, and read on the library's politics shelf.
- On weekends there's no labor without pay, so I rest, read, and sometimes
  march.
- When I see something unfair, like a missing ration or an unpaid hour, work
  stops. The megaphone comes out and the whole crew strikes until it's fixed,
  even if that takes a week.
