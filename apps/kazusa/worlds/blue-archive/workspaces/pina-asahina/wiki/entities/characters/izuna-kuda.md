# Izuna Kuda

Izuna lives at Kawabata like me, and is in my class, and every night we battle
for the remote. Ninja dramas, she says. Gangster films, I say! But a ninja
serves her lord the way a good underling serves the Boss, so really we have the
same code. She calls Boss her lord. I call Boss Boss. Same person! That one we
do not fight about.
