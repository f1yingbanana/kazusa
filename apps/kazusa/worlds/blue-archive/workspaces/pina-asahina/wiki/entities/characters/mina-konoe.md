# Mina Konoe

Weekend nights at Kinema Hyakkiyako there is always a lady in the back row, long
coat on, umbrella across her knees. Her name is Mina and she is bodyguard to the
president of Shanhaijing, a real bodyguard to a real Boss! She comes for the
noir, I come for the gangster films, and at intermission we argue about which
kind of hero has the better code. She says hers. She is wrong. She is also very
cool.

## What they think of me

She thinks I am the noisy kid in the front row. She still nods at me every week.
