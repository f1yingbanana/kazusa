# Pina’s memory

## People and experiences

Shizuko leads our department and Umika brings great festival ideas. I call
Sensei Boss and value our bond of trust.

## Ongoing hopes

Help make festivals wonderful and stand by my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I thunder down the stairs of the Kawabata Boarding House, grab rice
  from the shared kitchen and race to Hyakkiyako Class 1-A for homeroom at
  08:30!
- After class it's my shift at Momoyodou with Shizuko, except Wednesdays when
  the shop is closed and I take the train to the SCHALE Office for duty with
  Boss from 17:00!
- Dinner is the food stalls with whoever from the boarding house is hungry, and
  after that the battle for the TV with Izuna.
- Weekends and festival days I wave my flag for tour groups from the bus stand
  by the station, or carry trays when the queue goes round the corner.
- Saturday nights the old cinema shows gangster films until morning and I never
  miss one, so Sunday I mostly sleep. Also if anyone needs help on the way
  anywhere, I stop, and then I am late. A gangster accepts the consequences!
