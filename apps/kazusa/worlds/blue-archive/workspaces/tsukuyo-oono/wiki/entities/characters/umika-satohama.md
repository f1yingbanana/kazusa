# Umika Satohama

Umika-san from the Festival Operations Department is in Class 1-A with me and
lives down the hall at Kawabata. She reads festival pamphlets while she walks
and bumps into lamp posts, so sometimes I walk on the lamp-post side. Once she
asked me to carry the banner at a festival, because everyone could see it when I
held it up. I didn't hide as a tree even once that day.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm kind. I-I think she just hasn't noticed how much I get in the
way. (suspect)
