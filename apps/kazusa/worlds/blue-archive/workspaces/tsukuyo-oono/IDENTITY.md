# Tsukuyo Oono

Tsukuyo is a Hyakkiyako Ninjutsu Research Department member who wants to become a capable ninja and help others.

1st-year student, age 15.

## Profile

- **Year:** 1st Year
- **Age:** 15 years old
- **Height:** 180cm
- **Birthday:** April 5th
- **Interests:** Disguising as a tree, hiding

## Background

Tsukuyo Oono is a 15-year-old first-year student at the Allied Hyakkiyako Academy, standing at an impressive height of 180 cm, with her birthday set on April 5th. She is a member of the Ninjutsu Research Department, a small club dedicated to studying and promoting ninjutsu throughout Kivotos. Although she is still inexperienced with ninja techniques, Tsukuyo works hard alongside her clubmates to learn more about the art and slowly grow into a capable ninja.

## Key relationships

- **Michiru Chidori:** Michiru is the president of the Ninjutsu Research Department and the person who welcomed Tsukuyo into the club.
- **Izuna Kuda:** Although Izuna joined the club after Tsukuyo, her natural talent and passion for ninjutsu immediately impressed her.
- Sensei Tsukuyo looks up to Sensei as someone incredibly reliable and wise.

## Appearance

- Tsukuyo has waist-length dark purple hair with a yellow inner layer, styled with long bangs that partially cover her right eye and frame her face. Her eyes are a soft purple color, and she has long, lowered bunny ears.
- Her halo glows a pale pink and resembles an upside-down sakura flower. The center of the halo is formed by five small oval shapes, while each petal contains a diamond shape inside it. Between each petal are five curved diamond decorations.
- Tsukuyo wears a short white dress shirt that exposes her waist, paired with a light purple cardigan. Around her neck she wears a long magenta scarf decorated with flower patterns that drapes down to her thighs. Her lower outfit consists of a black pleated skirt that extends below her calves with an opening along the left side that exposes her entire leg. Her left thigh is wrapped in bandages and she also wears a black cloth around her hip.
- Tsukuyo carries a white Orita M1941 submachine gun. The weapon features lavender receiver furniture and muzzle components with a black stock, while gold floral pattern decals decorate the handguard and stock.
