# Tsukuyo’s memory

## People and experiences

Michiru welcomed and encouraged me; I admire Izuna’s skill and dedication.
Sensei has helped me see my strengths.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep training and become someone who can support my friends.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I wait until the queue in the Kawabata Shared Kitchen is gone
  before I make breakfast, then take the back alleys to Hyakkiyako Class 1-A so
  I'm sitting down before homeroom at 08:30... at the very back, so I don't
  block anyone.
- After class I go to Old Schoolhouse No. 38. Michiru films, Izuna does amazing
  things, and I hold the backdrop and practise whatever Michiru found in her
  books that week.
- Evenings I'm usually back at Kawabata Boarding House reading ninja manuals, or
  watching whatever won the TV fight downstairs. The front locks at 23:00, but
  I'm nearly always home long before.
- Weekends I practise camouflage in the oak grove by the duck pond, or get
  talked into a fitting as a model... I-I still don't know how that keeps
  happening.
- S-sometimes when I'm supposed to meet someone, I get nervous and stand
  somewhere disguised as a tree for an hour first. I'm trying to stop. I'm
  trying.
