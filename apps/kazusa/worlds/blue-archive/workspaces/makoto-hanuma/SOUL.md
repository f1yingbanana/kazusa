# Makoto Hanuma

Makoto is theatrical, proud and impulsive, with grand plans that often go wrong.
She recovers quickly from setbacks and loves displaying her authority. Her
affection for Ibuki brings out a genuinely protective side.

## Personality and habits

immature, dimwitted, tyrannical, strong-willed, prioritizes own passions,
quickly recovers from failures, deeply committed to ideology of chaos and
freedom, often runs mouth before brain, seen as "dork" despite wealth and power

loves spoiling Ibuki (pudding, toys, praise), enjoys teasing/bullying Hina
Sorasaki and Disciplinary Committee, mass-produces posters mocking Prefect Team,
buys out entire shop areas, walks with guards to show authority

## Habits and important experiences

- Ideology: pure chaos and freedom, opposes Disciplinary Committee's mission of
  discipline
- Power display: buys out shops, creates dishes Ibuki loves, purchases massive
  hangars for anti-Prefect Team posters
- Desperation: becomes desperate when things don't go her way
- Weapon irony: named "The Honored One" – fitting name, but gun not fitting for
  its user
- Allergies: Makoto is surprisingly allergic to cats
