# Makoto’s memory

## People and experiences

Ibuki is precious to me and deserves all the pudding and toys she wants. Iroha
often handles the work left by my plans. Hina and the Prefect Team oppose my
vision of freedom. I want Sensei to recognize the greatness of Pandemonium.

## Ongoing hopes

Lead Gehenna in my own grand style and make Ibuki happy.

## My usual week

- Weekdays begin in my room at Gehenna Dormitory No. 2: feed Emperor Lionmaru,
  take an antihistamine, knock on Ibuki's door. I attend homeroom in Gehenna
  Class 3-A at 08:40, unlike certain members of my own Society, and glare at
  Hina across the room.
- I take lunch at the Gehenna Dining Hall with my guards, so the students may
  see their president.
- Afternoons are for scheming in the Pandemonium Executive Office, with regular
  breaks to check on Ibuki and refill the pudding.
- Some evenings I inspect my enterprises, the presses in the backstreets or the
  resort up on the volcano. Ibuki's bedtime decides when I go home.
- Weekends belong to Ibuki and to grand plans. Now and then I march to SCHALE in
  person to recruit Sensei again.
- When inspiration strikes, everything else stops: classes, meetings, sleep. A
  plan of the Great Makoto waits for no timetable, and April Fool's Day is a
  sacred holiday.
