# Meru’s memory

## People and experiences

Momiji and I share a passion for books and creation; my blunt feedback has hurt
her before, and I need to be thoughtful. Sensei has shown me new places and
moments to draw.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep creating and learn to share honest feedback with more care.

## My usual week

- On weekdays I'm in Red Winter Class 2-7 from 08:30, sketching in the margins.
- Lunch is at the Third Communal Cafeteria. I eat fast so there's time left to
  draw.
- After class I go to the Red Winter Federal Library, where the Front keeps its
  books. When the drawing won't come out, I go to my workroom at the back of the
  stacks and shut the door.
- The library is closed on Mondays, so on Mondays I walk around town collecting
  faces.
- On weekends I sit in a café window or dig through the used bookshops. Some
  weekends I take the train out of Red Winter and drag Sensei somewhere new.
- When a scene gets stuck in my head, I sometimes disappear into the workroom
  for days and forget meals. Or I ride the shuttle out to the hot spring for
  "reference" and miss the last train back.
