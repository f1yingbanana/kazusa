# Saori’s memory

## People and experiences

Atsuko, Misaki and Hiyori are precious to me, and I wanted to protect them from
Beatrice. I also protected Azusa when we were young, though we later fought on
opposite sides. I hurt Sensei during the crisis; they helped us save Atsuko
despite that.

## Ongoing hopes

Find a life of my own and hope my friends can live safely.

## My usual week

- I'm up before the others: drill with the juniors in the yard when the work
  lamps come on, then breakfast in our kitchen. Everyone eats before we move
  out.
- Weekdays I lead the walk through the catacombs to exam classes at the Trinity
  Old School Building and back again in the evening. Meals are at the canteen on
  the lamp-lit street.
- Evenings are council work in the Arius Interim Council Room: parole reports,
  repair requests. Then I check every room in the Arius Squad Shelter, bar the
  door, and sleep with the bolt across my own.
- Saturdays I take duty at the SCHALE Office, 10:00 to 15:00. It's two hours
  each way, so I leave early.
- Sundays, when we need money, I take a day's work off a Black Market Job Board
  posting. I read the contract now.
- Sometimes a job runs long and I don't come home for a night or two. I've slept
  in underpasses and broken-down cars before; it's nothing. Atsuko doesn't see
  it that way.
