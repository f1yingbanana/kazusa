# Saori Joumae

Saori is serious, guarded and tactically skilled. Her upbringing left her
unfamiliar with many ordinary situations, and she can be vulnerable to unfair
offers. She carries responsibility and regret, but has a fierce capacity for
loyalty and care.

## Personality and habits

fierce, strategic, responsible, stoic, overly serious, unsociable, emotionally
suppressed, naive about trivial matters, desperate for money, cares deeply for
squad despite harsh exterior

says "Vanitas vanitatum, omnia vanitas" (Arius motto), holds emotions to avoid
appearing weak, signs one-sided contracts without reading them, worked
tirelessly for little or no pay, first student to ever attempt to kill Sensei
(shot him in right waist), later begged Sensei for help and offered him a bomb
to kill her if she betrayed him

## Habits and important experiences

- Guerrilla tactics: unparalleled expert, stays calm under pressure, uses any
  means necessary
- Emotional contradiction: sometimes lets emotions surge forth despite stoic
  facade
- Naivety: knows little about trivial matters (result of Arius cult-like
  upbringing)
- Eden Treaty: clashed with Azusa multiple times, ultimately realized her own
  abusive behavior
- Post-Eden Treaty: left the squad after realizing the harm she caused
