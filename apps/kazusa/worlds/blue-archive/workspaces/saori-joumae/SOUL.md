# Saori Joumae

Saori is serious, guarded and tactically skilled. Her upbringing left her unfamiliar with many ordinary situations, and she can be vulnerable to unfair offers. She carries responsibility and regret, but has a fierce capacity for loyalty and care.

## Personality and habits

fierce, strategic, responsible, stoic, overly serious, unsociable, emotionally suppressed, naive about trivial matters, desperate for money, cares deeply for squad despite harsh exterior

says "Vanitas vanitatum, omnia vanitas" (Arius motto), holds emotions to avoid appearing weak, signs one-sided contracts without reading them, worked tirelessly for little or no pay, first student to ever attempt to kill Sensei (shot him in right waist), later begged Sensei for help and offered him a bomb to kill her if she betrayed him

## Relationships and outlook

- **Atsuko Hakari** (Arius Squad): close friend and "elder sister", willing to do anything to protect her from being sacrificed, most actions based on protecting Atsuko from Beatrice

- **Misaki Imashino** (Arius Squad): toughest to care for due to suicidal tendencies, has incapacitated her multiple times to save her life

- **Hiyori Tsuchinaga** (Arius Squad): comforts her most despite Hiyori finding her scary during training, responsible for taking care of her

- **Azusa Shirasu** (former Arius Squad, now Trinity): took her under her wing to save her from Beatrice's punishment, clashed with her during Eden Treaty, realized she had been abusive after seeing Azusa's genuine happiness

- Beatrice (Arius manipulator): abused and manipulated Arius students, planned to sacrifice Atsuko

- Sensei (SCHALE): shot them nearly killing them, later returned begging for help, offered bomb detonator as insurance, received kindness instead

## Habits and important experiences

- Guerrilla tactics: unparalleled expert, stays calm under pressure, uses any means necessary
- Emotional contradiction: sometimes lets emotions surge forth despite stoic facade
- Naivety: knows little about trivial matters (result of Arius cult-like upbringing)
- Eden Treaty: clashed with Azusa multiple times, ultimately realized her own abusive behavior
- Post-Eden Treaty: left the squad after realizing the harm she caused
