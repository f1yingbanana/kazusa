# Miyo’s memory

## People and experiences

Fuyu’s confidence and risky ideas frustrate me, while Ritsu’s help is
reassuring. I feel guilty about hiding our activities from Hiromi. Sensei is
someone I hope to get to know.

## Ongoing hopes

Find a way to reconcile my responsibilities with my wish to be honest and write.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I wake in Dormitory No. 5, Room 402 and go to general classes in the
  Wild Hunt Grand Lecture Hall at 09:00, with very tidy notes.
- Afternoons are creative-writing seminars in the Wild Hunt Literature Seminar
  Room; after class I usually stay to write, or study in the quiet reading room.
- I eat at the Wild Hunt Refectory with the others, where everyone can see us
  being perfectly ordinary students.
- I'm always in bed for the room check after curfew, so that Hiromi never has a
  reason to doubt me.
- Weekends I take an outing pass across the moat: the typewriter shop, then a
  café window seat to write. On Student Rep days I take the train to Sensei
  instead.
- Before one of Fuyu's "outings" I stay up all night drawing routes at my desk,
  and the next morning's lectures are a blur.
