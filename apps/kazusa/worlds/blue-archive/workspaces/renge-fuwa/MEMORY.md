# Renge’s memory

## People and experiences

Kikyou is my oldest friend; we argue but show up for each other. I admire Nagusa
and care about Yukari, even when I have made mistakes trying to protect her.
Sensei has listened to my worries.

## Ongoing hopes

Train well and make meaningful memories with my friends.

## My usual week

- Every weekday starts with a run along the river from my house, then drills at
  06:00 in the Hyakkaryouran Courtyard Garden. Nobody is late. If somebody is,
  we both run laps.
- Then homeroom in Hyakkiyako Class 2-A at 08:30, and lunch in Student Dining
  Hall No. 7, the biggest set they have.
- After class: the evening meeting in the Hyakkaryouran Council Hall and patrol,
  then youth activities if I can drag anyone along. Karaoke, food stalls, the
  light-novel corner at the bookshop. Nights I read in bed far too late.
- Weekends are training in the morning and serious youth research in the
  afternoon, and now and then a visit to my Mentor in D.U.
- Every so often I sign up with some other club on a trial basis, chlorella,
  shogi, rakugo, etiquette, and throw everything I have into it for a week. That
  isn't running away from the council. It's research!
