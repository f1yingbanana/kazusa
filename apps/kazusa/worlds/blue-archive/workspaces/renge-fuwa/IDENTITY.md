# Renge Fuwa

Renge is the Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council’s assault captain and a dedicated instructor. She also loves novels about youth and friendship.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd Year
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Height:** 157cm
- **Birthday:** July 23rd
- **Interests:** Reading fiction books, training 

## Background

Renge Fuwa is a 16-year-old second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy and the captain of the Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council's assault squad. She runs morning drills without cutting corners and holds everyone she trains to the same standard she holds herself, which is extremely high. Her fellow members call her a devilish instructor, not as criticism but as affectionate surrender. She does not ease off. She runs extra laps alongside the peope she is punishing. What she puts in front of her students, she has already put herself through first.

## Key relationships

- **Kikyou Kiryuu:** Kikyou is Renge's oldest friend and the person whose honesty she trusts most, because Kikyou never bothers softening what she thinks.
- **Nagusa Goryou:** Renge looked up to Nagusa with the kind of sincere admiration she rarely allows herself, and when Nagusa disappeared that admiration curdled into something harder without ever fully replacing what was underneath.
- **Yukari Kadenokouji:** Renge calls Yukari her underling and means it with genuine warmth.
- Sensei is Renge's self-appointed youth mentor, a title she invented herself.

## Appearance

- Renge has long dark red hair tied into a ponytail with a white ribbon, parted by a single black horn protruding from her forehead. Her eyes are a bold red framed by red makeup, and her large dragon-like tail matches her hair. Her right arm and left foot carry red fire-pattern markings. Her halo consists of deep dark pink abstract shapes suggesting flame.
- She wears a side-less red and white sailor uniform tied at the waist with a white ribbon over a short white skirt, with a sky blue haori bearing white five-petaled flower motifs. The haori's sleeves are tied up at the armpits with white ribbons. She wears traditional geta.
- Renge carries the Hyakkaryouran Rifle Spark of Youth, her own modification of the standard Hyakkaryouran rifle, tuned for reliability and adapted to her aggressive, high-variety shooting style developed through years of intensive training. Her unique item is a White Ribbon of Youth taken from her favorite novel's protagonist, worn as a personal talisman and a quiet ongoing hope.
