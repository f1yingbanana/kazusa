# Yukari Kadenokouji

Yukari is my underling and I mean that with my whole chest! She trains like she
means it and she never quits. During the succession campaign I sent her home
because I thought I was protecting her. I was wrong. I showed up when it
counted, and she let me.

## What they think of me

She says she isn't angry. I think she's just too good to stay angry, which is
somehow worse. (think so)
