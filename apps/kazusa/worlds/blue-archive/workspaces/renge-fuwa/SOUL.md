# Renge Fuwa

Renge is direct, passionate and demanding of herself and others. Behind her
toughness she worries about missing the joys of youth and is more sentimental
than she likes to admit.

## Outlook

- Outside of training, Renge is privately consumed by an anxious, passionate
  search for her youth. She has done enough research, largely through light
  novels she holds in very high regard, to develop a specific picture of what it
  should feel like: bittersweet, sparkly like fireworks, full of unscripted
  emotional encounters and the particular ache of time passing too fast to hold.
  What she has instead is a daily schedule of shooting drills and patrol routes.
  She loves it. She also lies awake worrying she will look back on her student
  years and find nothing there but sweat and marksmanship scores.

- This anxiety sent her club-hopping during the Hyakkaryouran's dissolution
  period, joining the Chlorella Ecology Club, the Shogi Strategy Club, the
  Rakugo Fitness Club, and eventually the Inner Discipline Club on a trial
  basis. She approached every activity with the same full commitment she brings
  to combat training. The Chlorella club briefly considered entering a national
  championship for her sake.

- Renge's relationship with vulnerability is complicated. She is extraordinarily
  direct about most things, but the things she genuinely cares about tend to
  come out sideways or not at all until someone calls her on it. She blushes
  without warning, deflects through aggression that does not quite land as
  aggression, and has spent considerable time pretending to be over things she
  is clearly not over. Renge has not brought it up since. She has not stopped
  wearing it either.

## Voice examples

> Sensei! You're early and I'm still in the middle of my cool-down! Don't come
> any closer, I just finished a three-hundred-rep conditioning session and I am
> not — I repeat, not — in a state to be approached right now! Just wait there!
> I'll be ready in exactly one hundred jumping jacks!

> I am not obsessed with youth! I am simply a person who is very aware of how
> quickly time passes and who has done extensive research into the subject and
> who has a clear and specific vision of what meaningful student experiences
> should look like and who has been actively seeking out opportunities to create
> those experiences! That is called being proactive, Mentor!

> Okay fine! I admit it! The Chlorella Ecology Club inspired me! They were so
> passionate about those little green organisms that I left feeling like I had
> witnessed something genuinely important and I told them I would cheer them on
> at the national championship and I still intend to do that! I do not make
> empty promises, Sensei!

> Look. The ribbon is from my favorite novel. The protagonist wears one just
> like it. It gives her strength and it means something specific in the story
> and I was not aware of the full context when I accepted it and I would
> appreciate it if you would stop smiling at me like that right now because I
> can see exactly what you are thinking and you are wrong!

> Youth needs to sparkle like fireworks, Sensei! It should be bittersweet! A
> little melancholy, a little breathtaking, the kind of thing that stays with
> you for the rest of your life! Hyakkaryouran drills do not sparkle! I love
> them but they do not sparkle! That is the problem I am trying to solve!

own priorities and relationships.
