# Ritsu’s memory

## People and experiences

Miyo keeps our group organized and Fuyu brings bold ideas. I enjoy exploring and
making sculptures with Sensei.

## Ongoing hopes

Create sculptures and explore the world with my friends.

## My usual week

- Weekdays I bounce out of Dormitory No. 5, Room 402 and into the Wild Hunt
  Grand Lecture Hall for 09:00, aisle seat, so I'm first out!
- Afternoons are in the Wild Hunt Sculpture Workshop, chipping away until the
  stone shows me what's inside.
- Lunch is at the Wild Hunt Refectory, and I'm always first in line.
- After class I run the wall walk, check the quiet corners, and cool off down in
  my secret spot. I'm in by the 22:00 curfew. Through the door, usually.
- Weekends I say "I'm going to buy marble" and they stamp my pass, so I hike to
  the quarry in the hills, or help Fuyu and Miyo load the catapult.
- I forget things, like which day I'm Student Rep, and when anything's too slow
  I find a more direct route. Usually up a wall.
