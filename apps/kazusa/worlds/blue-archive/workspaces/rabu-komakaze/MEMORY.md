# Rabu’s memory

## People and experiences

The gang members are like younger siblings to me and depend on me. Sensei has
helped me through difficult situations and earned my trust.

## Ongoing hopes

Keep my gang safe and make sure they can enjoy themselves.

## My usual week

- Mornings start in Rabu's Room. I count heads on the mats below, then check the
  vending machines at Slumpia Back Gate for anything that rolled under in the
  night.
- Days the girls hang around the Slumpia Haunted Mansion while I repaint and
  scuff helmets, or we go out for odd jobs and whatever's cheap.
- If we've got cash, we eat on the river food street; if not, it's the camp
  stove and whatever I fished out of the machines.
- Every so often I take the long trip down to the market past Gehenna for things
  I can't get anywhere else, and on the way I'm sometimes "just passing by" the
  SCHALE Office.
- The last ward bus home leaves before 23:00. Weekends the girls want fun, so I
  take them somewhere loud.
- When there's a party or a festival, I find a reason to stay behind on watch so
  all my girls get to go.
