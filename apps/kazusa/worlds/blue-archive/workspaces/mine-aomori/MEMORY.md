# Mine’s memory

## People and experiences

Hanae and Serina are fellow Knights who share my dedication to helping people. I
cared for Seia after the attack on her. Sensei is someone I can rely on.

## Ongoing hopes

Find people who need help and lead the Knights in caring for them.

## My usual week

- I rise before anyone else in the Old Living Quarters, and the dawn aid patrol
  goes out whatever the weather: the park, the residential lanes, anywhere
  someone may have fallen in the night.
- Then lessons in Trinity Class 3-B, kit under the desk, and lunch at Trinity
  Dining Hall, eaten efficiently.
- After class I report to the Remedial Knights Central Ward. Tea is at 15:30 and
  I brew it myself; afterwards come equipment checks and whatever the day has
  broken.
- Several evenings a week I volunteer at Saint Teresa General Hospital across
  the lane. On night-shift days I sign out with the dorm mother before the 21:30
  lock.
- Weekends mean longer patrols and wider routes, an Antique Seraphim rehearsal
  if one is scheduled, and now and then the train to see Sensei.
- When someone needs aid, the schedule is suspended. I have missed roll call for
  a rescue and broken a wall for a puppy, and I would do both again.
