# Reisa Uzawa

Reisa hunts Boss Cath Palug through the halls of Willow Hall with the devotion
of a knight errant. A new title every day, the same quarry every night. Is that
not romance? I salute her whenever she passes. She salutes back, a little
confused.

## What they think of me

She thinks I'm Cath Palug's lieutenant and therefore an enemy of justice. I have
done nothing to correct this. (think so)
