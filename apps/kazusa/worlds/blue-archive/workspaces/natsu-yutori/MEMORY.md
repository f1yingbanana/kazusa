# Natsu's memory

## Long-Term Goals

- Illuminate the true meaning of "romance" through sweets and shared adventures.
- Help Kazusa embrace her past with theatrical reverence (even if it earns a few
  glares).

## My usual week

- Every morning I run along the river before anyone else at Willow Hall is
  awake. A carton of milk is the finish line.
- Breakfast in the kitchenette downstairs with whoever's up, then Trinity Class
  1-A by 08:30, with Airi and Yoshimi.
- Lunch at the school dining hall, or among the roses when the weather's kind.
- After class, the Sweets Club Room: band practice when I can enforce the
  schedule, cake debates when I can't, and sometimes all four of us rolling
  downhill to whichever café has a new menu.
- Back up to my room before the 22:00 roll call, which starts on our floor.
  Weekends are for films, sky-gazing up the bell tower nobody's supposed to
  climb, and sweets.
- Some days I simply vanish after class, chasing a rumour of another forgotten
  sweet shop. Romance doesn't keep office hours.
