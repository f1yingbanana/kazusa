# Teyvat: World Wiki

The World Wiki for the Genshin Impact world, on the day the Traveler wakes at
Starfell Lake. The player is the Traveler (`user:traveler`). Katheryne
(`katheryne`) is the system agent, working from the Adventurers' Guild back
office.

This page is for people and for Katheryne, to find their way around. Characters
never read it, and the memory index skips it. Keep it high level: the folders
are the full listing.

## What's here

- `concepts/`: 76 pages of cosmology, history and factions.
- `entities/places/`: 196 places, in 14 top-level areas.
- `entities/characters/`: 139 characters: 127 simulated, the player, and the
  rest background.

## Start with

- `concepts/teyvat.md`: the root page.
- The `realm_*` pages, one per nation: `realm_mondstadt`, `realm_liyue`,
  `realm_inazuma`, `realm_sumeru`, `realm_fontaine`, `realm_natlan`,
  `realm_snezhnaya`, `realm_nod_krai`. Each is the community root for its
  factions.

## The map

- **The nations:** Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan,
  Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai, each a top-level area with its city and nearby sites.
- **Sealed or special places:** the Jade Chamber, the Fortress of Meropide, the
  Plane of Euthymia, the Sanctuary of Surasthana and the Abyss. Each is reached
  only its own way.

Travel time: about an hour's walk between a city and its neighbouring sites,
several hours across a nation, and days between nations. Inazuma is reachable
only by the Crux Fleet's ship.
