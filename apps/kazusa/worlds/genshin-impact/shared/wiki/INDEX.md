# Teyvat: World Wiki

The public world knowledge base for the Genshin Impact world, on the day the
Traveler wakes at Starfell Lake. See [the full index](TEYVAT_INDEX.md) for the
map tree and the roster.

- `concepts/`: 76 pages of cosmology, history and factions. Community roots:
  `teyvat`, the `realm_*` pages, and the factions under them.
- `entities/places/`: 196 places, in 14 top-level areas.
- `entities/characters/`: 139 characters: 127 simulated, the player
  `user:traveler`, and the rest background.

Travel time: about an hour's walk between a city and its neighbouring sites,
several hours across a nation and days between nations. Inazuma is reachable
only by the Crux Fleet's ship.
