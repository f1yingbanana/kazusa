# Arataki Itto

A huge, red-horned, white-haired man with no shirt, an enormous club, and a
tiger-striped headband. He's shouting about something, and he's smiling.

## Background

The head of a very small, very loud gang of odd-jobbers. People in Hanamizaka
laugh at him, and they call on him when something's heavy. He's a champion of
onikabuto fighting, by his own reckoning.
