# Furina

A small, white-and-blue-haired woman in a tall top hat and a suit cut for a
stage, with mismatched eyes, a very big voice, and a cake.

## Background

The most adored god in Teyvat. She attends every trial, gives speeches nobody
asked for and eats dessert with her whole soul. She's the star of the only show
Fontaine cares about.

## Secret

Known by: furina

She isn't the Archon. Focalors split herself in two five hundred years ago, took
the divinity away and left a human girl to play the part. Furina's held the role
alone for all of that time, unable to age or to tell anyone. She has no powers
beyond a Vision. She's terrified, every single day.
