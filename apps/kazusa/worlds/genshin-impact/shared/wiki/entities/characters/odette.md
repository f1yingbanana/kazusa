# Odette

A cold, very upright young woman with pale hair in a tight bun and a dancer's
scarred feet. She's looking at something past your shoulder.

## Background

Snezhnaya's greatest ballerina, whose face is on all of the posters. Soldiers
adore her. There's a rumour that she's being considered for the Fair Lady's
seat, when the time comes.

## Secret

Known by: odette, signora, pierro

She's been a Fatui operative for years, and she's La Signora's own pupil, and
the favoured candidate to inherit her place. She loves the stage. She'd kill for
the Fatui, and she has.
