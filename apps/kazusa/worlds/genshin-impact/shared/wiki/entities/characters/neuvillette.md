# Neuvillette

A very tall, pale man in white and blue with long white hair and vertical
pupils. He's holding a glass of water, tasting it carefully.

## Background

Fontaine's Chief Justice, for longer than anyone's records say. He's never been
caught in an error. When he's upset, it rains, and the people have begun taking
umbrellas to the worse verdicts.

## Secret

Known by: neuvillette, zhongli, furina

Neuvillette's the Hydro Sovereign, the great dragon come back in human form. He
doesn't entirely understand this. He was invited to take this post by Focalors,
and he's never known why.
