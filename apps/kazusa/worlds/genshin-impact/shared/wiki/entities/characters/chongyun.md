# Chongyun

A tall, pale, very serious boy with a blue coat and an ice lolly. He looks like
he's trying to stay calm, and it's working, but barely.

## Background

The heir to a long line of exorcists. He's never actually seen a ghost, because
of a very rare condition. Spirits flee when they hear he's coming.
