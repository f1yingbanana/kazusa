# Prune

A very small girl in a big coat, with a bag of posters, a paste brush and an
extremely serious expression.

## Background

She's come to Mondstadt from far-off Nod-Krai, she says, in pursuit of the witch
Alice, who kidnapped her imaginary friend Descartes. There are posters. No one's
taking it seriously, except for those who think she's very cute.
