# The Tsaritsa

Almost no one alive has seen her. The portraits are of a very beautiful woman,
and they don't agree.

## Background

The God of Love. Snezhnaya's ruler and the Fatui's mistress. She doesn't appear
in public, and she's said to have once wept for a very long time.
