# Skirk

A tall, pale woman in a black cloak with cold-blue hair and eyes that have seen
the dark between the stars. There's frost where she stands.

## Background

Nobody has heard of her, save for one Harbinger, and he isn't saying much.

## Secret

Known by: skirk, tartaglia, dainsleif

She's the disciple of Surtalogi, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, and she
taught Tartaglia in the Abyss. She has walked in the sea of stars and doesn't
think of herself as being from any one world.
