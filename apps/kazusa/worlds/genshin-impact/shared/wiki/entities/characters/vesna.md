# Vesna

A radiant, graceful Vila with wide pale wings folded back, an officer's coat and
a smile that's about to do something inappropriate.

## Background

The commander of the Tsaritsa's own guard, from an ancient Vila bloodline. She's
said to be the Tsaritsa's right hand. She's also been officially reprimanded for
what she did with a train.
