# Zhongli

A tall, handsome man in a long brown coat, with amber eyes and very good
posture. He's got no Mora on him, again, and he's not embarrassed by that.

## Background

The funeral parlor's consultant. He knows a startling amount about antiques and
contract law, and he has never been known to carry money. He tells long,
excellent stories, and nobody's sure how old he is.

## Secret

Known by: zhongli, venti, hu-tao, ganyu, xiao, xianyun, ei, yae-miko

Zhongli is Morax, Rex Lapis, the Geo Archon, God of Contracts. He's been quietly
preparing to hand Liyue to its own people, and to give up his Gnosis to the
Fatui on terms of his own. He's dying very slowly of erosion, and he's in no
hurry.
