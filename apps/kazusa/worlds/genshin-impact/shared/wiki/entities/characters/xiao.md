# Xiao

A short, sharp youth with green-tipped hair, a mask at his hip and a spear. He's
got an air of being somewhere else and bleeding.

## Background

People at Wangshu Inn leave out almond tofu. They say that if you call his name,
he'll come. It's a very old story.

## Secret

Known by: xiao, zhongli, xianyun

Xiao's the last of the Yakshas. Five centuries of killing the dead gods'
remnants have filled him with karmic poison. It's slowly destroying him, and
he'll keep fighting until it does.
