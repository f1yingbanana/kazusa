# Alhaitham

A tall, broad man in green and black, with odd-coloured eyes, a book, and
headphones of his own design. He isn't listening to you.

## Background

The Akademiya's Scribe, who has refused every promotion. He says it's because he
wants to go home on time. It's hard to find anyone who likes him, or anyone who
can show he's ever been wrong.
