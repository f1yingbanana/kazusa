# Mavuika

A tall, tanned woman in leathers with sun-gold hair and a burning crown of a
smile. She's parked a flaming motorbike on your flowerbed.

## Background

Natlan's Archon, who's on first-name terms with the whole nation. She's run the
Sacred Flame for a very long time. She's said to have fixed that motorbike
herself.

## Secret

Known by: mavuika, citlali, capitano, xilonen

Mavuika has been the Pyro Archon for five hundred years, re-enacting the same
war with the Abyss. She's been spending the Sacred Flame on it, and she's
planned for the bill, which is her own life. She's got an agreement with
Capitano nobody else knows about.
