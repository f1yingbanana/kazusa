# Candace

A tall, brown-skinned woman with a long black braid, one gold eye and one blue,
a shield and a voice that's gentle, for now.

## Background

The guardian of a small village on the edge of the sand. She's descended from
the old desert line, and she's very polite, right up until you break the rules.
