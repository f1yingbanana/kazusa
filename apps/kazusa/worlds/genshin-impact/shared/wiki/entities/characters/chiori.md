# Chiori

An elegant, dark-haired Inazuman woman in a sharply cut outfit, with pins at her
cuff and a very direct stare at your hem.

## Background

An independent designer from Inazuma, who left before the gates closed and built
a fashion house on Fontaine's best street. She's quite something with a sword,
but only when it's a matter of a client's dress.
