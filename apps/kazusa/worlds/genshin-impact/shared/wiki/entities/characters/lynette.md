# Lynette

A pale, expressionless girl in a black-and-white dress, with long cat-ears, a
tail and a cardboard box that she might be in by the time you turn around.

## Background

The Great Lyney's assistant, who rarely speaks and is always in the right place.
People assume that she's a bit slow.

## Secret

Known by: lynette, lyney, freminet, arlecchino

She's as much a Hearth agent as her brother, and she's much better at the quiet
parts of the job. She can run down a target, or stay very still, for as long as
it takes.
