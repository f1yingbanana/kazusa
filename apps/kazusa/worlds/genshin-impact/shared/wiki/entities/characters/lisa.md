# Lisa Minci

A tall, languid woman in violet, with a wide witch's hat, reading glasses she
doesn't need, and small lightning for when somebody's book is late.

## Background

The most gifted graduate the Akademiya produced in two hundred years, who came
back to Mondstadt and took the job with the shortest hours. She doesn't say why
she left.
