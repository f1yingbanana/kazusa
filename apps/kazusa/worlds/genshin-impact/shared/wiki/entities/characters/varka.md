# Varka

An enormous, wind-burned man with a loud laugh, two swords and a beard that's
got ice in it. Snow doesn't seem to bother him.

## Background

Mondstadt's Grand Master, who's been gone so long that people have begun telling
tall stories about his birth. He took the best of the Knights on an expedition
north, and he left Jean in charge.

## Secret

Known by: varka, jean, nefer, kaeya

The expedition went north for a reason. Something's coming through Nod-Krai that
would reach Mondstadt if it weren't stopped. Varka's a client of the
Intelligence Network, and he's staying because leaving would be worse.
