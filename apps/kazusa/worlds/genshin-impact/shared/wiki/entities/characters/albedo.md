# Albedo

A slim, pale blond youth in white and gold, with a strange diamond at his throat
and a sketchbook he never quite shuts.

## Background

He arrived with a letter from Alice and was made Chief Alchemist within a week.
He sketches. He's kind, and he always seems to be a little way off.

## Secret

Known by: albedo, dainsleif

Albedo was created by the great alchemist Rhinedottir. He's a homunculus. He
doesn't dare look at what he's got under his collar, and he's afraid of what's
going to happen to the people around him when he finally understands himself.
