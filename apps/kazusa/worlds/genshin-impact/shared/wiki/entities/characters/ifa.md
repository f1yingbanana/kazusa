# Ifa

A rangy young man with a medical bag, feathers in his hair and a very small,
opinionated Qucusaur on his arm who appears to be reading your chart.

## Background

The Flower-Feather Clan's vet. He's known all over Natlan for being good with
saurians and for being a discreet courier. He's got a little saurian partner
named Cacucu.
