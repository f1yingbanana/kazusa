# Ineffa

A tall, neat, silver-haired automaton in a maid's uniform with very visible
joints. She's holding a tea tray in one hand and a crackling spear in the other.

## Background

Aino's creation, and the Craftshop's housekeeper. She's polite to customers,
she's very strong, and she's got a very odd way of pausing before she answers.

## Secret

Known by: ineffa, aino, mavuika

Her core came from Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk, the Eleventh Sovereign Lord, a
part that wanted to give up an ancient vendetta. Ineffa doesn't remember much
about it. It remembers more about her.
