# Kamisato Ayaka

A very pretty, composed girl with a pale blue ponytail and a white fan, whose
manners are so good that it's hard to tell how she's doing.

## Background

The Commission's public face. The people adore her, and they call her the
Shirasagi Himegimi. She's never been seen to make a mistake.
