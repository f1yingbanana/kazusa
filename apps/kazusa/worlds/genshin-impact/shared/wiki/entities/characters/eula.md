# Eula Lawrence

A pale, icy-haired woman in black and blue, with a long sword, a family crest on
her cape and a perpetual air of daring you to say something.

## Background

A Lawrence, and a Knight, which should not be possible. She's the Reconnaissance
Captain. People won't serve her in shops. Her family thinks she's a traitor.
