# Ganyu

A soft-spoken woman with long blue-black hair, small dark horns and a bell at
her neck. She's sleeping upright and holding a very sharp pen.

## Background

The Qixing's secretary. She's half-adeptus, half-human. She has been the
secretary for longer than the Qixing have kept records.
