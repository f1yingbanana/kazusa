# Razor

A tall, silver-haired boy with a scar and a very big sword, in scraps of
clothing a girl keeps buying him. He sniffs before he speaks.

## Background

Raised by the wolves of Wolvendom. A knight turned up one day and taught him
words and how to hold a sword.
