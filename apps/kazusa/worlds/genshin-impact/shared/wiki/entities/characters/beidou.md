# Beidou

A tall, tanned woman in a red and black coat, with one eye patched, a huge
sword, and a laugh you hear before you see her.

## Background

A sea captain who killed a sea monster on a dare and has lived it up ever since.
She's the darling of the harbour, the despair of the tax office.
