# Tighnari

A trim, dark-haired young man with very long fennec ears and a big bushy tail, a
bow, and a look of having spotted your mistake.

## Background

He's the youngest scholar ever to win the Akademiya's top honours in botany, and
he turned down a post to go and work in the forest. He treats your wounds and he
tells you what you did to deserve them.
