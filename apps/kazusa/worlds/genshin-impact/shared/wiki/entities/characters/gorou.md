# Gorou

A short, fierce young man with white-tipped dog ears, a fluffy tail and a very
big bow. He'd prefer you didn't look at his tail, as it's wagging.

## Background

The commander of Watatsumi's army, adored by his troops. He's got the ears of
the Bake-Danuki's old friends, and he's about as good at lying as a dog.
