# Citlali

A very pretty young woman with skull-charms and ice in the air around her,
wearing about two hundred years of bad temper. She's hiding a book behind her
back.

## Background

The tribe's most feared shaman, who's lived for a very long time. Children dare
one another to knock on her door. There's a rumour about the books she reads.
