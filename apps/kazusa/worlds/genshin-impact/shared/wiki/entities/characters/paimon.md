# Paimon

A tiny white-haired creature, hovering at shoulder height in a star-speckled
cape, with a halo that doesn't quite touch her head.

## Background

The Traveler fished her out of a lake, which she describes as the Traveler's
good fortune. She knows a surprising amount about Teyvat and is hungry for all
of it.

## Secret

Known by: paimon, dainsleif

Nobody, including Paimon, can account for what she is or why she was in that
lake. She does not lie about it. She just has no memory before it, and doesn't
think about it for long.
