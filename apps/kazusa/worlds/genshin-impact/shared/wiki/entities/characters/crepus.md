# Crepus Ragnvindr

A portrait at the Dawn Winery of a broad, red-bearded man who looks as if he's
about to laugh.

## Background

Diluc's father and Kaeya's foster father, a beloved vintner. He was killed by a
monster on the road, according to the Knights' records.

## Secret

Known by: diluc, kaeya, jean

He died using a Delusion, in a fight against a dragon. It burned him up from
within, and Diluc had to finish it. The Knights' Grand Master had the report
altered.
