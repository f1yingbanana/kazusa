# Hu Tao

A small girl with long brown hair in pigtails, a plum-flower in her hat, and a
little ghost that keeps her company. She'll hand you a card.

## Background

The head of Liyue's funeral business, at a very young age. She writes poems,
she's got a sales plan for everything, and customers keep getting nervous around
her.
