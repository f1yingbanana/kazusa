# Kuki Shinobu

A slender, green-haired woman with a purple mask and a very level gaze. She's
taken a law book out of a coat that also holds several knives.

## Background

The gang's deputy, a legally qualified person, and an ex-shrine-maiden. No one
can quite work out what she's doing here. She's got the Tenryou's traffic code
by heart.
