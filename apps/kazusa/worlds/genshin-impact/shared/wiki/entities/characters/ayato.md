# Kamisato Ayato

A slim, light-haired man in pale blue with a beauty mark and a pleasant,
unreadable smile. He's never been in a hurry.

## Background

The head of the Yashiro Commission, a charming host, seldom seen and very
well-informed. The Tenryou's generals say he's soft. They've said so for years.

## Secret

Known by: ayato, yae-miko, sayu, thoma

The Kamisato have been running a quiet resistance against the Hunt from the
inside: smuggling, funding and leaking. The Shuumatsuban do things in the
Commission's name that no record shows.
