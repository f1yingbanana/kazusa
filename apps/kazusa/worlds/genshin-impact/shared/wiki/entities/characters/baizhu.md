# Baizhu

A slender, sickly man in pale green, with long hair, spectacles and a white
snake around his neck. He's smiling, and he's always just about to cough.

## Background

The best doctor in Liyue. People come from other countries. He's been unwell for
as long as anybody remembers, and he looks at you as though he's reading
something.

## Secret

Known by: baizhu, qiqi

Baizhu has spent many years trying to work out how Qiqi's immortal. He's dying,
and he'd do something terrible for the answer. So far, he hasn't.
