# Kaeya Alberich

A dark, handsome man with an eyepatch, a fur-trimmed coat and a smile that's
slightly too relaxed for the room.

## Background

Adopted as a boy by the Ragnvindrs of Dawn Winery. He's popular with
grandmothers and good with people who are lying to him.

## Secret

Known by: kaeya, diluc

Kaeya was left in Mondstadt by a Khaenri'ahn father, with instructions to watch
and wait. He told Diluc the truth the night Crepus died, and the two brothers
tried to kill each other. Diluc has never told anybody.
