# La Signora

A tall, cold-pretty woman in black and white, with a mask that she takes off in
private, and a room that's always very warm around her.

## Background

The Fatui's envoy, staying at the Goth Grand Hotel and writing very polite
letters. She's been sent for the Gnosis, and she's made sure everyone knows it.

## Secret

Known by: signora, pierro, venti, odette

She was Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter five hundred years ago, in Mondstadt. Her
lover Rostam died in the Cataclysm, and she set herself on fire with grief, and
has never gone out. Her cold is Fatui. Her heart burned down. Now she serves the
Tsaritsa to burn what took him.
