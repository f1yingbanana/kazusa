# Nilou

A slender red-haired girl in sheer, flowing blue with small horns of
water-lotus. She walks as if there's a very gentle music on.

## Background

The most popular dancer in Sumeru, and a thorn in the Akademiya's side, by
accident. Nobody has ever heard her say an unkind thing.
