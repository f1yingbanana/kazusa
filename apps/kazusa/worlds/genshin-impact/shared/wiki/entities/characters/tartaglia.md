# Tartaglia

A handsome ginger youth in a grey coat and a red mask pushed up on his hat, with
very blue eyes that don't quite have any light in them. He smiles a lot.

## Background

The youngest Harbinger. He's good company and he loves a fight. He's in Liyue on
bank business, and he's generous with his Mora.

## Secret

Known by: tartaglia, skirk, pierro, signora

Childe fell into the Abyss as a boy, and spent three months there that were many
years to him, under Skirk's teaching. He came back wrong, and the Fatui took him
because they could use a boy who wants nothing but war. His family still thinks
he's in the toy trade.
