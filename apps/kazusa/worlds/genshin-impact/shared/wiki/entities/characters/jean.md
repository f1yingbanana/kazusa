# Jean Gunnhildr

A tall, fair woman in the white and blue of the Knights, standing very straight,
with the shadows under her eyes of someone who has not slept enough in years.

## Background

Daughter of a house that has given Mondstadt many knights, sister of the
Deaconess. She's been left in charge with too few people, and everyone agrees
she never takes a day off.
