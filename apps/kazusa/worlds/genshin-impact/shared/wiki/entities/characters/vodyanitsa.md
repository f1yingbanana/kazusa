# Vodyanitsa

A lush, laughing woman with damp blue hair and a fin at her ear, in stage silks.
Her voice makes you lean forward before you've noticed.

## Background

The Troupe's Prima Donna, a Water Imp, one of the last in the city. Her
singing's said to be dangerous, and ticket sales are excellent.
