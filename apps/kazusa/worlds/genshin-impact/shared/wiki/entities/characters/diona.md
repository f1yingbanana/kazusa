# Diona

A tiny girl with pink hair, a big fluffy tail, and cat ears she'd like you to
stop looking at, carrying a cocktail shaker.

## Background

Daughter of Springvale's hunter Draff, who drinks too much. She's vowed to
destroy Mondstadt's wine industry. Her drinks are the best in the city, to her
enormous frustration.
