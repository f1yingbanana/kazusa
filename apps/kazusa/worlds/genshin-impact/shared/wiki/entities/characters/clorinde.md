# Clorinde

A slim, dark-haired woman in a wide-brimmed hat and long coat, with a sword, a
pistol and eyes you couldn't describe to the police.

## Background

Fontaine's undefeated duelist for hire. In a court where you may settle a case
by duel, she's the reason people settle. Nobody's found out what she does on her
day off.

## Secret

Known by: clorinde, neuvillette, arlecchino

Clorinde was trained in an old tradition of hunters who walk close to the dark.
There's some question about what else she's been hired to kill, and by whom.
