# Nahida

A tiny, white-haired girl with green-ringed eyes, a leaf at her hair and a
stillness that doesn't fit a child's body.

## Background

Sumeru's god, of whom her own people say: too young, too small, and nothing like
the last one. She's rarely seen. She's said to be resting in the Akademiya.

## Secret

Known by: nahida, azar, zhongli, venti

The Sages keep Nahida locked in the Sanctuary, and cut her off. She can reach
people's dreams and the Akasha, a little. She's been watching the Sabzeruz
samsara from inside it, and she's been planning for five hundred years without
being able to touch anything.
