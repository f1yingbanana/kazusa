# Yae Miko

A beautiful, pink-haired woman with fox ears and a priestess's robes, who's
looking at you as if you were the punchline.

## Background

The head of the Grand Narukami Shrine, who's also, unaccountably, the country's
top publisher of romance light novels. Thought to be the only person in Inazuma
who isn't afraid of the Shogun.
