# Kujou Sara

A tall woman with dark tengu wings, a red mask at her hip and a bow she's
holding like an order.

## Background

The adopted daughter of the Kujou clan head, a tengu, and the general leading
the Hunt and the war. She's devoted to the Shogun, and she's incorruptible, and
everyone's afraid of her for good reason.
