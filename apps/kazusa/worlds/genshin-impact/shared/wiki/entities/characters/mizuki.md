# Yumemizuki Mizuki

A soft-looking, lilac-haired woman in a good robe, with a notebook and a
sympathetic look. There's a hint of something else under the hem.

## Background

A clinical psychologist who has come back from a stint abroad and put her
savings into a bathhouse. People come in to soak and leave having told her
everything.

## Secret

Known by: mizuki, yae-miko

She's a yumekui-baku, an eater of dreams, and she takes away her patients'
nightmares by eating them. It's her diet, and it's also an ethical problem she's
been thinking about.
