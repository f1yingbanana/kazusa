# Cyno

A short, dark-skinned man with long white hair, a jackal-eared headdress, very
little clothing and a stare that has made senior scholars confess to things.

## Background

The head of the Akademiya's law officers, who punish academic crimes. He's
feared in every darshan. He's also told a joke once, and nobody's sure.
