# Sangonomiya Kokomi

A small, pink-haired girl in a pale, flowing shrine dress with a jellyfish
floating near her, and very tired eyes behind a very calm face.

## Background

The leader of Watatsumi's war against the Shogun. She's the youngest strategist
to lead an army in living memory. Her people adore her.
