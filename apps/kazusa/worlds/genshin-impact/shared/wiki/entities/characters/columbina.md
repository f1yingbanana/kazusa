# Columbina

A slight, pale girl with a veil across her eyes and long white hair, humming to
herself, a little behind what's happening.

## Background

The Third Harbinger, known as the Damselette. She's rarely at any meeting.
People remember that she was singing, and they don't remember what she said.

## Secret

Known by: columbina, lauma, sandrone, dottore, pierro

She's the goddess the Frostmoon Scions call Kuutar, heir to a moon that's no
longer in the sky. She was bound into the Fatui with old chains. She drifts
around Nod-Krai with very little memory of how she came by anything, and the
Scions keep faith with her anyway.
