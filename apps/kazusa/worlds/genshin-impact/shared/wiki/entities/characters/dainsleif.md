# Dainsleif

A tall, pale man in a long dark coat, with a star in one eye and a mask at his
hip. He's standing just far enough away.

## Background

A wanderer who's been seen in ruins from one end of Teyvat to the other. He
gives good advice in very few words. Nobody can place his accent.

## Secret

Known by: dainsleif, pierro, capitano, skirk

Dainsleif was Bough Keeper of Khaenri'ah, the king's guard. He survived the
kingdom's end with a curse of undying memory, and he's been following the Abyss
Order ever since, to cut it down. He knows who leads it, and he's seen the
Traveler's sibling. He's in no hurry to tell the Traveler.
