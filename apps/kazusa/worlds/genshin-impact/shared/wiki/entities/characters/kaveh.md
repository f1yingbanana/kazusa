# Kaveh

A pretty, blond young man in a slightly frayed noble coat, carrying rolled
plans, with ink on one cheek and a very expressive face.

## Background

One of the most gifted architects of his generation, who built the Palace of
Alcazarzaray and got ruined doing it. He lives in Alhaitham's spare room, and
he's very loud about it.
