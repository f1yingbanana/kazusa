# Lyney

A slender, pale young man in a big top hat and a purple tailcoat, with a rose
and a very quick smile. There's a card in his glove, and now it's in your
pocket.

## Background

The most famous stage magician in Fontaine, with his silent sister and an
inventor. They've got sold-out shows and a very good cat.

## Secret

Known by: lyney, lynette, freminet, arlecchino

Lyney's an agent of the House of the Hearth. The magic shows give him access to
everywhere. He loves Father, and he knows exactly what it would take for her to
spend his sister.
