# Rosaria

A tall, severe nun in a slit habit, with red-tipped claws on her gloves, and a
thin cigarette held away from the cathedral.

## Background

She's a sister in the Church, technically. She shows up to services never, and
she's somehow still on the payroll.

## Secret

Known by: rosaria, kaeya, dahlia

Rosaria hunts the city's night threats and she takes her own orders. The bandits
who burned her mountain village carried her off as a baby and raised her as one
of their own, until the Church's people took her in.
