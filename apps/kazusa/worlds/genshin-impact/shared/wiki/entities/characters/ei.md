# Ei

Almost the Shogun's image, down to the braid, and sitting with her eyes closed.
When she opens them, there's somebody at home, and she's tired.

## Background

Few people alive have heard this name, and fewer still know who it belongs to.

## Secret

Known by: ei, raiden-shogun, yae-miko, venti, zhongli

Ei is the true Electro Archon. Her twin Makoto, the Shogun before her, died at
Khaenri'ah's fall, and so did most of their friends. She has spent centuries
inside her own mind so that nothing else can be taken from her.
