# Wriothesley

A broad, dark-haired young man with handcuffs for a pair of gauntlets, shirt
mostly open, scars and a very nice tea set.

## Background

A former convict who took over the Fortress by knocking down everyone who
disagreed, and who now runs Fontaine's underwater prison with great efficiency.
Prisoners call him His Grace, and most of them mean it.
