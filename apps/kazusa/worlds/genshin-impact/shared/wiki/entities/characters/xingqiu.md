# Xingqiu

A slim blue-haired youth with a book in one hand and a good sword he doesn't
advertise. He's very polite, and he's trying not to laugh.

## Background

He's the second son of the Feiyun family, a well-read young gentleman who is
said to do a lot of good deeds in other people's names. Yanfei's seen his
handwriting on some interesting legal forms.
