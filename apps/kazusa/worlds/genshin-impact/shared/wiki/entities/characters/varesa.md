# Varesa

A big, soft-spoken, muscular woman with cow horns, a big apron and a half-eaten
pie. There's a mask on a branch behind her.

## Background

Runs the Collective's biggest orchard. Children tell stories of the Masked Hero,
who shows up in the nick of time, and is about the same size as Varesa.
