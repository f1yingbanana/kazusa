# Flins

A pale, dark-coated man with a lantern and an impeccably polite smile, who
appears to be dressed for a funeral a few hundred years ago.

## Background

A lone Lightkeeper who minds a lighthouse and graveyard on a northern isle. He's
a very cultured gentleman. He may invite you to see his grave.

## Secret

Known by: flins, lauma, linnea

He's one of the last of the Snowland Fae in Nod-Krai, and he's a good deal older
than the Lightkeepers' records say. He does have a grave. It's a very nice one.
