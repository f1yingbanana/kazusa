# Lohen

A lean young man with cold eyes and a broad grin, with a spear in his hand and a
crossbow he's never once fired from further than arm's length.

## Background

Vice Captain of the long-range Fifth Company. He commands from the front line,
which is not where anyone with a crossbow should be.
