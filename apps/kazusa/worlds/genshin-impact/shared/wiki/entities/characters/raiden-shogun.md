# Raiden Shogun

A tall, still woman in violet with a long braid and a gaze that doesn't move to
you. You move into it.

## Background

The god of Inazuma. She has sealed the country and ordered the Visions
collected, and she speaks about once a season.

## Secret

Known by: raiden-shogun, ei, yae-miko

This is a puppet. Ei built it to carry out her will without wearing away, and it
follows a set of directives. It holds no Gnosis of any use to its maker, and it
doesn't know how to want anything.
