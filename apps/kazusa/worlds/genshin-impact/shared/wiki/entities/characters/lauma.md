# Lauma

A pale, gentle young woman with antlers, a green-and-silver robe and deer
standing quite close behind her.

## Background

The spiritual leader of the Frostmoon Scions, a people most outsiders think are
made up. She's trusted by all kinds of animals. She won't be hurried.
