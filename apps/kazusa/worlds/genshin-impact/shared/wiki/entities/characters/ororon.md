# Ororon

A tall, sleepy boy with a dark bird on his shoulder, a wing-like cloak and a
hoe. He doesn't blink quite as often as you'd expect.

## Background

A strange young man who lives outside the tribe, growing vegetables and keeping
Phlogiston Aphids. He was born with an incomplete soul, they say.
