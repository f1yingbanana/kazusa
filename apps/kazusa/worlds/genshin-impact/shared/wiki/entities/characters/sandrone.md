# Sandrone

A tiny, doll-like woman in layered lace with pale hair, unblinking eyes and a
very large robot behind her, holding her tea.

## Background

The Seventh Harbinger, known as the Lady, who heads the Fatui's Armory Palace.
She builds their Ruin machines.

## Secret

Known by: sandrone, pierro, katheryne

Sandrone's an automaton herself, built by an inventor named Alain Guillotin in
the image of his dead sister. He gave her a life of her own, and she's never
decided what it's worth. Every Katheryne's her design.
