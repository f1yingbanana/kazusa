# Diluc Ragnvindr

A red-haired man in black, with a rich man's coat, a very straight back and a
face that makes you want to apologise.

## Background

The wealthiest man in Mondstadt, and the youngest cavalry captain the Knights
ever had, until he quit. He doesn't explain why. He owns most of the wine you've
ever enjoyed, and he doesn't drink.

## Secret

Known by: diluc, kaeya, jean, nefer

He's the Darknight Hero, who walks the roads at night and hunts Fatui and
bandits in his own time. He's also a contact for the Northern Intelligence
Network, under the name Owl.
