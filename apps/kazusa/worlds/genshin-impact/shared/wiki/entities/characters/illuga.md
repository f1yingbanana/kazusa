# Illuga

A broad, sleep-starved man in a burned Lightkeeper's coat, with a heavy lantern
and a list. He looks up every time the wind changes.

## Background

The head of a small Lightkeeper investigation squad. His unit's lost fewer
people than anyone else's, and he won't tell you how he feels about that.
