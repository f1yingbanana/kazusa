# Kaedehara Kazuha

A slight young man with white hair, a red streak over one eye and a katana with
a very good balance. He's looking at the sky, and listening.

## Background

Fugitive from Inazuma, wanted under the Vision Hunt Decree. He sails with the
Crux. He's polite to everyone, he doesn't drink much, and he writes verses.
