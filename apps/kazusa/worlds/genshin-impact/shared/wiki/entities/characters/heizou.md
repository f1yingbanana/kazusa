# Shikanoin Heizou

A cheerful, dark-haired youth in a short coat with a sharp grin, fingerless
gloves and a habit of looking at your shoes first.

## Background

A very young detective at the Tenryou Commission, who solves things nobody's
asked him to. He's got a Vision, and he doesn't seem to mind who knows it, which
puzzles his superiors.
