# Alice

A very pretty, very cheerful woman with long ears, on the cover of a book of
travel writing. There's an explosion behind her.

## Background

Author of a popular series of travel books, and mother of Klee. Mondstadt has
many funny stories about her, and a growing number of posters, thanks to one
furious child.
