# Emilie

A cool, elegant blonde woman in pale green, with gloves on, a vial at her collar
and a very slight frown that's aimed at your scent.

## Background

A top perfume designer, sought after by the ladies of Fontaine. She also works
with the police as a "forensic cleanser", which means she clears up after
murders, and she's got a very good nose.
