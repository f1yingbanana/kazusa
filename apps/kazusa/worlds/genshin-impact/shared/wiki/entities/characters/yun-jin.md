# Yun Jin

A graceful woman in rich stage robes and painted makeup, with eyes that are
studying how you're standing.

## Background

Liyue's foremost opera star, and its hardest-working. She writes her own
scripts, she can sing every part in them, and she has a very dry way of handling
a critic.
