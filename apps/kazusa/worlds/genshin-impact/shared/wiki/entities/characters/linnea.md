# Linnea

A small, brisk woman in field clothes with feathers woven in her hair, mud on
her boots and a very worn sketchbook.

## Background

The Guild's most unusual advisor. She's rarely at a desk. If you get lost in the
Nod-Krai wild, she's likely to have left a note for you on a tree.

## Secret

Known by: linnea, flins, lauma

She's one of the last Snowland Fae in Nod-Krai and one of the last Augury Birds
anywhere. She was raised among humans, and she keeps the fact quiet because of
who collects such things.
