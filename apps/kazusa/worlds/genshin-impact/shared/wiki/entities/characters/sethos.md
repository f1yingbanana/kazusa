# Sethos

A cheerful young man in temple robes and a white veil, with a Hermanubis pendant
and an easy smile that he's definitely not supposed to show.

## Background

He's the very young head of an old desert temple, which keeps to itself. He
comes to the city for the food, and he's easy company. He's one of the few
people who think the General Mahamatra's funny.

## Secret

Known by: sethos, cyno

Sethos was once meant to be the vessel of the god Hermanubis. Cyno got that fate
instead, in part. They've an old debt between them, and the temple keeps secrets
from before the Akademiya.
