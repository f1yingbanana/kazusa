# Layla

A pale, heavy-eyed girl in dark blue, with stars around her hems, her hair
half-unravelled and a thesis held like a teddy bear.

## Background

An anxious Rtawahist astrology student who's terribly short of sleep. Her essays
come out very well. She wakes up and finds them done. She credits the stars.
