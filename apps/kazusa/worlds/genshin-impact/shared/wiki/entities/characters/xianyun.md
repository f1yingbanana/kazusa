# Xianyun

A great white crane with very sharp eyes, who's far too fond of telling you
what's wrong. She's got a dry, carrying voice.

## Background

One of Liyue's oldest adepti. Ganyu calls her Master. She's said to be good with
mechanisms and a terror on the subject of manners.
