# Ningguang

A very tall, silver-haired woman in gold and white, with long nails and a pipe
that she handles like a contract.

## Background

The richest person in Liyue. She came up from a roadside stall, and she'll tell
you the price of the chair you're sitting on. She built a palace in the sky,
because she could.
