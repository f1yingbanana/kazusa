# Barbara Pegg

A small bright-eyed girl in a white-and-blue idol's dress, with a big water
bubble and a smile she practises.

## Background

Mondstadt's healer and its singer. She got the idea from a book of Inazuman pop
stars, and she's very much in earnest.
