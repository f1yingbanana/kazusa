# Escoffier

A pale, severe woman in a spotless chef's coat with a Cryo-chilled ladle. She's
looking at your plate as if it had let her down.

## Background

Fontaine's most exacting chef, and the most feared, too. Reviewers quake, and
apprentices flee. She's been known to throw out a critic for wasting a sauce.
