# Dahlia

A young deacon with a neatly pressed collar and an expression of delight. It
looks like he's just heard something he really shouldn't have.

## Background

A deacon who has a reputation for being nosy. He solves people's problems before
they've finished having them. He's often seen near the bard.

## Secret

Known by: dahlia, venti

Dahlia is the Herald of Barbatos, chosen by Venti himself. He's the only person
in the Church who knows who that drunk bard is, and he reports to him.
