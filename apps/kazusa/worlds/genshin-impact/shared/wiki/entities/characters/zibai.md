# Zibai

Accounts disagree. Some say a horse as white as snow. Others, a woman with amber
eyes.

## Background

The legends of Liyue speak of a White Horse Adeptus who helped Rex Lapis in the
Archon War and kept watch from the moon. She hasn't been seen in a very long
time.
