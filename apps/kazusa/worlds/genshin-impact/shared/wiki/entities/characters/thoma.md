# Thoma

A big, easygoing blond man with a Mondstadt accent in Inazuman dress, carrying a
spear and somebody's shopping.

## Background

A half-Mondstadter who grew up in Inazuma. He's the Kamisato's housekeeper, and
he seems to know every stallholder and criminal in the city by name.
