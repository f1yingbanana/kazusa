# Dehya

A huge-hearted, tall, dark-skinned woman with a wild red-brown mane, gold
jewellery and arms like a stone bridge. She's grinning at you.

## Background

The most famous Eremite in Sumeru. She'll sell her sword, and she's refused a
very great deal of money for doing some jobs. People who've hired her generally
come back.
