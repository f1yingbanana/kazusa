# Bennett

A cheerful, bandaged boy in goggles, with a scorched jacket, a small limp and a
smile that has been knocked out and put back in again.

## Background

Abandoned as a baby at a ruin and raised by old adventurers, all of whom had to
retire for health reasons. No one will team with him. It's the luck. It's really
quite something.
