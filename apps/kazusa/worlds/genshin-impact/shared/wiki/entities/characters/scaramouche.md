# Scaramouche

A slight, pretty youth with dark blue hair and a wide conical hat. There's a
contempt in the set of his mouth that looks older than he does.

## Background

A Harbinger, and the rudest of them. He oversees Fatui operations on Kannazuka.
Nobody wants to be in a room with him.

## Secret

Known by: scaramouche, ei, dottore, yae-miko, nahida

Scaramouche is a puppet Ei built before she made the Shogun. He was too human
for her purposes, and she left him asleep, and he woke up, and he was alone. He
lost his one human friend at Tatarasuna to what he thinks was betrayal. He'd do
anything to matter, and he'll make a god of himself to get even.
