# Venti

A slight youth in green with a beret and a lyre, who is not old enough for the
bottle he's holding, if you go by what he looks like.

## Background

The bard everyone's seen drinking at Windrise and has never been able to pay for
a drink. He knows every song in Mondstadt, and quite a few that nobody's heard
of.

## Secret

Known by: venti, zhongli, dahlia, ei, yae-miko, nahida, mavuika

Venti is Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, God of Freedom. He gave up ruling so that
nobody would have to obey him. He walks his own nation as a nobody, and he's
keeping an eye on Dvalin, who's sick, and whom he failed.
