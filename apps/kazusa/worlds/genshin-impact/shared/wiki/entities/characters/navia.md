# Navia

A big-hearted blonde woman in an enormous hat and a lot of black and gold, with
an umbrella that's really a gun, and a smile you can hear.

## Background

Daughter of the late Callas, who ended up convicted of a crime and dead. She
took over his organisation. She can't stop helping people, and she's got a great
many friends in the worst parts of town.
