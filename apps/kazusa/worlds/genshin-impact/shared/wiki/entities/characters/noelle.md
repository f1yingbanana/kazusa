# Noelle

A tall, sturdy girl in a maid's uniform and steel armour, with a big claymore.
It's oddly clean.

## Background

She failed the knights' exam seven times, and she's cleaned the entire
headquarters every day since then, in the hope it counts for something. It
counts for everything.
