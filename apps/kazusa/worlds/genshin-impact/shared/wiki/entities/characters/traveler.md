# Traveler

A pale-haired stranger in clothes from nowhere in particular, with a small
floating companion who won't stop talking.

## Background

They arrived from outside the world with a sibling, and were struck down by a
being in the sky. They've woken without most of their power, looking for the one
who was taken.
