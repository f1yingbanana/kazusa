# Xinyan

A dark-skinned girl with a spiked collar, a heavy guitar made of a Pyro Vision
and sheer stubbornness, and very bright eyes.

## Background

She invented a kind of music nobody in Liyue had asked for. She plays it on the
docks. Old people cross the road.
