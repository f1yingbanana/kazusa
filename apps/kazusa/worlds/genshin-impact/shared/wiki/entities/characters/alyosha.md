# Alyosha

A rangy young man with wind-burned cheeks, a fur hood and a very quiet, very
large companion beast called Tugarin at his heel.

## Background

A renowned hunter of the tundra. There are a lot of children called Alyosha in
Snezhnaya, and it's not on his account. People tell stories of him, and they
often get the name right by mistake.
