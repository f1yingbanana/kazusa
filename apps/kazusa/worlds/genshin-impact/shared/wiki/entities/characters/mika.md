# Mika Schmidt

A slight, fair boy in a knight's coat, with a map case, a crossbow and an air of
apologising to the snow.

## Background

A junior knight of the Reconnaissance Company, attached to Grand Master Varka's
long expedition. He maps what's there. People forget he's on the team, and then
they find out he's the reason they're alive.
