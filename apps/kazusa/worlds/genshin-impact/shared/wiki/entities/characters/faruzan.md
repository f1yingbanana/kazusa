# Faruzan

A very young-looking, blue-haired woman in old-fashioned scholar's robes, with a
scandalised expression and a mechanism she's fiddling with.

## Background

A scholar in ancient scripts who solved her way out of a ruin after a hundred
years, and has returned to find nobody remembers her. Students have been told to
respect their elders, and this isn't helping.
