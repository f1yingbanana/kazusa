# Keqing

A sharp-looking young woman in purple, with hair done up in two loops that look
a bit like cat's ears, a long sword and a look at her watch.

## Background

The Qixing's youngest, in charge of land and buildings. She's in favour of
humans handling their own business. She hasn't taken a holiday, and she's
reviewed the idea.
