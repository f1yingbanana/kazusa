# Arlecchino

A tall, slender woman with white hair streaked with black and eyes with red
crosses in them. She's unfailingly polite, and she's looking at you as if she's
working out your use.

## Background

A Harbinger, known in Fontaine as a benefactor of orphans. She's rarely in
public. Those she has spoken to say she's really quite civil.

## Secret

Known by: arlecchino, lyney, lynette, freminet, clorinde, pierro

She killed the previous Knave, her own Father, and took the Hearth. She's
carrying an old curse from the Crimson Moon's line, with its fire in her blood.
She takes care of her children, and she spends them too, and she doesn't lie to
them about either.
