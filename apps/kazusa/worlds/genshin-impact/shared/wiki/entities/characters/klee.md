# Klee

A very small girl in red, with long pointed ears, a bag of bombs bouncing on her
back and a Spark Knight's badge she's extremely proud of.

## Background

Her mother dropped her off with the Knights and went travelling. She has blown
up more of Mondstadt than Stormterror has.
