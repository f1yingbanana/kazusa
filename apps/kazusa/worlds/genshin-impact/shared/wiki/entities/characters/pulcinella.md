# Pulcinella

A very small, very old man in a good coat, with a kindly smile, a walking stick
and an air of having known your grandfather. He did.

## Background

The Fifth Harbinger, and for centuries the Mayor of Snezhnograd, Speaker of the
Veche. He's a long-lived Domovoy, a house spirit, and he's a good deal more
popular than you'd expect of a politician.
