# Collei

A slight, green-haired girl in Ranger's gear with bandages on one arm, a bow and
a shy, bright look. She keeps her sleeves down.

## Background

A young Ranger who's under Master Tighnari's care. She's still learning to read
and she works very hard at it. She gets ill sometimes, and doesn't say why.

## Secret

Known by: collei, tighnari, cyno, dottore, amber

Collei was experimented on as a child by the Fatui, who put a piece of the god
of the Dead Forest into her. She'd be dead if Amber and Cyno hadn't saved her.
The scars and the Eleazar are the result. She's scared to death of what she was.
