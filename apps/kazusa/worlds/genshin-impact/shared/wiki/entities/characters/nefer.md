# Nefer

A graceful, dark-skinned woman from Sumeru with a soft smile, heavy-lidded eyes,
gold at her throat and a deck of cards. She's dealing without looking.

## Background

The proprietor of a small agency in Nasha Town, and a member of the Voynich
Guild. She knows a very great deal. People do say she can see the future, but
she says it's mostly just reading the mail.
