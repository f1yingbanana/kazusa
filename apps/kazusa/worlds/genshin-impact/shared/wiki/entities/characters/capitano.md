# Il Capitano

A very tall man in a dark helm and a great coat, whose men would plainly walk
off a cliff for him. He doesn't take off the helm. His voice is quiet.

## Background

The First Harbinger. The Fatui's great commander, the only one the other
Harbingers respect. Everyone he has fought says it was fair.

## Secret

Known by: capitano, pierro, mavuika, dainsleif

Thrain was a Khaenri'ahn knight, cursed with undeath at the Cataclysm, by
Ronova. He can't die, and he remembers everyone. He's here to take the Pyro
Gnosis, and he also wants to win Natlan's war, for reasons he hasn't told the
Doctor.
