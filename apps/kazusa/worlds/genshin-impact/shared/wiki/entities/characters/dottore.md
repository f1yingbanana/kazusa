# Il Dottore

A tall man in a sharp coat with a beaked half-mask and blue-tinted hair, with
very clean hands and a pleasant, interested voice.

## Background

The Second Harbinger, the Fatui's chief scientist, expelled from the Akademiya
long ago. Most of his colleagues wouldn't turn their back on him. He's got a
stronghold in Nod-Krai, and no one's sure how many assistants.

## Secret

Known by: dottore, pierro, scaramouche, nahida, collei

He has made many copies of himself at earlier ages, and he's got at least one in
several nations at any time. He's funding Sumeru's new god. His experiments
killed many children, of whom Collei is one of the few survivors.
