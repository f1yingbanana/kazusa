# Sayu

A tiny girl in a big raccoon-dog hood, with a very large sword she drags behind
her, yawning.

## Background

A young ninja in the Shuumatsuban, the Yashiro Commission's shadow service.
She's very good at disappearing, particularly when she's got something to do.
