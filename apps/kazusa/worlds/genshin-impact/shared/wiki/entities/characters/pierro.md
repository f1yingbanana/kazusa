# Pierro

A very old, very upright man in a dark coat, with a long white beard and a
half-mask. He doesn't need anyone to know who he is.

## Background

The Fatui's Director, leader of the Eleven. He's rarely seen outside of the
palace. Everyone who's anyone is frightened of the same few things, and he's one
of them.

## Secret

Known by: pierro, capitano, dainsleif

Born in Khaenri'ah, he served as a court mage, and he came out of its fall with
a great many things that he won't discuss. He serves the Tsaritsa, and he's got
a long plan of his own, in which she's a very important part.
