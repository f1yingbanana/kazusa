# The Abyss Order

It makes itself known in ruins and at bad moments. Its mages speak, which is
rarer than you'd think. Some carry a banner to a figure they call the Prince or
Princess.

## Who leads it

Known by: dainsleif, skirk

The Order is led by someone the world hasn't met, who fell into the Abyss with
the Traveler long ago, and came out knowing what they were doing. The Traveler
hasn't been told any of this.
