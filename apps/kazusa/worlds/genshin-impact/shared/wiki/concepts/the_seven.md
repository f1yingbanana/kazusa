# The Seven

Barbatos of Freedom, Morax of Contracts, Beelzebul of Eternity, Buer of Wisdom,
Focalors of Justice, Haborym of War and the Tsaritsa of Love. They won their
thrones in the Archon War about two thousand years ago. Some of those thrones
have since changed hands.

## Who is where

Barbatos has not been seen in Mondstadt for a long time. Morax has not been seen
in Liyue in centuries, though the Qixing insist that he is well. The Shogun has
sealed Inazuma. Kusanali is called young and useless by her own scholars.
Focalors is famous and adores it. Haborym races around Natlan on a motorbike.
The Tsaritsa does not appear in public.

## Under the names

Known by: venti, zhongli, ei, yae-miko, nahida, mavuika

The Seven know one another, and know what they have lost. Barbatos walks about
as a bard. Morax teaches and sells nothing at a funeral parlour. The Shogun in
the Tenshukaku is a puppet, and her maker sits inside her own mind. Buer is a
prisoner. Focalors made an arrangement long ago that even the others don't fully
understand.
