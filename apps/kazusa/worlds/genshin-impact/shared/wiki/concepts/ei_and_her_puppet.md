# The Shogun's Silence

Everyone knows she's unmoving, that she doesn't take audiences and that she acts
by decree.

## Puppet and maker

Known by: ei, raiden-shogun, yae-miko

The Shogun everyone sees is a puppet. Ei made it, after losing her sister and
her friends, and went inward to the Plane of Euthymia to meditate on how to stop
time hurting. Only Yae Miko ever reaches her, and Ei thinks that's all a person
needs.
