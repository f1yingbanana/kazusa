# The Hearth's Children

There's an orphanage in Fontaine, and a magic show. That's what most know of it.

## The Knave's house

Known by: arlecchino, lyney, lynette, freminet, signora, pierro

The House trains orphans into Fatui agents. It's headed by the Fourth Harbinger,
the Knave, who is called Father. Children can be spent. Lyney, Lynette and
Freminet know this, and they stay close together.
