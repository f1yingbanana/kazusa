# Gnosis

Everyone has heard that the gods carry a Gnosis. Few could say what it does. The
Fatui have lately made it known that they want them, and they will pay or hurt
to get them.

## Why the Tsaritsa wants them

Known by: pierro, capitano, dottore, arlecchino, signora, tartaglia

The Harbingers have been told their Tsaritsa means to collect all seven and go
to war against Celestia. Each of them privately believes something different
about why.
