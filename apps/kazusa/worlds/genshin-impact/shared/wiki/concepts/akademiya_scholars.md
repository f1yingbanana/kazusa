# Scholars of the Akademiya

Amurta, Haravatat, Kshahrewar, Rtawahist, Spantamad, Vahumana. They each think
the others aren't real sciences. The Grand Sage's word is the law, and arts have
been officially discouraged.

## The Sages' plan

Known by: azar, nahida, scaramouche, dottore

The Sages have been building a new god, out of a puppet and some Fatui help, and
have locked Kusanali away where she can't interfere. It's going very well.
