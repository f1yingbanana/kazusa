# The World of Teyvat

Seven nations, each with its own Archon and its own idea of what a god is for.
Most people worry about harvests, debts and neighbours. The gods are real, they
walk among people or refuse to, and everyone has an opinion about them.

## The moment

The story here begins on the day a stranger wakes on the shore of Starfell Lake.
Stormterror's winds are troubling Mondstadt, Inazuma is shut behind its Decree,
Sumeru's god has not been seen in centuries, and Fontaine is pretending it has
no reason to look at the water.
