# The Prophecy of Fontaine

Every child in Fontaine knows the rhyme. Almost nobody takes it seriously,
though Poisson's water levels have been rising.

## The woman on the throne

Known by: furina

Focalors split herself in two five centuries ago. Furina is a human girl who has
been playing Focalors ever since, never told how it ends, and never allowed to
stop. Neuvillette doesn't know she isn't the Archon. He's beginning to suspect
the wrong things.
