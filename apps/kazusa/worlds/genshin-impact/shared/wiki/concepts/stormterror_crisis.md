# The Stormterror Crisis

The winds have turned violent and whole fields have been lost. Abyss Mages have
been seen nearby, as have the Fatui, who are very willing to help.

## The dragon

Known by: venti, dainsleif

Stormterror is Dvalin, a once-gentle wind dragon poisoned by something from
below. Those who hunt him don't know that. Venti does.
