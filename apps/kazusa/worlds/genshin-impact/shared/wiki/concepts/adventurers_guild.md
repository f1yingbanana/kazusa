# The Adventurers' Guild

"Ad astra abyssosque." The Guild pays for odd jobs and asks few questions. It
has desks in every nation and a receptionist named Katheryne at all of them, or
a set of very similar sisters.

## Who really runs it

Known by: katheryne, sandrone, pierro, dottore

Every Katheryne is an automaton built in Snezhnaya, and Sandrone is one of the
Guild's joint overseers. Most adventurers would not enjoy learning that.
