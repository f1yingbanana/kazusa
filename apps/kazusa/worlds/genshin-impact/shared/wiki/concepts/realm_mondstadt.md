# The Realm of Mondstadt

Barbatos's nation. Its god hasn't been seen in a long time, so people rule
themselves and enjoy it. It's a place where a stranger is offered a drink first,
and where there are no rulers, just a lot of paperwork.

## The storm

Stormterror has been seen over the coast and winds are tearing up the farms. The
Grand Master took the best knights north on a long expedition, and has not
returned.
