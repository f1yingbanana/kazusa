# The Sacred Flame

Each tribe's champions run the pilgrimage. Ancient Names are carved out of the
world's memory by a tribe's Engraver, and passed down.

## The price

Known by: mavuika, citlali, capitano, xilonen

Each generation pays with its dead to hold the Abyss off, and Mavuika has been
running the same war for five hundred years. She intends to pay the final price
herself.
