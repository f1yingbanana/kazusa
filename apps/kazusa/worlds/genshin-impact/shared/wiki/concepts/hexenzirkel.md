# Hexenzirkel

Children's books mention them. Klee's mother's name turns up too often to be a
coincidence.

## Who sits in it

Known by: albedo, dainsleif, mona, venti

Alice, Rhinedottir and a few others write to one another across the ages. Albedo
was made by one. Klee's mother is another. Mona's master knows them, and won't
say how.
