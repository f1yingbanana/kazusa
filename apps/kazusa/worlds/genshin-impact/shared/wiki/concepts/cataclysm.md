# The Cataclysm

Khaenri'ah had no god and did well without one, and then it was destroyed in a
night. Hilichurls came up afterward, and nobody asks where they came from.
Nations remember it for what it cost them, Dragonspine and Liyue especially.

## What the Khaenri'ahns became

Known by: dainsleif, capitano, pierro, kaeya, albedo, venti, zhongli

Survivors of Khaenri'ah were cursed. Most of them lost their minds and shapes,
and became the hilichurls. A few were given undying life, and the memory of it.
Dainsleif is one of those. Kaeya's family is another story that he doesn't tell.
