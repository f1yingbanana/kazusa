# The Eleven Fatui Harbingers

Pierro the Jester, Capitano the Captain, Dottore the Doctor, Columbina the
Damselette, Arlecchino the Knave, Pulcinella the Rooster, Scaramouche the
Balladeer, Sandrone the Marionette, Pantalone the Regrator, La Signora the Fair
Lady and Tartaglia the Childe. Some of them have a personal reason to dislike
the ones next to them.

## Below the masks

Known by: pierro, capitano, signora, arlecchino, dottore, scaramouche

Capitano and Pierro were Khaenri'ahns, and they survived what most didn't.
Signora once wore another name, and burns for a lover five hundred years dead.
Scaramouche was made by a god, and would very much like revenge.
