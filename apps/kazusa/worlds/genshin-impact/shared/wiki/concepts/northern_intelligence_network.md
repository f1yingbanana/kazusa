# Northern Intelligence Network

Everyone's in it who knows what it is. It's sold out of a small shop in Nasha
Town, by a woman with very good cards.

## The Owl

Known by: nefer, diluc, jahoda

The Network's contact in Mondstadt, codename Owl, is Diluc Ragnvindr. He uses it
to hunt the Fatui.
