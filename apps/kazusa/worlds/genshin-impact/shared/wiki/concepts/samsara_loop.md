# The Samsara of Sabzeruz

It's a joyful festival in Sumeru City, and no one has any reason to say more.

## The repeating days

Known by: nahida, azar

The Sages have used Irminsul to run the festival over and over, harvesting the
city's dreams. People wake each day with faint déjà vu. Nahida has been
counting.
