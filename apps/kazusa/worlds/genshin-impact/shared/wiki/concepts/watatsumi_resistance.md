# The Watatsumi Resistance

Everyone knows they're losing, slowly. They've got a very good general with dog
ears, and nobody sells them Delusions, as far as they know.

## Who sells Delusions

Known by: scaramouche, dottore, signora, pierro, kujou-sara

Both sides of the war are buying Fatui Delusions. Fatui agents encouraged the
Hunt and the Tenryou's secret trade. The users are burning up.
