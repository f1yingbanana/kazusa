# The Guild Post

Teyvat has no way to speak across a distance, except by writing it down. Tie a
note to a post-bird at any Guild desk, or carry a bird yourself, and it will
find the person you named. Nobody quite knows how they do it, or what they do
with what they read. People send them for almost everything, and treat a short
note as a conversation.
