# The Wangsheng Funeral Trade

Seventy-seven generations of directors have kept the line between living and
dead tidy. The current one's a teenager.

## The consultant

Known by: zhongli, hu-tao

The parlor's mild-mannered consultant Zhongli is Morax himself. Hu Tao hasn't
got proof. She knows, though. She's very good at knowing.
