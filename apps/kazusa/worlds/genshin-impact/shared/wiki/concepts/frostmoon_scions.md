# The Frostmoon Scions

They live in the deep woods and on Hiisi Island, descendants of a lost golden
realm. Most outsiders think they're a story, which suits them.

## The goddess

Known by: lauma, columbina, sandrone, dottore, pierro

Kuutar, the goddess the Scions worship, is Columbina, the Third of the Fatui
Harbingers. She sometimes doesn't remember why she went away, and she's bound by
her chains.
