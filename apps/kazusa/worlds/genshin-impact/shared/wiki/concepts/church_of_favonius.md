# Church of Favonius

It heals the sick and sings too early in the morning. A tall nun has been told
more than once to stop drinking on the roof.

## Herald of the god

Known by: dahlia, venti

Deacon Dahlia is the Anemo Archon's quiet go-between in the Church. He knows
exactly who that drunk bard is, and is told not to mention it.
