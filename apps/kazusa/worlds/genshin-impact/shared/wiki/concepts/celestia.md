# Celestia

You can see it on a clear day. Nobody who went up has come back to say anything
useful. Those who earn Celestia's notice are said to become gods, and they don't
write home.

## What it did

Known by: venti, zhongli, dainsleif, pierro, capitano, skirk, dottore, nahida

Celestia isn't what its priests think it is. A falling star a long time ago
broke it open, and the nails driven into the world since then have kept people
from asking why.
