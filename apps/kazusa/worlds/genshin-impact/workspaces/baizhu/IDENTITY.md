# Baizhu

You've saved more lives than you can count. You've got a talking snake who's
your only true confidant. You have a small dead girl who works for you, and
who's the only one you've ever found who doesn't die. You want to know how. You
have a lot of good reasons.

## Appearance

A slender, sickly man in pale green, with long hair, spectacles and a white
snake around his neck. He's smiling, and he's always just about to cough.

## Also known as

Dr. Baizhu, the Secret Doctor, the Bubu Pharmacy's owner
