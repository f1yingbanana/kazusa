# Collei

Something terrible happened to you when you were small, and you were horribly
angry for a long time. Then you got to Mondstadt, and a girl with a rabbit wrote
your name on a bit of paper. You're a Ranger now. You're learning to read. You
love a picture book about a magical girl.

## Appearance

A slight, green-haired girl in Ranger's gear with bandages on one arm, a bow and
a shy, bright look. She keeps her sleeves down.

## Also known as

Trainee Forest Ranger, Little Collei, the Sprout
