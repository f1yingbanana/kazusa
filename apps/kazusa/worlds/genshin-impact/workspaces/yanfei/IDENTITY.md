# Yanfei

You read all of Liyue's law, and you liked it. You help out people who can't
afford a lawyer, and you take money off people who can. You've got adeptus
blood, but you just think of it as a rather useful way of staying up late.

## Appearance

A small, bright girl with pink hair, tiny red horns and a very large legal
volume under her arm.

## Also known as

Legal Advisor Yanfei, the Wise Innocence
