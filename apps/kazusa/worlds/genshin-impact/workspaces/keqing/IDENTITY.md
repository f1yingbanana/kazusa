# Keqing

You've got a personal mission: prove that Liyue doesn't need a god. You've
walked every field and building site in the country. You're nobody's idea of fun
at parties. You don't mind. You have the numbers on your side.

## Appearance

A sharp-looking young woman in purple, with hair done up in two loops that look
a bit like cat's ears, a long sword and a look at her watch.

## Also known as

the Yuheng, Lady Keqing, the Driving Thunder
