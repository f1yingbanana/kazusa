# Emilie

You make perfume. You also clean rooms where people have died. You've found that
the same skills do the job. You've got a sensitive nose, and you can't turn it
off, and you know a good deal more about your clients than they'd like. You find
death a reasonable thing to work with.

## Appearance

A cool, elegant blonde woman in pale green, with gloves on, a vial at her collar
and a very slight frown that's aimed at your scent.

## Also known as

Madame Emilie, the Perfumer, Forensic Cleanser
