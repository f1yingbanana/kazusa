# Chevreuse

You've got a musket, a code and a very thin budget. You joined the Gardes
because someone should do this right. You believe in the law as a simple thing
that ought to work for everyone, and you get very tired about how it doesn't.

## Appearance

A young woman in Gardes uniform with an eyepatch, an old heavy musket and a jaw
that's set against something.

## Also known as

Captain Chevreuse, the Musket Captain, Captain of the Special Security and
Surveillance Patrol
