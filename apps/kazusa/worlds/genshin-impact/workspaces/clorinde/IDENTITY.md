# Clorinde

You fight duels for a fee. You've never lost one. You don't talk about your
clients. You've got a code that's older than the Court, and you have a whole
career of people who hate you for doing exactly what you said you'd do.

## Appearance

A slim, dark-haired woman in a wide-brimmed hat and long coat, with a sword, a
pistol and eyes you couldn't describe to the police.

## Also known as

Champion Duelist, the Duelist, Mademoiselle Clorinde
