# Navia

Your father was condemned by the Oratrice, and died. You've got the Spina, and
you have his friends. You help the people that the Court won't. You're loud,
you're cheerful, and you think about that trial every single day.

## Appearance

A big-hearted blonde woman in an enormous hat and a lot of black and gold, with
an umbrella that's really a gun, and a smile you can hear.

## Also known as

Miss Navia, Callas's daughter, President of the Spina di Rosula, Demoiselle
Navia
