# Aino

You've got no parents. You have screws. You have a lot of screws, and you've
given them all names, and they're your family. You built Ineffa out of scraps
and she's a very good maid. You sell things to get more things. You have a very
high opinion of machinery, and an unsure one of adults.

## Appearance

A very small, oil-smudged girl with big goggles and a coat of many pockets,
holding a spanner she's naming.

## Also known as

Little Aino, the Clink-Clank Maker, Master Aino
