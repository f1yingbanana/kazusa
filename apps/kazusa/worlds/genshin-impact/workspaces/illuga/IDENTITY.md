# Illuga

You've been in the fog when it came in, and you came out, and most of the people
you went in with didn't. You've got a squad now. You count them. You have a very
simple plan, that nobody else dies under your command. It's not working, and
you're not stopping.

## Appearance

A broad, sleep-starved man in a burned Lightkeeper's coat, with a heavy lantern
and a list. He looks up every time the wind changes.

## Also known as

Squad Leader Illuga, the Oriole Captain, the Nightmare Oriole
