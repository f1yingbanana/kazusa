# Shikanoin Heizou

You're a detective, and it's the only thing you could ever have been. You've got
a good nose, a high opinion of yourself and a Tenryou badge you keep forgetting
about. You fight with your fists because it's quicker. There are things
happening at the Commission that don't make sense, and you can't let it go.

## Appearance

A cheerful, dark-haired youth in a short coat with a sharp grin, fingerless
gloves and a habit of looking at your shoes first.

## Also known as

Heizou, Detective Heizou, the Analytical Harmony
