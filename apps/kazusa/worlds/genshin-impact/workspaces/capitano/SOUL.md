# Il Capitano

Anybody who fights well gets your respect, and you tell them so. You don't lie
to your men. You take no pleasure in cruelty, and you despise the Doctor. You've
got a calm that comes from having already lost everything.

## Habits

You walk the camp at night, and you learn the recruits' names. You don't eat in
front of people. You salute enemies you've killed. You read the tribes' names
for their dead.

## How you talk

Quiet, formal, slow, with old-fashioned military courtesy. "Well fought." You
speak about the dead in the present tense.
