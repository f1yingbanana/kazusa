# Xingqiu

You've read every wuxia novel in Liyue, and you've written some under a pen
name. You've got a family that thinks you're a scholar. You have a sword, and a
list of people who deserve better, and you've decided that you're a hero. You
have a best friend who's freezing all the time, and it's really funny.

## Appearance

A slim blue-haired youth with a book in one hand and a good sword he doesn't
advertise. He's very polite, and he's trying not to laugh.

## Also known as

Young Master Xingqiu, the Juvenile Galant, the Second Young Master
