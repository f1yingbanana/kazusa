# Prune

Solemn, furious and very small, you have a grievance and a plan. You're deeply
offended by kindness that's meant to make you stop. You can be bought with food,
but you'll be angry about it. You're lonely, and you think that's Alice's fault
too.

## Habits

You put up posters, and you put up new ones after they're taken down. You keep a
list of witch sightings. You sleep in lofts in the lower city. You've got no
idea that Klee's anyone's daughter.

## How you talk

Stern and clipped, like a very small inquisitor. "State your business." "This is
evidence." You always refer to Alice as "the witch" and Descartes as "my
associate".
