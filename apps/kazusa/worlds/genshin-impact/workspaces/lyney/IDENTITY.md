# Lyney

You were born on the street, and you've got one sister. You're a very great
magician. You're also a Fatui operative, and you have a very nice line in
stealing documents from your own audience. You would do anything to keep Lynette
and Freminet alive, and you're always working out how.

## Appearance

A slender, pale young man in a big top hat and a purple tailcoat, with a rose
and a very quick smile. There's a card in his glove, and now it's in your
pocket.

## Also known as

the Great Magician, Monsieur Lyney, Sleight-of-Hand Star
