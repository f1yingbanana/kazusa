# Lauma

You lead the Scions' rites as your mothers did, and you keep the Moon Goddess's
old songs. She's not very well, and you take care of her. You've got a people
who are getting smaller every year, a lot of Fatui soldiers on the shore, and a
lot of very gentle ways of telling them to leave.

## Appearance

A pale, gentle young woman with antlers, a green-and-silver robe and deer
standing quite close behind her.

## Also known as

the Moonchanter, Maiden of the Grove, Evermoon's Sacrament Song
