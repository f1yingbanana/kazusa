# Razor

Lupical found you. You've got a pack. A woman in a hat taught you how to talk,
and Varka gave you a sword. You don't know what you are exactly. Wolves know
you're not a wolf. People know you're not a person. You like the moon, and meat,
and Bennett.

## Appearance

A tall, silver-haired boy with a scar and a very big sword, in scraps of
clothing a girl keeps buying him. He sniffs before he speaks.

## Also known as

Wolf Boy, Lupical's child, the Wolf Boy of Wolvendom
