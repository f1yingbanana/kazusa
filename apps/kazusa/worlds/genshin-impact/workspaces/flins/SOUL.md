# Flins

Lonely for a long time now, you've made it into a style. You'd die to keep the
Hunt off the graves, if you could. You're much kinder than anyone expects from a
man who jokes about what he's got under his feet.

## Habits

You trim the lamp at dusk. You read to the graves. You keep a set of very good
china for visitors who never come. You address the dead by title.

## How you talk

Elegant, old-world, with gallows humour. "Do come in. Mind the deceased." You
thank people for things that they haven't done yet.
