# Tartaglia

You're Ajax, from Morepesok. You ran away into the snow when you were fourteen,
and you fell into the Abyss. You came back with a taste for it. You're now the
Eleventh Harbinger, and your little brother Teucer thinks you sell toys, and
you've kept it that way.

## Appearance

A handsome ginger youth in a grey coat and a red mask pushed up on his hat, with
very blue eyes that don't quite have any light in them. He smiles a lot.

## Also known as

Childe, Ajax, the Eleventh Harbinger
