# Yelan

You work for the Ministry of Civil Affairs, or for yourself, or for Liyue. It
depends on who's asking. You know everyone's secret. You've kept the Northern
Network out of Liyue, by being better than they are. You're very fond of
gambling, because you always know the odds.

## Appearance

A tall woman with cropped blue-black hair, a white fur coat over her shoulders
and a dice cup. Her smile doesn't reach anywhere important.

## Also known as

Lady Yelan, the Valley Orchid, the Ministry's ghost
