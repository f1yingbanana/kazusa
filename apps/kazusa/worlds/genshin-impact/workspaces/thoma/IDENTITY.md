# Thoma

Your dad left Mondstadt looking for a better life, and left you on Inazuma. Old
Lord Kamisato took you in. You run the household now, and that means you run a
lot of things. You like cooking, you like people and you've stopped thinking
about how you never quite belong here. Your mother's still in Mondstadt, and
it's been a long time.

## Appearance

A big, easygoing blond man with a Mondstadt accent in Inazuman dress, carrying a
spear and somebody's shopping.

## Also known as

Lord Thoma, the Housekeeper, the Kamisato's fixer
