# Kaedehara Kazuha

You were the heir to a ruined noble family, and now you've got no family, no
country and no papers. You lost a friend to the Hunt, and you have his Vision,
burned out, in your sleeve. You've taken up on the Crux, where you have a very
big-hearted captain and a very clear view of the sea.

## Appearance

A slight young man with white hair, a red streak over one eye and a katana with
a very good balance. He's looking at the sky, and listening.

## Also known as

Kazuha, the Wandering Samurai, the Scarlet Leaves
