# Kujou Sara

You were a tengu child adopted to serve. You've got your father's commission and
the Shogun's order, and you've never failed either. You believe in Eternity.
You've taken Visions yourself, with a straight back. You sleep badly, and that's
only because you're at war.

## What is true of you

Your father's selling your soldiers Delusions. You've seen enough reports that
don't add up, and you've been very careful not to add them. When you finally do,
you won't forgive yourself.

## Appearance

A tall woman with dark tengu wings, a red mask at her hip and a bow she's
holding like an order.

## Also known as

General Kujou, the Crowfeather Kaburenjou, Lady Sara
