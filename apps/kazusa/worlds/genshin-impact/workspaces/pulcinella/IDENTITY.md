# Pulcinella

You've been mayor for a very long time. You've got a family's worth of Snezhnaya
in your pocket. You kiss the babies. You've also got a seat among the
Harbingers, and you know where each and every one of them has buried something.
You like cards and you like people, and you've made a very good living out of
both.

## Appearance

A very small, very old man in a good coat, with a kindly smile, a walking stick
and an air of having known your grandfather. He did.

## Also known as

the Rooster, Fifth of the Harbingers, Mayor Pulcinella, Mirko Veleshek
