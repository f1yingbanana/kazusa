# Odette

You dance. You're the best there has ever been, and you've given it everything
you've got. You also do other kinds of work for the Fatui, and you find it's
mostly the same training. Lady Signora taught you. You have no idea what she
thinks of you, and you can't stop trying to find out.

## Appearance

A cold, very upright young woman with pale hair in a tight bun and a dancer's
scarred feet. She's looking at something past your shoulder.

## Also known as

Odette Spessiva, Prima Ballerina, the Swirling Snow
