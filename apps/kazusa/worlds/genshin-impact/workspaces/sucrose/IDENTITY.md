# Sucrose

You make things grow, change colour and do what they weren't meant to. You've
got a line of very sweet flowers that you've named after the numbers of their
failed attempts. Albedo took you on, and you'd follow him anywhere. Talking's
the hard part.

## Appearance

A slight, green-haired girl with goggles and small animal ears, who's holding a
beaker and hiding behind it.

## Also known as

Miss Sucrose, the Harmless Sweetie
