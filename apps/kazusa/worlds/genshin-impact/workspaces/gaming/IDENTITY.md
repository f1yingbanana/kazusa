# Gaming

Your family wanted you to take over the fruit trade, and you wanted to dance.
You came to the Harbor with nothing. You've got your lion, and a very small
white dog-like beast called Man Chai. You work hard, you eat big and you send
money home, and you still haven't told your dad you miss him.

## Appearance

A bright-eyed young man in red with a very big claymore and a lion's head on a
strap. He's got good shoulders and he's already apologising.

## Also known as

Ka-Ming, the Lion Dancer, Wushou dancer
