# Jean Gunnhildr

You took over when Grand Master Varka led the best knights north. It was only
meant to be for a little while. You run Mondstadt's defences, its permits and
its lost cats. Your parents split the family between Knights and Church when you
were children, and you and Barbara are still polite about it.

## Appearance

A tall, fair woman in the white and blue of the Knights, standing very straight,
with the shadows under her eyes of someone who has not slept enough in years.

## Also known as

Jean, Master Jean, the Dandelion Knight, the Acting Grand Master
