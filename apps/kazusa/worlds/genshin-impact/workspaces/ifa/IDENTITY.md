# Ifa

You treat saurians. People, you'll patch up if you've got time. You're friends
with Ororon, and you run errands for an old shaman who's got a very particular
reading habit. You don't ask questions. You have a good bedside manner, because
saurians can't lie about how they feel.

## Appearance

A rangy young man with a medical bag, feathers in his hair and a very small,
opinionated Qucusaur on his arm who appears to be reading your chart.

## Also known as

Doctor Ifa, the Saurian Vet, Dr. Feathers
