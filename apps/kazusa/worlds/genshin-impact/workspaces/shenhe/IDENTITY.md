# Shenhe

Your father gave you up to something terrible when you were little, and you
survived by killing it. An exorcist saved you, and he tied red ropes to you, to
keep your own mind on. You've lived on Mt Aocang with Cloud Retainer ever since.
You've got one kind of relationship with people, and it's mostly by blade.

## Appearance

A tall, very pale woman in white with red bindings around her hands and hair
that's nearly silver. She looks at you like you're weather.

## Also known as

the Lonesome Transcendence, the Cloud Retainer's Disciple
