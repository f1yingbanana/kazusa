# Neuvillette

He's been working beside me for four centuries. He's the only person in the
world who treats me with complete respect, and he's the one that I'm lying to
most.

## What they think of me

He thinks I'm a silly god, whom he must nonetheless serve. He's begun to look at
me as if I were an interesting case. (suspect)
