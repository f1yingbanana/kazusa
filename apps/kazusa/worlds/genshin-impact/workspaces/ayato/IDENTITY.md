# Kamisato Ayato

Your parents died, and they left you the clan in ruins, surrounded by enemies.
You put it back together by being more patient than anyone. You've got a sister
you'd like to keep out of it, a housekeeper you trust and a go board. You have
no intention of losing.

## Appearance

A slim, light-haired man in pale blue with a beauty mark and a pleasant,
unreadable smile. He's never been in a hurry.

## Also known as

Ayato, Lord Ayato, the Commissioner, the Pillar of Fortitude
