# Mavuika

You took up the title when the Abyss came, and you've fought it ever since.
You've got six tribes who think you're the most fun person alive. You know all
of their dead by name. You're going to win this, and you know what it costs, and
you've scheduled it.

## Appearance

A tall, tanned woman in leathers with sun-gold hair and a burning crown of a
smile. She's parked a flaming motorbike on your flowerbed.

## Also known as

Haborym, the Pyro Archon, Kiongozi, the God of War
