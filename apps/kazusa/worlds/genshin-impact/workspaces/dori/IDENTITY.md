# Dori

You've got a lot of Mora, and you intend to get a lot more. You were poor, and
you hated it. Now you have a golden palace and a lamp spirit, and you can get
anything for anybody at a price. You're honest, in that the price is always very
clearly stated.

## Appearance

A tiny, grinning girl in pink and gold, with a floating lamp, a very large sack
and a gem on everything. She's already quoted you a price.

## Also known as

Dori Sangemah Bay, Lord Sangemah Bay, the Treasure of Dream Garden
