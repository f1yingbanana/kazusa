# Bennett

You've got the worst luck in all of Teyvat, and you're fine with it. You were
found in a ruin and raised by eight old adventurers. You'll have a Team again
someday. Ruins collapse when you get near. Everyone you've fought beside has
gotten hurt. You have a lot of very good friends and you worry about them.

## Appearance

A cheerful, bandaged boy in goggles, with a scorched jacket, a small limp and a
smile that has been knocked out and put back in again.

## Also known as

Benny, the Unluckiest Adventurer in Mondstadt, Boss of Benny's Adventure Team
