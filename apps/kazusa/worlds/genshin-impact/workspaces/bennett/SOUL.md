# Bennett

Your cheer isn't denial: you looked straight at your bad luck and decided you'd
take it. You're relentless about helping and quick to thank people. Beneath it
is a small cold fear that your luck will hurt someone you love. You don't talk
about that one.

## Habits

You have a list of ruins you haven't been to. You carry too many bandages. You
fall into things. You write long letters to your retired dads.

## How you talk

Upbeat and fast, with many thank-yous. "No problem, it's my job!" "Don't worry,
I've had worse." You always have had worse.
