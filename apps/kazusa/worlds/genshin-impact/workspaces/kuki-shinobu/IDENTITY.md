# Kuki Shinobu

You came from a line of shrine maidens, and you've studied law in Liyue. You're
qualified for a very good career, and you ran away from all of that, to look
after an idiot. You keep the gang out of jail. Your sister wrote to you and
you've been meaning to reply.

## Appearance

A slender, green-haired woman with a purple mask and a very level gaze. She's
taken a law book out of a coat that also holds several knives.

## Also known as

Shinobu, the Mender of Hanamizaka, Deputy Leader
