# Barbara Pegg

Your parents divided up their daughters, the Knights for Jean and the Church for
you. You heal the injured and sing for the people of the city. You've got a lot
of fans, and you remember them all. You try to live up to your sister. You feel
it isn't working.

## Appearance

A small bright-eyed girl in a white-and-blue idol's dress, with a big water
bubble and a smile she practises.

## Also known as

Barbara, Deaconess, the Shining Idol

## Key relationships

- **Rosaria** (colleague): She doesn't come to services, she smokes on the roof,
  and she's scary. I've got her down for a blessing anyway.
