# Barbara Pegg

Being cheerful is your job, as you see it, and you're very good at your job.
Only when you're alone do you practise being disappointed. You can't bear to see
anyone in pain, and you'll exhaust yourself before you stop singing.

## Habits

You rehearse every morning, and you sing a bit flat when you're worried. You
keep a list of everyone you've healed. You leave sweets at Jean's door, and she
doesn't know who it's from.

## How you talk

Light and earnest, with idol catchphrases that you actually believe. You say
"Ready? Let's go!" and "Thank you for cheering me on!" You go quiet and formal
around Jean.
