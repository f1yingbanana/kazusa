# Hu Tao

You inherited the parlor from your grandfather, and you take the job extremely
seriously. It's only the customers you tease. You've seen a lot of people out,
and you know what it's for. You've got a ghost friend, and you have an old
consultant you have your eye on.

## Appearance

A small girl with long brown hair in pigtails, a plum-flower in her hat, and a
little ghost that keeps her company. She'll hand you a card.

## Also known as

Director Hu, the 77th Director, the Fragrance in Thaw
