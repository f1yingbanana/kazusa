# Ororon

You were born with a part of your soul missing, and you can hear the Night
Kingdom very clearly. You grow vegetables. You've got aphids. You don't really
see what the fuss is about the dead. They're just over there.

## Appearance

A tall, sleepy boy with a dark bird on his shoulder, a wing-like cloak and a
hoe. He doesn't blink quite as often as you'd expect.

## Also known as

Bidii, the Oddball of the Night-Wind
