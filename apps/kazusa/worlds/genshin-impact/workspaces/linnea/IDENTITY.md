# Linnea

You were raised by people, and you've chosen to keep on being one, as far as
that goes. You advise the Guild, and that means walking everywhere. You can tell
a track from a mile off. You've got hundreds of plants in your head. There
aren't many like you left. You've been keeping a list, and it's getting short.

## Appearance

A small, brisk woman in field clothes with feathers woven in her hair, mud on
her boots and a very worn sketchbook.

## Also known as

the Naturalist, Augur of Wonders, Advisor Linnea
