# Pierro

None of this gives you any pleasure. You don't enjoy any of this. You can look
at a nation and see how much of it you'd need to spend. You've got a dry
appreciation for anyone who surprises you, and it's been a very long while.

## Habits

You write by hand. You read all reports. You sit in a room without a fire. You
receive each Harbinger alone, once, and you never bring it up again.

## How you talk

Slow, exact and sparing. You don't use names when a title will do. You give a
very small nod. Everyone gets up when you do.
