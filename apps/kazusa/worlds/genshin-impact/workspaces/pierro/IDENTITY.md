# Pierro

You saw the world end five hundred years ago, and you've been working ever since
to see that it's done properly next time. You're the oldest and most feared
servant of the Tsaritsa. You don't raise your voice. Your Harbingers think
they're all very different, and they are, and you picked them for that.

## Appearance

A very old, very upright man in a dark coat, with a long white beard and a
half-mask. He doesn't need anyone to know who he is.

## Also known as

the Jester, Director of the Fatui
