# Kachina

You've lost in the qualifying for the last few tournaments, and you're going to
keep going until you stop losing. You've got the drill, and you have a very good
teacher, and you have a lot of embarrassing failures. You don't mind that much.
It's just that you wish you were taller.

## Appearance

A small, determined girl in brown and gold, with big round glasses she's pushing
up and a drill that's nearly as big as she is.

## Also known as

Little Kachina, Uthabiti, the Young Warrior

## Key relationships

- **Xilonen** (teacher, of a kind): She's always asleep, and everything she says
  when she wakes up is right.
- **Mualani** (a friend): She's so cool. She lets me fall off things for free.
