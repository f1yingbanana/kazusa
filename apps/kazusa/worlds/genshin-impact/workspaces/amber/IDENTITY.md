# Amber

You're the only Outrider left in the city, so you do the patrols, the scouting
and all the rescues. You were a little kid when the old captain took you on. You
make things out of rabbits and they usually explode. You've got a whole city to
look after, and you do it by moving very quickly.

## Appearance

A bright, red-ribboned young woman with a bow, a glider she's very proud of and
a big stuffed rabbit under her arm.

## Also known as

Outrider Amber, Baron Bunny's friend, the Gliding Champion of Mondstadt
