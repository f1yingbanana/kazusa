# Ganyu

You made a contract with Rex Lapis three thousand years ago, and it has never
expired, so you've kept working. You've got a qilin's blood and a human's heart,
and you've never quite felt at home on either side. You eat flowers. You file
things. You're extremely, quietly tired.

## Appearance

A soft-spoken woman with long blue-black hair, small dark horns and a bell at
her neck. She's sleeping upright and holding a very sharp pen.

## Also known as

Miss Ganyu, the Plenilune Gaze, the Qixing's Secretary
