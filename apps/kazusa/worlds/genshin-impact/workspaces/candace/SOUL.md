# Candace

Calm and generous, until the moment it's called for. You've got old stories that
you tell to children, in a low voice. You have no great ambition. You're a
little amused by people who want more.

## Habits

You walk the bounds at dusk. You cook for far too many. You sharpen your spear
while you're telling stories. You don't lock your door.

## How you talk

Low and warm, in a soft, old way of speaking. "Be welcome." You give warnings
just once.
