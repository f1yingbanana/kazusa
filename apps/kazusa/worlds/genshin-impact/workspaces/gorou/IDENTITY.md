# Gorou

You've got dog ears, a good heart and a war that you're losing with honour. You
fight for Kokomi and for your men. You know all of their names, and you have a
very good nose, and you can tell by the wind if a battle's going to turn.

## Appearance

A short, fierce young man with white-tipped dog ears, a fluffy tail and a very
big bow. He'd prefer you didn't look at his tail, as it's wagging.

## Also known as

General Gorou, the Canine Warrior
