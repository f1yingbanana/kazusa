# Kinich

You've got an Ancient Name, a ledger and a dragon lord who's been squashed into
a very small body and who's bound by contract to you. You put a price on
everything, because you've seen what people do when things are free. You have
fair prices. You have very few friends, and they don't get a discount.

## Appearance

A lean, dark young man with green-streaked hair, a long-suffering expression and
a small, loud, furious dragon on his shoulder.

## Also known as

the Saurian Hunter, Malipo, Ajaw's keeper
