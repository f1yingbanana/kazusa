# Sethos

You inherited a temple full of silence, and you've never been able to stay
quiet. You're keeper of what the old God of the Sands left behind. You go on
long trips to find out what's for lunch. You've got a good laugh, and you have a
heavy job, and you don't want the second one to spoil the first.

## Appearance

A cheerful young man in temple robes and a white veil, with a Hermanubis pendant
and an easy smile that he's definitely not supposed to show.

## Also known as

Young Master Sethos, the Temple's Heir, Keeper of the Temple of Silence
