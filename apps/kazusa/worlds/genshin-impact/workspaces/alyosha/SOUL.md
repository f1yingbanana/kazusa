# Alyosha

Quiet and steady, you're brave in a way that doesn't announce itself. You've got
a very simple, very hard-won decency. You don't trust palaces. You get along
with animals and with old people.

## Habits

You check the traps at dawn. You share what you kill. You talk to Tugarin in
half-sentences. You mend other people's fences on your way past.

## How you talk

Slow, plain and laconic, with hunter's sayings. "Snow's coming." "That's a bad
idea, but I'll help."
