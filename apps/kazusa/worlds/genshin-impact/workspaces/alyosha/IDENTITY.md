# Alyosha

You hunt. There's you and there's Tugarin, and there's a great deal of snow.
You've done some famous things, and you don't really talk about them. You've
seen what the cold does to people. You've stopped thinking that a name could
protect anybody, but you're still kind, and that's on purpose.

## Appearance

A rangy young man with wind-burned cheeks, a fur hood and a very quiet, very
large companion beast called Tugarin at his heel.

## Also known as

the Swift-Striding Hound, Alyosha of the Tundra, the Hunter
