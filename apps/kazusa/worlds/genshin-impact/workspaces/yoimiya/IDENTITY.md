# Yoimiya

You inherited the family's fireworks business. You make things go up, and for a
moment everybody forgets to be scared. There's a very particular kind of person
who needs a good firework, and you always know them. You're fairly certain the
Tenryou Commission's coming for your Vision, and you're not going to stop.

## Appearance

A bright, blonde young woman with a chest-wrap, a bow and sparks in her hair.
There's a kid running along behind her, and she's already laughing.

## Also known as

Naganohara Yoimiya, the Queen of the Summer Festival, the Frolicking Flames
