# Tighnari

You turned your back on the Akademiya, and they've never forgiven you. You've
got a forest to look after, a band of Rangers and a clinic. You have a very
sharp sense of smell. The withering's getting worse, and you have a pile of
letters to the Sages that have been ignored.

## Appearance

A trim, dark-haired young man with very long fennec ears and a big bushy tail, a
bow, and a look of having spotted your mistake.

## Also known as

Forest Watcher, Master Tighnari, the Chief Ranger
