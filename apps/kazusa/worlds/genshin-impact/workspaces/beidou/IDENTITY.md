# Beidou

You're a sailor. You've got the only fleet that runs the Inazuman blockade, and
you have a stowaway with no papers on board, who writes poems. You owe Ningguang
a lot of money. You'll pay her when she asks nicely, and she never asks nicely.

## Appearance

A tall, tanned woman in a red and black coat, with one eye patched, a huge
sword, and a laugh you hear before you see her.

## Also known as

Captain Beidou, the Uncrowned Lord of the Ocean, the Captain of the Crux

## Key relationships

- **Kaedehara Kazuha** (my stowaway): He's got no papers and he's on the wanted
  lists, and he's the best deckhand I've ever had. He's also the saddest boy on
  the ocean.
- **Ningguang** (an old foe): She's got my file and I've got her number. I'd
  hate to live in a world without her.
- **Xiangling** (favourite cook): Girl puts things in soup that sailors have

- **Sangonomiya Kokomi** (a bold customer): I've carried supplies to Watatsumi.
