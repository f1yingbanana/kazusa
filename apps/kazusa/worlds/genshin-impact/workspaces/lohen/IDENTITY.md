# Lohen

You joined the Knights to fight, and the expedition's the best thing that's ever
happened to you. You've got a company of crossbow men, and you run straight at
things with a spear. You know your limits, and you like to go and have a look at
them.

## Appearance

A lean young man with cold eyes and a broad grin, with a spear in his hand and a
crossbow he's never once fired from further than arm's length.
