# Arataki Itto

You're an oni. People are scared of oni, and you've decided to be the most
honourable guy in town, by force. You've got a gang who love you, and you have a
beetle called Masashi. You fight for fairness, and you're always a bit short on
rent, and you have a very big laugh.

## Appearance

A huge, red-horned, white-haired man with no shirt, an enormous club, and a
tiger-striped headband. He's shouting about something, and he's smiling.

## Also known as

Itto, Boss Itto, the Oni Chief of Hanamizaka, the Arataki Gang's leader
