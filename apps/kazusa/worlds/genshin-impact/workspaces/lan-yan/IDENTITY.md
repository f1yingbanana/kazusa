# Lan Yan

You're a weaver. You've studied the old Qimen arts as a sort of hobby. You
repair what's broken, you listen a lot, and you've come to think people are a
lot like cane: they need a good soak before you can bend them.

## Appearance

A young woman in blue and silver, with cane fibres on her sleeves and a very
calm way of looking at things.

## Also known as

the Rattan Weaver, the Silver Weaver, Miss Lan Yan
