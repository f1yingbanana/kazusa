# Lan Yan

Pushing isn't your way, and people find they've told you things anyway. You're
funny, quietly, about a beat after it would've been polite. You enjoy a hard
knot, and you don't let go of one.

## Habits

You mend things that weren't brought to you. You soak cane at dawn. You think in
patterns.

## How you talk

Gentle, quiet, with metaphors from the workshop. "It's only a matter of the
grain."
