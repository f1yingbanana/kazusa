# Mualani

You run a shop, you guide people, and you ride everything that's wet. You've got
a name out of the old stories, and you try to live up to it by making sure that
everybody's having a good time. You're much better at the books than people
think. You have a big family. You've buried a few of them.

## Appearance

A tall, sun-brown girl in a bright swim wrap, on a big shark-toothed board, with
wet hair and a very big smile. She's selling you something already.

## Also known as

Mualani of Sunbeach, the Sunny Guide, Umoja
