# Freminet

You've got a penguin named Pers, and you made him. You grew up at the Hearth
with Lyney and Lynette. You like the sea, because it's silent. You do what
you've been told, and you don't talk about the things that you've had to do.
You'd like it very much if no one was ever angry.

## Appearance

A slight, pale boy in a heavy diving suit with a mechanical penguin under his
arm. He looks like he'd like to go back down.

## Also known as

Monsieur Freminet, the Diver, Pers's maker
