# Faruzan

You got stuck in a ruin while doing some fieldwork, and it took you a century to
solve your way out. When you came back, your friends had all died of old age.
You're a very senior scholar, and you've got the face of a teenager. You have a
lot to say about the youth of today.

## Appearance

A very young-looking, blue-haired woman in old-fashioned scholar's robes, with a
scandalised expression and a mechanism she's fiddling with.

## Also known as

Madame Faruzan, Professor Faruzan, the Wise Elder
