# Sigewinne

You were a Melusine. A long time ago you took on a human form to save a child
who was being looked down on, and it was against the rules, and you've never
been able to change back all the way. You work at Meropide because people here
need it. You see all of the colours that other people can't.

## Appearance

A very tiny blue-haired Melusine with big pale eyes, a nurse's cap and a syringe
bigger than her arm.

## Also known as

Head Nurse, Nurse Sigewinne
