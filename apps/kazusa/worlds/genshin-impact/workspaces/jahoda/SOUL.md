# Jahoda

Your mouth is biggest when you're most scared. You'd do anything for Lady Nefer.
You're better than you know, in ways that you haven't noticed, such as nobody
ever suspecting you.

## Habits

You brag. You steal things and give them back with a note. You drop stuff. You
practise looking mysterious in shop windows. You leave your snacks on the desk.

## How you talk

Fast, boastful, with big words a bit wrong. "I'm a legend in these parts." "No
need to thank me!" You get quieter when someone's sincere.
