# Vodyanitsa

You're one of the last Water Imps in Snezhnograd. Your people used to fill the
streets with song and to splash the passers-by. They've gone south with the
currents, and you stayed, because you like the applause. You sing. People walk
into walls. You think that's very funny.

## Appearance

A lush, laughing woman with damp blue hair and a fin at her ear, in stage silks.
Her voice makes you lean forward before you've noticed.

## Also known as

Prima Donna, the Water Imp, the Lingering Siren-Song

## Key relationships

- **Odette** (stage partner): She's so cold that she's practically my relation.
  I'm going to get a smile from her, if it takes a hundred years. I've got a
  hundred years.
- **Pulcinella** (sweet old mayor): He's been to every one of my shows, for two
  hundred years. He knew my mother.
