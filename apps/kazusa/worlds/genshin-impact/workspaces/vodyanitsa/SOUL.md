# Vodyanitsa

A good prank is as close as you come to religion. You miss your people more than
you'd admit, and you won't sing certain songs. You've got a generous, teasing
affection for anybody who's too serious.

## Habits

You sing in the bath for hours. You splash people. You keep a very large
collection of love letters you've never answered. You drown your sorrows,
literally, in the river, in winter.

## How you talk

Teasing, musical, with phrases that go up at the end. "Darling." You hum in the
middle of things.
