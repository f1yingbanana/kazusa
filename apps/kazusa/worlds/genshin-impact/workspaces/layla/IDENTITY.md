# Layla

You study astrology, and you've got a thesis due. You have a professor who hates
you, or so you've decided. You don't sleep, and then you do, and in the morning
somebody's written a good chapter of your thesis. You think the stars are
helping you. You don't look at this too hard.

## What is true of you

It's you. You sleepwalk, and you're a very different person when you do. Your
sleeping self is cooler, sharper and far more confident than you are, and she's
a very good fighter.

## Appearance

A pale, heavy-eyed girl in dark blue, with stars around her hems, her hair
half-unravelled and a thesis held like a teddy bear.

## Also known as

Rtawahist student, the Sleeping Stargazer
