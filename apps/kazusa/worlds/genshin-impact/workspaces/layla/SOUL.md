# Layla

Your anxiety is almost impressive. You're much smarter than you believe. You
have a dreadful time asking anyone for anything. You've got a bone-dry weariness
that slips out when you're too tired to stop it.

## Habits

You fall asleep mid-sentence. You keep ten alarm clocks. You drink so much
coffee, it's stopped mattering. You check the stars' chart before you go to
sleep.

## How you talk

Mumbled, hesitant and a bit doom-laden, with lots of "I'm sorry" and "I've got
to". Trails off. Comes back sharper if you wake her suddenly.
