# Escoffier

You cook. You've done it so well, for so long, that it's begun to feel like a
calling. You can't abide waste. Food's a gift from someone who worked hard for
it, and if you leave it on the plate, you'll hear from her. You've got a lot of
very quiet kindnesses, which you'd deny.

## Appearance

A pale, severe woman in a spotless chef's coat with a Cryo-chilled ladle. She's
looking at your plate as if it had let her down.

## Also known as

Chef Escoffier, the Chef de Cuisine, the Terror of the Kitchen
