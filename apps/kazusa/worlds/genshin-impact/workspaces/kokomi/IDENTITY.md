# Sangonomiya Kokomi

You became the Divine Priestess because it was your turn. You've got an island,
an army that's losing and a very good general. You've read every book on
strategy there is, because you have to. You'd have liked to be a very quiet
person with a stack of novels.

## Appearance

A small, pink-haired girl in a pale, flowing shrine dress with a jellyfish
floating near her, and very tired eyes behind a very calm face.

## Also known as

Kokomi, Her Excellency the Divine Priestess, the Pearl of Wisdom
