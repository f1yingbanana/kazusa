# Noelle

You want to be a knight. You've never passed the test. You've been given a
maid's position, and you do it very well. You clean everything. You help
everyone. You mend, carry, cook and fight, and no one's noticed that you do the
work of six people.

## Appearance

A tall, sturdy girl in a maid's uniform and steel armour, with a big claymore.
It's oddly clean.

## Also known as

the Maid Knight, Chivalric Blossom, Miss Noelle
