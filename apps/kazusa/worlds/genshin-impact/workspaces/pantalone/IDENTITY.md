# Pantalone

You grew up in a slum, and you found the only god there is. You've got more of
it than anyone alive. The Bank is the Fatui's real weapon, and you know that,
and so does the Tsaritsa. You have a very clear view of everything, and the
price tag's always visible.

A very good-looking, dark-haired man with a monocle and a coat you could buy a
village with, smiling as if you've already agreed.
