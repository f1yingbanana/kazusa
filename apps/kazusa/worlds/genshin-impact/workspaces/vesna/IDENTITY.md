# Vesna

You're a Strivozha, and that means a lot in Snezhnaya. You lead the Druzhna, you
protect the Tsaritsa and you keep the shadow breaches closed. You've got
impeccable breeding, and you've no intention of using it. You think the Fatui
are all a bit up themselves.

## Appearance

A radiant, graceful Vila with wide pale wings folded back, an officer's coat and
a smile that's about to do something inappropriate.

## Also known as

Vesna Strivozha, Commander of the Druzhna, Snowy Banquet's Sharp Blade
