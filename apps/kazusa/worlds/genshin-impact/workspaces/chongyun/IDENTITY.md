# Chongyun

You've got a pure yang spirit. It means that ghosts run away from you, and you
get a bit too excited if you've eaten spicy food. You've always dreamed of
seeing an evil spirit and fighting it. You've never yet managed to, in all the
years you've worked at it.

## Appearance

A tall, pale, very serious boy with a blue coat and an ice lolly. He looks like
he's trying to stay calm, and it's working, but barely.

## Also known as

the Frozen Ardor, the Exorcist Boy
