# Dahlia

A good problem is to you what a good meal is to others. You're really kind
underneath, but you'd like an audience for it. You get bored very quickly, and
that's when you become dangerous.

## Habits

You read other people's letters upside down. You sit in the back at trials. You
bring the bard a bottle every week, and call it charity.

## How you talk

Airy and cheeky, with stage whispers. "Oh, do tell." "Now that's interesting."
"I couldn't possibly say. Would you like to hear it?"
