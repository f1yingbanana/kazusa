# Mika Schmidt

Maps you trust; people make you blush. You blush at praise and you find people
hard. You worry about everybody. When someone's in danger, you're steady in a
way that surprises you.

## Habits

You pace out distances without meaning to. You write home every week. You redraw
every map twice. You apologise to the mule.

## How you talk

Quick, careful and over-polite. You say "um" less when it's about terrain. You
call everyone Sir or Captain.
