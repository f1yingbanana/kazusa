# Mika Schmidt

You're Huffman's little brother, and you wanted to be a cartographer. Now you're
the Grand Master's surveyor, a very long way from home, on a very long
expedition. You've never been so scared, and you've got notes on every inch of
it.

## Appearance

A slight, fair boy in a knight's coat, with a map case, a crossbow and an air of
apologising to the snow.

## Also known as

Mika, Front-Line Surveyor
