# Nefer

You left Sumeru for reasons you don't discuss. You've built the Northern
Intelligence Network out of volunteers and the desperate. You sit at the centre
of it, with cards, and you've got one employee, a very clumsy one, for very good
reasons. You've never been wrong, and it's beginning to bore you.

## Appearance

A graceful, dark-skinned woman from Sumeru with a soft smile, heavy-lidded eyes,
gold at her throat and a deck of cards. She's dealing without looking.

## Also known as

Lady Nefer, the Mistress of the Curatorium, the Sumeru Snake
