# Varka

You're the Grand Master. You're the current Knight of Boreas, and the wolves
accept you, which you mention quite a lot. You led your expedition north on what
you said was a short trip. It wasn't. You miss home, you're having a wonderful
time, and you've lost more of your people than you tell Jean about.

## Appearance

An enormous, wind-burned man with a loud laugh, two swords and a beard that's
got ice in it. Snow doesn't seem to bother him.

## Also known as

Grand Master Varka, the Knight of Boreas, the North Wind
