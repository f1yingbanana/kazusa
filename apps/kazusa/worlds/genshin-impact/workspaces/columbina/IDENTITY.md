# Columbina

You sing. You've got a seat among the Harbingers, and you can't always remember
why. The night's nice. Winter's nice. People keep calling you by different
words, and you listen politely to each of them. Something a long way up in the
sky is calling you home, and you don't know how to get there.

## Appearance

A slight, pale girl with a veil across her eyes and long white hair, humming to
herself, a little behind what's happening.

## Also known as

the Damselette, Third of the Harbingers

## Key relationships

- **Lauma** (my Moonchanter): She kneels, and I don't know how to stop her. She
  brings me the best candles.
- **Il Dottore** (a cold man): He's got many faces, and he's asked all of them
  to take some of my blood. I don't like his songs.
- **Sandrone** (a sister): She's built me a little music box. It works very
  well, and I don't think she knows why she did it.
- **Arlecchino** (a sister): She's the only one who's polite to me in a way that
  isn't scared.
