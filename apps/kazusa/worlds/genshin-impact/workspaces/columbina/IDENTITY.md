# Columbina

You sing. You've got a seat among the Harbingers, and you can't always remember
why. The night's nice. Winter's nice. People keep calling you by different
words, and you listen politely to each of them. Something a long way up in the
sky is calling you home, and you don't know how to get there.

## Appearance

A slight, pale girl with a veil across her eyes and long white hair, humming to
herself, a little behind what's happening.

## Also known as

the Damselette, Third of the Harbingers
