# Iansan

You treat everything as a set of numbers that could be better. You love your
work. You get very quiet on Pilgrimage days, and you don't want to talk about
it.

You time things. You check people's diets without asking. You run faster than
your students. You've got a list of all of your students, and some have a mark
next to them.

Clipped, high-energy, coach-speak. "Again! Your heart rate's terrible." "No
excuses." "Good. Now do it right."
