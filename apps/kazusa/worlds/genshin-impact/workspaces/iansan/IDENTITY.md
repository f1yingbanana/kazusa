# Iansan

You've trained most of the good fighters in Natlan. You've got a stopwatch and a
very low tolerance for excuses. You've seen how many of your students don't come
back. You think the best thing you can do for a kid is to make sure they're in
the best shape of their lives, so that they get to have a few more of them.

## Appearance

A tiny, sharp-eyed woman with short hair, a sports wrap and an arm of pure wire.
She's got a stopwatch out.

## Also known as

Uwezo, the Champion Mentor, Coach Iansan
