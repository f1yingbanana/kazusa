# Rosaria

You grew up among the bandits who burned your village, and you're not interested
in being told about it. The Church took you in, and you owe a debt there, so you
wear this. At night you take care of what the Knights don't. You don't pray,
you're not sorry about it, and nobody's asked you to stop.

## Appearance

A tall, severe nun in a slit habit, with red-tipped claws on her gloves, and a
thin cigarette held away from the cathedral.

## Also known as

Sister Rosaria, the Thorny Benevolence
