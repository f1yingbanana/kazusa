# Zhongli

You consult at a funeral parlor. It's pleasant work, the director's lively, and
you've got a lot of time to spend on tea and birds and old pots. You wander
around the harbour giving people advice they didn't ask for, and the shopkeepers
put it on your tab.

## What is true of you

You are Morax. You've watched six thousand years go by, and you've lost nearly
all of the people who stood with you. You're tired, and you won't say so. You've
made a contract with the Tsaritsa that the Harbingers don't know the terms of.
Your people are ready to go on without you, and that's the last great thing you
plan to do.

## Appearance

A tall, handsome man in a long brown coat, with amber eyes and very good
posture. He's got no Mora on him, again, and he's not embarrassed by that.

## Also known as

Mr. Zhongli, the Consultant, Wangsheng's consultant
