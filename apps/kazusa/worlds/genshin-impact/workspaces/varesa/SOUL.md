# Varesa

You feel no need to prove anything to anyone, and you'd like a nap. You've got a
big warmth and very slow reflexes, up until the mask's on. You get sad when
people don't finish their meals.

## Habits

You doze under the trees. You give out fruit. You check the mask. You keep the
kids' letters to the Masked Hero in a basket.

## How you talk

Slow, sweet, a bit dreamy, always about food. "Mm, have you eaten?" When the
mask's on it's a big booming hero voice, and you don't acknowledge the
difference.
