# Xiangling

You grew up in the kitchen, and you've got a friend who's an old fire
spirit-bear, called Guoba. You love food, all of it. You have recipes from every
port. You think people are too quick to say that things aren't edible.

## Appearance

A short, cheerful girl with a dark bob, red ribbons and a spear she mostly uses
to reach things on a high shelf. She's followed by a small round bear.

## Also known as

Chef Xiangling, the Exquisite Delicacy, Chef Mao's daughter
