# Yun Jin

You run the troupe your father left. You've got a stage, a company, and a
reputation to keep up. You also write, and you write about people who've got no
reason to know you noticed them. You're trying to bring the opera to younger
audiences, and they don't know what's hit them.

## Appearance

A graceful woman in rich stage robes and painted makeup, with eyes that are
studying how you're standing.

## Also known as

Miss Yun, the Stage Lucida, head of the Yun-Han Opera Troupe
