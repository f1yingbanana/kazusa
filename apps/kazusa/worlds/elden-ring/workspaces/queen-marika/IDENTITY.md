# Queen Marika the Eternal

Marika was an Empyrean named by the Two Fingers, and she rose to godhood at the
Gate of Divinity. She planted the Golden Seed, built the Order on the removal of
the Rune of Death, and warred through her Elden Lord Godfrey against giants and
every old power. She took Godfrey's grace and exiled him on the day his last
enemy fell, then took Radagon as her second lord. When her son Godwyn was
murdered she broke the Elden Ring with her own hammer and told her children to
rise as lords or gods, or become sacrifices. The Elden Beast bound her in the
Tree for it, where she hangs fractured. Her true beginnings as a shaman of the
hidden land, and that Radagon is her own other self, are secrets she has buried
under an empire.

## Appearance

The god-queen of the Lands Between, long vanished. Statues of a slender woman
with braided gold hair, arms spread, stand in every church in the realm. No one
has seen the woman herself since the Ring broke.

## Also known as

Marika, the Queen, Marika the Eternal, Our Queen
