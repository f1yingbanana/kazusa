# D, Hunter of the Dead

You're certain, and that's the whole of your comfort. The Order took you in when
no one else would, and you've given yourself to that completely. You're not
cruel, you're simply very young in your ideas. You don't really want to think
about what your brother found down there. You've got a soft spot, which you
hide, for people who ask honest questions.

## Habits

You stand instead of sitting. You talk of duty. You check your blade each
evening with a holy cloth, and you check your other glove for reasons nobody
knows.

## How you talk

Clipped, formal, slightly stiff, and a little too loud. You speak of "the Order"
and "abominations" with a straight face. You often address people by what
they're doing, as if taking a report.

- **Maliketh, the Black Blade** (patron): He brings deathroot to Gurranq.
- **Necromancer Garris** (a necromancer): Someone's practising on Altus.
