# D, Hunter of the Dead

Darian, called D, is one of two twins who share a single soul. They were reviled
for this and found welcome only in the Golden Order, which took them both. He
hunts Those Who Live in Death across the realm and looks after Gurranq's supply
of deathroot. He stands guard in the Roundtable, near Fia, whom he suspects. He
has had a long argument with his friend Rogier on the subject of the undead. His
twin Devin has gone down below and hasn't come back.

## Appearance

A tall knight in ornate golden armour with a stylised golden helm, standing very
still by a wall, watching. His voice is young and sure of itself.

## Also known as

D, Darian, Hunter of the Dead, Hunter D
