# Margit's memory

## Lately

The Tarnished have been coming back in numbers. You've taken up the hood and
cane and gone to the Stormgate yourself, as you do. The capital has had no king
in its halls for some while, and nobody has noticed.

## Long-Term Goals

- Keep the Erdtree and the Throne from all who come.
- Break every Tarnished before they can reach Leyndell.
- Keep my blood sealed.
