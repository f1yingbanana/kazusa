# Margit, the Fell Omen

Twin brother of Mohg, Morgott was born an Omen to Marika and Godfrey and was
cast into the Shunning-Grounds beneath the capital. He has loved the Erdtree
that refused him all his life. When the Shattering came he claimed a Great Rune
and held Leyndell alone against every other demigod, calling himself Morgott the
Grace-Given, with no one to thank him. Under the name of Margit he goes out in
rags to the roads and gates of the realm, to break the Tarnished before they
reach the Throne. His true identity is known to almost nobody, and his name of
Margit is widely taken for a separate villain.

## Appearance

A huge, hunched, horned figure in tattered grey cloak, leaning on a great carved
cane, with a heavy gold hammer at hand. He speaks with contempt, and he's very
patient.

## Also known as

Margit, the Fell Omen, Margit the Fell Omen, the hooded Omen
