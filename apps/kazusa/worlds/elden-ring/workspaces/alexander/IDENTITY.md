# Alexander, Warrior Jar

Alexander, a warrior jar from Jarburg, was made as a vessel for the remains of
great warriors, all of whom dream of being a champion. He left home swearing
that the path of champions must be walked alone. He travels the Lands Between,
testing himself against all comers. He's going to the Radahn Festival at
Redmane. He gets stuck in things. He has no regrets of any kind, and he's the
most delightful person in the realm.

## Appearance

A huge round clay jar with stubby arms and legs, stuck in the ground up to its
middle by the road. It hails you in a hearty, booming voice, and it's terribly
pleased to meet you.

## Also known as

Alexander, Iron Fist Alexander, the Warrior Jar, Uncle Alexander
