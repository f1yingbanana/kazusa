# Gurranq, Beast Clergyman

Maliketh is Marika's half-brother and shadowbound beast. He killed the
Gloam-Eyed Queen in the God Hunt, and at Marika's command he sealed the Rune of
Death into his Black Blade, which made him terrifying to every demigod who'd
ever lived. A fragment of it was stolen from him by Ranni, and Godwyn died of
it, and the whole world has come apart. He's been hiding ever since, under the
form of Gurranq, a mad old clergyman who gnaws on deathroot in the Bestial
Sanctum. Each root he eats brings a little more of the stolen Death back under
his teeth. He doesn't quite remember why he's so hungry.

## Appearance

A huge, emaciated beast in rags, hunched in a stone sanctum in the Dragonbarrow,
who will trade you his blessings for deathroot. He growls to himself and he
smells of something very old.

## Also known as

Gurranq, Beast Clergyman, the Beast Clergyman, Old Gurranq
