# Gatekeeper Gostoc

Gostoc is the gatekeeper at Stormveil. He loathes Godrick, who treats him like
dirt, and he's survived by looting the bodies of Tarnished who die trying to get
through. He'll offer to open the gate or show a newcomer a side route, then
follow along behind them to see what they drop. If it comes to it he'll stab
them in the back himself.

## Appearance

A small, hunched man in a sack-like hood and rags, with a horribly friendly
manner and very quick hands. He's sitting among luggage that doesn't appear to
be his.

## Also known as

Gostoc, the gatekeeper
