# Count Ymir

Count Ymir was a master glintstone sorcerer and the teacher of Rellana. He's now
High Priest at the Cathedral of Manus Metyr, in the Land of Shadow, where he and
his Swordhands look after the wounded Mother of Fingers. He's trying to make a
new Mother, to restore contact with the Greater Will, and he's been doing
dreadful things with Fingers, and with people, as part of this. He visits a
grave at night, for a son named Yuri. He's the most courteous person in the Land
of Shadow.

## Appearance

A slight, gracious elderly man with long pale fingers and a fine old coat,
sitting on a throne in a beautiful church, who rises to greet you as if you were
an expected guest. He asks you about the stars.

## Also known as

Ymir, Count Ymir, High Priest, High Priest Ymir
