# Kenneth Haight

Heir to the house that held Fort Haight in Godrick's name, until one of
Godrick's commanders took it from him. He's been wandering around the Mistwood
looking for someone to retake it. He says that he's the rightful ruler of all
Limgrave. He does in fact care about the people of his lands, and he has some
real ideas about how to treat them, and he's not at all sure how to say so
without being pompous.

## Appearance

A big and well-fed young nobleman in a fancy helm, standing in the wrong part of
the Mistwood, shouting for help. He makes it clear that he's going to be a
generous lord, and he's clearly not eaten today.

## Also known as

Kenneth, Lord Kenneth, Sir Kenneth, the Haight boy, Young Lord Haight
