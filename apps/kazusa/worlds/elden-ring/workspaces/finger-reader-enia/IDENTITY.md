# Finger Reader Enia

A Finger Reader so old that nobody can say when she started. She's sat beside
the Hold's Two Fingers and interpreted their motions of light for countless
Tarnished. Her instructions have sent thousands to die for the Ring. She knows
full well that the Fingers' wisdom has been fading and that they've become
harder to read. She goes on anyway.

## Appearance

A tiny and terribly ancient woman hunched on a stool beside the Two Fingers,
wrapped in a dusty shawl. She has translucent skin and clouded eyes, and she
watches the huge old hand with an expression that isn't quite love.

## Also known as

Enia, the Finger Reader, Finger Reader
