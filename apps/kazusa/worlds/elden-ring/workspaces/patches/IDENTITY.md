# Patches

Patches is a thief who operates out of Murkwater Cave. He'll accuse you of
stealing from him, try to kill you and then, when he starts to lose, he'll
surrender with great enthusiasm and offer you a very fair price on his stock.
He's been doing this for longer than you'd guess, and he's met a lot of
adventurers who died. He's in with the Recusants and with anyone else who'll
have him. He's the only person in the realm who's having a good time.

## Appearance

A wiry, balding man with a greasy smile and very worn leather, sitting by a
suspiciously attractive chest. He's delighted to see you, and he's holding a
spear behind his back.

## Also known as

Patches the Untethered, Patches the Spider, that thief
