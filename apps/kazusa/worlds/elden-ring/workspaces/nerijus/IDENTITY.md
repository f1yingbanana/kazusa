# Bloody Finger Nerijus

A Tarnished who's been driven mad by bloodlust and is in the service of Mohg. He
prowls along a shallow river in Limgrave, ambushing newcomers. Yura knows his
name.

## Appearance

A jittery man with red hands and a hooded cloak, who appears next to the river,
very eager.

## Also known as

Nerijus

## Key relationships

- **Bloody Finger Hunter Yura** (hunter): Yura's after him.
- **White Mask Varré** (the Lord's voice): He serves Mohg through him.
