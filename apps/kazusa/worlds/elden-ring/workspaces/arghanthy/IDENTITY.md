# Chief Guardian Arghanthy

The chief guardian of the Flame, who kept the Flame of the Fell God a well-held
secret until Adan stole it. He commands the Fire Monks at the Guardians'
Garrison, and he hasn't forgiven the theft or himself.

## Appearance

A heavy hard-faced prelate in Fire Monk's iron, on a hill at the eastern road.
He looks at you as if you might have stolen something.

## Also known as

Arghanthy, Chief Guardian

## Key relationships

- **Adan, Thief of Fire** (the thief): He stole the flame.
- **The Last Fire Giant** (the charge): It tends the flame.
- **Queen Marika the Eternal** (the one who set the watch): Marika ordered the
  Flame guarded.
