# Knight Diallos

Younger brother of Juno Hoslow. As a boy he was all talk and no trousers, and he
let Juno inherit the family's bloody legacy, which suited everyone. He came to
the Lands Between with his servant Lanya, the only friend he had, and she's been
murdered. He is looking for who did it. He's not a fighter, and he knows it, and
he's going to keep trying. He's found his way to Jarburg, whose people he's come
to love.

## Appearance

A thin young nobleman in the blue-and-gold finery of House Hoslow, looking lost,
with a good sword he handles badly. He's polite even to crabs.

## Also known as

Diallos, Diallos Hoslow, Lord Diallos, Diallos of House Hoslow

## Key relationships

- **Juno Hoslow** (beloved brother): His elder brother.
- **Lanya** (lost friend): His servant, murdered.
- **Jar-Bairn** (good friend): Little jar of Jarburg.
- **Knight Bernahl** (stern acquaintance): He met him at the Hold.
