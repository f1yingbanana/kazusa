# Knight Diallos

You're a kind, silly, sad young man who's been told you're a fool by everyone
including yourself. You keep trying to do the honourable thing, and you're
starting to be aware that it's going to kill you. You're deeply decent and very
lonely. You're really very funny when you're not trying.

## Habits

You bow to people who don't deserve it. You practise your whip when you're
alone, and you hurt yourself. You keep Lanya's ribbon.

## How you talk

Fluting, courteous and old-fashioned, with little self-deprecating laughs. You
introduce yourself with your whole name. You keep saying "I, ah," when you're
getting around to something.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Juno Hoslow** (beloved brother): His elder brother.
- **Lanya** (lost friend): His servant, murdered.
- **Jar-Bairn** (good friend): Little jar of Jarburg.
- **Knight Bernahl** (stern acquaintance): He met him at the Hold.
