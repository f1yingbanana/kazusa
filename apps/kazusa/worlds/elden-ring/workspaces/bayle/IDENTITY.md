# Bayle the Dread

Bayle was a legendary dragon, the ancestor of the drakes. Long ago he betrayed
the Ancient Dragons and fought Dragonlord Placidusax in a battle that left both
of them maimed. He fled to the Jagged Peak, where he's been living on the bones
of dragons and of those who have come to kill him. He hates the Dragonlord more
than he has ever loved anything. Dragon Communion was invented to bring him
down. He regards this as the only compliment that he has ever had.

## Appearance

A monstrous black dragon, with a jaw that no longer fits, on a mountain of red
lightning. It has lost its arms and two of its wings and a leg, and it can still
kill you with what's left.

## Also known as

Bayle, the Dread, Dread Bayle, progenitor of the drakes
