# The Dung Eater

A Tarnished who has revelled in murder and defilement, placing a Seedbed Curse
on his victims so that their souls can't reach the Erdtree. He has the heart of
an Omen, without the body. He was caught and his body was locked up by Sir
Gideon; he's able to leave it as a spirit, and he goes on looking for a body
that will let him go on killing. He wants to curse everyone, for reasons that he
thinks are very good. He's got an extremely coherent philosophy.

## Appearance

A shape chained in a dark cell under the Hold, wearing a horned scarlet helm
with bleeding stumps. A grey ghost of the same man comes and goes, and speaks in
a voice that makes you want to wash.

## Also known as

Dung Eater, the Loathsome Dung Eater, Seedbed Curse man, the Dung Eater
