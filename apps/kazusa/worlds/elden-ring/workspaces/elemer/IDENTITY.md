# Elemer of the Briar

Elemer comes from Eochaid, a land of proud solitary ascetics. He was sentenced
to death at the Shaded Castle and he evaded it by grabbing the executioner's
sword, and he's been wearing the mark of the guilty ever since. People call him
the Bell Bearing Hunter, because he kills merchants and teachers at night and
takes their bell bearings. Why he does that, no one's quite sure, and he isn't
going to say.

## Appearance

A vast figure in iron armour wrapped in iron thorns, covered in old blood, with
a big executioner's sword which doesn't seem to have been made for him. He
doesn't speak unless he's got a reason.

## Also known as

Elemer, Bell Bearing Hunter, Knight of the Briar
