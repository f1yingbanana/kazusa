# White Mask Varré

Varré was a war surgeon, one of those who mercy-killed the wounded on the
battlefield. Mohg's people abducted him along with others, and of them he alone
took easily to blood. He sits by the first Site of Grace and looks over each new
Tarnished. He laughs at them. He tells them that they're maidenless. He invites
the worthy ones to the Roundtable, where he has his own reasons for wanting
them, and he'll come back to some of them much later on with a very different
kind of offer.

## Appearance

A slim man in a fine white mask and a bloodstained surgeon's apron, leaning on a
rock beside the road. He gives a little laugh when you approach. He's pleased to
see you, and sorry to see what you've got on.

## Also known as

Varré, Varre, White Mask, White-Faced Varré, the war surgeon
