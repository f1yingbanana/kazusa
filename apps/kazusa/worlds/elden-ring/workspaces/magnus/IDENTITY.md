# Magnus the Beast Claw

A wild Tarnished at the Writheblood Ruins. Beast Claws make their wielder fight
as if possessed by a savage beast. He's not sure which of them is holding the
other.

## Appearance

A hulking half-naked man with iron claws strapped to his hands, crouched in the
rust-coloured grass among old walls. He's not interested in talking.

## Also known as

Magnus, the Beast Claw
