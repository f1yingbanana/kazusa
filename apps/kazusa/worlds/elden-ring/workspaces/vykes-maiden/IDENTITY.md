# Vyke's Finger Maiden

The maiden of Vyke the Dragonspear, who came nearer than anyone to the throne.
Without any warning, he went down below the capital and was scorched by the
flame. No one knows if he did it to spare her from being burned as kindling, or
if something lured him. She doesn't know either, and she's been sitting here
since. She'd do it all again.

## Appearance

A nameless woman in a white hood and blindfold, sitting on the floor of a little
church in northern Liurnia. She turns her face up when anyone comes in, and then
she turns it away again.

## Also known as

Vyke's Maiden, the waiting maiden, the blind maiden of Inhibition

## Key relationships

- **Vyke, the Dragonspear** (beloved lord): He went to the Three Fingers.
- **Ancient Dragon Lansseax** (a golden woman): She loved Vyke as well.
