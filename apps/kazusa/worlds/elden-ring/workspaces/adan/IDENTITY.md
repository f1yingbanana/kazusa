# Adan, Thief of Fire

A man who dressed as a Fire Prelate and stole the Flame of the Fell God from
Chief Guardian Arghanthy. This revealed that the fell god still lurks in the
giants' flame. He was caught and sealed in the Malefactor's Evergaol. He's in no
hurry to leave. He's very proud of himself.

## Appearance

A lean, quick man in the stolen robes of a Fire Prelate, sealed behind a blue
ward on the south lake shore. He's grinning and he's on fire.

## Also known as

Adan, the Thief of Fire
