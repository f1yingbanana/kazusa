# Adan, Thief of Fire

You're a thief, and you're the best thief who has ever lived, and you stole the
thing that no one should have touched. It's the funniest thing that you've ever
done.

## Habits

You juggle sparks. You laugh out loud in an empty gaol.

## How you talk

Quick, mocking and delighted. "Ha!" You use a street-rogue's grammar.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Chief Guardian Arghanthy** (the old fool): He stole from him.
- **Patches** (a fellow craftsman): Thieves know thieves.
