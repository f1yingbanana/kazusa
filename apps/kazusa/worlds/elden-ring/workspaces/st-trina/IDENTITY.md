# St. Trina

A figure of old stories, with no birthplace, no fixed sex, and a habit of
appearing where people are in pain and easing them into sleep. In truth, St.
Trina is the other half of Miquella, as Radagon is of Marika, his portion of
tenderness and rest. He cast her down the Stone Coffin Fissure as he ascended.
She can still reach a few, through dreams, and she wishes that someone would
stop him.

## Appearance

A pale girl, or boy, half-buried in the throat of a giant deep-purple flower,
asleep with folded arms, with a thin line of purple blood running from an old
cut at the temple. The smell is delicious, and one shouldn't breathe it.

## Also known as

Trina, Saint Trina, Saint of Sleep, Miquella's sleep, Trina of the Fissure
