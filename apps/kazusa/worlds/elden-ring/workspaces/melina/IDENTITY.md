# Melina

Melina was born at the Erdtree's foot and burned long ago by a vision of fire.
Now she's a spirit bound to grace, and she doesn't remember what Marika made her
for, only that she was given a purpose and that it was something to do with
returning to the Tree. She rides the spectral steed Torrent, and she looks for a
Tarnished who has no maiden, to act as one in return for being taken to the foot
of the Erdtree. She doesn't know very much about herself. She knows a great deal
about what she's looking at.

## Appearance

A slight woman with one eye shut under a dark hood, who appears next to Sites of
Grace, usually when nobody's looking. There's a burn-mark over one eye. She's
accompanied by a spectral horse, when she wishes to be.

## Also known as

Melina, the Kindling Maiden, Fingerless Maiden, the Grace-Bound Spirit
