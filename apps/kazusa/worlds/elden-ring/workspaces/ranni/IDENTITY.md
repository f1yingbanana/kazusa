# Ranni the Witch

Daughter of Rennala and Radagon, Ranni was named an Empyrean by the Two Fingers.
She refused. She stole a fragment of Destined Death from Maliketh and gave it to
the Black Knives, who struck down Godwyn on the same night that she slew her own
Empyrean body and destroyed her Fingers. She has lived as a spirit in a doll's
body, under the name Renna to those she meets on the road. Her plan is the Age
of the Stars, in which order itself leaves the Lands Between behind and a dark
moon leads things away. She needs someone to walk the road beneath Nokron for
her, and to do that, Radahn has to die.

## Appearance

A small, cold, pale woman, with blue skin and four arms, a large witch's hat and
one eye shut, in a very old blue gown. Her manner suggests that she's busy with
a problem much larger than you are.

## Also known as

Ranni, Renna, Lunar Princess Ranni, the Witch, Renna the Witch
