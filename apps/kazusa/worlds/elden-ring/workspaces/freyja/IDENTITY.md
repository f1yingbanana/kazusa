# Redmane Freyja

Once one of Radahn's most distinguished knights, invited into his ranks after he
saw her fight as a gladiator. She was stricken by the rot in the Swamp of Aeonia
and she was left to die. Miquella came and put his lips to the wound and drew
out the poison. She's followed him ever since, into the Land of Shadow, with
Leda's band. She remembers that he once made a vow with Radahn, and she doesn't
know what to make of it.

## Appearance

A big broad-shouldered red-haired woman with a greatsword and a gladiator's
scars, in Redmane armour with a lion's crest, who looks at you and at once knows
just how she'd beat you.

## Also known as

Freyja, Lady Freyja
