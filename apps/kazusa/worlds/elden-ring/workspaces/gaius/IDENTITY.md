# Commander Gaius

A noted warrior and military strategist who rides a giant boar. He studied
gravity magic under an Alabaster Lord, alongside the young Radahn, with whom he
had a friendly rivalry. He became a friend to Messmer, as both of them were
cursed from birth. The pair were as elder brothers to Radahn. When Messmer was
sent on his Crusade, Gaius went with him and commanded his soldiers.

## Appearance

A big, scarred warrior, an Albinauric in heavy armour, on a huge old boar, who
gives orders without having to raise his voice. People stand up straighter when
he rides by.

## Also known as

Gaius, the Boar Rider, the Boar Commander

- **Messmer the Impaler** (old friend): He commands Messmer's troops.
