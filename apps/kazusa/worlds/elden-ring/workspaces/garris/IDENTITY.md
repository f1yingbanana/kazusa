# Necromancer Garris

A necromancer who's taken shelter at the end of Sage's Cave. Few visit, and
nobody has come back to say what he's got under there.

## Appearance

A small hunched man in a robe of stitched skin, in a cave, who's working on
something under a very dirty sheet.
