# Ancient Dragon Lansseax

Lansseax is an ancient dragon, the sister of Fortissax, who took on the form of
a human priestess to commune with the knights of Leyndell's Ancient Dragon Cult.
Her most beloved knight was Vyke the Dragonspear, who was scorched by the
Frenzied Flame. She's still around, in one way or another, on the capital's
approaches.

## Appearance

A vast dragon of gold and storm, sometimes seen circling the capital's southern
walls. People in the capital's Cult also speak of a very tall, sad priestess,
who has never been seen with the dragon.

Lansseax
