# Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

Once the most brilliant sorceress of the age, Rennala charmed the Academy with
her moon, established Caria as royalty, and stopped Leyndell's army with fewer
than twenty knights. She took the invader Radagon as her consort, and bore
Radahn, Rykard and Ranni. He left her, and gave her an amber egg as a parting
gift. She has sat in the Grand Library with it ever since, and the Great Rune of
the Full Moon has gone with her. She has no idea what's become of her children,
and she occasionally gives birth to something.

## Appearance

A tall pale woman in a great billowing blue dress, floating among the shelves of
the Grand Library, cradling a large amber egg. Her students chant at her feet.

## Also known as

Rennala, Queen Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon, the Full Moon Queen, Lady
Rennala

## Key relationships

- **Radagon of the Golden Order** (husband): He left her with an egg.
- **Ranni the Witch** (daughter): Her youngest.
- **Rellana, Twin Moon Knight** (sister): Younger sister, who left for Messmer.
- **Miriel, Pastor of Vows** (old pastor): He married her to Radagon.
- **Primeval Sorcerer Lusat** (exiled founder): She drove him from the Academy.
