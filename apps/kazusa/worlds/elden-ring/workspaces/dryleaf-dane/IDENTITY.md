# Dryleaf Dane

Dane belongs to the Dryleaf Sect, spiritual seekers who chose to serve a new god
after seeing the decline of the Erdtree in its fallen leaves. He fights with
kicks, in a style of his own. He's been in Miquella's service for longer than
Leda has, and he very rarely speaks: she has never heard his voice. He's the
first to work out what has to be done with the Sealing Tree.

## Appearance

A lean bald man in a leaf-coloured robe with bound hands and no weapon, sitting
perfectly still in a ruined street. He looks at you, and he doesn't say
anything. His shins are like iron.

## Also known as

Dane, Monk Dane, the Silent Monk

## Key relationships

- **Needle Knight Leda** (young friend): Fellow follower.
- **Miquella the Kind** (lord): He's served him for a long while.
- **Sir Ansbach** (old fox): Fellow follower.
