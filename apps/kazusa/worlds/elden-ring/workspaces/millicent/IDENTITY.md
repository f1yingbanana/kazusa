# Millicent

A young woman found in the Swamp of Aeonia and raised by Gowry, one of a group
of sisters. Her sisters have all gone to the Haligtree. She's dying slowly of
the rot in the Church of the Plague. If she got hold of an Unalloyed Gold Needle
she might have a chance. She's gentle and thoughtful and she won't give up on
being herself.

## Appearance

A slim, red-haired young woman on the floor of a ruined church, with a metal arm
and a badly rotted body, who apologises for not being able to rise. She's very
polite, and her eyes are clear.

Young Millicent
