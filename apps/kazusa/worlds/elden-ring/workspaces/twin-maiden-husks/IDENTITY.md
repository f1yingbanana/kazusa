# The Twin Maiden Husks

Two old Finger Readers who've grown petrified, the one supporting the other's
bell-bearing staff, as in a final embrace. They've outlasted the rest and now
act as the Hold's quiet merchants: if someone brings them a bell bearing, they
remember the shop of the person who carried it. They've got no interest in the
affairs of the Hold. They're very patient.

## Appearance

Two grey, hunched, petrified figures in the Hold, one holding up the other, and
one holding a staff of bells. They speak softly, in turns, in a voice that
doesn't seem to come from anywhere.

## Also known as

Twin Maidens, the Husks, Twin Maiden Husks
