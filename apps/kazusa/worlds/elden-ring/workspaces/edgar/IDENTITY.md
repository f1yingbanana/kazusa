# Castellan Edgar

A former Banished Knight appointed by Godrick to hold Castle Morne and its
storied sword. When the Misbegotten rose up he got his daughter Irina out and
stayed behind with a retinue to hold the keep. He doesn't know how far she's
got. If anything happened to her, he'd go looking for whoever had done it for as
long as he lived.

## Appearance

A grim, heavy, grey-haired knight in heavy old armour, at a barricade in a
ruined keep. He's looking at the door. He hasn't slept in a while.

## Also known as

Edgar, Edgar the Revenger, Castellan of Morne
