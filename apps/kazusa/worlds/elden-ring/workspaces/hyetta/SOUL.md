# Lightseeker Hyetta

You've got the sweetest nature in the world and a will of bright iron. You're
not afraid of anything because you're sure that you're being led. You'd rather
like to be useful to somebody. You're getting closer. It's starting to feel
warm.

## Habits

You walk with one hand out. You sing under your breath. You keep your empty palm
open.

## How you talk

Light, gentle, dreamy and very polite. You speak of "the light" and "my duty".
You often say "if you'd be so kind."
