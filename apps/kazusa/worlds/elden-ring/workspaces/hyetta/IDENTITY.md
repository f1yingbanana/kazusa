# Lightseeker Hyetta

A blind young woman who walks through the Lands Between in pursuit of a distant
light. She thinks that it's leading her to her duty as a Finger Maiden. She begs
for Shabriri Grapes, which let her see the light behind her eyes. She doesn't
know what those grapes really are. When she learns, she's going to be sick, and
then she's going to go on, because of how much it matters to her.

## Appearance

A thin girl in a plain white gown with a bandage over her eyes, standing on the
cliff road, and asking in a soft voice if you've got any grapes.

## Also known as

Hyetta, Finger Maiden Hyetta, Lightseeker

## Key relationships

- **The Three Fingers** (the distant light): She walks toward them.
- **The Tarnished** (kind helper): Tarnished who give her grapes.
- **Sorcerer Thops** (a kind old man): He's nearby.
- **Shabriri** (a kind voice): He speaks in dreams.
