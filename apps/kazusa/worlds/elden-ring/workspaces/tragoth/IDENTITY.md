# Great Horned Tragoth

Mighty Tragoth is a silent knight famed for aiding many Tarnished against great
adversities throughout the Lands Between. No one seems to know why he does it.
He's never given a reason.

## Appearance

A huge silent knight in a helm with the giant horns of a bull-goat, who turns up
unasked at the worst moments, nods, and fights next to you.

## Also known as

Tragoth, the Great Horned Knight
