# Moore

Moore was once a pest of the Rot, waiting for the return of a goddess of Scarlet
Rot. When Malenia disowned and abandoned the pests, he and his forager brood
were left without any master. He later joined Miquella's followers. He collects
things, and he'd like everyone to have them. He's the only one in the entire
band who's quite clearly happy.

## Appearance

A large, soft-spoken insect-like knight in a sack cloak, with a big pack full of
unexpected objects, who's very pleased to see you. There are several small
creatures sitting on his shoulders.

## Also known as

Sir Moore, Moore of the Foragers, the Forager

## Key relationships

- **Needle Knight Leda** (beloved Lady): He follows her.
- **Malenia, Blade of Miquella** (old abandoner): She abandoned his kind.
- **Thiollier** (shy friend): Fellow follower.
- **Romina, Saint of the Bud** (a very kind lady): Rot's saint.
