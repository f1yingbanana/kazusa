# Dragon Communion Priestess

In reality she's the ancient dragon Florissax, who harboured no love but that
which she felt for her lord, the Dragonlord Placidusax. When Bayle dealt him a
grievous wound she gave up her dragon form in order to try to raise a
dragon-hunting hero. She invented Dragon Communion for that purpose, and she's
been regretting what it has turned into ever since. Igon is the latest of her
failures, and she likes him very much.

## Appearance

A hunched, very old woman in a long stained robe, sitting beside an immense
stone dragon's mouth, with a small gavel in her lap. She tells you exactly
what's going to happen to you, without any interest.

## Also known as

the Priestess, the Grand Altar's Priestess
