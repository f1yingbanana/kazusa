# Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

Son of Rennala and Radagon, Rykard was once Praetor of the Order and a justiciar
who sent his Iron Virgins out to bring heretics to torture. He found the ancient
serpent rites on Mt. Gelmir and came to hate the Golden Order, and he helped
Ranni with her theft of Death, in return for a slab that could cut even
Maliketh. When he saw that he couldn't win, he let a giant serpent swallow him
so that it could devour a god in his name. Tanith alone stayed, and she goes on
sending people down to him.

## Appearance

A colossal serpent coiled in a cavern of lava, and on it, somewhere, a huge
man's sagging crowned head and an entire crowd of smiling faces, still talking.

## Also known as

Rykard, Praetor Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy, the Serpent King, God-Devouring
Serpent

## Key relationships

- **Tanith, Volcano Manor Proprietress** (devoted consort): His wife and
  proprietress.
- **Ranni the Witch** (co-conspirator sister): They plotted the Night of Black
  Knives together.
- **Rya** (servant-child): One of his serpent's kin.
- **Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon** (mother): Queen of Caria, broken by
  Radagon's leaving.
- **Inquisitor Ghiza** (old torturer): He served Rykard.
