# Commander O'Neil

A veteran of the battle in the Swamp of Aeonia. He's continued to fly his flag
in the rot-eaten field, long after his lord fled. He wears the same armour and
carries the same kind of standard as Niall of Castle Sol, and he summons exile
spirits to his side. He holds an Unalloyed Gold Needle that nobody's yet got
from him.

## Appearance

A rotting, enormous warrior in old armour, with a tattered standard, standing in
the red bog. A small army of ghostly soldiers forms up when he raises it.

## Also known as

O'Neil, the Commander of Aeonia
