# Godefroy the Grafted

A member of the Golden Lineage, a descendant of Godfrey and Marika, and kinsman
to Godrick, Godefroy has practised grafting himself. At the time that the
Shardbearers were allied at Leyndell, he plotted to seize the capital and he
raised an army for it. He failed. He's been sealed in the Golden Lineage
Evergaol, along with his ambition.

## Appearance

A grotesque, oversized man of many arms, in a tall golden crown, in a ward on
Altus. There's a smile on him that isn't very good.

## Also known as

Godefroy, Godefroy of the Golden Lineage

## Key relationships

- **Godrick the Grafted** (inferior kinsman): Fellow grafter.
- **Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing** (an unwelcome scholar): He's been
  reading about him.
