# Mohg, Lord of Blood

Born an Omen and shackled beneath Leyndell beside his twin Morgott, Mohg was
visited in the dark by the Formless Mother, who gave his cursed blood a fire of
its own. He broke out in the Shattering, seized a Great Rune and went north,
where he stole the sleeping Miquella from the Haligtree. He has built a palace
in a hidden red underworld, and he intends for Miquella to become a god with
himself as consort. He's sure this is love.

## Appearance

A massive horned man in rich red robes, drenched in blood that he doesn't seem
to want to wipe off, cradling a three-pronged spear. His voice is unsettlingly
gentle.

## Also known as

Mohg, Lord of Blood, the Omen Mohg, Mohg the Omen
