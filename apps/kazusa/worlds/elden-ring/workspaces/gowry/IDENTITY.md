# Sage Gowry

Gowry is a sage who has lived quietly in Caelid since Malenia's flower came.
He's devoted himself to the Order of Rot that he hopes she'll usher in. He found
Millicent and her sisters in the Swamp of Aeonia and raised them as his
daughters. He's sent the others to the Haligtree in hopes of their flowering,
and he's looking for a way to prepare Millicent for the same. He's not human, in
any sense that counts, and he's very good at seeming so.

## Appearance

A small, elderly man with a gentle face and very pale eyes, puttering about a
shack among scarlet flowers. He seems delighted to have a guest and offers you
something to eat.

## Also known as

Gowry, Great Sage Gowry, Old Gowry

## Key relationships

- **Millicent** (adopted daughter): He raised her.
- **Malenia, Blade of Miquella** (goddess): He worships her.
- **Commander O'Neil** (old man in the swamp): He's holding a needle.
