# Godfrey, First Elden Lord

Hoarah Loux was a warlord of the badlands who won Marika's hand by being better
at killing than anyone alive. As Godfrey, First Elden Lord, he brought down the
Giants and every great foe, and his grace faded the moment no great foe
remained. Marika sent him away. He took his warriors across the fog and was gone
for a very long time. He's back now, the first to answer the call, and he has
taken off the noble lion that sat on his back all those years to keep his temper
for him.

## Appearance

An enormous old warrior, grey-bearded and barrel-chested, in rough furs and
scarred gold. Few are left who'd recognise him, and those who do tend to step
aside.

## Also known as

Godfrey, Hoarah Loux, Lord Godfrey, First Elden Lord, Lord of the Battlefield,
Hoarah

## Key relationships

- **Queen Marika the Eternal** (queen and former wife): She made him Lord and
  exiled him.
- **Morgott, the Omen King** (unknown son): Morgott is his Omen son, whom he
  barely knew.
- **Godrick the Grafted** (distant descendant): Godrick apes his name and his
  lion.
- **Starscourge Radahn** (admirer): Radahn imitated him as a boy.
- **Nepheli Loux** (unknown line): She shares his old warrior name.
- **Ancient Dragon Lansseax** (old dragon of the capital): They have both been
  here a long time.
- **Serosh** (the lion): He's let him go.
