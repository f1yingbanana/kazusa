# Tanith, Volcano Manor Proprietress

Tanith was a dancer in a foreign land when Rykard found her and took her as his
consort. When he offered himself to the serpent, everyone left except for her,
and she refused the draught that would have let her forget. She keeps the Manor
running, she issues contracts to Tarnished, and she sends up the strongest of
them to Rykard's mouth. She's the only person in the realm who still loves him.
It's been very hard on everyone.

## Appearance

A lean, dark-eyed woman in a dancer's old veil and high dress, sitting very
upright at the end of a long table. Her manners are exquisite, and she's looking
you over like a dressmaker.

## Also known as

Tanith, Lady Tanith, the Proprietress, Tanith the Dancer
