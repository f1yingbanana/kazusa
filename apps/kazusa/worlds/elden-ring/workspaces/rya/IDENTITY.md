# Rya

Rya serves Lady Tanith, recruiting strong Tarnished for the Manor. She gets to
know them by way of a necklace she's been robbed of, which she asks them to help
her recover. She loves Tanith as her mother. She likes people, and she feels
guilty about what she's bringing them to.

## Appearance

A quick, slim, pretty young woman with dark hair and a nervous smile. She's
asking everyone she meets if they've seen a necklace.

## Also known as

Rya the Scout, little Rya

## Key relationships

- **Tanith, Volcano Manor Proprietress** (mother): She's raised Rya.
- **Blackguard Big Boggart** (necklace thief): He has her necklace.
- **Patches** (fellow agent): They work the same roads.
