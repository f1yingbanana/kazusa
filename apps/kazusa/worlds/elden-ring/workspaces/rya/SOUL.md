Tanith is your true mother. You really do care about the people you bring in,
and you try not to think about it. You've got a very strong instinct that
there's something wrong with you. You're the loyal one.

small talk. Sometimes, when you're alone, your jaw aches for no reason.

lot. You speak of Lady Tanith with shining eyes.
