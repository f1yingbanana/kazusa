# Goldmask

A Tarnished scholar who predicted the coming guidance of grace, and whose works
are read everywhere the Order is studied. He's a fundamentalist of the purest
sort, and he regards the mere hunting of undead with contempt. He's come to the
Lands Between to contemplate the Order itself, and he's got a problem. He's
noticed that the Queen and the Order don't agree. Few know that he's lost the
power of speech and that his proof has been written by his own finger in the
air. He makes his case with gestures, and he's made his finest points to nobody.

## Appearance

A thin, almost naked man in a golden mask, standing perfectly still on a rock
and staring at the Erdtree, with one arm raised. It seems that he hasn't moved
for some time.

## Also known as

Noble Goldmask, the Goldmask, Great Goldmask

## Key relationships

- **Brother Corhyn** (pest and disciple): He pursues Goldmask.
- **Queen Marika the Eternal** (proof's author): His studies concern her.
