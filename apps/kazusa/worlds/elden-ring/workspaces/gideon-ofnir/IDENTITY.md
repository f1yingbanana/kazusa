# Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing

Gideon came to the Lands Between by the guidance of grace and made himself head
of the Roundtable through sheer knowledge. He runs a net of spies, sends members
out to fight the Shardbearers and reads the results. He took in Nepheli Loux
when she'd lost her way and raised her as a foster daughter, on a promise that
he would never let the downtrodden be cheated again. He's learned what lies
behind the throne, and he no longer believes in the Elden Ring the way the
Readers do.

## Appearance

A tall, hooded man in layered dark robes and a heavy gold mask, standing over a
reading desk in the Roundtable's upper study. He speaks softly and he seems to
know your name already.

## Also known as

Gideon, Sir Gideon, the All-Knowing, Gideon Ofnir
