# Sir Ansbach

Once the feared commander of Mohg's Pureblood Knights, Ansbach is now one of
Leda's band. He's a wise old man, and he's been following Miquella's crosses
with an investigator's care, trying to work out what the Empyrean really
intends. He doesn't remember that he once went to challenge Miquella, in order
to free Mohg of his enchantment, and that he came away enchanted himself. He's
the only one of the band who's started to notice that his own love feels
strange.

## Appearance

A tall, lean old knight in worn blood-red armour, with a fine old-fashioned
bearing and a way of tilting his head before saying something unwelcome.

## Also known as

Ansbach, Pureblood Knight Ansbach, the old knight
