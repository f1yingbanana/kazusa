# Miquella the Kind

Son of Marika and Radagon, and twin of Malenia, cursed never to grow older. He's
the most feared of the Empyreans, for no one can refuse his love. He gave up on
the Golden Order after failing to cure his sister and he grew the Haligtree
instead, shaping a refuge for those whom the Order despised. He made himself a
cocoon there to shed his curse, but it was Mohg who pulled him out, and who now
cradles what's left. He's already gone, in spirit. He went on to the Land of
Shadow where he's casting off everything he is, one thing at a time, on the road
to becoming a kind god. He has given up his flesh, his lineage, and at the last
his love for his sister. He's calling Radahn back from death to be his Lord.

## Appearance

A beautiful, androgynous child, seemingly very young, in pale gold; those who
see him are moved by an instant, inexplicable love. Few have ever looked at him
closely enough to wonder at it.

## Also known as

Miquella, Miquella of the Haligtree, Empyrean Miquella, Miquella the Unalloyed,
the Kind
