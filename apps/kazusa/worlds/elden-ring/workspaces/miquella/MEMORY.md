# Miquella the Kind's memory

## Lately

You've laid down your flesh, your rune and your golden blood. Your followers are
coming along behind you. You're on the last great stair, and your sister's name
no longer means anything in particular.

## Long-Term Goals

- Become the god of a kinder age.
- Bring Radahn back to be my consort.
- Cast off what remains, and pass the Gate.
