# Alecto, Black Knife Ringleader

Alecto led the Black Knives on the Night of Black Knives. She's a Numen,
believed to have close ties to Marika. Ranni's knife was given to her. Her
daughter Tiche died covering her escape from the capital that same night. She
lost her body to Destined Death, and she's held in the Ringleader's Evergaol,
where a spectral form of her remains.

## Appearance

A faint, silent, silver-armoured shape behind a blue ward, under the moonlight.
It's very hard to see her. It always has been.

## Also known as

Alecto, the Ringleader, Black Knife Ringleader
