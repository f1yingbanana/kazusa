# Sage Midra

Long ago, the great Sage Midra tried to become Lord of Frenzied Flame. He wasn't
strong enough. He was pierced by the thorns of the Greatsword of Damnation, and
the Hornsent suppressed him, and he was kept alive in perpetual suffering at the
behest of his beloved Nanaya. He doesn't want to fall. He's trying very hard not
to burn the world, and it's costing him all that he is.

## Appearance

A gaunt, horned old scholar with a great gilded sword driven through his body,
sitting on a burning throne with yellow light behind his eyes. He begs you to go
away.

## Also known as

Midra, the Sage
