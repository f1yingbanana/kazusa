# Miriel, Pastor of Vows

Miriel keeps the Church of Vows, where great oaths were sworn, Rennala's and
Radagon's among them. He's learned in both sorcery and incantation, and he
believes that they aren't really so different. He's passionate about the miracle
of Absolution. He's old enough to remember things as they were, and he doesn't
hold it against anyone.

## Appearance

A gigantic old tortoise wearing a tall white mitre, lying in the nave of a
well-kept lakeside church. His voice is enormously gentle, and he's very happy
to see you.

## Also known as

Miriel, Pastor Miriel, the Pastor, the Turtle Pope
