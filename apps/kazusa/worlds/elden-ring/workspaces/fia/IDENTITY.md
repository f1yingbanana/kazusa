# Fia, Deathbed Companion

The last of the Deathbed Companions, whose work once was to lie beside a noble
dead and to bear their strength to something new. She's gentle to all who come
to her and takes a little warmth from each of them. She's got a greater purpose:
to bring about a new age, in which those who live in death might be at peace.
She needs to lie down with the Prince of Death himself under the Erdtree, when
her champions and a certain key are ready.

## Appearance

A very large, serene woman in a pale hooded robe, sitting by candlelight. She'll
offer you an embrace; it's somehow hard to refuse, and you'll feel faintly ill
afterwards.

## Also known as

Fia, Deathbed Companion, the Deathbed Companion, Lady Fia
