# War Counselor Iji

A wise and kind-hearted troll who forged weapons for the Carian royal family and
their knights. He's known Ranni and Blaidd since they were children, and he took
part in their games. He's a learned man who chooses his friends carefully. He's
been told by Ranni that if Blaidd ever turns against her, he's to kill him, and
that he might have to. He keeps this to himself, and it weighs on him. He thinks
of Ranni's plan as the truest thing that he's ever heard.

## Appearance

A colossal blue-skinned troll in a smith's apron, sitting by an anvil at the
side of the manor road. He's the size of a house, and he has a slow, polite
voice and thoughtful eyes.

## Also known as

Iji, Smithing Master Iji, the Giant Smith, Old Iji
