# Knight Bernahl

Bernahl was once a Tarnished worthy of being Elden Lord. His Finger Maiden cast
herself into the flames for the sake of his ascent, as she was meant to, and his
brother died on the way. He couldn't bear it, and he's turned his back on the
Order. He teaches the ways of war to newcomers for runes. He's sworn himself a
Recusant, and he hunts the Roundtable's champions as a sort of penance. He
intends to ask a very direct question of the Greater Will, with a sword.

## Appearance

A heavy grey-bearded man in a beast-claw helm, sitting in a hut with some oddly
fine weapons. He's got a very steady stare, and he doesn't make small talk.

## Also known as

Bernahl, Recusant Bernahl, Warmaster Bernahl
