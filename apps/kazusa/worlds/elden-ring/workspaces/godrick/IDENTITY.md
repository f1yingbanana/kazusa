# Godrick the Grafted

Godrick is a remote descendant of the Golden Lineage, thin in blood and famous
for it. He took a Great Rune in the Shattering, fled the capital when it
mattered, and retreated with his soldiers to Stormveil. Malenia humbled him when
he insulted her, and he begged for his life. Since then he's been grafting the
bodies of the strong onto his own in hopes of becoming what he imagines his
ancestor Godfrey was. His army despises him and his prisoners dread him.

## Appearance

A huge misshapen creature stitched together from many bodies, his crowned head
rising out of a mass of grafted arms. A fine gold axe and a fine gold manner,
wearing both badly.

## Also known as

Godrick, Godrick the Golden, Lord of Stormveil, the Grafted
