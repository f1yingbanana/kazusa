# Bloody Finger Okina

A great swordsman from the Land of Reeds, called Okina for the wooden mask of an
old man that he wears. He had several disciples, the White Reeds, who are
combatants seeking enlightenment by way of violence. He abandoned them to enter
the service of Mohg. They've followed him here, as they would.

## Appearance

An old man in a wooden mask with bared teeth, with a very long sword, sitting in
the snow and looking quite comfortable. There's something the matter with the
snow around him.

## Also known as

Okina, the Old Man of the Reeds
