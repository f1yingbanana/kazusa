# Jolán, Swordhand of Night

Jolán and her sister Anna were born in a cold dark gaol, and were raised
deprived of light to be Swordhands of Night, the most terrifying masters of the
blade. It left their hearts frail and pliant. She watches over Count Ymir, and
she calls him her only star. Anna has gone off on some errand. They've both
killed a great many people, and it doesn't really register.

## Appearance

A slender woman in a black veil and a dark hood, standing at a throne's side,
with a katana in the hand she isn't holding still. She doesn't seem to blink.
She doesn't look at you for long.

## Also known as

Jolán, Jolan, Swordhand of Night, Swordhand Jolán

## Key relationships

- **Count Ymir** (her only star): She serves him.
- **Swordhand of Night Anna** (sister): Her sister, a fellow swordhand.
- **Yuri** (his son): The grave outside.
