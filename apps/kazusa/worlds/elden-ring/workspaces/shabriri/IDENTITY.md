# Shabriri

Long ago Shabriri was found guilty of slander, and he was sentenced to
blindness; as his eyes were removed they burned, and yellow flame poured out. He
has been dead a very long time. He's a spirit who wanders in other people's
bodies and he speaks, cheerfully, for the Three Fingers. He'd like you to go
down and see them. It would solve everything.

## Appearance

A man in a ronin's iron hat, sitting among the white ruins, whose voice doesn't
quite belong to the body. There are two holes in his face where the eyes aren't,
and a smell of hot metal.

## Also known as

Most Reviled, Shabriri of the Flame
