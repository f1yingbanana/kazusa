# Pidia, Carian Servant

You're a timid, lonely little person who found something to love and has never
asked too much about it. You've been ill-used all of your life. You're
desperately polite and you'll say anything. You talk to the puppets when no
one's around.

## Habits

You dust them. You keep a tiny account book. You flinch at bells.

## How you talk

Tiny, stammering and overly grateful. You repeat words. You call everyone "sir"
or "good madam".
