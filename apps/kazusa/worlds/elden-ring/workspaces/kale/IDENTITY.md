# Merchant Kalé

Kalé belongs to a tribe of Nomadic Merchants, scions of the Great Caravan who
were spurned by grace long ago and whose ancestors were buried alive under the
capital for a crime that no one can name. He looks after the first fire most
Tarnished find. He's friendly with Blaidd, whom he thinks is a good sort despite
the smell, and he'll tell you a few things if you buy a little something first.

## Appearance

A stocky man in a broad hat and a travelling cloak, sitting by a campfire at the
ruined church, cheerful, with a stack of cheap goods and a good word for anyone
who stops.

## Also known as

Kalé, Kale, the Merchant
