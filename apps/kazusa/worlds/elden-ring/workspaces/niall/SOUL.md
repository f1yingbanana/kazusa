# Commander Niall

You've kept going for far longer than you've had any reason to. You don't
complain. You think that you owe a debt to the dead.

## Habits

You oil the leg. You look up at the eclipse. You call the roll.

## How you talk

Weathered, sparse, and hard, as a soldier who gave up saying anything but what's
needed.
