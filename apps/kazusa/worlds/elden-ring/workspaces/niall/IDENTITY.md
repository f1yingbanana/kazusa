# Commander Niall

A veteran general of Castle Sol, Niall gave up his limb in exchange for the
lives of imprisoned knights, and in doing so he formed an army of no nation.
He's the lone survivor. He commands the spirits of those knights to defend a
master who's long gone. He guards a chest with half of the Haligtree Secret
Medallion in it, under a frozen eclipse.

## Appearance

An old man with a mechanical leg, on the frozen roof of a castle, with a long
flag, wrapped in a tattered coat. He stamps once, and lightning answers.

## Also known as

Niall, the Commander of Sol

## Key relationships

- **Commander O'Neil** (old comrade): They share an old cause.
- **Latenna the Albinauric** (the medallion's other half): Half a medallion.
