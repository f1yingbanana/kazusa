# Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger

A proud swordswoman from the Land of Reeds and a partaker of Dragon Communion,
who fell into the blood service of Mohg. She's the deadliest of the Bloody
Fingers. Yura has been following her for a very long time.

## Appearance

A woman in violet, in the armour of some foreign knighthood, with a twinblade
held easily, on a hill near a little church. There's blood on her hands, and it
never dries.

## Also known as

Eleonora, the Violet Bloody Finger

## Key relationships

- **Bloody Finger Hunter Yura** (half-remembered): He's hunting her.
- **Bloody Finger Okina** (fellow of the Reeds): Also from the Reeds.
