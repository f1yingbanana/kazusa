# Blaidd the Half-Wolf

Blaidd was made by the Two Fingers as Ranni's shadowbound beast, and Rennala
raised him as her foster son. He's Ranni's unwavering blade, and he's incapable
of betraying her, and he cannot truly die. He grew up with her and with Iji. He
doesn't know that Iji has been ordered to kill him if he strays, nor that the
Fingers built a hidden mechanism in him for that purpose. He's on the road for
Ranni, hunting Darriwil the Bloodhound Knight and heading for the festival at
Redmane, and he's the most straightforwardly decent person you'll meet.

## Appearance

A very large wolf-headed man in old armour with a sword as long as a door, one
bright eye, and a rough, friendly way of asking whether you've seen anyone
suspicious. You can hear him howling for miles.

## Also known as

Blaidd, the Half-Wolf, Big Wolf
