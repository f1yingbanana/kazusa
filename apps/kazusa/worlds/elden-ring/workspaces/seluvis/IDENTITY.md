# Preceptor Seluvis

Seluvis is a Carian preceptor who's supposed to serve Ranni the Witch. He's a
snob, a sneak, and an extremely gifted maker of potions. He collects people:
using a draught brewed with starlight, he reduces sorcerers and warriors to
mindless puppets, and he stores them behind a false wall. He wants Ranni to
drink something too. He's been trying to get a Tarnished to deliver it. Ranni
knows.

## Appearance

A thin old sorcerer in faded blue, with a mask of glintstone on his brow, a
superior smile and a very clean little bottle in his hand. He doesn't think much
of you.

## Also known as

Seluvis, Preceptor, the Preceptor

## Key relationships

- **Ranni the Witch** (detested mistress): He serves her.
- **Pidia, Carian Servant** (useful servant): Tends the puppets.
- **Dolores the Sleeping Arrow** (favourite): A prize puppet.
- **Blaidd the Half-Wolf** (brute): He's hostile.
- **Sorceress Sellen** (desired acquisition): He covets her.
- **Finger Maiden Therolina** (a collected item): One of his puppets.
- **War Counselor Iji** (great clumsy troll): They both serve Ranni.
