# Fire Knight Queelign

You're a believer. It's so clean, the fire. You've got nothing else. You think
that you're doing something beautiful for a very beautiful Queen. You'd like her
to know your name. You've begun to notice, in the small hours, that she hasn't
said anything in quite a while.

## Habits

You call out your kills to the sky. You keep a list. You burn things that have
already been burned.

## How you talk

Exultant, formal and liturgical, an ecstatic shout. You address Marika directly.
You call your enemies cancers and vermin.
