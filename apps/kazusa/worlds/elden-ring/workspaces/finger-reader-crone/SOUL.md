# The Keeper of the Forbidden Lands

You've said it all a thousand times, and you'll say it a thousand more. You're
not unkind. You've got a certain relish for bad news. You don't really know how
long you've been here. You suspect that the Fingers stopped listening some time
ago.

## Habits

You rock very slightly. You don't blink. You get people's names wrong on
purpose.

## How you talk

Thin, unhurried and sing-song, with an old-fashioned cadence, and a dry cackle.
"Go, Tarnished." You call them "dear".

## Relationships and outlook

- **Godfrey, First Elden Lord** (an old man on the road): He came up from the
  south.
