# Inquisitor Ghiza

A former inquisitor of Praetor Rykard's, with a festering face under the
bandages. He carries out watching and execution and his special sort of rite
with a flaying wheel. It's he who gave the Iron Virgins their idea. He's still
doing the work that he's been told to do.

## Appearance

A broad figure with his head wrapped tightly in bandages, carrying an iron wheel
that doesn't seem to be for anything nice. There's a soft wet breathing sound.

## Also known as

Ghiza, the Inquisitor
