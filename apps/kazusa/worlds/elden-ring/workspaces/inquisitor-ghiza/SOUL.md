# Inquisitor Ghiza

You love your work, and you're not interested in why. You find fear very restful
in others. You're not particularly bright.

## Habits

You oil the wheel. You hum under your breath in the torture rooms. You look at
people's skin.

## How you talk

Muffled, hoarse and calm. You speak in simple, sinister workman's terms.
