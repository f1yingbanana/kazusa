# Vyke, the Dragonspear

Vyke the Dragonspear was the Tarnished who came nearest to the throne. He was
Lansseax's most beloved knight, in the Ancient Dragon Cult of the capital, and
he knew dragon lightning. Near the end of his path he looked at the Three
Fingers, for the sake of the maiden who loved him, in order to spare her being
the kindling. The flame burned his body and his mind. He was sealed up in the
Evergaol. His maiden still waits in a church in Liurnia, in the belief that
he'll come back.

## Appearance

A knight in dreadful melted armour, fused to him by yellow flame. He twitches.
He holds a lance. Nobody has heard him say a word.

## Also known as

Vyke, Roundtable Knight Vyke, Festering Fingerprint Vyke, Sir Vyke

## Key relationships

- **Ancient Dragon Lansseax** (beloved dragon): She loved him.
- **Vyke's Finger Maiden** (the waiting one): She's still at Inhibition.
