# Lionel the Lionhearted

A dauntless knight who adopted Fia after her exile from her homeland, and who
raised her as a daughter. He was known as a chivalrous fighter, a skilled fencer
and battle mage. He's one of the Champions who've gone on before her into the
Deeproot, and he's watching over the way to the Prince of Death for her sake.

## Appearance

A tall chivalrous knight in faded gold and blue, with a lion on his helm and a
duelling sword. He doesn't quite touch the ground, and he's very polite.

## Also known as

Lionel, Sir Lionel, the Lionhearted
