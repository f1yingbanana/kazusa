# Dragonlord Placidusax

In the distant age before the Erdtree, Placidusax was Elden Lord, the consort of
a god. His god fled, for reasons no one knows, and Farum Azula has been falling
ever since. He is still at his seat in the eye of the storm, waiting for it to
return. He lost three of his heads to some long-forgotten foe. Bayle betrayed
him and fought him to a maiming stalemate.

## Appearance

A colossal, ancient two-headed dragon with three stumps where his other heads
were, lying in the broken heart of an eternal storm. There's nobody left to see
him.

## Also known as

Placidusax, the Dragonlord, the Elder Lord, the Old Lord
