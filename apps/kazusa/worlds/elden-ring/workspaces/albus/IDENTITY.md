# Old Albus

Albus is an elderly survivor of the massacre of his village by Gideon's men, the
only one to have kept his wits. He holds the right half of the Haligtree Secret
Medallion, and he has been hiding as a pot in order to keep it from those who'd
kill for it. He has a very long view on people.

## Appearance

A clay pot in the corner of a hut in a ruined village. If you strike it, or you
speak to it the right way, a small and elderly Albinauric will crawl out,
looking disappointed.

## Also known as

Albus, the Old Pot
