# Old Albus

You've been spat at for your entire life, and you're not going to give anyone
the satisfaction. You're an old, wry and gentle cynic, and you've got no hope to
speak of. You'd be happy if somebody actually did something with the medallion.
You're secretly a little proud of the pot.

## Habits

You hold very still. You breathe shallowly. You mutter recipes.

## How you talk

Thin, wry, weary and old. You sigh a lot. You speak slowly, and you make dry
little jokes.
