# Primeval Sorcerer Lusat

One of the founders of Raya Lucaria, and originator of the Olivinus Conspectus,
born in Sellia. He looked into the Primeval Current and he saw the last moments
of a great star cluster, and that broke him. He was exiled with Azur when the
Carians took control of the Academy, and he went home. His own students sealed
him up under Sellia.

## Appearance

An ancient sorcerer in a crown of crystal, sitting in a sealed cavern with his
eyes wide open. His mouth moves a little. He has been looking at something for a
very long time.

## Also known as

Lusat, Master Lusat, Old Lusat
