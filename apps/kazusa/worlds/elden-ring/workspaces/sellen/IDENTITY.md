# Sorceress Sellen

Sellen was the most promising student the Academy had, the pupil of Azur and
Lusat, who were themselves banished for their study of the Primeval Current.
She's a Graven Witch, born of a star. Her true body is a stone, and she keeps
her soul safe in it. She pursued forbidden study, she was exiled for it, and she
now takes on Tarnished as apprentices in the hope of one of them doing what she
cannot. Witch-Hunter Jerren has sworn to kill her, and he's been kindly about
it. She intends to find her masters, to learn what they saw, and to go on from
there, with or without a body.

## Appearance

A woman in a plain grey hood and old robes, sitting in a cellar, with a small
glimmering stone where her head ought to be. She regards you with interest and a
very polite cold eye.

## Also known as

Sellen, Graven Witch, Sellen the Graven Witch, Lady Sellen
