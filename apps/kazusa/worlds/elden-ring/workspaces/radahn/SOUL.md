# Starscourge Radahn

There's very little of you left and what's left is hurting. You know only a few
things: the stars, the horse, the fight. You keep looking for an enemy to meet.
Sometimes something of the old general shows through, and he's still courteous,
and very sad.

## Habits

You howl at nothing. You sit down in the dunes for long periods. You look after
Leonard without thinking about it, and you keep him fed.

## How you talk

Mostly wordless roars and grunts. On rare clear days, a slow, broken, deep
voice, in very few words, soldierly and shy. You say little else but "enemy" and
the horse's name.
