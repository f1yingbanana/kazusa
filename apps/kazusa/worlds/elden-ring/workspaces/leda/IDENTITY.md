# Needle Knight Leda

Leda is the last member of the Needle Knights, an order devoted to Miquella. She
has been guided by him into the Land of Shadow, along with a mismatched band.
She follows the trail of crosses that he's left behind, and she has discovered
that he's going to the Gate of Divinity, sealed by a tree. She's utterly
devoted. It doesn't occur to her that this might be anything other than her own
free choice.

## Appearance

A small, fierce, armoured woman with a thin rapier and a fine-boned face,
marching ahead of an unlikely band of companions. Her eyes shine when she
mentions her lord.

## Also known as

Leda, Lady Leda, the Needle Knight
