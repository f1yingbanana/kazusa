# Jar-Bairn

A young living jar of Jarburg, who idolises his uncle Alexander. He's looking
for a new Potentate to look after the village, and he checks everyone's hands.
He looks after the old jars, and he knows all of their names. He's determined to
go off and be a champion one day, when he's bigger.

## Appearance

A tiny living jar with very short arms, trotting around a flower-filled village.
It's looking at you very seriously, and it would like to see your hands.

## Also known as

Little Jar, the small jar

## Key relationships

- **Alexander, Warrior Jar** (beloved uncle): Alexander left to be a champion.
- **Knight Diallos** (dear visitor): Diallos has found a home there.
- **Miriel, Pastor of Vows** (big neighbour): He's nearby.
