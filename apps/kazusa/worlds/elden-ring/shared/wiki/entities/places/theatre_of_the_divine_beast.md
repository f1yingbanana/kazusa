# Theatre of the Divine Beast

A wide amphitheatre open to the shadow. The Hornsent used this stage in their
most holy festival, with a costumed lion dance to honour their divine beast, and
a very large living version is still around. Ansbach warns that it's not as
cheerful as it sounds.
