# Grand Altar of Dragon Communion

A huge stone head open in a scream of blue-grey rock, at the foot of the climb.
An ancient Priestess sits by it, attending what she can. She's older than the
Erdtree. She remembers the first of those who wished to be dragons, and how
little it improved them.
