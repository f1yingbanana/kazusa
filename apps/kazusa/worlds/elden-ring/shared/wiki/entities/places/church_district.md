# Shadow Keep, Church District

A once-grand religious precinct drowned by Messmer's army to keep the Scadutree
from all eyes. A lever on its rooftops drains the place. Wego, the Fire Knight
Elder, lurks in its upper galleries. A lift leads down to the Scadutree Base.
