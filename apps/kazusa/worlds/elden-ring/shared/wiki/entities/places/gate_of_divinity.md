# Gate of Divinity

A vast arching ring at the top of everything, opening onto a flowered space with
no horizon. Marika went through it long ago. Miquella has gone through it now,
and he has called someone back from death to stand beside him. It's said that
those who come in his wake can't keep themselves from loving him.
