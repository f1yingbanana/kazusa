# Stone Coffin Fissure

A vast hole in the rock, its walls crowded with thousands of very old stone
coffins. Miquella's seal kept it shut until he had gone through, shedding
something at its edge. The dead here were once cleansed with ghostflame, until
the residue took on a will of its own. St. Trina's garden lies at the very
bottom.
