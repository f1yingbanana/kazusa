# Shadow Keep

An enormous dark castle at the centre of the Land of Shadow, built on old
Hornsent ground, and the seat of Marika's abandoned son. Messmer's Fire Knights
and Black Knights hold its walls, still following the orders of the queen who
locked them in here. Its great gate has burned for a very long time. The Church
District and Storehouse open behind it.
