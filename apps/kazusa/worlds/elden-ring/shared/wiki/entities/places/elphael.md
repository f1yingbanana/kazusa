# Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree

A huge old pile of stone and gold at the Tree's root where the knights of the
order are housed, and where the faith of the Haligtree is most fierce. It was
built as a brace for the tree. Rot pools on its lower levels and the whole place
reeks of it. The Grand Prayer Room still opens at certain times.
