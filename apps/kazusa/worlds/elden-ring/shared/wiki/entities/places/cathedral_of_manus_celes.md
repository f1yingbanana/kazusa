# Cathedral of Manus Celes

A great open-roofed cathedral fallen to moss and starlight. In a hollow behind
its altar lie the withered remains of Ranni's Two Fingers, and it is here that
her empty doll-body keeps its vigil. The dragon Adula guards the grounds in her
service.
