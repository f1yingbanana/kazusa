# Cerulean Coast

The far south of the Land of Shadow, a hushed coast where the world's dead and
things beyond wash ashore. Blue flowers grow in sheets over shallow water. Huge
stone arks rest in the sand where they've beached. Things on this coast are
confused and don't attack quickly. It's the most beautiful place in the Land of
Shadow and among the saddest.
