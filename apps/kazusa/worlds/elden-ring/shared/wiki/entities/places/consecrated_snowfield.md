# Consecrated Snowfield

A wide secret tableland of snow, opened only by uniting both halves of the
Secret Medallion at Rold. Small golden lights mark a path through fog so thick a
person can lose their hand. Night's Cavalry escort the Great Caravans across it,
and old frost-bitten ruins hide what they've buried.
