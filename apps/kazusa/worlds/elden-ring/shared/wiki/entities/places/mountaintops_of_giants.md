# Mountaintops of the Giants

High country above the capital: snow-buried roads, black stone, dead trees, and
the bones of giants that were put to the sword here at the Erdtree's birth. The
Fire Monks keep watch over the Flame of Ruin that was shut up in the Forge at
the top. There isn't much else, except for a very cold wind and the sense that
someone is still angry about it.
