# Church of Irith

A shell of a church, its roof partly collapsed, surrounded by shallows and reed.
A cracked statue of Marika stands here. Sorcerer Thops, an old Academy man
locked out of his own school, sits against the wall and turns over the same few
ideas about the Academy's barrier.
