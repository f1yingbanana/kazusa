# Royal Moongazing Grounds

A wide paved plateau open to the sky behind the Manor, with the spirit of Royal
Knight Loretta waiting at the steps. The Carian queens watched the moon from
here. From its edge, stairs lead up to Ranni's Rise and the Three Sisters.
