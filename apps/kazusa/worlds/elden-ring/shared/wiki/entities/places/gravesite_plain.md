# Gravesite Plain

The first country a traveller finds in the Land of Shadow: rolling grey-gold
grass, ancient standing stones and countless graves under a dim shadow-veil.
Hornsent stragglers and Messmer's soldiers both hold pieces of it. Marika hid
all of this away behind the Scadutree, and nobody from the Lands Between is
supposed to have seen it.
