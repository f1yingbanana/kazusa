# Road of Iniquity

A narrow road that winds along the cliffs behind the Manor, marked by dead
servants and the Manor's own bad reputation. A wandering nameless Tarnished
called Goldmask has been seen on a rock nearby, watching the Erdtree with no
apparent concern.
