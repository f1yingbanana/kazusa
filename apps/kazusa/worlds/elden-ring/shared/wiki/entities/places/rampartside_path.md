# Rampartside Path

A long grassy rise on the south side of the capital's walls, with the Erdtree
impossibly large above. Ancient Dragon Lansseax has been sighted circling here,
and she goes about in the form of a priestess when she cares to. Pilgrims keep
their heads down.
