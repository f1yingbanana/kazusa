# Shaman Village

A handful of stone cottages on a sunlit hill with flowers around them. These
were persecuted people, long before the Erdtree. Their spirit-women are mostly
gone. A carved silver-haired maiden looks out from an old tree on the rise, and
her face is one you've seen somewhere before.
