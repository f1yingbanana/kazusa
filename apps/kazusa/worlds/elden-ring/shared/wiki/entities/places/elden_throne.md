# Elden Throne

A great plain stone court at the end of the Sanctuary, open to the Erdtree's
falling leaves. The Elden Throne itself is a simple thing on a high dais. No one
has sat on it for an age, and Morgott fights anyone who tries. Behind it rises
the Erdtree's trunk, and a thorn wall closes the way in.
