# Castle Morne

A shattered sea-fortress at the Peninsula's south-east tip, under siege from
within. Its Misbegotten servants, long abused, have risen against their masters
and overrun the place, with Sword-Saint Edgar's handful of knights clinging to
the inner walls. A huge Leonine Misbegotten leads the rebels.
