# Crumbling Farum Azula

A broken stone city adrift in the air, slowly and always falling in a storm that
never ends, cut off from the world's time. It was once the seat of Placidusax's
dragon kingdom. Beastmen in priests' robes keep its wrecked temples, ancient
dragons roost on its fragments, and the Black Blade's hidden seal lies in its
deepest part. It's reached by very few roads, mostly the storm-gate behind the
Forge.
