# Midra's Manse

A tall dark house behind a line of ten beheaded retainers, in the Woods' south.
Sage Midra, the old Hornsent scholar, was consumed by Frenzied Flame and impaled
here by the Inquisitors with the help of his Lady. He's still holding it in,
with an effort that hasn't let up for many long years.
