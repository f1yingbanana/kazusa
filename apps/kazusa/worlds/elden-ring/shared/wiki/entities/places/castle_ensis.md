# Castle Ensis

A very large fortress on a cliff, the only gate between the plain and the
highlands. The Carian princess Rellana, sister to Rennala and sworn to Messmer,
keeps this place in his name. It's guarded by Fire Knights and Messmer's
soldiers. Its moonlit chamber is the hardest room to reach on the Plain.
