# Village of the Albinaurics

A poor huddle of wooden huts on a stinking poisoned tarn at the foot of the
Moonlight plateau. Albinauric folk hide among the mud walls, butchered in
numbers by the Omenkillers who hold the place. Hardly anyone's left, and a good
many old clay pots stand about.
