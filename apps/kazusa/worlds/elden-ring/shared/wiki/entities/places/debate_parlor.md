# Debate Parlor

A circular amphitheatre at the Academy's heart, where sorcerers once argued the
nature of stars. The Red Wolf of Radagon, a burning spectral beast, now roams
its ruined floor. Stairs climb from here to the upper academy and the Grand
Library.
