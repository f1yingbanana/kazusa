# Waypoint Ruins

A cracked square of stones on the road near Agheel Lake's eastern side, with Mad
Pumpkin Head loose among the walls. A hidden stair goes down to a cellar where
Sorceress Sellen, exiled from Raya Lucaria, sits in a pale circle of light and
takes pupils. There's a Site of Grace in the cellar.
