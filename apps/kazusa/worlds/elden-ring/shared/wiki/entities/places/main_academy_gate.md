# Main Academy Gate

A huge carved portal facing the Gate Town across the last of the bridge. A blue
seal holds it, and the Academy's own wards turn away those who do not carry a
key. The arrival of a key-holder is enough to bring a thousand-eye sentry out of
the stones.
