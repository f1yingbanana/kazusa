# Church of Dragon Communion

A low stone church tucked behind the southern cliffs and the Coastal Cave, with
a great stone dragon-head at the altar. A shrouded priest gives the Tarnished
the choice to eat dragon hearts and take their power, with the warning that it
changes what one is. Few find it by accident.
