# Scadutree Base

A vast space below the tree's lowest boughs, roofed by branches and hung with
shadow. The Scadutree Avatar is here, patient and many-limbed. The way up the
trunk to nowhere begins here. Those who come feel their hearts lift, and then
they feel something else.
