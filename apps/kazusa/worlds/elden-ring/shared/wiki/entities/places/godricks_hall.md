# Godrick's Great Hall

A long windswept court behind the great hall, open to the sky, its stones dark
with old offerings. Godrick holds audience here and practises his art on the
fallen, adding limb to limb in the name of the Golden Lineage. A storm-whipped
terrace at the far end looks out over the sea. Nepheli Loux means to bring him
down in this room, when she is ready.
