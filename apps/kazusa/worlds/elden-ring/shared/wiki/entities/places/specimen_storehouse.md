# Specimen Storehouse

Floor upon floor of shelves holding the preserved bodies and works of the old
world: Hornsent, trolls, Crucible beings. Messmer's orders were to burn it all,
but Fire Knight Hilde and Salza refused and were executed for it. Some of its
specimens are still alive.
