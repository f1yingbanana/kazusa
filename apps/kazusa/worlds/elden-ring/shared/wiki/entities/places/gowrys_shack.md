# Gowry's Shack

A snug hut in a surprisingly healthy patch of scarlet flowers just north of the
Church of the Plague. It belongs to Sage Gowry, a small and unbearably gentle
being who tends the flowers and has been expecting someone. He gives out cures
and advice to all comers. He isn't what he appears to be.
