# Raya Lucaria Grand Library

The Academy's highest chamber, a soaring drum of shelves filled with floating
tomes and pale students in a slow eternal chant. At its centre, Rennala, Queen
of the Full Moon, sits with the amber egg her former husband left her, deaf to
all but it. Students attend her and strike at any who come near. A single Site
of Grace lies at the foot of her dais.
