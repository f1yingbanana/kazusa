# Wailing Dunes

Sandy dunes along Caelid's eastern coast, scattered with the graves and weapons
of warriors who came to end Radahn. The general himself wanders here, enormous
and rotting, astride a horse that should have died under him long ago, and he
doesn't stop until something makes him. Comets occasionally come down.
