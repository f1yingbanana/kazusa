# Dragonlord's Seat

The still heart of the storm, reached by dropping from a broken bridge. An
enormous two-headed dragon, once five-headed, rests in the ruin of his throne.
Placidusax was once Elden Lord, in a time before the Erdtree, and he's waiting
for a god who fled and may never come back.
