# Stranded Graveyard

A sealed chamber of stone and bones far below the southern hills, where
Tarnished were laid to rest and left, and where the grace of old has lately
begun to wake some of them. The Cave of Knowledge opens off it, its walls still
whispering the rites of those who came before. A single lift rises to the chapel
above and, beyond it, the world.
