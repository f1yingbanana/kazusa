# Altus Plateau

A broad high tableland raised above the lake country by cliffs on every side,
its woods and pastures tinged with the Erdtree's gold. Reaching it means the
Grand Lifts or the bad road through the Precipice. Leyndell's soldiers hold the
roads against the Recusants of Mt. Gelmir, and the Erdtree fills the whole
eastern sky.
