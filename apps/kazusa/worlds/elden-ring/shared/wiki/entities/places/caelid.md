# Caelid

A red land under a red sky, in the far south-east. Malenia's flower bloomed here
at the Battle of Aeonia and the land has never recovered: the rivers run sick,
the air is thick with spores and everything left alive has grown wrong. Radahn's
Redmanes still man burning walls to hold the rot back from what little remains.
Colossal things have fallen here, and their remains lie where they dropped.
