# Belurat, Tower Settlement

A sprawling cliffside settlement of old stone, carved in and around the base of
the shadow-veiled Tower. It was the Hornsent's sacred town when Messmer's
crusade arrived. Its people were impaled on spikes from every gate. Parts are
still standing, with their spirits, and the old woman of the place sits in a
back room.
