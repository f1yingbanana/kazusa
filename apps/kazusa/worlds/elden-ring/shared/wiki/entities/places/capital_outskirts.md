# Capital Outskirts

The wide land in front of the capital's outer wall, churned and rebuilt by more
sieges than anyone has tallied. Leyndell soldiers man the ramparts and giant
ballistae face outward. Gargoyles patrol the upper battlements, and a Draconic
Tree Sentinel rides the great stair.
