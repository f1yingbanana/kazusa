# Enir-Ilim

A tower of enormous size rising from Belurat's heart, hidden in shadow by
Marika's sealing tree. At its top lies the Gate of Divinity where she herself
became a god, and the road to the same gate that Miquella now walks. Its inner
stages are haunted by Ansbach's friends, and it's not on any map.
