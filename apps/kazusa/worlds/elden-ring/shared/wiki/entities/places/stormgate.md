# The Stormgate

A broad paved road that runs up through ruined arches to Stormveil's main gate,
across a yawning drop. Here Margit the Fell Omen waits with golden hammer and
gilded chains, to turn back any Tarnished who think themselves worthy of the
castle or the Ring. Few come back the same way.
