# The Four Belfries

Four grey bell towers on a northern height, standing in a ragged square. Each
holds a sending portal to a remote part of the world, opened with an imbued
sword key: the Chapel of Anticipation and Nokron among them. A third is said to
have once reached the sky-city of Farum Azula. Their bells have not rung in a
long time.
