# The Hold's Cellar

A low stone basement with one locked room. Sir Gideon has shut the Dung Eater
here, by a means that he doesn't discuss. The Dung Eater is a spirit, and what's
behind the door is the body he uses. He still talks. It would be better if he
didn't.
