# Cocoon of the Empyrean

A great flat lake of blood at the palace's bottom. Mohg is here with the great
cocoon, within which Miquella has lain for a very long time, an arm hanging out.
He comes here to bathe the thing in his own blood. The withered hand, touched,
will take you somewhere that isn't on any map.
