# Astel's Chamber

A vast cold stone hall, open to a sky that is not a sky. Astel, a huge skeletal
insectile thing from beyond the sky, hangs here. It was a star-born void which
destroyed an Eternal City and was then shut up. Ranni's road goes past it.
