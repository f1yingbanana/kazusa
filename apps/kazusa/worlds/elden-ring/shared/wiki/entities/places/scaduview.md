# Scaduview

A narrow wooded ridge on the Land's north side, with the Scadutree's great dark
trunk filling the sky. Ghost-trolls and shy old folk live here. A hidden hamlet
of shamans lies on the eastern slope. It's the only place in the Land of Shadow
with any real light.
