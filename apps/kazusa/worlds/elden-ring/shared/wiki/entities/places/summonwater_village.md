# Summonwater Village

A low flooded village of rotted thatch, reached by a stairs down from the
Stormhill road. A spectral ferryman poles a boat through the shallows, summoning
the bodies of the villagers from the water to walk again. D, Hunter of the Dead,
comes here because of it.
