# Redmane Castle

A huge battered fortress on a tidal island at the south of Caelid, seat of the
Redmanes and their red-haired general. His men still man its walls, though
Radahn himself has been gone for years, mad with rot on the eastern dunes. Soon
they mean to hold a festival to give him the warrior's death he always wanted,
and champions are coming from everywhere.
