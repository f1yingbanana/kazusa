# Deeproot Depths

A vast dim cavern entirely filled by the Erdtree's lowest roots, with streams of
grey water and a sick sky. Those Who Live in Death fester here, and a Nameless
Eternal City rots at the deepest end. It's Fia's chosen place, and Godwyn's body
is here.
