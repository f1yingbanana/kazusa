# Seaside Ruins

A pile of ancient stones high on the Limgrave south coast, near the road to the
peninsula. Yura the Bloody Finger Hunter lodges here between hunts and warns
newcomers about the invaders, and about a certain Eleonora. A nomad's tent marks
its landward side.
