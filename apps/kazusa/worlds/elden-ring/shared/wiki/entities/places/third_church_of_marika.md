# Third Church of Marika

A small white ruin among flower meadows in Limgrave's far east, and the last
wholesome place before Caelid's blight begins. A Sacred Tear and a Site of Grace
make it a welcome halt, and a sending gate behind the ruin leads to the Bestial
Sanctum. Many pause here and decide not to go on.
