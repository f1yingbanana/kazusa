# Dragon-Burnt Ruins

A village reduced to blackened footings by dragonfire, on the lake's north-west
shore. Nothing grows quickly here. Rats, a few bandits and a cellar remain, and
the chest in the cellar will carry anyone who opens it to the Sellia Crystal
Tunnel, in a sense.
