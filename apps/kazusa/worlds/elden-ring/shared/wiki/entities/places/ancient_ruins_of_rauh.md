# Ancient Ruins of Rauh

Very old stone, split in half by a great crevasse and hung with water. The Rauh
civilisation built these streets before the Erdtree and perhaps before the
Crucible. Its broad aqueducts run on through sheer habit. Its people became
something else, and their huge stone goddess statues still stand.
