# Mohgwyn Palace

A huge blood-coloured underground sea and stone mausoleum, in a part of the
world no road reaches. Mohg, Lord of Blood, is raising a palace here among the
ruins of an old dynasty. The red lake is alive with abominations, the
Albinaurics here are mad with bleeding, and Miquella lies in a cocoon at its
heart.
