# Stormhill Evergaol

A slab of ancient stone on a hill, ringed by the faint blue wall of a gaol that
holds its occupant apart from time. Inside waits a Crucible Knight imprisoned
for a reason no record now gives. Passers-by can see it pacing if they look
closely.
