# Chamber of the Two Fingers

A close, warm, dim room off the main hall, in which the Two Fingers themselves
stand on a raised altar, twitching. Finger Reader Enia has sat at their side for
longer than the Hold has existed. It's said that the Fingers grow dimmer each
year, and that their guidance has become difficult to interpret.
