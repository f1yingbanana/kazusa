# Leyndell, Royal Capital

The great city of the Golden Order, built on terraces at the very roots of the
Erdtree, its towers plated in gold and its avenues choked with fallen leaves.
The carcass of Gransax lies across its central district where he fell, a spear
of red lightning still in hand. Morgott rules as the last of Marika's kin to
defend it. Knights, Oracle Envoys, nobles and lost pilgrims walk its streets.
Everything worth having in the realm has a claim on this place.
