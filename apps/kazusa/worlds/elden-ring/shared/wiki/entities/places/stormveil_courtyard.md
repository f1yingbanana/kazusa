# Stormveil Great Courtyard

The muddy heart of the castle, overlooked on all sides by galleries full of
archers. Grafted Scions lurk at its edges and the banished knights take their
posts in the arches. It is where the garrison trains, eats, quarrels and buries
its dead without ceremony.
