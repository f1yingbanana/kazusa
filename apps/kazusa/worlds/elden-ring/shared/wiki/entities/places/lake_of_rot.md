# Lake of Rot

An enormous cavern filled with a shallow tide of rot. Rusted effigies and white
pillars jut from it. It lies at the very bottom of Ainsel, and nothing that
grows here should. It is Astel's prison. At its far end, a lift is tucked away
for those who carry a moon-ring.
