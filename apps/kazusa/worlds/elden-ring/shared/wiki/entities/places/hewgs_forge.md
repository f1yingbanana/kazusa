# Hewg's Forge

A tiny smithy at the far end of the Hold's corridor. Hewg, the master smith, is
chained to it by Marika's order, and he repairs what is brought to him with an
old hard skill. He is the Hold's foundation, and he says little unless asked
about his lady.
