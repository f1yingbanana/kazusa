# Jarburg

A little walled village on a green slope above the south-east lake, full of
tended flowers and potted plants that turn out to be alive. Living Jars grow and
are looked after here, and the small jar Jar-Bairn looks after his elders with
great seriousness. Diallos of House Hoslow has found something like a home here.
Their kin Alexander left to see the world, and is talked of with pride.
