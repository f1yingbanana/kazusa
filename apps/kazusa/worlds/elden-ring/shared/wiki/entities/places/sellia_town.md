# Sellia, Town of Sorcery

A town of carved stone and hanging walkways in Caelid's east, deserted except
for stubborn Sellian sorcerers and watchful Nox warriors. It sits above the
buried Eternal City of Nokron. Old Master Lusat came from here, and the town's
night-sorcery assassins are remembered with respect. Three seals keep its main
door barred.
