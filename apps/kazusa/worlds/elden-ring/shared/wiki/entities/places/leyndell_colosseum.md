# Leyndell Colosseum

A large marble arena where Godfrey's warriors once sparred before cheering
crowds. It's been empty for a long age, but the door opens now and then, and
lately the sounds of fighting have come from inside. Corhyn and Goldmask camp
nearby when the Tree's light shifts and they look for answers.
