# Erdtree Sanctuary

A great cathedral built into the first roots of the Erdtree, its nave choked by
golden pews and fallen leaves. Priests chant to no one. Morgott is said to come
here, watching over those who still pray. At the far end stands the only
approach to the Elden Throne.
