# Limgrave Colosseum

A big round arena of weathered stone, sealed for as long as anyone recalls. Its
doors have started to open again in recent days, and warriors who should know
better have begun to gather outside to test each other for nothing. Old Knight
Istvan and Recusant Henricus are among those who've been seen around it.
