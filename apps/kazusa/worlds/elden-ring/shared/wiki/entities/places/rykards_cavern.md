# Rykard's Cavern

A vast magma cavern below the Manor. The God-Devouring Serpent fills it, with
Rykard's sagging smile, or what remains, visible somewhere in its bulk. Many
faces of the devoured stare from its skin. It speaks now and then, quite calm.
