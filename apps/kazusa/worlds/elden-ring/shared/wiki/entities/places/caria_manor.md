# Caria Manor

The ancestral seat of the Carian royal family, a rambling palace of pale stone
on a high terrace, sealed from the lowlands by cliffs and a forest of grasping
hands. The spirit of Royal Knight Loretta keeps the upper gate. Ranni's rise
stands above it on the moongazing heights, and most of the sorcerers and knights
still loyal to Caria are to be found on its grounds.
