# Cathedral of Manus Metyr

A beautiful grey cathedral on the western Altus, with stained windows showing
long pale hands. Count Ymir, a soft-spoken Finger-Weaver, holds a kind of court
here and takes in those who've been hurt by the world. Jolán, Swordhand of
Night, stands at his side. A false floor under his chair leads to the Ruins of
Miyr.
