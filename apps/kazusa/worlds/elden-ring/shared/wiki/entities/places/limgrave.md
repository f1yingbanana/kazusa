# Limgrave

The southernmost green land of the realm: soft hills, wind-bent grass and
crumbled stonework, with Stormveil Castle glowering from the western cliffs.
Roads run from the Stormhill gate east toward Caelid, and the Erdtree's light
still falls here clear and gold. Godrick claims it all by right of Stormveil,
though his soldiers hold little beyond the roads and forts, and the wilds belong
to Demi-Humans, trolls and whatever walks at night.
