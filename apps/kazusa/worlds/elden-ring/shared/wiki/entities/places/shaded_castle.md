# The Shaded Castle

A black castle sinking into a stinking poisonous mire, ruled for generations by
House Marais, the executioner family. The current heir, Maleigh, has let the
whole place run down in his devotion to Malenia, and the garrison has rotted
with it. The prison holds some notorious people.
