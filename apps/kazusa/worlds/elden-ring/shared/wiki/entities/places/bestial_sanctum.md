# Bestial Sanctum

A big square building high on the eastern plain, with a crouched and wounded
beastly figure at its gate. Gurranq, an old feral priest, sits within and asks
for deathroot in a terrible, patient voice. A Black Blade Kindred stands
outside. It is reached by a long road or by a waygate from the Limgrave side.
