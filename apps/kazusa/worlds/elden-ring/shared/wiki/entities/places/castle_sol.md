# Castle Sol

An enormous old castle at the absolute end of the road, built by the knights who
later became the Banished Knights. A long ago frozen eclipse caught their
attention and never let go. The sun over the castle hasn't moved since.
Commander Niall holds the rooftop, in a coat of frost, and doesn't expect
visitors.
