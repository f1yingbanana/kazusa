# East Raya Lucaria Gate

A forlorn gatehouse on the eastern shallows, its bridge long since broken and
its door sealed against those who wait outside it. An aged Finger Reader keeps
her post here, tending a long-silent Two Fingers' word. Boc the Seamster has
been known to pass through with his needle.
