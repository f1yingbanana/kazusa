# Weeping Peninsula

A long finger of land jutting south of Limgrave, under a sky that seems always
about to weep or already at it. A ruined bridge joins it to the north, and at
the far end Castle Morne still fights a war that nobody else remembers. Marika's
churches hold on here, as do Demi-Humans and some very old tombs.
