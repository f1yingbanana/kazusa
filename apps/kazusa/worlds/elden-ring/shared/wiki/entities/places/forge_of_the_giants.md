# Forge of the Giants

An enormous stone hearth, built by the giants before their fall and held by the
last of their kind. The Flame of Ruin is shut up inside it under Marika's order.
It's said that this flame alone could burn the Erdtree. A narrow road runs
around its rim, and a Site of Grace burns there in defiance.
