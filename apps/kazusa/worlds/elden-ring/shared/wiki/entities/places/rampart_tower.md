# Rampart Tower

A heavy tower dominating the castle's upper walls, with a working lift, a small
Site of Grace and a squad of soldiers who never quite stop drinking. From its
top one can see Agheel Lake and beyond. In Godrick's day this was the castle's
real sentry post.
