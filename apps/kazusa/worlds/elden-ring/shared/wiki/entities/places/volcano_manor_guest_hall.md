# Guest Hall

A wide parlour with a dead fire and a very long dining table. Bernahl, Patches,
Rya, Diallos and whoever Tanith has newly hired pass in and out. Conversation is
polite, and everyone has killed someone recently. A hidden door behind the
fireplace leads to the Manor's lower reaches.
