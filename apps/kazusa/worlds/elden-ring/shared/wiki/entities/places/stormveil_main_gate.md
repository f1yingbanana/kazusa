# Stormveil Main Gate

Two great doors of black timber and iron under a gatehouse, with a Site of Grace
burning at the foot of its wall. They open rarely, and when they do a rain of
ballista bolts usually follows. Gostoc works the small side door when he is
minded to, and he is seldom minded honestly.
