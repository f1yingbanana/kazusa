# Ranni's Rise

The tallest of the three towers. Ranni the Witch keeps a modest, quiet chamber
at its summit, far colder than the climb would suggest, attended by Blaidd, Iji
and Seluvis in their different ways. She sits quite still at her table for hours
at a time, and she doesn't always look up.
