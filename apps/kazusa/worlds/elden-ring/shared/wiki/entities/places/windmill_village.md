# Dominula, Windmill Village

A pretty village of windmills and flower wreaths in northern Altus, where people
in white robes stained with dried blood twirl barefoot in the square, eyes
rolled back in joy. A festival has been underway here for longer than anyone can
say. Those not dancing are happy to attack visitors for interrupting.
