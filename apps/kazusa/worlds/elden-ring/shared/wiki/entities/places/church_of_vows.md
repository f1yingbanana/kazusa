# Church of Vows

A pretty stone church on a bare knoll above the lake's eastern shallows, kept
with quiet care. Pastor Miriel, an enormous old turtle with a mitre on his
shell, tends what vows remain and speaks softly to any who come. It is a place
where oaths were sworn before the Golden Order, and one of the very few still
kept with care. Merchants who shelter nearby have been found dead by the Bell
Bearing Hunter.
