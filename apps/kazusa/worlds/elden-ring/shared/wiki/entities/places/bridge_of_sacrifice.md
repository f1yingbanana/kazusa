# Bridge of Sacrifice

A broad old bridge over the sea at the Peninsula's neck, with a Site of Grace on
its southern approach. Irina of Morne sits here holding a letter to her father,
begging passers-by to carry it to the castle. A Godrick lieutenant's squad and a
Night's Cavalry ride patrol its span.
