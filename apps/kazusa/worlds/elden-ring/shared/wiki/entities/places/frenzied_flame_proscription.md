# Frenzied Flame Proscription

At the bottom of the sewers, behind a door that has to be opened naked of
everything, the Three Fingers lie in a burning yellow chamber. This is where
Hyetta has walked, and where Vyke once took his way. It has been forbidden to
all for ages.
