# Volcano Manor

A grand mansion of dark stone and red carpets perched on a smoking lake of
magma. Praetor Rykard ruled the Recusants from here until he fed himself to the
serpent in its depths. Now his consort Lady Tanith runs the house and extends
her invitation to those who hunt other Tarnished. The guests are given rooms and
contracts. Beneath, the serpent is still hungry.
