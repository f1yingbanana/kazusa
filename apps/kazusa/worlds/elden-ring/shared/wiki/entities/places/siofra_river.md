# Siofra River

An enormous lightless cave beneath the south of the realm, somehow lit by an
artificial night full of false stars. The Siofra flows through it past the ruins
of the Eternal Cities. Ancestral Followers hunt among the deer of the bone
forests, and there's an unsettling peace about it all.
