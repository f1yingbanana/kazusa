# Guardians' Garrison

A bleak thick-walled fort in sight of the Freezing Lake, where the Fire Monks
set a watch on the Forge. Chief Guardian Arghanthy, still furious about what
Adan stole, commands it. The Guilty, thorn sorcerers banished north for their
crimes, serve under him.
