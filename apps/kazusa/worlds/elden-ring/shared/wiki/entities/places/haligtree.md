# Miquella's Haligtree

A vast twisted tree on a high frozen rock at the very top of the world, raised
by Miquella with his own blood as a shelter for Albinaurics, Misbegotten and all
others cast out by the Order. Haligtree Town clings to its boughs and Elphael to
its base. Malenia waits for her brother at its roots, and the rot is spreading
down from her. It hasn't flowered. It may be dying.
