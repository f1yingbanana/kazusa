# Moonlight Altar

A lonely moonlit plateau, high above the lake, closed off from all roads. Its
only way up is a sealed lift out of the Lake of Rot, for those with Ranni's
ring. Glintstone flowers shine under a moon that does not move, and a glintstone
dragon roams the cathedral grounds.
