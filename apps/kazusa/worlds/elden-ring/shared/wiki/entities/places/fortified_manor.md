# Fortified Manor

A square keep in the upper city, whose great hall was once where the champions
gathered to ask the Two Fingers' counsel. The Roundtable Hold is its copy. Its
current occupants are Leyndell's household knights, and its stair leads to the
Queen's Bedchamber.
