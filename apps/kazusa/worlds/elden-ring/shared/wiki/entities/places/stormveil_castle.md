# Stormveil Castle

A vast old stronghold perched on a cliff above the sea, built under the kings of
the storm before the Erdtree and held now by Godrick the Grafted, last of the
demigods to claim the name of Golden Lineage with any show of grace. Its
courtyards are crammed with exiled soldiers, banished knights and hawks with
blades lashed to their talons, and its walls are mottled with thorn-lined
craters left by something older. Prisoners are taken inside; parts of them stay.
