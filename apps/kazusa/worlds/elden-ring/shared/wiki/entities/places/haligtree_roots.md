# Haligtree Roots

A huge dim cave under the Haligtree, filled with stagnant rot and the very
bottom of its roots. Miquella's empty cocoon is here, split open. Malenia sits
waiting in a tangle of rotting flowers, very patient, very polite, and she has
never lost a fight.
