# Liftside Chamber

A central junction deep in the castle, with a Site of Grace on its floor. The
passages leading off it go to the armoury, the kitchens and the rooms where
Godrick's surgeons work, and the smell does not stay in those rooms. Many of the
castle's prisoners were last seen here.
