# Mt. Gelmir

A smoking mountain of steep black rock, wrapped in dust and ash. The Seethewater
River runs dry below it and only boils in spurts. Capital soldiers camp at its
foot in a long, stalled siege against the Manor at its summit, and neither side
is winning.
