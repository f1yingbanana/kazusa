# Chapel of Anticipation

What remains of a holy place on a shard of rock in the air south of the Lands
Between. There's no road to it, and a sending gate at the Four Belfries is the
only way in. Rain and wind tear through its open roof. Godrick's grafted scion
lurks in its nave, set to meet any Tarnished the grace wakes below. It is named
for waiting, and little else has ever been done here.
