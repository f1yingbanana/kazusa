# Liurnia of the Lakes

The west of the realm is one enormous shallow lake, slowly swallowing the towns
along its shore. Fog lies on it most days, and the Academy of Raya Lucaria rises
on its island at the centre, with the Carian manor on the western heights and
the Bellum Highway running along the east. Sorcerers, thieves, crystalline
things and the very old all live here, without much love for each other.
