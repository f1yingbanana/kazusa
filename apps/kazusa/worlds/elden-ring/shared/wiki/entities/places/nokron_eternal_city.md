# Nokron, Eternal City

An enormous silent city of pale stone at the bottom of the world, sealed by the
Greater Will for the Nox's offence against it. Its sealed road is said to wait
on the fall of a star. Silver blobs of living mimic and ancient Nox swordsmen
walk its streets. Its sacred ground holds the hidden sword of the Night, which
Ranni wants very badly.
