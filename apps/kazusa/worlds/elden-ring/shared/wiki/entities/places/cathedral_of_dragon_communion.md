# Cathedral of Dragon Communion

A grand ruined church of old stone with a great dragon-maw altar, near where
Decaying Ekzykes roosts. A pale priest of Dragon Communion accepts dragon hearts
and gives power in return. The Beastmen and drakes of the area give it room.
