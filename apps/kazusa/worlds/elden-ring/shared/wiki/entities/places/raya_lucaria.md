# Academy of Raya Lucaria

The great school of glintstone sorcery, built on an island of stone in the
lake's middle and sealed behind its main gate since the Academy broke with the
Carians. Inside are spiralling towers, stair-choked courtyards and rooms that
shift if one does not watch them. Conspectus scholars, crowned with glintstone,
pursue the stars in their several ways while a demented queen cradles an empty
egg in the library. Only those who hold a glintstone key may enter by the front.
