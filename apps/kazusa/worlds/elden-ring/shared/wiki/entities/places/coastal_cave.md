# Coastal Cave

A damp cavern in the south shore's rocks, smelling of tide and rot. Two
Demi-Human Chiefs have made it their court, and Blackguard Big Boggart cooks
prawns on the beach nearby. Boggart's cooking is the only reason anyone goes
there on purpose.
