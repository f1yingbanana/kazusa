# Lord Contender's Evergaol

A slab of stone in the deep snow. Vyke of the Roundtable, the Dragonspear, who
came closest of them all to the throne, stands inside it, his eyes burnt and his
armour fused by the Frenzied Flame. He's not able to say anything anymore.
