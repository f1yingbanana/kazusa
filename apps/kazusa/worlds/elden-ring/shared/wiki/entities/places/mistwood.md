# Mistwood

An old dark wood of huge trunks between Limgrave's fields and Fort Haight. Fog
pools in its hollows and the wounded Runebear prowls its deep paths. A well with
an old lift leads down from here to the Siofra River. Blaidd keeps watch on the
woods for Ranni, and his howl is what most people know of him.
