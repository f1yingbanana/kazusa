# Greyoll's Dragonbarrow

A rocky upland north of the scarlet valley, looked over by red peaks and the
huge dying bulk of Greyoll, mother of many dragons. Lesser dragons roost on the
Fort Faroth cliffs. The way up from Caelid is long, and Radahn's soldiers don't
come here.
