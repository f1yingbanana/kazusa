# Finger Ruins of Miyr

A very long stair goes down through the Cathedral's floor to a pale grotto,
where a colossal pulsing finger-being is found. Metyr, Mother of the Fingers,
was sent down by the Greater Will with its orders. It has stopped sending any
more.
