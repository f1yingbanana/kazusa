# Swamp of Aeonia

A great shallow valley of red water and thick air, where Malenia's flower
erupted at the end of her duel with Radahn. Giant rotted flowers still release
spores into the air, and Kindred of Rot swarm in the mud. Nothing passes through
unharmed. In the centre, Commander O'Neil carries on fighting, with his army of
dead, a war that ended long ago.
