# Scadu Altus

The great highland in the Land's middle, a mirror of the plateau back home with
paler gold on its trees. Messmer's forces keep the roads. Ruined Hornsent towns
sit at the bottom of ravines, and there's a slow fall of leaves that never quite
reaches the ground.
