# Inside the Erdtree

A vast gold-lit hollow inside the Tree, beyond the thorns that even Morgott
can't pass. Here the Elden Ring's broken remains turn in the air. Queen Marika
hangs here with a shard through her, cracked like old porcelain, and within her
waits the man who was also her. The golden beast that rules them both sleeps
somewhere further in. No one living has been here.
