# The Dung Eater

A shape chained in a dark cell under the Hold, wearing a horned scarlet helm
with bleeding stumps. A grey ghost of the same man comes and goes, and speaks in
a voice that makes you want to wash.

## Background

A Tarnished, a notorious murderer and defiler who places a curse on his victims
so that their souls can't reach the Erdtree. He's said to have been caught.
People don't ask by whom.

## Secret

Known by: dung-eater, gideon-ofnir, big-boggart

Sir Gideon holds his body in the Hold's cellar, and his spirit still comes and
goes. He has the heart of an Omen, without the body.
