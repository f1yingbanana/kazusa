# Primeval Sorcerer Azur

A big crouching figure in the remains of a blue-green robe, on a high ledge of
Mt. Gelmir, gazing at the sky. It's hard to believe that he's alive.

## Background

One of the founders of the Academy and originator of the Karolos Conspectus. He
was Sellen's first teacher, and a stern judge of character. He looked into the
Current and saw darkness, an abyss that bewitched him and frightened him. Exiled
from the Academy, he settled above the Hermit Village and he hasn't really moved
since.
