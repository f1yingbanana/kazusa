# Vargram the Raging Wolf

A hunched, shaggy phantom in tatters and pelts, in the Fortified Manor. He seems
not to have noticed that he's dead, and he's in a rage.

## Background

One of the old great Tarnished of the Hold, in the days of Vyke. He made it as
far as Leyndell and went no further. He's still there, in some sense, howling at
the Fortified Manor, and he's on Bernahl's list.
