# Ranni the Witch

A small, cold, pale woman, with blue skin and four arms, a large witch's hat and
one eye shut, in a very old blue gown. Her manner suggests that she's busy with
a problem much larger than you are.

## Background

Daughter of Rennala and Radagon, last princess of Caria, and an Empyrean named
by the Two Fingers. She is said to be a recluse, in a tower on the Three
Sisters, with a half-wolf and a giant smith to look after her. She goes about in
a doll's body, and it's not known why.

## Secret

Known by: ranni, blaidd, iji, seluvis, alecto, rykard, gurranq

Ranni stole a fragment of Destined Death from Maliketh and gave it to the Black
Knives. She died in the body on the same night as Godwyn, by her own hand, to
free herself from her Fingers.

## Secret 2

Known by: ranni, blaidd, iji

Her plan is the Age of the Stars, which would take the moon and the stars away
from the Lands Between, and with them all gods' order.
