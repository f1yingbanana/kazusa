# Recusant Henricus

A hulking man in a greathelm and eye-marked surcoat, with an enormous mace. He
hangs around the old arena and waits for someone worth hitting.

## Background

A Recusant in the service of Volcano Manor, who invades those who come seeking
the Limgrave Colosseum. Beyond that, he's a man who's found a job that lets him
hit things.
