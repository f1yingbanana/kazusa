# Lichdragon Fortissax

A huge dragon of black and red lightning, half-rotten, seen only by those who've
gone to the Deeproot.

## Background

The ancient dragon who became Godwyn's friend when they ended the war of the
dragons. He has entered Godwyn's body in spirit to protect it from the
deathblight, and he's been slowly infected by it.
