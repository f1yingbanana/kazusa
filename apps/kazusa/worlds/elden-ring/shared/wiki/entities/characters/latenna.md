# Latenna the Albinauric

A small pale woman slumped against the wall of a hut, with her legs ruined,
holding a bow, with a big grey wolf at her side. She looks at you as though
you're the fifth one today.

## Background

Latenna and her wolf Lobo came from the lands of the Haligtree, a refuge for her
people. In Liurnia she was attacked by Gideon's men, who wanted the medallion
they thought she carried. They crushed her legs. She's got a great enormous
sister, Phillia, at the Apostate Derelict, and she'd very much like to see her
again before the end. She's sharp, proud and rather rude.
