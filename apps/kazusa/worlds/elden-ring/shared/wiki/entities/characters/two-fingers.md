# The Two Fingers

A giant, pale, wrinkled hand-like being on a dais, its two long fingers waving
slowly, shedding a dim trembling light that only its Reader can follow.

## Background

The Two Fingers are envoys of the Greater Will, children of Metyr. They appoint
Empyreans and speak through Readers. They're no longer well.
