# Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree

A tall figure in pale armour on an armoured horse, holding a great glaive and a
very good bow, on a Haligtree bough. There's no way past her and she's sure
about that.

## Background

Loretta was a Royal Knight of Caria, a personal guard to its royalty, and a fine
archer. She left all her honours behind when she heard about Miquella's refuge
for the unwanted, and she went north to guard it, as Knight of the Haligtree. An
oath-bound spectral image of her still keeps the Carian Manor's upper terrace.
She regards the Albinaurics and others under her protection as her own people.
