# Sage Gowry

A small, elderly man with a gentle face and very pale eyes, puttering about a
shack among scarlet flowers. He seems delighted to have a guest and offers you
something to eat.

## Background

A gentle old sage who's lived quietly in Caelid since Malenia's flower came. He
raised Millicent and her sisters as his daughters. He's generous with cures and
advice to all comers.

## Secret

Known by: gowry, malenia

Gowry is no man. He's devoted to the Order of Rot, and he's raising Malenia's
buds to flower, Millicent among them.
