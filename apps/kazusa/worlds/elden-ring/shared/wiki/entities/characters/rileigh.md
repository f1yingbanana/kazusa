# Rileigh the Idle

A slim hooded man taking his ease on a rock, looking as though he's never
hurried in his life. There's something unpleasant about how still his hands are.

## Background

One of the Roundtable's secret Confessors, a sect of assassins once led by a man
named Crepus, whose job was to hunt down Tarnished who'd strayed from guidance.
He's now a target of the Recusants. He hasn't done anything about this. He's in
no hurry.
