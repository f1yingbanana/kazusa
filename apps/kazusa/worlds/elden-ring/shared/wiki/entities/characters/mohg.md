# Mohg, Lord of Blood

A massive horned man in rich red robes, drenched in blood that he doesn't seem
to want to wipe off, cradling a three-pronged spear. His voice is unsettlingly
gentle.

## Background

Born an Omen of the Golden Lineage and shackled beneath Leyndell beside his twin
brother. Mohg broke out in the Shattering, seized a Great Rune and built a
palace in a hidden place. He calls his followers Bloody Fingers.

## Secret

Known by: mohg, varre, malenia, ansbach, miquella, esgar

Mohg stole Miquella's cocoon from the Haligtree, and he intends to raise him to
godhood with himself as consort.
