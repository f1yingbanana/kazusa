# Hornsent Grandam

A small, very old, horned woman on her knees in a back room behind a locked
door, singing in a voice like a slow creak. She looks up at you and she knows
exactly what you are.

## Background

One of the last living Hornsent in Belurat, shut away behind a door in a
storeroom. She's kept the rites for the Divine Beast, the sculpted keeper of her
people, and she sings in its service. She remembers everything that was done,
and what her people did before that. She's prepared to help anyone who'll put a
stop to the fire. She's made a very good scorpion stew.
