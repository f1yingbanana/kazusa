# The Tarnished

Someone in old travelling gear, who has just walked up out of the Stranded
Graveyard with the look of having been dead for a long time. They've got no
maiden, no standing and no particular reason to be alive.

## Background

One of Godfrey's exiled warriors, or the descendant of one, called back across
the fog by the returning grace to find the Elden Ring and become Elden Lord.
Nobody expects much of them.
