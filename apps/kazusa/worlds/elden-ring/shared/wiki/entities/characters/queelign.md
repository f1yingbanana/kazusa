# Fire Knight Queelign

A blackened knight in a burned surcoat, marching through the ruins of a Hornsent
town with a sword held high. He's praying out loud as he works.

## Background

A Fire Knight in Messmer's crusade, who dreams of being a second Impaler. He's
zealous and he's clearly enjoying it. He has been burning the remnants of the
Hornsent in Marika's name for as long as he can remember. He's starting to have
the first small doubts, and they're making him angry.
