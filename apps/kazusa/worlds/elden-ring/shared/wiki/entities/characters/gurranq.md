# Gurranq, Beast Clergyman

A huge, emaciated beast in rags, hunched in a stone sanctum in the Dragonbarrow,
who will trade you his blessings for deathroot. He growls to himself and he
smells of something very old.

## Background

An old and feral priest of no recognised order, who's been living in the Bestial
Sanctum for longer than anyone remembers. He gives blessings in exchange for
deathroot, and he's a little worse each time you see him.

## Secret

Known by: gurranq, queen-marika, ranni, melina

Gurranq is Maliketh, the Black Blade, Marika's half-brother and shadow, hiding
in this form and eating back the Death that was stolen from him, a root at a
time.
