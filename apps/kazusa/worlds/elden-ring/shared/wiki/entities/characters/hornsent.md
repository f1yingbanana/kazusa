# The Hornsent

A tall, gaunt man with a crown of horns, in worn ceremonial wrappings, who
stares at you with cold loathing, and who has decided, for now, not to do
anything about it.

## Background

A Hornsent survivor of Messmer's crusade, travelling among Miquella's followers
for reasons of his own. He's bluntly scornful of Tarnished and the Erdtree. He
helps to find what Miquella has left behind in the Land of Shadow because he
thinks that it may lead him to the one thing he wants: Messmer's death, and the
chance to take the news to Marika herself. His people were cruel long before
they were burned, and he has never once admitted that.
