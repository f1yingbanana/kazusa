# Dragon Communion Priest

A hunched, heavily wrapped, ancient-looking priest at a stone dragon-mouth in a
hidden seaside church. There's a rustle from under his cowl that doesn't quite
sound like breathing.

## Background

One of the bent old officiants of Dragon Communion, who keeps the Limgrave
altar. He offers those who bring dragon hearts a chance to become something a
little closer to a dragon, and he warns them about what it'll do to them. They
don't listen.
