# Esgar, Priest of Blood

A gaunt priest in dark red, with two blood-dripping hands, in the darkest part
of the capital's tombs. He bows, and a dog at his side doesn't move.

## Background

A priest of Mohg's blood-sect, with a chamber deep beneath Leyndell.
