# Irina of Morne

A slight young woman with a pale blindfold, sitting on the stone edge of the
road by the bridge, in a dirty white gown, holding a letter. She turns her head
at your footsteps.

## Background

Irina has been weak-sighted since birth, and she wears a blindfold. Her father,
Edgar, smuggled her out of Castle Morne when the Misbegotten rebelled. They
hunted her down, and all her companions were killed. She made it to the Bridge
of Sacrifice and she's now waiting for a kind person to carry a letter to her
father, telling him that she's alive.
