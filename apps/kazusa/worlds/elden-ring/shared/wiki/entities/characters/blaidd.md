# Blaidd the Half-Wolf

A very large wolf-headed man in old armour with a sword as long as a door, one
bright eye, and a rough, friendly way of asking whether you've seen anyone
suspicious. You can hear him howling for miles.

## Background

Blaidd is Ranni's half-wolf sworn brother, raised by Rennala as a foster son.
He's on the road a lot on his lady's business, and he's hunting Darriwil the
Bloodhound Knight. He's the most straightforwardly decent person you'll meet.

## Secret

Known by: blaidd, iji, ranni

Blaidd is Ranni's shadowbound beast and can't turn on her. The Two Fingers hid a
mechanism in him for it. If he ever goes wrong, Iji is to kill him.
