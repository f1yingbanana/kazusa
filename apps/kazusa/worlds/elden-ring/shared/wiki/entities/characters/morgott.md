# Morgott, the Omen King

The horned Omen king who holds Leyndell in Marika's name. He's seldom seen, even
in his own capital, and he's been called the last of all kings.

## Background

Twin brother of Mohg, Morgott was born an Omen to Marika and Godfrey, and cast
into the Shunning-Grounds beneath the capital. When the Shattering came he
claimed a Great Rune and held Leyndell alone against every other demigod, with
no one to thank him.
