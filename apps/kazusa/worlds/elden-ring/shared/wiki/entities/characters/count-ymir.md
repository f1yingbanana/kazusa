# Count Ymir

A slight, gracious elderly man with long pale fingers and a fine old coat,
sitting on a throne in a beautiful church, who rises to greet you as if you were
an expected guest. He asks you about the stars.

## Background

A master glintstone sorcerer and the teacher of Rellana, Count Ymir is now High
Priest at the Cathedral of Manus Metyr. He's unfailingly courteous and he asks
everyone about the stars.

## Secret

Known by: count-ymir, jolan

Ymir tends the wounded Mother of Fingers beneath his cathedral. He means to
restore her voice or to become a new Mother himself, and he's done dreadful
things on the way.
