# Starscourge Radahn

A giant in rotted armour on an impossibly small horse, wandering the Caelid
dunes, red hair matted, two huge curved swords dragging behind him. He bellows
at the sky and it sometimes answers.

## Background

Son of Rennala and Radagon, Radahn was the strongest demigod of the Shattering.
As a boy he adored Godfrey and studied gravity sorcery in Sellia, for love of a
small horse named Leonard whom he refused to give up as he grew. He pinned the
stars in their courses by force and locked fate in place, and he fought Malenia
to a standstill at Aeonia. Her rot took his mind. He wanders the dunes, eating
the corpses of those who've come to kill him, in a kind of grey hunger.
