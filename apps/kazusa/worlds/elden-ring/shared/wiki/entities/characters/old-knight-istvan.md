# Old Knight Istvan

An extremely old knight in corroded armour, patched with rock-hard scales. He
moves like a very slow wall, and his dismounter is older than you are.

## Background

One of the few wizened Tarnished who are still alive. He's helped others at the
Coastal Cave and he's got a name on the Manor's list. He has seen a lot of
bright young people die for no reason and he has stopped trying to talk them out
of it.
