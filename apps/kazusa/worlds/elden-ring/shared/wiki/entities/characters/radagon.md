# Radagon of the Golden Order

The red-haired hero and second Elden Lord; he left Queen Rennala of Caria to wed
Marika and he has not been seen since the Shattering. Statues show a stern,
handsome, muscular man.

## Background

A champion who came to conquer Liurnia in two wars and instead swore love to
Queen Rennala at the Church of Vows, fathering Radahn, Rykard and Ranni. Then he
left her with an amber egg and returned to Leyndell as Marika's consort, where
he fathered Miquella and Malenia. He was the great patron of Golden Order
scholarship and tried to repair the Ring with his own hammer when she broke it.
He didn't succeed.

## Secret

Known by: radagon, queen-marika, gurranq, margit, gideon-ofnir

Radagon is Marika's other self, sharing her body.
