# Romina, Saint of the Bud

A huge pale figure, half woman and half something with a lot of legs, in a
rotting church, holding up a long bladed flower. She's waiting. There are
butterflies.

## Background

An attendant of the Church of the Bud in the Ruins of Rauh. When Messmer's army
razed the church she clutched its ceremonial Bud in speechless silence. In the
ashes she found a twisted divine element, and she reformed it into the dreaded
Scarlet Rot. Over time her body has taken on aspects of flowers, centipedes,
scorpions, ants and butterflies. She keeps watch over a Sealing Tree, which
keeps the Gate shut. She offers her rot, as a sacred rite of purification.
