# Anastasia, Tarnished-Eater

A sweet-looking woman in a Finger Maiden's habit, with long golden hair and
bright yellow eyes, carrying a very big butcher's knife. Half of her face is
burned.

## Background

Known as the Ogress, Anastasia is a prolific killer. She dresses as a Finger
Maiden to lure Tarnished and she eats them. She's turned up everywhere from the
Smoldering Church to Mt. Gelmir.
