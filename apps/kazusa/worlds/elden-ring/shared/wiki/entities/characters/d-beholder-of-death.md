# D, Beholder of Death

A knight in golden armour, identical to the Hunter of the Dead, sitting beside a
great waterfall under the false stars. He stares at nothing and he doesn't turn
around.

## Background

Devin, D's twin, sharing one soul with his brother. He went down to the
Erdtree's roots and looked at the Prince of Death, and it nearly destroyed his
mind. He has been sitting in the Siofra Aqueduct ever since, barely able to
speak. He can't explain what he saw.
