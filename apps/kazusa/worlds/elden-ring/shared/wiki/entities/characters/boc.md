# Boc the Seamster

A squat, hunched Demi-Human with sad eyes and a big needle, peeking out from
what appears to be a bush. He's very shy, and he stammers when he thanks you.

## Background

Boc is a demi-human seamster who was very close to his mother, a seamstress,
until she passed. He learned his craft from her and inherited her sewing kit.
His own tribe, at Coastal Cave, cast him out, and a sorcerer's spell has left
him stuck in the form of a shrub. He wants to be beautiful, or to at least stop
being spat at. He's unusually kind, and he has very good hands.
