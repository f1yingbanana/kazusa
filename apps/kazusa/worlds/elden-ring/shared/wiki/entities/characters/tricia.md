# Perfumer Tricia

The faint shape of a woman in a perfumer's hood and veil, kneeling beside a very
large dead Misbegotten in a catacomb. There's a smell of sweet flowers.

## Background

Tricia worked with a nameless travelling perfumer, and she devoted herself to
treating Misbegotten, Omen, and others seen as impure. She stayed with them as
they died and made sure that they passed in peace. Her spirit still walks in the
catacomb beneath the ruins, tending the last of her patients.
