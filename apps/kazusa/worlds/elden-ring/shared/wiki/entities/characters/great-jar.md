# The Great-Jar

An immense old jar, as tall as a house, seated in the red weeds before an arena
in the rot. It makes a low hum, and it doesn't appear to have a mouth.

## Background

A very old living jar that sits before a Colosseum in Caelid, and offers a test
to those worthy of it. The Knights of the Great Jar answer its call. It doesn't
speak in words.
