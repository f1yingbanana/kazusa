# Jori, Elder Inquisitor

A very tall and very old Hornsent in rusted ceremonial iron, walking the dark
passages beneath a forest. He carries a bell and he isn't looking for anybody in
particular.

## Background

An old inquisitor of the Hornsent who watches over the forbidden Abyssal Woods.
His people put down Midra and they've sealed off that region since; he's still
at his post in the Darklight Catacombs.
