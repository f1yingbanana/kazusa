# Preceptor Miriam

A severe masked woman in a blue preceptor's robe, walking the Study Hall alone
with an air of being very much in the middle of a lecture.

## Background

A teacher of sorcery at the Carian Study Hall, Miriam has served the royals
since the old days and now guards the hall from intruders, following an
instruction that nobody ever came back to lift. She continues to give classes to
no one.
