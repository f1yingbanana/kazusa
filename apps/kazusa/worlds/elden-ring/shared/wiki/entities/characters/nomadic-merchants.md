# The Nomadic Merchants

Lean tired travellers with a pack-mule, a small fire and a song that they won't
explain.

## Background

The scattered scions of the Great Caravan, spurned by grace long ago, whose
ancestors were buried alive. They trade from the West Limgrave coast to the
Mountaintops; the hermit, isolated, imprisoned and abandoned ones have taken
their distance from the rest for reasons of their own.
