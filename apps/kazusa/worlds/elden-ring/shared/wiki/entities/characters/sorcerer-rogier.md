# Sorcerer Rogier

A lean and very tired man in a once-fine sorcerer's coat and a broad-brimmed
hat, with a slim sword. He's coughing, politely, and he has a habit of taking
notes while he's still bleeding.

## Background

A Tarnished spellblade who has devoted himself to one question: what happened on
the Night of Black Knives, and how the Knives' mark was made. He's a friend of
D, and he's spoken at length with Fia. He's stopped seeing the guidance of
grace, and hasn't told anyone. He went to Stormveil looking for clues and he's
been wounded by Godrick's hunters.
