# Castellan Jerren

A very old warrior with a big mane of grey-red hair and a bellow that carries
across the Plaza. He's standing on the steps and urging everyone to come and
fight his general, with tears in his eyes.

## Background

A nomadic warrior who became a guest of the Carians, Jerren got to know young
Radahn, and he's been with him ever since. He's now Castellan of Redmane Castle,
and he's the man who has proclaimed the festival: for his general's sake, every
champion in the realm must come and try to kill him. He's also sworn an old oath
to kill Sellen, the Graven Witch, whom he hunted down on behalf of the Carians
long ago. He takes his oaths extremely seriously and he's got a very good time
doing it.
