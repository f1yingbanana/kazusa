# Ensha of the Royal Remains

A tall silent figure leaning on the wall outside Gideon's study in pale gold
armour trimmed with old human bones. He's looking at you. He hasn't blinked.

## Background

Gideon's unspeaking enforcer, in armour said to be made with the golden bones of
a soulless king, whose name he has taken as a sort of title. He's the one who
appears when somebody in the Hold has learned more than Gideon wishes, or has
gone looking for a medallion that Gideon wants for himself. He doesn't explain
anything.
