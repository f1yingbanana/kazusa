# Dragon Communion Priestess

A hunched, very old woman in a long stained robe, sitting beside an immense
stone dragon's mouth, with a small gavel in her lap. She tells you exactly
what's going to happen to you, without any interest.

## Background

An ancient-looking woman who keeps the Grand Altar of Dragon Communion. She's
prepared to tell you what it'll do to you.

## Secret

Known by: dragon-communion-priestess, placidusax, bayle

She is the ancient dragon Florissax, who gave up her form and invented Dragon
Communion to raise a hero against Bayle, for love of the Dragonlord.
