# Thiollier

A slim young man in a mask with a tranquil sleeping face and silver hair, and
garments sewn with countless needles. He apologises for being in your way. He'd
like to know if you've ever slept really well.

## Background

A Tarnished, an isolated young man who once met St. Trina and was granted the
sweet repose of velvet sleep. He's spent the rest of his days trying to find it
again. He thinks that he's weak and useless, and he's secretly an expert in
poisons. Miquella's enchantment brought him here, but St. Trina's the one he's
really following.
