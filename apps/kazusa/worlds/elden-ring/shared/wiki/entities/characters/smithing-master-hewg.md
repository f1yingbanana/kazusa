# Smithing Master Hewg

A bent, scaly old Misbegotten at a small red forge, with a heavy chain around
one ankle, and hands that move with absolute precision. He rarely looks up.

## Background

A Scaly Misbegotten, chained to the Hold at its foundation. He practises
smithing as it was before the Order, as something near divine. Long ago a spirit
tuner with distinctive eyes helped him, and he's waited for someone like that
ever since. He's waited out every great Tarnished who ever sat at the table,
sharpening their weapons and watching them go. He's very tired, and his loyalty
is to the Queen who put the chain there, and he'd like to see the end of this.
