# Astel, Naturalborn of the Void

A huge insectile thing from beyond the sky, which has got a skull and many
wings.

## Background

A malformed star of the Void, unleashed by the Greater Will upon the Eternal
Cities. It destroyed one of them, stole their sky and was bound under the Lake
of Rot.
