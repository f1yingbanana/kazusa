# St. Trina

A pale girl, or boy, half-buried in the throat of a giant deep-purple flower,
asleep with folded arms, with a thin line of purple blood running from an old
cut at the temple. The smell is delicious, and one shouldn't breathe it.

## Background

A figure of old stories, with no birthplace and no fixed sex, who appears where
people are in pain and eases them into sleep. Some say a comely girl, others a
boy. The Saint's sudden coming and going are the only certainty.

## Secret

Known by: st-trina, miquella, thiollier, ansbach

St. Trina is the other half of Miquella, as Radagon is of Marika, and has been
cast down the Stone Coffin Fissure.
