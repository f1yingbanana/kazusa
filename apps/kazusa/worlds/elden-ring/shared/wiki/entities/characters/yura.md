# Bloody Finger Hunter Yura

A tall thin man in an iron kasa and old foreign armour, sitting among the ruins,
with a very long, very sharp blade. He tips the hat and he warns you to stay
away from the lake.

## Background

Yura is a warrior from the Land of Reeds who hunts Mohg's Bloody Fingers. He's
looking in particular for Eleonora, the Violet Bloody Finger, a former Drake
Knight whom he loved, and he wants to kill her out of mercy. He's a wise and
melancholy man, and he'll warn off anyone from Dragon Communion. In time his
body will be taken by something much worse, though he doesn't know that.
