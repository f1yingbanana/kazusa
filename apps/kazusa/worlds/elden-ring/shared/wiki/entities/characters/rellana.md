# Rellana, Twin Moon Knight

A tall armoured woman with two moon-blue blades and a long braid of black hair,
which isn't hers, tied at the shoulder. She carries herself like a queen who
gave it up on purpose.

## Background

Younger sister of Rennala, and as much a prodigy as she. She and her sister met
twin moons as girls. She threw away her birthright, in open ritual combat at the
colosseum, to follow Messmer, knowing that it would come to nothing, and Rennala
let her go with a braid of her hair. In the Crusade she broke a linchpin stone
that sank the town of Moorth into the earth. She holds Castle Ensis in Messmer's
name and she will not be moved.
