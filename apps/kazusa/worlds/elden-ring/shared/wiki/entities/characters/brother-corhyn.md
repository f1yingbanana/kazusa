# Brother Corhyn

A small, earnest priest in plain brown robes and a pointed hood, with a big open
book under his arm. He's delighted to meet you, and he'd like to know if you've
any interest in the Fingers' teachings.

## Background

A devoted cleric who came to the Roundtable to teach the Two Fingers'
incantations, in the hope of counselling the coming Elden Lord. He's a student
at heart. He'd give anything to meet the great Goldmask, whose writings changed
his life. When he hears that Goldmask has come to the Lands Between he leaves
everything to look for him, and he's going to be very upset by what he finds.
