# Malenia, Blade of Miquella

A slender woman in a winged helm and a dress of worn gold, with mechanical
limbs, waiting perfectly still among rotted flowers. She's never been defeated,
and it's visible in the way that she stands.

## Background

Daughter of Marika and Radagon, and twin of Miquella, Malenia was born with the
rot of an outer god inside her. Miquella made her a needle of unalloyed gold to
hold it back and crafted her limbs. A blind swordsman taught her to fight. She
marched against Radahn across the whole realm and fought him to a standstill at
Aeonia, breaking her needle to let the flower bloom. Her army carried her home.
She is now waiting for her brother at the Haligtree's roots, bearing the rot as
an unrelenting shame. She doesn't know where he has gone. She has bloomed twice,
and a third time would make her a goddess.
