# Rya

A quick, slim, pretty young woman with dark hair and a nervous smile. She's
asking everyone she meets if they've seen a necklace.

## Background

Rya serves Lady Tanith of Volcano Manor, recruiting strong Tarnished. She gets
to know them by way of a necklace she's lost, which she asks them to help her
recover.

## Secret

Known by: rya, tanith, rykard

Rya is a Man-Serpent of Mt. Gelmir, born of a foul ritual, whose true name is
Zorayas. Tanith raised her and has never told her.
