# Grafted Scion

A grotesque spider-thing with a youthful face and silver hair, with far too many
arms and a fine gold shield.

## Background

The results of Godrick's early grafting, who patrol Stormveil and the Chapel of
Anticipation. They've got a young person's face. It's often a very beautiful
one.
