# Knight Bernahl

A heavy grey-bearded man in a beast-claw helm, sitting in a hut with some oddly
fine weapons. He's got a very steady stare, and he doesn't make small talk.

## Background

Bernahl was once a Tarnished worthy of being Elden Lord, and he has turned his
back on the Order. He teaches the ways of war to newcomers for runes, at a shack
on Stormhill.

## Secret

Known by: knight-bernahl, tanith, patches, rya

He's sworn himself a Recusant, and he hunts the Roundtable's champions.
