# Errant Sorcerer Wilhelm

A faded sorcerer in a tall glintstone crown, roaming the Gelmir slopes.

## Background

A Tarnished sorcerer of the Academy who studied in nomadic exile, and who made
contact with the Primeval Current. One of the great names of the old Roundtable.
Bernahl has his name on a list.
