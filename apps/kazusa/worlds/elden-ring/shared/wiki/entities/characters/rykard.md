# Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

A colossal serpent coiled in a cavern of lava, and on it, somewhere, a huge
man's sagging crowned head and an entire crowd of smiling faces, still talking.

## Background

Son of Rennala and Radagon, Rykard was once Praetor of the Order. He found the
ancient serpent rites on Mt. Gelmir, turned against the Golden Order, and let a
great serpent swallow him so that it could devour a god. His Recusants left when
they saw it. Tanith alone stayed.

## Secret

Known by: rykard, ranni, tanith, alecto

Rykard helped with Ranni's theft of Death, and in exchange he got a slab that
could cut even Maliketh.
