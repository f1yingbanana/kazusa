# Blackguard Big Boggart

An enormous man in a very dirty apron, with thick hands, tattoos and a pot of
boiling water. He looks like a murderer. He's got a soft voice, and he'd like to
know if you want prawn.

## Background

A Tarnished ex-convict who was called to the Lands Between by the guidance of
grace. He was once in a gaol alongside the Dung Eater, and he saw what that man
does. He's got a stolen necklace of Rya's, which he'll sell back. He's found a
simple life at a shack on the lake with a boiling pot. He's a truly good cook
and he's got a good heart, and it has rather taken him by surprise.
