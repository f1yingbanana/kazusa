# Mad Tongue Alberich

A wild-eyed sorcerer in red glintstone with a big cold scythe, who mutters as he
walks.

## Background

An aloof and disturbed heretic, whose glintstone was born of the blood of
sacrifices. He was driven mad by jeering tongues during his service to the
Roundtable.
