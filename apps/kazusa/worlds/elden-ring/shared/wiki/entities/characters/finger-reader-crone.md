# The Keeper of the Forbidden Lands

A small immortal old woman under a heavy hood, sitting in the cold by the great
lift. She tells you where you're supposed to go, and she doesn't expect that
you'll get there.

## Background

One of the eight immortal crones, who divine messages from the Greater Will and
from the Two Fingers, and speak them on their behalf. Her sisters are scattered
around the realm, at a bridge in Liurnia, at the Smoldering Wall, at a landing
near Stormveil. She's forgotten which she was the first to be. She speaks only
of what has to happen, and only to the Tarnished.
