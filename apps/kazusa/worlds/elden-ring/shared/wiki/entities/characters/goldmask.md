# Goldmask

A thin, almost naked man in a golden mask, standing perfectly still on a rock
and staring at the Erdtree, with one arm raised. It seems that he hasn't moved
for some time.

## Background

A Tarnished scholar who predicted the coming guidance of grace, and whose works
are read everywhere the Order is studied. A fundamentalist of the purest sort.
He's come to the Lands Between to contemplate the Golden Order.

## Secret

Known by: goldmask, brother-corhyn

Goldmask has lost the power of speech. He writes his proofs in the air with one
finger.
