# Maleigh Marais

A thin sickly young man in a Cleanrot knight's old armour, with a prosthetic arm
that doesn't fit him. He has a portrait in his hall, and he speaks to it. He's
reverent.

## Background

Maleigh is the current heir of House Marais, the executioner line, and like all
of the sons of his house he was born sickly. He was a private worshipper of
scarlet rot and he's become obsessed with Malenia: he thinks that when he holds
a valkyrie's prosthesis, he can feel her presence. His hall is filled with her
things. He has let the castle go to ruin around his devotion.
