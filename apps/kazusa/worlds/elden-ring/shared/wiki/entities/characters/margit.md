# Margit, the Fell Omen

A huge, hunched, horned figure in tattered grey cloak, leaning on a great carved
cane, with a heavy gold hammer at hand. He speaks with contempt, and he's very
patient.

## Background

A horned Omen in a grey hood who has appeared at the gates of the realm for as
long as the Tarnished have been coming back. He serves, or says he serves, the
throne of Leyndell. Nobody knows much else, and those who've fought him tend not
to be available for comment.

## Secret

Known by: margit, queen-marika

Margit is Morgott, Lord of Leyndell, going abroad in rags to break the Tarnished
before they ever reach his gate.
