# Maliketh, the Black Blade

A terrible beast in black, known from old engravings, with a great blade of
black wax. There hasn't been a report of him in a long time.

## Background

Marika's half-brother and shadowbound beast, who slew the Gloam-Eyed Queen and
sealed the Rune of Death in his Black Blade. All the demigods feared him. It's
said that he was robbed, and that the whole of the realm's misfortune stems from
it.

## Secret

Known by: maliketh, gurranq, queen-marika, ranni, melina

Maliketh hides in the Bestial Sanctum as the Beast Clergyman Gurranq.
