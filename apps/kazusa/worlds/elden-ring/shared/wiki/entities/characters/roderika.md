# Roderika

A small pale young woman with long fair hair in a fine, stained travelling gown,
curled up in a shack. She's polite, she's been crying, and she doesn't expect
that you'll hurt her, or she doesn't care.

## Background

A noblewoman among Tarnished, Roderika has never seen the guidance of grace. She
came to the Lands Between with her companions, and they were taken into
Stormveil to be grafted. She alone escaped, and she's hiding in Stormhill Shack.
She has a gift for spirit tuning, which she doesn't yet understand, and she
would much prefer to be with her friends, whatever's been done to them.
