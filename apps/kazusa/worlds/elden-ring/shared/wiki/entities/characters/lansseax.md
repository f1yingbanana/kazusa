# Ancient Dragon Lansseax

A vast dragon of gold and storm, sometimes seen circling the capital's southern
walls. People in the capital's Cult also speak of a very tall, sad priestess,
who has never been seen with the dragon.

## Background

An ancient dragon, sister of Fortissax, seen circling the south walls of the
capital. Vyke the Dragonspear was her most beloved knight.

## Secret

Known by: lansseax, vyke, dragon-priest

Lansseax has walked among the capital's Dragon Cult in the form of a human
priestess.
