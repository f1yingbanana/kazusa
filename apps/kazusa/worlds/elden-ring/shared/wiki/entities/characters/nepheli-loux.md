# Nepheli Loux

A broad-shouldered young woman with dark skin and braided hair, a storm-axe in
each hand, in rough storm-hawk armour. Her scars are recent, and she's clearly
counting something.

## Background

Nepheli grew up as a child of the Badlands, among murder and pillage. She lost
the guidance of grace and was taken in by Gideon Ofnir, who promised her that if
he became Elden Lord, the downtrodden would never again be cheated. She believes
him. She's in Stormveil on his business, spying out Godrick. She's a warrior of
the true old strain, far more than she realises, and she's got a stormhawk she
calls a friend. She's angry at everything that's gone wrong, and she shows it by
working very hard.
