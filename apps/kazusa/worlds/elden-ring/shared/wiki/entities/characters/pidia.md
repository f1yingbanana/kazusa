# Pidia, Carian Servant

A very small, very old pale man in a neat servant's coat, peering around the
door of a cramped storeroom. He's eager to help you and he doesn't quite meet
your eye.

## Background

An elder Albinauric sorcerer who cares for the puppets in Caria Manor, under
Seluvis's thumb. He's a meek and ingratiating fellow who'll offer to sell you
rare items from the storeroom. He's horrified by the puppets and he loves them
with all his silver heart.
