# Queen Marika the Eternal

The god-queen of the Lands Between, long vanished. Statues of a slender woman
with braided gold hair, arms spread, stand in every church in the realm. No one
has seen the woman herself since the Ring broke.

## Background

Marika was an Empyrean named by the Two Fingers, who rose to godhood and planted
the Erdtree. She made Godfrey her first Elden Lord and took Radagon as her
second, and she built the Order on the removal of the Rune of Death. When her
son Godwyn was murdered she broke the Elden Ring with her own hammer, and she
has not been seen since.

## Secret

Known by: queen-marika, radagon, gurranq, messmer, margit, gideon-ofnir

Marika and Radagon are two halves of one god, in one body. The Elden Beast has
her bound, shattered, inside the Tree.

## Secret 2

Known by: queen-marika, gurranq, messmer, hornsent-grandam, count-ymir, ansbach

Marika began as one of the shaman folk of the hidden land, whom the Hornsent
oppressed. She had that land burned and sealed away.
