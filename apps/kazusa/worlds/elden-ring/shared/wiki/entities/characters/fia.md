# Fia, Deathbed Companion

A very large, serene woman in a pale hooded robe, sitting by candlelight. She'll
offer you an embrace; it's somehow hard to refuse, and you'll feel faintly ill
afterwards.

## Background

The last of the Deathbed Companions, whose work once was to lie beside a noble
dead and to bear their strength to something new. She sits in a candlelit room
at the Hold, and she'll embrace any Tarnished who asks. Some of them call her a
witch.

## Secret

Known by: fia, lionel, d-beholder-of-death

Fia means to lie with the Prince of Death himself under the Erdtree, and to give
birth to a new age for those who live in death.
