# Miquella the Kind

A beautiful, androgynous child, seemingly very young, in pale gold; those who
see him are moved by an instant, inexplicable love. Few have ever looked at him
closely enough to wonder at it.

## Background

Son of Marika and Radagon, twin of Malenia, an Empyrean cursed never to grow
older. He gave up on the Golden Order when it couldn't cure his sister, and grew
the Haligtree as a refuge for the unwanted. He slept in a cocoon at its root
until Mohg stole him away. No one knows anything since.

## Secret

Known by: miquella, st-trina, ansbach, freyja, leda, hornsent, moore, thiollier,
dryleaf-dane, count-ymir

He has gone into the Land of Shadow, casting off his flesh, his lineage and his
love, to become a kind god. No one can refuse his love, and he has used this on
everyone who's followed him.
