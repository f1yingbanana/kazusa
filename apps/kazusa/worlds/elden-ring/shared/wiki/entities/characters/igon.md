# Igon

A huge old ruin of a man sitting on a rock, scarred from head to foot, with a
harpoon, muttering to himself. If you mention dragons he'll look up, and you'll
wish you hadn't.

## Background

Igon was once a poor scavenger of battlefields, who found honour in the old art
of Dragon Communion, as a hunter of dragons. He became a fearless drake warrior
and went up to the top of the Jagged Peak to challenge Bayle the Dread, and
Bayle broke his body and his mind. He's still alive, with a heart full of fear
that he has refused to acknowledge. He wants very much to have another try.
