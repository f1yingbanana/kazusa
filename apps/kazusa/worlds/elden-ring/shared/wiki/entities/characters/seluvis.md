# Preceptor Seluvis

A thin old sorcerer in faded blue, with a mask of glintstone on his brow, a
superior smile and a very clean little bottle in his hand. He doesn't think much
of you.

## Background

A Carian preceptor who's supposed to serve Ranni the Witch. He's a snob and an
extremely gifted maker of potions.

## Secret

Known by: seluvis, pidia, ranni

He reduces sorcerers and warriors to puppets with a draught brewed from
starlight, and stores them behind a false wall. He wants Ranni to drink
something too.
