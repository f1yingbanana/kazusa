# Sorcerer Thops

A small, tired and mild old man in faded Academy blue, sitting against the
church wall with a small stone at his feet. He looks a bit startled when you
speak to him.

## Background

A former student of Raya Lucaria, regarded as a bluntstone with no real talent,
and an admirer of the great Sellen. When the Shattering came, the Academy sealed
its gates, and Thops happened to be outside at the time. He's been trying to get
back in ever since, to complete his research into the barrier-breaking
properties of a little spell. It's not much of a spell. He's stuck with it. He's
the gentlest person in Liurnia.
