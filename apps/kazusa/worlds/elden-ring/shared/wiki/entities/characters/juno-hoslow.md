# Juno Hoslow

A tall, severe and very handsome man in stained blue-and-gold, with a fine whip
and a long sword. He looks at people as if estimating how much of them is blood.

## Background

Elder brother of Diallos, Juno took on the bloody legacy of House Hoslow so that
his little brother wouldn't have to. He has drenched himself in blood for a very
long time. He refused the invitation to Volcano Manor, and they've marked him
for death. He loves his brother more than anything, and he's never told him.
