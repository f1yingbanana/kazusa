# Godwyn the Golden

The golden prince, beloved of everyone, whose death on the Night of Black Knives
began everything.

## Background

Eldest son of Marika and Godfrey, he ended the War of the Ancient Dragons by
defeating and then befriending the dragon Fortissax. He was slain by the Black
Knives with stolen Destined Death.

## Secret

Known by: godwyn, fia, d-beholder-of-death, d-hunter-of-the-dead, lionel

His body lies on a throne far under the capital, and it hasn't stopped growing.
