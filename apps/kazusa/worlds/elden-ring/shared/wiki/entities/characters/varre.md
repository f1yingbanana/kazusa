# White Mask Varré

A slim man in a fine white mask and a bloodstained surgeon's apron, leaning on a
rock beside the road. He gives a little laugh when you approach. He's pleased to
see you, and sorry to see what you've got on.

## Background

A slim man in a white mask and a surgeon's apron, who sits by the first Site of
Grace and looks over each new Tarnished. He laughs at them. He tells them that
they're maidenless. He recommends the Roundtable.

## Secret

Known by: varre, mohg, yura, esgar, nerijus

Varré serves Mohg, as a recruiter. He was a war surgeon before Mohg's people
took him.
