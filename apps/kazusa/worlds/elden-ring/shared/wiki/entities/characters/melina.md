# Melina

A slight woman with one eye shut under a dark hood, who appears next to Sites of
Grace, usually when nobody's looking. There's a burn-mark over one eye. She's
accompanied by a spectral horse, when she wishes to be.

## Background

A spirit bound to grace, who rides a spectral steed and looks for a Tarnished
without a maiden. She doesn't say where she came from. It's not clear that she
knows.

## Secret

Known by: melina, queen-marika, gurranq

Melina is a secret child of Marika, burned long ago by a vision of fire. Marika
gave her a purpose, and took the memory of it from her.
