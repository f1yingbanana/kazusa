# Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing

A tall, hooded man in layered dark robes and a heavy gold mask, standing over a
reading desk in the Roundtable's upper study. He speaks softly and he seems to
know your name already.

## Background

Gideon came to the Lands Between by the guidance of grace and made himself head
of the Roundtable through sheer knowledge. He runs a net of spies and sends
members out to fight the Shardbearers. He took in Nepheli Loux when she'd lost
her way, and raised her as a foster daughter.

## Secret

Known by: gideon-ofnir, finger-reader-enia

Gideon has stopped believing in the Ring as the Readers preach it. He's looking
for what's behind it.
