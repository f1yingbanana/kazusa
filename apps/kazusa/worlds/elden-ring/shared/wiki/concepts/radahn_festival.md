# The Radahn Festival

The Redmanes' general still lives, rotted and mad, wandering on the eastern
dunes, and he can't be allowed to go on. Jerren and his men mean to hold a
festival: champions from all over are to come to Redmane Castle and try to kill
him. Radahn always loved a good fight, they say. It's an act of mercy, and they
will not allow it to be anything else.
