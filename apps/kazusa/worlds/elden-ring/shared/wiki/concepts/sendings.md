# Sendings

When someone wishes to reach a person who isn't with them, they speak a few
words and a small spirit carries them off. It reaches its recipient wherever
they are, and it waits until they attend to it. The rich and the poor do this
alike, and nobody thinks much about it. A sending can be ignored. It can't be
overheard. It says only what you said.
