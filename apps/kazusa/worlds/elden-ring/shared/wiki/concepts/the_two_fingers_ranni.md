# Ranni's Rebellion

Ranni was an Empyrean, and her Two Fingers meant to make a god of her. She cut
them apart with a blade she stole from the Eternal Cities, abandoned her
Empyrean's body and now lives in a doll. Her Baleful Shadow is still hunting the
realm for her, and for those who help her.
