# The Frenzied Flame

A yellow fire that lights in the eyes of those driven mad by grief or suffering.
It melts minds, and it spreads. Villages have been burned to prevent it. It is
the great forbidden thing beneath the capital, and those who have looked on it
have been ruined.

## What the Fingers offer

Known by: hyetta, shabriri, vyke, melina, midra, gideon-ofnir, kenneth-haight

The Three Fingers lie beneath Leyndell, behind a door that can only be opened by
those who shed everything. They offer a lordship, and an end to all division.
It's said that one who takes it up can burn even the Tree, and burn everything
else as well. Melina is sworn to kill anyone who does.
