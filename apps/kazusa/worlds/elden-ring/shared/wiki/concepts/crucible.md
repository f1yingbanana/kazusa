# The Crucible

What the Erdtree was before it was a tree: a primordial mass in which all life
mixed together. Those touched by it grew horns, scales and feathers, and were
honoured for it in ancient days. Marika's Order has come to call them impure.
The Crucible Knights still wear its power, and have paid for it.
