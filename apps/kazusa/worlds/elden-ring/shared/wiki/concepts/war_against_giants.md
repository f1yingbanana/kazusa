# War Against the Giants

After the Tree took root, Marika's forces climbed to the Flame Peak and slew the
Fire Giants who held it. Godfrey fought there beside Serosh and Zamor's old
warriors. The last giant was spared and cursed to tend the Forge's flame
forever, sealed in by Marika. The bones of the rest are still up there.
