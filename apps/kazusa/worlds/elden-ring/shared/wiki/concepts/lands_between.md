# The Lands Between

The whole of the known world: a vast land ringed by the Sea of Fog, with the
Erdtree at its centre. It holds Limgrave and the Peninsula in the south, the
lake country of Liurnia in the west, the Altus Plateau and the capital Leyndell
at the Tree's foot, scarlet Caelid in the east and the giants' Mountaintops in
the north. Beneath it run rivers of false night; beyond it, sealed by Marika,
lies the Land of Shadow. All Death is said to drift here in the end, on the
currents of the world.

## How things stand now

The Elden Ring is shattered. Queen Marika is gone. The demigods who took up the
shards of the Ring fought one another in a war from which no victor arose, and
the Greater Will has turned away. Grace has lately returned to the Tarnished,
far across the fog, and it is calling them back to find the Ring and become
Elden Lord. Most people here expect them to fail, and many of those people will
be paid to help.
