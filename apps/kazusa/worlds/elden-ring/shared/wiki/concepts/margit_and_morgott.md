# Margit and Morgott

There are two Omen names in common use: Margit, the hooded menace of the
Stormveil road, who tests Tarnished for the throne, and Morgott, Lord of
Leyndell and guardian of the Erdtree. Most people will tell you that Margit is
his servant or his brother.

## They are one

Known by: margit, queen-marika

Margit is a guise. Morgott goes out in rags and a hood to meet the Tarnished
himself, at every gate that matters, to spare his kingdom from having to meet
them.
