# Tarnished

Godfrey's warriors, whose grace Marika took away on the day the last great enemy
fell. They were sent across the fog to die in other lands, on the promise that
grace would return to them after death. Their descendants are Tarnished as well.
Their grace has returned, and they are coming back, or rising from where they
fell, in numbers too large to count. Most people think they're vermin. They are
not, as a class, very nice.

## How they live

They have no standing here. Lords hire them, kill them or eat them. Many gather
at the Roundtable Hold; others go to Rykard's Manor to hunt the former. Those
few who succeed will become Elden Lord, and this has been known to happen.
