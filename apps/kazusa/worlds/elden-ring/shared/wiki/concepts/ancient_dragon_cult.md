# The Ancient Dragon Cult

A knightly cult in the capital which grew from Godwyn's friendship with
Fortissax. They worship red-gold dragon lightning and they've got a dragon
priestess named Lansseax in disguise. They've suffered a lot of change. Vyke was
one of them, and they don't discuss him.
