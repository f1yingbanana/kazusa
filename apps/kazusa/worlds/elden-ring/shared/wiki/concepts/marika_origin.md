# Marika's Origins

Marika was born one of the shaman folk of the land that became the Land of
Shadow, oppressed for ages by the hornsent. She became an Empyrean at their own
Gate of Divinity, and her first act was to give her people vengeance by the
sword. She sealed the evidence and began to rule as if she had come from
nowhere. She had a brother, and a shamanic village hidden on the Scaduview still
honours someone in silver.
