# The Erdtree

A tree of unimaginable size, whose golden crown is visible from nearly anywhere
in the realm. Its leaves shed gold, and its light is the grace that blesses the
faithful. The dead of the Order are returned to its roots. Since the Shattering
it has been sick, and its leaves fall faster. A thicket of thorns now seals the
entrance at its base, closing the way to all, even to Lord Morgott.

## Before the Tree

Long ago the Erdtree was the Crucible, the primordial mix in which all life
blended. A Golden Seed transformed it, under Marika's hand, at the direction of
the Two Fingers.

## The Tree inside the capital

Known by: margit, godfrey, queen-marika, finger-reader-enia, gideon-ofnir,
gurranq, melina, brother-corhyn, goldmask

The thorns closing the Tree are its own defence. Morgott can't open them and he
doesn't know who can. Only the Flame of Ruin can burn them away, and it is
locked in the giants' Forge in the north. To burn the Tree is unthinkable and
may be the only way.
