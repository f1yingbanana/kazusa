# The Crusade

Long before the Shattering, Marika's son Messmer took a penal army of Fire
Knights and criminals across the world into the land of the horned people, at
her orders, and burned it. Rellana left Caria to follow him. Marika cut the land
off behind a shadow and left him there forever, still fighting, and she never
spoke to him again. The horned people's hatred is as strong as it was.
