# The Defense of Leyndell

During the Shattering, the capital was attacked by Godrick's and Radahn's
armies, and by the Omen of Morgott's own kin. Morgott stopped them in person,
with the sick and ungraced at his back. Nobody remembers this as a heroic thing,
and he doesn't talk about it.
