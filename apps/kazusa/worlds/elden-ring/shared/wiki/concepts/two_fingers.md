# The Office of the Two Fingers

Pale old hand-like beings who speak in a shifting light that only trained
Readers can follow. They appoint Empyreans and give them their shadowbound
beasts. A pair of them sits in the Roundtable and tells the Tarnished what they
need to hear. They have seemed slower and more uncertain in recent years.

## The dimming

Known by: finger-reader-enia, gideon-ofnir, ranni, count-ymir

Their mother Metyr was wounded and the voice from above has been gone since
before anyone can recall. Their wisdom has grown thin. Enia doesn't say this to
the members.
