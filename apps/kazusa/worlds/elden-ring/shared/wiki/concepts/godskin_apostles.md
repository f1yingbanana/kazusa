# Godskins

Those who still serve the old cause of the Gloam-Eyed Queen. They wear flayed
godskin, sewn on, and the skinny Apostles and huge bloated Nobles are both given
to rolling up on people with a gleeful sort of malice. They're very old and they
aren't trying hard to hide.
