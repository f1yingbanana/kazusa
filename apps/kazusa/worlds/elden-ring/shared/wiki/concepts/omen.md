# Omen

Children born with horns are Omen. Royal Omen have been buried alive in the
capital's sewers on Order law. Lower-born ones have their horns cut off, and
most of them die of it. Nobody talks about it. Omenkillers do the rest, and they
enjoy it. Morgott and Mohg are both Omen, and they went in opposite directions.
