# Liurnian Wars

Leyndell's first campaign against Liurnia was stopped by the Carians under
Rennala, and it ended when she took the red-haired hero Radagon as her consort.
He returned to Leyndell later to become Elden Lord, and her Full Moon has never
quite recovered. Then the Academy turned against the Carians with the help of
the Knights of the Cuckoo, and Caria's fortunes went downhill from there. The
royal family is confined to its Manor now.
