# The Golden Prince's Legacy

Eldest son of Marika and Godfrey, famous for making peace with the dragon
Fortissax, and for being the sort of man others loved. His golden scales remain
in the cult's sacred objects. He was the first demigod to die, and the whole of
the realm's unhappiness has flowed from that moment. His tomb is in the
capital's roots.
