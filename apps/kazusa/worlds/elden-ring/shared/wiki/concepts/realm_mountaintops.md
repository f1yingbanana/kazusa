# The Realm of the Mountaintops

A cold high place of ruins and giant bones, and not much else. Fire Monks,
Zamor's ghosts, old knights and a few mad wanderers are its only people. The
Haligtree lies somewhere hidden beyond them, and that isn't common knowledge.
