# Beastmen of Farum Azula

Robed beast-people who've served the Dragonlord's fallen city for longer than
the Erdtree has existed. They guard its graves and old altars, and they carry
lightning in their weapons. Some of them live in caves in Limgrave and the
Dragonbarrow, and they don't explain why.
