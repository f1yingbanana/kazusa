# Empyreans

Those chosen by the Fingers for a chance at godhood, and granted a shadowbound
beast to guard them. There have been only a few. Marika is one. Miquella,
Malenia and Ranni are the three of this age. The Gloam-Eyed Queen was another,
long ago, before she was destroyed.
