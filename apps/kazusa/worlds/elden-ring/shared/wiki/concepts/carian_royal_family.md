# House Caria

The royal family of Liurnia, sworn to the moon and given to long dynastic
memories. They ruled the western lake and the old Academy, until civil war with
the Academy and the Cuckoo reduced them. Their manor holds on. Rennala is queen
in name, Rykard is lost and Ranni, though the youngest, is the one their people
still answer to. Their knights are spectral now, and very loyal.
