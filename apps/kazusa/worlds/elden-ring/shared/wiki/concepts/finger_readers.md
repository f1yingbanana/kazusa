# Finger Readers and Maidens

The Readers sit at far-flung posts and interpret what the Fingers say; the great
majority of them are very old. Finger Maidens are younger and serve individual
Tarnished, as guides. Those who have lost their Tarnished often sit where they
were left, for a very long time.
