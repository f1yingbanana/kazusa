# House Haight

The family who once held Fort Haight, on behalf of Godrick's line, and who have
lost it to Godrick's own soldiers. Their heir is Kenneth Haight, who's a fool
and knows this less well than everyone else. He's nonetheless sincere in caring
about his people.
