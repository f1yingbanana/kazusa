# Ancestral Followers

A silent people of the Siofra who wear animal masks and bone, and who've kept up
an old rite of ancestor worship. They've got little to do with the Nox above
them. They hunt the bone-white deer of the river. When the altars are lit they
believe something comes to walk among them, and it does.
