# Grafting

Godrick's specialty, which he calls a sacred rite. He and his surgeons sew the
parts of the strong to the bodies of their lords, in hope of drawing out their
power. The result is ugly. The Grafted Scions of Stormveil are its products, and
those who've been taken for it don't come back.
