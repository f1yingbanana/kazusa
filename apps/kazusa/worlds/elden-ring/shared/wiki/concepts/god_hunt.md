# The God Hunt

Before the Golden Order, an Empyrean called the Gloam-Eyed Queen led Godskin
Apostles in a hunt for gods with black flame. Maliketh, Marika's beast,
destroyed her, and the Rune of Death was taken from the Ring soon afterwards.
Her name was afterwards removed from nearly all records.
