# Dragon Communion

A very old rite in which a dragon's heart is eaten, and in which the eater grows
closer to dragons in body and hatred. It takes place at the great stone maws, in
the churches at Limgrave and Caelid and at the Grand Altar in the Land of
Shadow. It began among men who wished to be greater than they were, and it has
rarely improved them.
