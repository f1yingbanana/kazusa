# The Land of Shadow

A land folded away beneath the world's notice, hidden by Marika behind a veil of
shadow. It's the true origin of the Crucible and of Marika herself. Messmer
still holds it, fighting a war nobody else remembers, against Hornsent who are
still there. It's reached by touching Miquella's withered arm, or it isn't
reached at all. Everything washed ashore there is dead, or will be.
