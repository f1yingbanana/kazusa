# The Greater Will

The unseen power beyond the stars from which the Golden Order is said to flow.
It speaks to no one directly. Its will reaches the world through its vassals,
and the faithful take it on trust.

## What the wise say of it

Scholars of the Order hold that it sent a golden star bearing a beast of living
Order, which became the Elden Ring, and that the Two Fingers are its envoys. It
has abandoned the Lands Between since the Shattering, or so the Readers quietly
admit.

## Its daughter and its silence

Known by: count-ymir, finger-reader-enia, hyetta, gideon-ofnir, ranni

Before the Beast came a first star, bearing Metyr, the Mother of Fingers, who
was the Greater Will's daughter and its only voice. The Nox wounded her with the
Fingerslayer Blade long ago. She has heard nothing since, and neither have her
children, who have gone on interpreting silence for an age.

## The One Great

Known by: hyetta, shabriri, vyke, midra, d-beholder-of-death, count-ymir

Before the Greater Will there was the One Great, undivided. The Will broke it,
and so came division, birth, souls and all suffering. The servants of the Three
Fingers hold this to have been the first and worst mistake, and they mean to
mend it by burning away everything that divides.
