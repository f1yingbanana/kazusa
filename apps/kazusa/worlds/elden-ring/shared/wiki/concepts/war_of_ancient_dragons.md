# War of the Ancient Dragons

Long ago, the dragon Gransax fell on Leyndell, the only time its walls have been
breached, and he lies there still, spear in hand. War followed. Godwyn the
Golden ended it by besting the dragon Fortissax and then becoming his friend, a
surprise to all parties. The Ancient Dragon Cult of Leyndell has its roots in
that friendship.
