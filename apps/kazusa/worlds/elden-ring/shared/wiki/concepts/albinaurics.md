# Albinaurics

A race of pale, weak people made from silver fluid by some sorcerers long ago,
in imitation of life. The Order counts them as impure and many people hunt them
for sport. Latenna and Albus are among the very few who've lived long enough to
become old. A new home at the Haligtree was promised to them, and some have
gone.
