# The Moons of the Carians

The royal family of Caria hold that each of them encounters a moon in youth, and
that it defines them. Rennala's Full Moon drew her into sorcery and charmed the
Academy. Ranni's is said to be a cold dark one, of her own making, and she keeps
it quietly to herself.

## What Ranni means to do

Known by: ranni, blaidd, iji, seluvis

Ranni will not accept the Order at all. She intends to lead the stars and moons
far away from this land, and to leave the Lands Between to the people who live
there, for a thousand years of something other than gods' meddling. Her consort,
if she can get one, will stand with her in the dark.
