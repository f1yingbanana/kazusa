# Scarlet Rot

A red sickness that eats body and mind alike. Fire can hold it back, and nothing
else reliably can. It came from an outer god, and it has been carried by Malenia
since her birth. When she let it bloom against Radahn, the whole of southern
Caelid became what it is today. Some, like Sage Gowry, tend it. Some worship it.

## The Goddess of Rot

Known by: malenia, gowry, millicent, miquella, maleigh-marais, oneil

A flower is meant to bloom, in time. Malenia has already bloomed twice. The
third blooming would make her a goddess of rot, and the realm would not survive
that. The Sage Gowry wishes this to happen. Millicent was made for it, and she
doesn't know yet.
