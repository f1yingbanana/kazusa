# Stormveil's Court

Godrick rules from Stormveil, in Limgrave, over a garrison of exiled soldiers,
Banished Knights and the carrion-hawks of the storm. Its old lords served the
Storm Lord before anyone had heard of the Erdtree. It has none of the glamour of
the Order's other courts, and Godrick is far from secure.
