# Glintstone

A blue crystal, found in veins and falling from the sky, said to contain the
life of the stars. Sorcerers use it for spellcasting; the most devoted cast
their own heads into it, as glintstone crowns, in the belief that this will
bring them closer to the Current. Carian royals favour its deep lapis variety,
and glintstone dragons have grown scales of it by eating it.
