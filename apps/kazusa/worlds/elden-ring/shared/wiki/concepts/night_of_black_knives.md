# Night of the Black Knives

The golden prince Godwyn was murdered in Leyndell by assassins who carried the
power of true Death. It was the first demigod death in history, and no one knows
who was behind it, officially. Marika shattered the Ring soon after. The Black
Knives have been a byword for horror ever since.

## Who did it

Known by: ranni, blaidd, iji, alecto, rykard, seluvis, gurranq

The Black Knives were the Numen women of Alecto's band, and the knives held a
piece of Destined Death stolen by Ranni. Rykard knew and helped. She killed her
own body at the same moment, so that she might be free of the Fingers, which is
why the deaths were partial. Ranni is still living as a spirit, in a doll.

## What people say

It's said that Marika must have had a hand in it, since the assassins were
Numen, like her. Others blame the Golden Lineage's enemies, or the Carians.
Nobody can prove any of this.
