# Gravity Sorcery

A rare magic of weight and falling. It came down with the Fallingstars, along
with their beasts and their Alabaster and Onyx Lords. Radahn studied it at
Sellia as a young man, for no better reason than to go on riding his beloved
small horse Leonard, which he could otherwise have crushed.
