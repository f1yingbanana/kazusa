# Primeval Current

Scholars of the Academy hold that the stars flow in a procession toward the
Lands Between, and that glintstone is their amber. Those who look upon the
Current for long are changed. Azur and Lusat looked on it, and both of them are
now sitting silent on rocks. Its power can be touched by sorcery, and the most
gifted students aspire to be turned into stars themselves.
