# Messmer's Army

Messmer's great crusader host, which was left behind when Marika hid the land.
The Fire Knights were disowned noble sons who took a torch against the Order's
own rule. The Black Knights were the warriors of a Crucible order who had
followed him from Leyndell. They have burned a lot of the country and held the
rest. Some have got tired of it.
