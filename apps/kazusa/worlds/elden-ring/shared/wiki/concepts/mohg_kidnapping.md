# The Theft of Miquella

Mohg, Lord of Blood, came north during the fighting and carried off the sleeping
Miquella in his cocoon, to his palace beneath the world. He has bathed that
cocoon in blood since then. He thinks he's in love. Nobody's quite sure what
Miquella thinks of it, or whether it's quite as Mohg believes.
