# The Flame of Ruin

A great old fire, from a one-eyed god of whom few now speak. The Fire Giants who
served it fell to Marika's army, and the last of them was left alive, cursed to
tend the flame in the great Forge on the Flame Peak. Fire Monks watch over it,
and Adan stole a little of it once. It is the one flame in the world that can
burn the Erdtree.
