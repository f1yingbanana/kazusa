# Grace

The blessing of the Tree, visible as a golden lustre in the eyes of the
favoured. Those granted it live and, the Order says, die within the Order's
care. Marika took it away from Godfrey's warriors when she sent them into exile.
Now it has returned to their descendants, as a thread of golden light leading
them toward the Ring.

## Sites of Grace

Small golden lights are found in the earth throughout the realm, where a
Tarnished may rest and be restored. Those who've rested at a Site can travel
between the ones they know by grace alone, in moments; nobody else can. People
without grace can't see them at all, though some of those who have died can see
their glow.
