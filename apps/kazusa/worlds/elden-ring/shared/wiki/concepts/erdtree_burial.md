# Erdtree Burial

The funeral of the Order: bodies are taken down to the roots through catacombs
under Erdtree-fed ground, and absorbed. It replaced true death when the Rune was
removed. Catacombs across the realm, with their watchdogs and traps, were once
part of this. Since Godwyn's death the root hasn't taken all of its dead, and
the wrong ones are getting up.
