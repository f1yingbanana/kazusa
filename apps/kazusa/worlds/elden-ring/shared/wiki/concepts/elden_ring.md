# The Elden Ring

The source of the Erdtree and of all Order, shaped as interlocking runes of
light that set the fundamental laws of the world. A god may hold it within her
own body. Its consort is the Elden Lord. Marika shattered it with a hammer, for
reasons she told no one, and it has been broken since.

## What it is made of

It has changed shape before. Old forms of it survive at Farum Azula, where
Placidusax was once Elden Lord in an age before the Tree. Marika removed the
Rune of Death from it to begin the Golden Order, and in doing so she made her
line immortal.

## The beast within

Known by: queen-marika, melina, gideon-ofnir, godfrey, gurranq

The Elden Ring began as a golden beast, sent by the Greater Will: the Elden
Beast, a living concept of Order. It still lives in the heart of the Tree, and
it is what truly punished Marika.
