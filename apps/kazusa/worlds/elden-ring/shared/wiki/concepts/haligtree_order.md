# The Haligtree's Order

Miquella grew this tree as an alternative to the Erdtree, for all those whom the
Order calls impure. Albinaurics and Misbegotten in particular found a home here.
Its existence isn't well known and its road is secret. Miquella's asleep in his
cocoon, or so the folk there believe. They're praying for his return.

## The empty cocoon

Known by: malenia, loretta, millicent

Miquella's cocoon was carried off from the roots. Malenia sits waiting there
beside the empty space, still rotting. The Haligtree isn't going to flower. Its
people are beginning to suspect this.
