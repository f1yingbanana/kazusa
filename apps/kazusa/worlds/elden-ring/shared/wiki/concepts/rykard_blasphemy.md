# Rykard's Blasphemy

Praetor Rykard, a son of Rennala and Radagon, turned against the Order and made
his manor the home of all its enemies. Seeing no other way to win, he fed
himself to a giant serpent that had been eating mountains. He lives on in it.
Most of his Recusants left when they saw what he'd done. Lady Tanith didn't.
