# Great Runes

When the Ring shattered, its great runes fell upon Marika's children. Those who
took them became Shardbearers, and the power corrupted every one of them in some
way that suited them. A Great Rune held by a Tarnished is merely a dead weight
until restored at its Divine Tower.

## Who holds what

Godrick, Radahn, Morgott, Rykard, Mohg and Malenia hold them. Radagon's Rune is
his own, within the Tree. Miquella bears one too, and is about to cast it off.
The Rune of Death was never part of the sharing; it was cut out long before.
