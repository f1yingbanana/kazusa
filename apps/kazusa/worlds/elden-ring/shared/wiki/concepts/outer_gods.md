# Outer Gods

The Golden Order teaches of one Greater Will, but scholars know of other wills
pressing in from without. They are not worshipped openly, and they are not all
one. Each has its element, and each is apt to choose a vessel.

## Those named

The god of rot, who pressed its seed upon Malenia. The Formless Mother, who
loves wounds and gave Mohg his bloodflame. The Three Fingers, called the
Frenzied Flame. The fell god of fire, behind the giants. An old god of death,
whose Rune Marika cut out of the Ring. Serpents, too, have been called divine,
and the God-Devouring Serpent has its worshippers.
