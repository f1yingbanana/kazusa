# The Nox and the Eternal Cities

A veiled people who built great cities under a false sky, Nokron, Nokstella, and
a nameless third. They rebelled against the Greater Will, and were banished
underground for it. Their masks of silver and glass block the gaze of the sky.
They forged the Mimic Tears. They believe a Lord of Night will one day lead them
to an Age of Stars.

## Numen

Known by: queen-marika, alecto, ranni, gurranq

The Numen were a separate folk from beyond the sea, and Marika's kin. The Black
Knife women were Numen too. People assume all such women are Nox, and it has
been convenient.
