# Radahn's Holding of the Stars

Radahn is the giant Redmane general who, in his youth, held back the very stars
from their courses by the power of his magic. His lands have been at peace with
them, and fate itself has been locked in place: nothing can happen in the Carian
prophecies while the stars don't move. Ranni needs them to move. If the general
were to die, the fixed stars would fall and the sealed cities would open. People
don't really talk about this at the festival.
