# The Scholars of Raya Lucaria

A great school for all who study the stars, founded by Azur and Lusat, who are
no longer in a position to run it. Its scholars divide into Conspectuses,
Karolos and Olivinus above all, and they wear crowns of glintstone over their
heads. It has been shut to outsiders since it broke with the Carians, and the
main gate won't open without a key.

## The rot inside

Known by: rennala, sellen, thops, preceptor-miriam, seluvis

The Academy no longer has a head. Rennala, its queen, holds nothing but an egg.
Senior scholars have been expelled for asking whether the Conspectus can really
be right. Sellen and Thops have both been cast out, each for what they tried to
learn; Thops was barred for wanting to know how a barrier works.
