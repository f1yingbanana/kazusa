# The Rune of Death

What the Ring held once, and holds no longer: the fated, final death of all, up
to and including gods. Marika took it out of the Ring at the founding of her
Order, and sealed it with her shadow Maliketh, in his Black Blade. That is why
demigods don't die as others do.

## What happened to it

Known by: ranni, gurranq, queen-marika, iji, blaidd, rykard, alecto, seluvis

A fragment was stolen from Maliketh long ago, and it became the Black Knives.
Destined Death is shared only with those who are meant to wield it, and it can't
be taken back by Maliketh, not for love or anything.
