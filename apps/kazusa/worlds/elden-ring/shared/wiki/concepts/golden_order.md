# The Golden Order

The ruling faith and governing principle of the realm since Marika's ascent. It
holds that Marika is the one true god, that the Erdtree's grace blesses the
worthy, and that the dead return to the Tree. Its priests and scholars see all
that has happened since the Shattering as a test. It tolerates more than it
admits to: the dragon cult, the Academy's sorcery, and old local rites, so long
as they bow.

## Fundamentalists

Scholars within the Order, Goldmask and Corhyn among them, hold that the Order
itself can be understood, proved and improved, and that it failed only because
Marika and Radagon fell short of it. They are not universally thanked for this.
Their hunters, D among them, kill Those Who Live in Death on sight.

## Impurity

The Order brands all things of the Crucible as impure: horns, feathers, scales.
The Omen, the Misbegotten and many others live under this judgment. It wasn't
always so. In the old days they were holy.

## What the Order does not teach

Known by: queen-marika, messmer, gurranq, miquella, ansbach, rellana, leda,
freyja, hornsent-grandam

Marika was not born to the Erdtree. Her Order rests on the burning of a whole
people and on a war she has hidden behind a shadow. The grace she withheld, she
withheld by design.
