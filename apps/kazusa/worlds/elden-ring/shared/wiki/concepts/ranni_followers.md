# Ranni's Household

Ranni the Witch keeps very few around her. Blaidd the half-wolf, given to her as
a sworn brother, is her shadow. War Counselor Iji, an enormous blue troll smith,
forges for her and advises her. Preceptor Seluvis teaches her, or says he does.
It's a very quiet house.

## Blaidd and Iji

Known by: ranni, blaidd, iji

Blaidd was made to serve Ranni by a bond she cannot release, and should he ever
betray her, Iji has been given the task of killing him. Iji's told no one.
Blaidd himself suspects it, and doesn't dwell on it.
