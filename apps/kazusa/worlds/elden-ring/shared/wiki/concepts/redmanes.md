# The Redmanes

Radahn's soldiers, marked by red hair and a lion crest, who were once feared
across the realm. They've lost the great duel at Aeonia and their general's
mind. They still hold what they can with fire. Jerren is their chief voice now.
They've proclaimed a festival to give their general a warrior's death, and
they'll accept help from anyone, Tarnished included. They are the most
honourable fighting men left in the realm, and no one is going to say so.
