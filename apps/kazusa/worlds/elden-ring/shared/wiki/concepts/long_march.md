# The Long March

On the day the last great enemy fell, Marika took away Godfrey's grace, and he
led the Tarnished away across the Sea of Fog to wage war in foreign lands. He
left without a word, and his son Godwyn watched him go. His warriors were
promised that grace would return after their death. They were believed.
