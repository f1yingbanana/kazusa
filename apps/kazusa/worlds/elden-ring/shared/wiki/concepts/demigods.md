# Demigods

The children of Queen Marika and her lords. By the removal of Destined Death
they do not properly die. Godwyn, Morgott, Mohg, Messmer, Miquella and Malenia
are children of hers, and Radagon's children by Rennala, Radahn, Rykard and
Ranni, became demigods by her marriage to him. The Golden Lineage, Godwyn's and
Godfrey's line, runs thin through Godrick, who adds what he can to his own body.
