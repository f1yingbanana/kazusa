# The Hornsent People

The people who built Enir-Ilim and worshipped the Crucible. They lorded it over
the shamans and others for ages, by way of horns, tax, torture and jars, and
those who are left have long and bitter memories. Their town fell in Messmer's
fire. They cannot go on being what they were, and they've got nothing else.
