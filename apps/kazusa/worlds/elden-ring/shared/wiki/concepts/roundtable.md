# The Company of the Roundtable

A gathering of Tarnished in a hall that exists only for them, built around the
Two Fingers and their Reader. Long ago it hosted the very greatest Tarnished:
Vyke, Dolores, Wilhelm and Vargram among them. Now it has Sir Gideon, a smith, a
Reader, and a handful of damaged people. There's not much friendship.

## Gideon's real business

Known by: gideon-ofnir, finger-reader-enia, brother-corhyn,
d-hunter-of-the-dead, sorcerer-rogier

Gideon has given up on the Ring. He's collecting knowledge, for the sake of
something else that he's unwilling to speak about. He sends members out to die
in order to find out what kills people.
