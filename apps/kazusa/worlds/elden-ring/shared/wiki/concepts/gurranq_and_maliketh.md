# The Bestial Sanctum's Priest

A hulking crouched beast in rags, who lives in the Bestial Sanctum and exchanges
his blessings for pieces of deathroot. He talks to himself. He's hungry.

## Who he is

Known by: gurranq, queen-marika, melina, ranni

He is Maliketh, Marika's half-brother and shadowbound beast, forced to hide
himself in this form. Every root he eats is one more scrap of Destined Death
brought back under his control, to set right what he allowed to be stolen.
