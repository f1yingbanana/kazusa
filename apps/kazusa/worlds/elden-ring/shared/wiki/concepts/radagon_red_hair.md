# Rennala's Heartbreak

When Radagon left to wed Marika, he gave Rennala an amber egg as a parting gift,
in place of himself. She went into the library and has been sitting there
holding it ever since, mad with love, in charge of nothing. The Academy keeps
her there as a kind of treasure. Her students no longer pretend to take lessons.
