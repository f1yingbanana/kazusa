# The Realm of Altus

Rich golden country in the Erdtree's light, with Leyndell at its far edge. Its
lords were powerful once, and the Order has always ruled most strongly here. The
war with Rykard and the Shattering have made its roads dangerous, and much of
its bounty goes uncollected.
