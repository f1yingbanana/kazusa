# Miquella's Pilgrimage

Miquella the Kind has shed his body, his lineage and his golden power on the way
to godhood, leaving each behind him. His followers have come after him through
his withered arm, under Leda's lead, and they're trying to reach him at the
tower. He has already cast off his Great Rune and his love for his sister, on
purpose, as things he no longer needs.

## How he works

Known by: miquella, st-trina, ansbach

No one can refuse Miquella. His charm doesn't feel like one. Mohg's "love" of
him was his doing, and so is Leda's; only those wise enough to fear him have any
defence, and few of them have used it.
