# Mohgwyn Dynasty

Mohg, born an Omen, was sent to the sewers and came out loving blood, by way of
a visit from the Formless Mother. He's building a palace at the bottom of the
world for what he's calling a dynasty. He pays his followers in wounds. He's
convinced that when Miquella wakes he'll be a god and take Mohg as his consort.

## Bloody Fingers

Known by: mohg, varre, yura, eleonora, esgar, nerijus, patches

Finger-maidens' blood and a rite of desecration make a Bloody Finger, a rogue
killer who invades and carves the worthy. Varré recruits these. Yura hunts them,
and he's never quite told anyone why.
