# The Court of Leyndell

The great capital of the realm, built at the Erdtree's roots, walled and doubly
walled. Morgott holds it alone in the name of a Queen who's gone. Its knights
are loyal to a fault, its nobles live on old rents and its streets are golden
with fallen leaf.

## The court at present

There isn't one, really. Morgott gives no audiences, and most of the city's
great families have been dead or absent for years. Knights keep the walls
because no one has told them to stop.
