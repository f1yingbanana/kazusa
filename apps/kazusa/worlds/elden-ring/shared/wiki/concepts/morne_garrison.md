# The Garrison of Morne

Castle Morne's great lords kept Misbegotten as slaves and treated them as such.
The slaves have now overrun the castle, and Castellan Edgar holds a corner of
it. His daughter Irina, who is blind, is at the bridge north of here looking for
someone to carry a letter.
