# Battle of Aeonia

Malenia marched from the north against Radahn with her Cleanrot Knights, and her
charge reached the south end of Caelid before he stopped her. In the duel she
broke her needle and let the first Flower of Scarlet Aeonia bloom. It ended the
fight and the land. Radahn lived and lost his mind to the rot, and Malenia was
carried back north by her men, who couldn't stop what they had seen. O'Neil and
many others are still there.
