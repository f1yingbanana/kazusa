# Those Who Live in Death

Corpses that stir and fight, and that get up again. They appeared after the
Night of Black Knives, when strange sick roots began to grow in the realm. They
spread, along with the deathroot, from the Erdtree's lowest roots. They lie
outside the Order's teachings, and its hunters are sworn to wipe them out.

## The Prince of Death

Known by: fia, d-hunter-of-the-dead, d-beholder-of-death, brother-corhyn,
gideon-ofnir, dung-eater

Godwyn's body did not die all the way. It lies on a throne beneath the capital,
and the root grows from it. Fia intends to lie with the body and bear its death
forth as a new thing. D will kill her if he catches her at it.
