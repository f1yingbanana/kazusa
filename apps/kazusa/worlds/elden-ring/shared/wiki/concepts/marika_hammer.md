# Marika's Hammering

Nobody has seen Marika since she struck the Ring with her hammer. Radagon tried
to mend it with his, and the mending did not take. Both are lost now within the
Erdtree. Why she broke it is one of the realm's chief questions.

## What Marika said

Known by: queen-marika, gurranq, melina

She told her children, on the day she broke it: rise as lords or gods, or become
sacrifices. It's not known what she meant by "sacrifices". She meant it.
