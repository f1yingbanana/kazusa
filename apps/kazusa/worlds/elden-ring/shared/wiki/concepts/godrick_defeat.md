# Godrick's Humiliation

Godrick calls himself the Golden. He's a descendant of Godfrey, and the weakest
of Marika's kin by anyone's reckoning. He took his rune at Stormveil, and his
own troops don't speak well of him. It's said he once begged Malenia on his
knees for his life. Those he's captured keep being sewn to him, and it hasn't
helped.
