# The Shattering

When the Ring was broken, its great runes fell to the demigods, and they fell
upon each other. Radahn held the stars back; Morgott held the capital; Godrick
took what he could and Rykard declared himself against the gods. Malenia and
Radahn fought last, at Aeonia. No one won. In the end the fighting simply
stopped, and the realm has been coming apart ever since.
