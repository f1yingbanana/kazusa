# The Recusants

A great dark mansion on the rim of a volcano, whose lady is hospitable to all
who hate the Erdtree. Its guests are called Recusants. They take contracts on
Tarnished of the Roundtable. The most promising guests get an audience with Lord
Rykard, and those don't come back up. Everybody suspects as much.

## What the invitation means

Known by: tanith, rya, knight-bernahl, patches, rileigh, inquisitor-ghiza

Tanith is feeding the Serpent. Rya is its very daughter, or something like it,
and is too attached to Lady Tanith to notice all of this. Bernahl thinks it's as
good a place as any to carry on hating. Patches is there for the wine.
