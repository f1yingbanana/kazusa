# Marika's Second Consort

The red-haired hero who came to conquer Liurnia, married its queen and then left
her to become Marika's consort and Elden Lord. A pious scholar of the Order, he
was a great influence on the fundamentalists. He has not been seen since the
Shattering, and he's believed to have gone into the Tree with his wife.

## Marika and Radagon are one

Known by: queen-marika, radagon, gurranq, margit, melina, gideon-ofnir

Radagon is Marika. They share one body, and are two halves of a single god.
Rennala didn't know this.
