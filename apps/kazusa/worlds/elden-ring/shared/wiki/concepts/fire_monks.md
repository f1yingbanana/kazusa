# Fire Monks

Order priests of an unusual kind: Marika ordered them to keep watch on the
giants' flame on the Peak, and they did, with strange fire rites of their own.
Their strength is mostly in Guardians' Garrison, and they've spread out as far
as Mt. Gelmir. They have been stuck with this duty for ages, and it has made
them difficult.
