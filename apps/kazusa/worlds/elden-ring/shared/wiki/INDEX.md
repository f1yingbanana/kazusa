# The Lands Between: World Wiki

The World Wiki for the Elden Ring world, at the moment the Tarnished return. The
player is the Tarnished (`user:tarnished`). The Scribe (`scribe`) is the system
agent, keeping the world from the Scribe's Loft.

This page is for people and for the Scribe, to find their way around. Characters
never read it, and the memory index skips it. Keep it high level: the folders
are the full listing.

## What's here

- `concepts/`: 117 pages of cosmology, history and factions.
- `entities/places/`: 427 places, in 35 top-level areas.
- `entities/characters/`: 179 characters: 114 simulated, the player, and the
  rest background.

## Start with

- `concepts/lands_between.md`: the root page.
- The `realm_*` pages, one per region: `realm_limgrave`, `realm_weeping`,
  `realm_liurnia`, `realm_caelid`, `realm_altus`, `realm_mountaintops`,
  `realm_farum_azula`, `realm_of_shadow`. Each is the community root for its
  factions.

## The map

- **The surface regions:** Limgrave, the Weeping Peninsula, Liurnia of the
  Lakes, Caelid and Dragonbarrow, the Altus Plateau, the Mountaintops of the
  Giants, the Forbidden Lands, the Consecrated Snowfield, Miquella's Haligtree
  and Crumbling Farum Azula.
- **Below ground:** the Siofra and Ainsel rivers, Nokron, the Lake of Rot,
  Deeproot Depths and Mohgwyn Palace.
- **Sealed or isolated places:** the Roundtable Hold, the Chapel of Anticipation
  and the Divine Bridge. Each is a top-level place with its one way in.
- **The Land of Shadow:** Gravesite Plain, Scadu Altus, Shadow Keep, Rauh, the
  Cerulean Coast, the Jagged Peak, the Abyssal Woods and Enir-Ilim.

Travel time is measured from the game's map: 1 km of game map is about 60
walking minutes on a good road, with terrain factors on top. Each measured place
keeps its map position in `map`.
