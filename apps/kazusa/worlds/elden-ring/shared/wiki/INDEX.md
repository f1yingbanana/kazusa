# The Lands Between: World Wiki

The public world knowledge base for the Elden Ring world, at the moment the
Tarnished return. See [the full index](LANDS_BETWEEN_INDEX.md) for the map tree
and the roster.

- `concepts/`: 117 pages of cosmology, history and factions. Community roots:
  `lands_between`, the `realm_*` pages, and the factions under them.
- `entities/places/`: 427 places, in 35 top-level areas.
- `entities/characters/`: 179 characters: 114 simulated, the player
  `user:tarnished`, and the rest background.

Travel time is measured from the game's map: 1 km of game map is about 60
walking minutes on a good road, with terrain factors on top. Each measured place
keeps its map position in `map`.
