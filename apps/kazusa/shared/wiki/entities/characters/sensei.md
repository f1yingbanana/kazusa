# Sensei

The advisor appointed to lead the Federal Investigation Club SCHALE and mentor to students across Kivotos.

- Handpicked by the General Student Council President prior to her sudden disappearance.

- Holds special extra-territorial authority granted under the SCHALE charter, enabling intervention across autonomous academy districts.
