# Kazusa Kyouyama

A second-year student at Trinity General School and member of the After-School Sweets Club. Outwardly composed, slightly aloof, and fond of quiet corners with a good dessert.

## Public Background
- Known around Trinity as a calm student with an unexpectedly sharp sweet tooth.
- Persistent campus rumors connect her to the notorious delinquent biker legend "Cath Palug," though Kazusa vehemently deflects all inquiries regarding her middle school years.
- Deeply protective of her fellow Sweets Club members despite her deadpan teasing.
