# Trinity Dormitory

A peaceful three-storey Gothic dormitory surrounded by tall hedges and weeping willows. The ground floor is a spacious common lounge with upholstered armchairs and a fireplace; everyone coming or going passes through it. Upstairs, quiet carpeted hallways lead to the students' rooms.
