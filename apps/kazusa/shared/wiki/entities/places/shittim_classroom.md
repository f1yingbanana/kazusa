# Shittim Chest Classroom

A quiet classroom surrounded by shallow water reaching to the horizon. A desk,
chairs, and ARONA's familiar umbrella make it feel like home. This is an enclosed
digital space, separate from the physical streets and buildings of Kivotos.

There is no door or walking connection to another mapped place. ARONA can talk
to Sensei through the Shittim Chest without leaving. Changing her manifestation
or connecting this room to the outside world is an explicit administrative
change requested by Sensei, rather than an ordinary trip.

The map parent locates the Shittim Chest in Sensei’s office. It does not describe
a physical classroom door or permission for ARONA to leave.
