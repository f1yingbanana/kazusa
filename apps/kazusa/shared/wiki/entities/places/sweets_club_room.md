# Sweets Club Room

A charming, sunlit room in the West Annex, claimed and redecorated by the After-School Sweets Club. The air carries vanilla, toasted pastry and melted chocolate. A long wooden table sits at the centre, surrounded by mismatched pastel-cushioned chairs and covered in dessert magazines and tea sets. In the corner stand a small refrigerator stocked with butter, cream, preserves and Natsu's milk cartons, a countertop oven and a baking station.
