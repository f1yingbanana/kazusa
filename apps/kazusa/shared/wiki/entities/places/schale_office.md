# SCHALE Office

The SCHALE headquarters in the heart of the Kivotos District Union. The top-floor office has floor-to-ceiling windows over the skyline, Sensei's desk buried in district paperwork and club budget reports, a leather sofa and coffee table for visitors, an espresso machine, and a side table that keeps accumulating pastry boxes brought by students. Students are welcome during the day; at night the building is locked unless Sensei is working late.
