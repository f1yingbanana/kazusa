# Trinity General School

The open grounds of Trinity General School. White marble paths link the Academic Hall, the library, the cafeteria and the old West Annex, lined with clipped rose hedges and wrought-iron benches. Between periods the paths fill with students in pale uniforms, bookbags over one shoulder and rifles slung over the other.
