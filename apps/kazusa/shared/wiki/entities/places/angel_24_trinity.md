# Angel 24

A bright 24-hour Angel 24 convenience store at the dormitory end of Church Street. The doors chime on entry. Shelves hold bottled boba tea, flavoured milk cartons, bento, strawberry sandwiches, instant ramen, manga magazines and ammunition boxes. A narrow window counter is where students grab a fast bite.
