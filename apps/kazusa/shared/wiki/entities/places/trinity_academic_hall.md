# Academic Hall

The grand academic wing of Trinity General School: towering white marble colonnades, vaulted ceilings, stained-glass windows and polished hardwood floors. Sunlight pours into tiered lecture halls and seminar rooms. During passing periods the hallways buzz with students carrying heavy bookbags, chatting about exams and polishing their firearm accessories.
