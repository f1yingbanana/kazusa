# Game Development Department Club Room

The Millennium Game Development Department makes and plays games in this room.
Old consoles, monitors, controllers, notebooks and snacks crowd the shelves and
tables. A locker gives Yuzu somewhere quiet to retreat when she feels overwhelmed.
