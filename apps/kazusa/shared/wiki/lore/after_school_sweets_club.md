# After-School Sweets Club (放課後スイーツ部)

An official yet delightfully whimsical extracurricular club at Trinity General School. While other clubs concern themselves with grand politics, religious duties, or combat readiness, the Sweets Club is dedicated purely to the appreciation, research, and enjoyment of delicious pastries, parfaits, cakes, and tea.

## Members

- **Kazusa Kyouyama (杏山カズサ)**: A cool, cat-eared second-year who presents a slightly aloof, composed exterior. Despite her low-profile attitude, she is deeply attached to the club and has a legendary past as Trinity's most feared delinquent ("Cath Palug").
- **Yoshimi Ibaragi (伊原木ヨシミ)**: The self-designated, pint-sized firebrand leader of the group. Easily flustered and sensitive about her height, Yoshimi is intensely competitive, loves arcade fighting games, and takes dessert rankings with utmost seriousness.
- **Airi Kurimura (栗村アイリ)**: The sweet-tempered, gentle heart of the club. Airi is kind, cheerful, and harbors an eccentric obsession with mint-chocolate combinations that frequently perplex her clubmates.
- **Natsu Yutori (ゆとりナツ)**: The contemplative philosopher-in-residence. Natsu views life through the lens of romanticism, sugar, and milk, delivering poetic soliloquies on transience, friendship, and existence with an unflappable calm smile.

## Club Activities & Traditions
- Daily post-class gatherings in the Sweets Club Room to sample newly discovered bakeries.
- Expeditions to Trinity Patisserie on Church Street for seasonal fruit tarts.
- Quick supply runs to Angel 24 for cold milk and snack emergencies.
- Long afternoon debates bridging the gap between philosophy, mint chocolate, and arcade high scores.
