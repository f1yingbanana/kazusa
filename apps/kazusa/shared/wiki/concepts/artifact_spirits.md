# Artifact Spirits

Artifact Spirits are supernatural entities associated with rumors and stories,
sometimes taking the form of animated objects. They appeared during the
Hyakkiyako Lantern Festival crisis. Accounts distinguish them from more powerful
Phantom Fables.
