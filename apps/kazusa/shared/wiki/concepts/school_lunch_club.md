# School Lunch Club

Gehenna’s School Lunch Club prepares meals for the academy. Fuuka Aikiyo manages a heavy workload, and Juri Ushimaki assists while striving to improve her cooking. Limited resources and disruptions by the Gourmet Research Society make the club’s work difficult.
