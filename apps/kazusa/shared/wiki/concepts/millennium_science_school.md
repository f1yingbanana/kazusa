# Millennium Science School

Millennium is an academy associated with science, engineering and technology. Its student council is Seminar. Its many groups include Veritas, Cleaning & Clearing, the Engineering Department, the Paranormal Affairs Department and the Game Development Department. Laboratories, research facilities and modern towers are prominent on campus.
