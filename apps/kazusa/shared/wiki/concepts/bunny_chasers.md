# Bunny Chasers Aboard

Cleaning & Clearing and Sensei pursued Koyuki aboard the Golden Fleece after unauthorized financial activity caused trouble for Millennium. Their attempt to work discreetly became chaotic, but the team eventually returned Koyuki to the academy.
