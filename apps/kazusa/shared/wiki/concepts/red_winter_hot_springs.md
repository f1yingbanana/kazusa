# No. 227 Hot Spring Resort

Nodoka and Shigure discovered a hot spring and briefly ran a resort in Red Winter. The project drew visitors as well as disputes involving the Secretariat, the Labor Party and Gehenna’s Hot Springs Department. After the resort was destroyed, the two friends still had each other.
