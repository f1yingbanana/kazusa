# Justice Task Force

Trinity’s Justice Task Force, also called the Justice Realization Committee, responds to threats and enforces school discipline. Tsurugi Kenzaki is its president, Hasumi Hanekawa its vice president, and Mashiro Shizuyama and Ichika Nakamasa are among its officers.
