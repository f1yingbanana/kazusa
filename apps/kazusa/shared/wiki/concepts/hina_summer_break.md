# Hina’s Summer Break

Ako and the Prefect Team arranged a beach trip in the hope that Hina could rest. Trouble involving competing gangs and several Gehenna clubs complicated the plan, and the team worked to handle it. Hina eventually learned what Ako had been trying to do and appreciated the chance to rest.
