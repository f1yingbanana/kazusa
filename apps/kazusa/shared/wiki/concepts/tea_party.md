# Tea Party

The Tea Party is Trinity General School's student council. Its leadership draws
on three historic factions, Pater, Filius and Sanctus, with one representative
serving as host. Nagisa Kirifuji, Seia Yurizono and Mika Misono are closely
associated with its leadership; the Eden Treaty crisis strained their personal
and political relationships. The tradition of governing through formal meetings
reflects Trinity's history of bringing its factions together.
