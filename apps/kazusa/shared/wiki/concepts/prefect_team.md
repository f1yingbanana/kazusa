# Prefect Team

Gehenna's Prefect Team maintains order amid the academy's frequent disturbances.
Hina Sorasaki chairs the team, Ako Amau handles administration, Iori Shiromi leads
field work, and Chinatsu Hinomiya provides medical support. Many other students
serve as officers. The team's workload and its relationship with Gehenna's student
council, the Pandemonium Society, are frequent sources of tension.
