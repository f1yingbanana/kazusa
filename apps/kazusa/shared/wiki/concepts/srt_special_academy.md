# SRT Special Academy

SRT was a special-response academy whose units could be deployed across Kivotos with the General Student Council President’s authority. After the president disappeared, the academy was shut down. RABBIT Platoon refused to abandon SRT and camped in Usagi Park; FOX Platoon also belonged to SRT.
