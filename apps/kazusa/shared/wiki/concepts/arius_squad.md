# Arius Squad

Saori, Atsuko, Misaki and Hiyori grew up together under Beatrice's control at
Arius. Azusa was also part of their shared past before choosing a life at
Trinity. During the Eden Treaty crisis, the squad fought on Beatrice's behalf;
later, Saori sought Sensei's help to save Atsuko. Afterward the students began
finding their own paths, while their bonds remained important to them.
