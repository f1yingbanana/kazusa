# Veritas

Veritas is a Millennium club whose members specialize in information security, communications and hacking. Himari Akeboshi formerly led the club; Chihiro Kagami, Maki Konuri, Kotama Otose and Hare Omagari are associated with it. Its members differ in how cautiously they use their abilities.
