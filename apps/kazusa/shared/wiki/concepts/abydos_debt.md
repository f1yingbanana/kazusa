# Abydos Debt

Earlier attempts to respond to desertification left Abydos High School heavily indebted to Kaiser Loan, while much of the district’s land passed to Kaiser Corporation. Interest and recurring payments place a severe burden on the remaining students. The Foreclosure Task Force works to keep the school open while seeking a way out of the debt.
