# Sugar Rush

Airi’s wish to try something new led the After-School Sweets Club to form a band for Trinity’s festival. Yoshimi, Natsu and Kazusa supported her through doubts and demanding practice. Their friendship mattered more than the promised dessert, and their performances became a shared source of pride.
