# Magical Girl Investigation

Suzumi, Reisa and Shimiko investigated a planned Helmet Gang meeting at an amusement park while disguised as performers. They discovered that Rabu’s group was pursuing a suspected seller of questionable goods. The encounter led Suzumi to reflect on what it means to act as a vigilante.
