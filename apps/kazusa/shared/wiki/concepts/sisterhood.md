# Sisterhood

The Sisterhood is an influential Trinity organization associated with the
cathedral, spiritual guidance and care for students. Sakurako Utazumi leads it;
Mari Iochi and Hinata Wakaba are members. The organization became more directly
involved in Trinity affairs during the Eden Treaty crisis.
