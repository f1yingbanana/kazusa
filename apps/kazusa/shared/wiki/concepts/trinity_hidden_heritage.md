# Trinity’s Hidden Heritage Expedition

Ui joined Hanako, Koharu, Hinata and Sensei on an expedition to investigate old Trinity ruins. Their findings pointed to traces of past students’ personal memories. Ui chose to respect their privacy, and the group returned with a shared experience and new appreciation for one another.
