# Gehenna Academy

Gehenna is a large academy known for its culture of freedom and the unruly activities of its clubs. The Pandemonium Society acts as its student council, while the Prefect Team tries to maintain order. Its clubs include the School Lunch Club, Emergency Medicine Club and Gourmet Research Society. Gehenna and Trinity have a history of rivalry addressed by the Eden Treaty.
