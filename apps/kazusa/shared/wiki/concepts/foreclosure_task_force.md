# Foreclosure Task Force

The Foreclosure Task Force, also called the Countermeasures Committee, is the group of Abydos students working to keep their school and district alive despite desertification and debt. Its core members are Hoshino Takanashi, Shiroko Sunaookami, Serika Kuromi, Ayane Okusora and Nonomi Izayoi. They take jobs, defend the school and seek help from Sensei.
