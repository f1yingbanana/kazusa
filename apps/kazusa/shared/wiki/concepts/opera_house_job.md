# The Opera House Job

Problem Solver 68 accepted a commission concerning Don Arancino at the Opera House, where Sensei also happened to be present. Saori was working to protect the same target. As rival operatives converged, the situation descended into fighting; a misunderstanding caused the Arancino gang to turn on Saori. Aru’s team helped her, and the aftermath left the agency without its expected payment. The group and Sensei later shared a meal.
