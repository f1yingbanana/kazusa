# Shanhaijing Senior Secondary School

Shanhaijing is an academy known for tradition, commerce, gardens and tiled-roof architecture. The Xuanlong Office governs the school, while the Xuanwu Merchant Association, or Black Tortoise Promenade, represents another commercial interest. The school has a restrictive approach to outsiders, though some public areas can be visited.
