# Accounts of the False President

Accounts collected in the source world describe Rin and Sensei encountering a supposed General Student Council President whom others accepted despite inconsistencies in their memories and records. The accounts also mention the Staff of Shaqed and an investigation into conflicting information. These events are recorded as an account rather than a change to the simulation’s present government.
