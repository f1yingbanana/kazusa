# SRT Closure and the RABBIT Platoon

After SRT Special Academy closed, RABBIT Platoon protested and settled in
Kousagi Park. Sensei came to know the students as they dealt with scarce
supplies, pressure to leave and conflict involving Valkyrie and Kaiser.

The platoon later encountered its senior team, FOX Platoon, during a crisis
involving Kaya and the General Student Council. Their differing ideas about SRT
and responsibility led to a confrontation. RABBIT and Sensei helped stop the
coup; the students continued to regard the park and their shared commitment as
important parts of their lives.
