# Kivotos: The Academy City

Kivotos is a sprawling, autonomous megacity divided into numerous semi-independent academy districts. Thousands of academies coexist across distinct cultural, political, and architectural territories, ranging from the gothic-cathedral courtyards of Trinity General School to the corporate high-rises of Millennium and the sprawling desert ruins of Abydos.

## Fundamental Principles of Kivotos

### Halos & Resilience
- **Halos**: Every student in Kivotos possesses a unique, glowing halo floating above their head. The halo reflects the student's consciousness, vitality, and mystic identity. When a student falls asleep or is knocked unconscious, their halo disappears until they awaken.
- **Physical Resilience**: Halos grant students immense physical durability. Normal firearm rounds, explosions, and impacts that would be fatal to regular humans cause little more than bruises, fatigue, or scrapes to students. However, heavy ordnance and extreme cumulative damage can incapacitate them.

### Firearms as Everyday Items
- Carrying firearms is ubiquitous throughout Kivotos. Assault rifles, submachine guns, shotguns, and sniper rifles are treated as standard personal accessories, carried casually into classrooms, cafes, shopping centers, and parks.
- Disagreements between factions or delinquent gangs are frequently settled with gunfire, typically resulting in property damage and bruised pride rather than mortal casualties.

### Governance & SCHALE
- **General Student Council (GSC)**: The centralized governing body of Kivotos, headquartered in the Sanctum Tower at the center of the D.U. (District of Utopia).
- **Federal Investigation Club (SCHALE)**: An extra-judicial, city-wide club established by the vanished General Student Council President. Led by the adult "Sensei", SCHALE possesses authority across all academy boundaries to resolve crises, provide mentorship, and support students across Kivotos.
