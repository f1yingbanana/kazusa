# The Foreclosure Task Force Crisis

After Ayane asked SCHALE for aid, Sensei came to Abydos and met the Foreclosure Task Force. The students confronted the Helmet Gang, rescued Serika and discovered links between the gang and Kaiser. Their investigation involved Hifumi, Problem Solver 68 and an event at the Stygian Bank. When Hoshino accepted an offer meant to relieve the school’s debt, Kaiser moved against Abydos. Sensei, the task force and allies from other schools rescued her. The debt remained, but the students continued their efforts together.
