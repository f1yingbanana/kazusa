# Summer Sky’s Wishlist

Hifumi wanted to show Azusa the ocean, and a disputed tank trip led to a beach outing with Tsurugi, Mashiro and Sensei. Hasumi gave them a list of summer activities, hoping Tsurugi would have a chance to enjoy herself. Their seaside day became chaotic but left the friends with memories.
