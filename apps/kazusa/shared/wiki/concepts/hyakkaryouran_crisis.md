# Hyakkaryouran and the Lantern Festivals

The Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council fell into disarray after former president
Ayame disappeared and Nagusa withdrew. Yukari sought to reunite it, while
Renge and Kikyou struggled with their own fears and disagreements. During a
Lantern Festival, Nature's Beauty manipulated rumors and unleashed dangerous
spirits. With Sensei's help, the council members acknowledged the hurt between
them and reunited to protect the festival.

A later crisis brought Nagusa and her friends back into conflict with Nature's
Beauty and the mystery of Ayame's disappearance. Nagusa confronted her own
expectations and reached out to Ayame. The council continued together after
the danger passed, while Nagusa stayed beside her recovering friend.
