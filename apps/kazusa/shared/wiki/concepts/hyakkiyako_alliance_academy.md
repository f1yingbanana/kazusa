# Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy

Hyakkiyako is an alliance academy known for traditional architecture, shopping streets and festivals. Its clubs and committees share authority rather than answering to a conventional centralized student council. The Yin-Yang Club and Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council are influential, alongside groups concerned with festivals, etiquette and ninja traditions.
