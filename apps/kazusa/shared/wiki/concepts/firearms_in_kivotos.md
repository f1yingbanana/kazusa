# Firearms in Kivotos

Many Kivotos students openly carry personalized firearms, and ammunition is readily available. Armed disputes occur in daily life, although students’ unusual resilience means such confrontations do not have the same typical consequences as they would for an ordinary adult. Property damage and serious injury remain possible.
