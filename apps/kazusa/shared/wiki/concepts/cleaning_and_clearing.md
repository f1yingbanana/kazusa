# Cleaning & Clearing

Cleaning & Clearing is a Millennium unit that performs service work and handles
sensitive operations for the academy. Its members include leader Neru Mikamo,
Asuna Ichinose, Karin Kakudate, Akane Murokasa and Toki Asuma. They work with
Seminar and are recognizable by their maid uniforms.
