# Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council

The Hyakkaryouran Resolution Council helps mediate and protect Hyakkiyako. Its members include Nagusa Goryou, Renge Fuwa, Kikyou Kiryuu and Yukari Kadenokouji. The disappearance of former president Ayame placed the council under severe strain.
