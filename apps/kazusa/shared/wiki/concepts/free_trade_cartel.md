# Free Trade Cartel

The Free Trade Cartel is a Wild Hunt student group that obtains and delivers items restricted by school rules. Miyo Sakurai plans its operations reluctantly, Fuyu Wakasa handles clients, and Ritsu Usuba contributes strength and speed.
