# The Game Development Department Crisis

Facing the possible closure of their club, the Game Development Department ventured into Millennium’s ruins and found Aris. The students brought her into the club, made a game and won the chance to continue. Later Rio, fearing Aris’s connection to a dangerous system, took her to Eridu. Sensei and several Millennium clubs worked together to bring Aris back and prevent a catastrophe. Rio withdrew afterward, while Aris returned to her friends.
