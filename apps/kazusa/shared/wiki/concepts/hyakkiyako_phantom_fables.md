# Phantom Fables and Artifact Spirits

Accounts from Hyakkiyako describe supernatural beings associated with rumors and stories. Lesser Artifact Spirits can appear as animated objects; more powerful Phantom Fables include Kurokage and the One-Eyed One. The Hyakkaryouran’s inherited authority has been used against them.
