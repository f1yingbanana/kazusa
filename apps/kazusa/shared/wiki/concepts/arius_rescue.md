# Rescue at Arius Basilica

After the attack on the Eden Treaty signing, Saori sought Sensei’s help to save Atsuko from Beatrice. Saori, Hiyori and Misaki traveled toward the basilica with Sensei. Mika initially pursued the squad in anger, then recognized their shared struggle and helped hold off attackers. Atsuko was rescued and Beatrice’s intended sacrifice was stopped. Afterward Saori chose to step away from the squad to find her own path.
