# Xuanlong Office

The Xuanlong Office, also known as Genryumon, is Shanhaijing’s student council.
Kisaki Ryuuge is its president, and Mina Konoe serves as an executive and
bodyguard. The office operates in a school known for its guarded approach to
outsiders.
