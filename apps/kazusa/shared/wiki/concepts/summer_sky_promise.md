# Summer Sky’s Promise

Mika and Seia wanted Nagisa to take a break from her Tea Party workload, so students arranged a beach trip. Ichika and Hasumi became involved as the Hot Springs Department’s activities complicated the visit. The outing brought together old tensions, practical problems and concern for an overworked friend.
