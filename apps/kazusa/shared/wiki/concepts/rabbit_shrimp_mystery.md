# RABBIT Squad’s Shrimp Mystery

RABBIT Squad investigated a shrimp shortage in a coastal village. Their initial suspicions about local legends proved misleading; by helping residents and examining the evidence, they uncovered a smuggling operation. The grateful villagers later shared shrimp with them.
