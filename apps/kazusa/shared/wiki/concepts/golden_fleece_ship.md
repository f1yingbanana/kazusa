# The Golden Fleece

The Golden Fleece is a luxury ship associated with Odyssey Maritime School and used for entertainment and games. Millennium’s C&C pursued Koyuki aboard it, and it later hosted a Mushiqueen tournament during the Momoyodou resort incident.
