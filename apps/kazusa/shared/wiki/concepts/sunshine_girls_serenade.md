# Sunshine Girls’ Serenade

Makoto enlisted Sensei for Gehenna’s Special Party and arranged for Hina to perform a piano recital. Hina accepted the challenge and prepared despite her responsibilities as head prefect. Rivalry between the Pandemonium Society and the Prefect Team complicated the party preparations.
