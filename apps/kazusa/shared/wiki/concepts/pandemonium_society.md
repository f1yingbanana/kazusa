# Pandemonium Society

The Pandemonium Society is Gehenna Academy’s student council, led by Makoto Hanuma. Its members include Iroha Natsume and Ibuki Tanga. The society celebrates Gehenna’s freedom and frequently clashes with Hina’s Prefect Team over discipline and authority.
