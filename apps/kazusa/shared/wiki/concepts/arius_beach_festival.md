# Arius Squad at the Beach Festival

Saori took a job at Outlaw Beach while Atsuko, Misaki and Hiyori visited the festival with Sensei. The squad quietly looked out for Saori as she worked, and eventually reunited with her amid trouble at the event. Their time together offered the friends a chance to enjoy summer while finding their own paths.
