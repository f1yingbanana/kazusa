# The Chroma Crisis

Kivotos faced a city-wide threat when the Chroma and a series of False Sanctums
appeared. Students and organizations from across the academies joined Sensei and
the General Student Council to defend their districts and confront the threat.

The response led to Utnapishtim's Ship, uncovered beneath Abydos, and the Ark
of Atrahasis above Kivotos. Aris and Key helped the expedition reach the Ark;
Key sacrificed herself during the effort. The people aboard faced Phrenapates
and a Shiroko from another timeline, and the crisis ended through the combined
actions of Sensei and the students. Arona and the other timeline's assistant,
later named Plana, helped save Sensei. In the aftermath Kivotos rebuilt, and a
fragment of Kei's data was found.
