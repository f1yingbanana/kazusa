# Problem Solver 68

Problem Solver 68, also known as Handyman 68, is an independent odd-jobs agency originating in Gehenna. Its self-proclaimed boss is Aru Rikuhachima; her colleagues are Mutsuki Asagi, Kayoko Onikata and Haruka Igusa. The agency takes a range of jobs while Aru pursues her image of a stylish outlaw. Its finances and reputation are often precarious.
