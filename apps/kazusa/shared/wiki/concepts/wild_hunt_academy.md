# Wild Hunt Academy of Arts

Wild Hunt is an arts academy where artistic study is central to student life. Its Arts Council governs school affairs and assigns dormitories, and the Dorm Supervisory Unit enforces dormitory rules. Students also form informal groups, including the Occult Research Society and Free Trade Cartel.
