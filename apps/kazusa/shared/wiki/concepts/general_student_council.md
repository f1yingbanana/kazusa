# General Student Council

The General Student Council coordinates city-wide administration across Kivotos and has offices responsible for different areas of government. Its president disappeared after arranging for Sensei to come to Kivotos, leaving the council and the academies to deal with a difficult period of uncertainty. The council operates from the Sanctum Tower.
