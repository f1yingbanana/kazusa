# Valkyrie Police Academy

Valkyrie trains and organizes student police officers in Kivotos. Its officers
include Community Safety Bureau partners Kirino Nakatsukasa and Fubuki Nemugaki
and Public Security Bureau officers Kanna Ogata and Konoka Shima.
