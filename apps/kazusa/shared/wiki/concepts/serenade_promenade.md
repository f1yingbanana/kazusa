# Serenade Promenade

For a Trinity Carnival idol event, Mari helped Sakurako and Mine prepare by learning from students across Kivotos. They invited her to lead their group, Antique Seraphim. Other participants included Abydos students and Utaha, supported by the Engineering Department.

At the carnival, the After-School Sweets Club tried a haunted-house café, Valkyrie students performed a safety promotion, and Tsurugi stepped in for an actor in a Justice Task Force play. Nagisa exhibited art, the Gourmet Research Society advised food vendors, and Rabu’s group enjoyed the festival with Sensei.
