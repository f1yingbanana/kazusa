# RABBIT Platoon

RABBIT Platoon is a four-member team from SRT Special Academy: Tsukiyuki
Miyako, Saki Sorai, Moe Kazekura and Miyu Kasumizawa. After SRT closed, they
occupied Usagi Park, also known as Kousagi Park, to protest the decision and
continued living and training there. Sensei has supported the team.
