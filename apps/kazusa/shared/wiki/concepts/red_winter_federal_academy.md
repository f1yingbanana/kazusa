# Red Winter Federal Academy

Red Winter is an academy in a snowy district known for frequent coups and
political reversals. Its president, Cherino Renkawa, repeatedly returns to
power; Tomoe Sashiro assists her and Marina Ikekura leads the Security
Committee.
