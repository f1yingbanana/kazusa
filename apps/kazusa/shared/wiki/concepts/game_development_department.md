# Game Development Department

Millennium's Game Development Department makes games, including games inspired
by older consoles and pixel art. Yuzu Hanaoka leads development; Momoi Saiba
writes scenarios, Midori Saiba creates art, and Aris Tendou plays and helps the
team. Their club room is packed with games, consoles and development supplies.
