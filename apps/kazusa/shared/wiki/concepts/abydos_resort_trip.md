# Abydos Resort Trip

The Foreclosure Task Force’s promised resort vacation led to an abandoned island resort. The students repaired and cleaned the site, dealt with its defenses and clashed with the Splash Helmet Gang and Wakamo. Despite the trouble, they shared meals and time together before returning.
