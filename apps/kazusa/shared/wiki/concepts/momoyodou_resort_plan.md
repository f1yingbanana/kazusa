# Momoyodou Resort Plan

Shizuko pursued a beach-house expansion and became caught in a fraudulent resort-rights scheme. Students from Hyakkiyako and Abydos helped confront the resulting competition and Kaiser’s involvement. The experience brought the groups together around the idea of genuine hospitality.
