# Moonlight Dream Exchange

An exchange brought Cherino, Tomoe and Marina from Red Winter to Shanhaijing. Kisaki’s efforts to engage with outsiders met resistance from Kaguya, whose devotion to tradition became entangled with a challenge to Genryumon. Mina and the visitors became involved in the dispute.
