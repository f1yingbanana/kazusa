# Eden Treaty Crisis

Trinity's Make-Up Work Club was formed while Nagisa feared an infiltrator would
sabotage the Eden Treaty. Hifumi, Azusa, Koharu and Hanako studied together
under Sensei and formed a friendship despite the pressure. Azusa, who came from
Arius, chose to protect her friends and Trinity rather than follow the plan she
had been sent to carry out.

Arius attacked the treaty signing. Students from Trinity and Gehenna rallied,
with Sensei's help, and resisted the attack. The crisis exposed the role of
Beatrice in manipulating Arius and left Mika, Nagisa and Seia with difficult
personal and political consequences. Saori later sought Sensei's help to save
Atsuko from Beatrice; Mika and the Arius students helped bring the crisis to an
end. The Make-Up Work Club's friendships survived its original purpose.
