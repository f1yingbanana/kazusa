# FOX Platoon

FOX Platoon is a senior SRT team whose members include Niko Yoshino, Kurumi Takakura and Otogi Tenjinyama. The team’s response to SRT’s closure put it in conflict with the younger RABBIT Platoon, though members also showed concern for their juniors.
