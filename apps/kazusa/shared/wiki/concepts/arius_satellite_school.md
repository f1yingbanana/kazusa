# Arius Satellite School

Arius separated from the schools that became Trinity. Its students suffered
under Beatrice, who used the school and its squad for violent plans during the
Eden Treaty crisis. After Beatrice's defeat, Arius students faced the difficult
work of rebuilding their lives and relationships.
