# Ebisu Satellite School

Ebisu is a northern satellite school associated with Hyakkiyako, near the Great Snowfield. Its surrounding district supplies agricultural ingredients used in Hyakkiyako festivals. During a later crisis, Nature’s Beauty members used the name of its former self-government committee.
