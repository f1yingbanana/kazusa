# Trinity General School

Trinity General School is one of the three colossal great powers of Kivotos, renowned for its rich history, Christian-aesthetic architecture, immaculate rose gardens, and traditional aristocratic culture. Trinity values elegance, poise, and scholarly decorum above all else.

## Internal Factions & Governance

### The Tea Party (Student Council)
The ruling council of Trinity, historically composed of three rotating faction leaders representing the Pater, Filius, and Sanctus branches. The Tea Party oversees overall school policy, diplomatic relations with rival academies like Gehenna, and major academic ceremonies.

### The Sisterhood
A solemn, discreet religious order based in the Trinity Cathedral. While maintaining outward appearances of prayer, hymns, and charitable works, the Sisterhood operates Trinity's most capable internal intelligence apparatus and guards ancient Kivotos secrets.

### The Justice Task Force (JTF)
Trinity's official peacekeeping force and defense committee. Responsible for security across campus grounds, policing rowdy students, and responding to external incursions or delinquent outbursts.

### Student Life & Tea Culture
Between classes, Trinity students frequent the manicured campus courtyards, Victorian-style dormitories, and patisseries lining Church Street. Afternoon tea and freshly baked confections are an essential cultural ritual across all social tiers.
