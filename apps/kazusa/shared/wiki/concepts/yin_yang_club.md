# Yin-Yang Club

The Yin-Yang Club is an influential Hyakkiyako organization concerned with the district’s affairs. Niya Amachi is president, Kaho Kuwakami is vice president and Chise Waraku is a member. The club operates within the academy’s decentralized alliance of factions.
