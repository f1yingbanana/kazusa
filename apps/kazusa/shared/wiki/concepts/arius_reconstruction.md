# Arius Reconstruction and the Comprehensive Exam

Some Arius students began studying at Trinity while others remained in Arius.
Sensei, the Arius Squad and Trinity representatives arranged lessons and an
opportunity to take a comprehensive exam. Subaru Kakehashi and the students of
the Nicomedia Troop struggled with distrust of outsiders and the weight of
Arius's history.

A crisis at Porta Pacis brought those tensions to a head. Maia, the Squad and
Sensei reached out to Subaru, and students from several schools helped provide
care. Afterward the students continued rebuilding Arius and studying together.
Nagisa arranged space for Arius students, and the Arius Squad received parole
with support from Sensei and other schools.
