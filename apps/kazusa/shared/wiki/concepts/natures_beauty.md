# Nature’s Beauty

Nature’s Beauty is a Hyakkiyako group associated with ghost stories and supernatural incidents. Kokuriko, Yabuki Shuro and Habu Azami are associated with its activities. Their actions brought them into conflict with the Hyakkaryouran during festival crises.
