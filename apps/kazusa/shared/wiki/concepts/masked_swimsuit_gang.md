# Masked Swimsuit Gang

The Masked Swimsuit Gang is a name used during an operation involving Abydos students and Hifumi. Wearing improvised disguises, they sought records linking debt payments to criminal activity at the Stygian Bank. Hifumi used the name Faust during the incident.
