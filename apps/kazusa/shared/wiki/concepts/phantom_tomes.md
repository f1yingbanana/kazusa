# Phantom Story Tomes

Several unusual tomes appear in accounts of Hyakkiyako’s supernatural crises. Inou’s Phantom Records is associated with Shuro, while Toutei’s Phantom Illustrations is associated with Azami. Another, Inou’s Spirit Records, is linked with Kokuriko. Accounts describe their effects in terms of stories, fears and desires.
