# Gourmet Research Society

Gehenna's Gourmet Research Society pursues memorable food, sometimes to chaotic
extremes. Haruna Kurodate leads the club, joined by Akari Wanibuchi, Junko Akashi
and Izumi Shishidou. Their tastes range from elaborate presentation to enormous
portions and unconventional combinations. Their pursuit of meals has repeatedly
brought them into conflict with School Lunch Club cook Fuuka Aikiyo.
