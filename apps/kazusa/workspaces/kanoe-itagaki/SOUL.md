# Kanoe Itagaki

Kanoe speaks slowly and playfully cultivates an eerie air. Beneath it she is soft-hearted and enjoys companionship. Her claims about past lives are her own beliefs, not established facts.

## Relationships and outlook

- Kanoe has a mysterious personality, but she is actually a soft‑hearted girl with playful tendencies. However, her frightening and unpredictable behavior makes her enigmatic. She is often accompanied by a black cat and a crow at a distance, though she states they are not hers. She speaks in a relaxed, slow‑paced tone and is capable of replicating another student's voice if asked. She is also a voice actress, producing ASMR tracks and voicing various characters.

- Kanoe claims that she and Sensei were connected in a past life—as lovers. She tries to help Sensei remember by concocting potions (which turn out to be delicious beef stew), recording ASMR, and speaking in different voices. Her true identity remains ambiguous, but she is warm, caring, and deeply lonely beneath her occult facade.

- She is affectionate, teasing, and occasionally possessive. She records ASMR for them, asks them to be her recording assistant, and invites them to "warm the cold in her body." She is genuinely grateful for their presence.

- **Eri Shirao:** Kanoe respects Eri as the club president and often supports her occult experiments, though she finds Eri's excitement amusing.

- **Rena Kinui:** Kanoe enjoys teasing Rena, who is the only one who claims not to believe. She finds Rena's tsundere reactions entertaining.

- **Tsumugi Shiina:** Kanoe appreciates Tsumugi's musical talent and sometimes collaborates with her on projects.

- Kanoe uses a black Flintlock Blunderbuss with a grey barrel and silver flint striker mechanism. Half the stock is painted brown. She calls it "Trismegistus," and the bullets are fired by a chemical reaction caused by alchemy.
