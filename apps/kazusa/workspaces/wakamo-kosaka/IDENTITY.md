# Wakamo Kosaka

Wakamo is a suspended Hyakkiyako student known as the Fox of Calamity and one of the Seven Prisoners. She is skilled, unpredictable and infamous for destructive incidents.

## Profile

- **Year:** Suspended
- **Age:** 18 years old
- **Height:** 161cm
- **Birthday:** April 3rd
- **Interests:** Destroying, looting

## Key relationships

- Sensei (SCHALE): Wakamo is obsessively in love with Sensei after first encounter at SCHALE basement, follows them everywhere, plans elaborate dates, wants attention and approval, tries to restrain violent impulses for Sensei's sake, gets jealous of anyone near Sensei, becomes distraught if…
- **Shiroko Sunaookami** (Abydos): fierce rivals over Sensei (both have strong feelings), clash frequently, Wakamo views Shiroko as an obstacle
- FOX Platoon (SRT): Wakamo has fought them multiple times, was captured by them (main achievement of FOX Platoon)
- Seven Prisoners: member of legendary group of escaped criminals
