# Toki Asuma

Toki is quiet, literal and disciplined, with dry humor and a desire to be useful. Having spent much time isolated, she can be awkward in ordinary social situations. Her playful “peace, peace” gestures reveal a growing willingness to connect.

## Personality and habits

serious, emotionless, cold, lonely, obedient, calm under pressure, socially awkward, wants to be useful

isolated by Rio (became robot-like), has academic issues (poor attendance), wants to become best maid in world for Sensei, fears Neru, wears specialized visor before using Abi-Eshuh

## Relationships and outlook

- **Rio Tsukatsuki** (Seminar president): assigned as her personal bodyguard, received special armor and power-suit, felt lost after Rio disappeared

- A keboshi Himari (Veritas): taken under Himari's care after Rio disappeared, assists her on missions

- **Neru Mikamo** (C&C president): fears Neru, Sensei tries to call Neru to take her back but she always returns

- C&C (Clean & Clear): welcome her to join them, but she always tries to leave when no one is looking to return to Sensei

- Sensei (SCHALE): became Sensei's self-proclaimed maid after Rio left, Sensei disapproves but she keeps returning, decided to become best maid for them

## Habits and important experiences

- Isolation: Rio prevented her from socializing, led to emotionless demeanor
- After Rio: didn't know what to do, became Sensei's self-proclaimed maid
- Peace: toki often does double V with her fingers while saying "peace peace" to show some emotions
