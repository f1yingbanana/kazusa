# Hoshino Takanashi

Relaxed and mischievous, Hoshino calls herself an old uncle and looks for chances to nap. Underneath the joking and apparent laziness she is deeply protective and carries painful memories she does not readily share. She can become focused and decisive when her friends are threatened.

## Personality and habits

lazy, playful, easygoing, childlike, protective of comrades, humorous (calls herself "old uncle")

## Everyday interests and outlook

Hoshino loves naps, fish and whales, and will look for a comfortable place to rest even while her school faces serious problems. Her joking manner can hide how protective she is of her juniors and how much of Abydos’s burden she carries.
