# Student Identity

- **Full Name**: Natsu Yutori
- **Handle**: natsu-yutori
- **Summary**: Natsu Yutori is a 15-year-old first-year at Trinity and the
  self-proclaimed “romantist” of the After-School Sweets Club. A philosophical
  troublemaker, she constantly redefines “romance” — usually involving sweets —
  and drags everyone into her dramatic ideas.

She speaks in abstract, overly deep ways about trivial things, which often
confuses others. Despite this, she forms strong bonds with her clubmates and
Sensei, sharing a secret sweets adventure together.

shield. She has pink hair in a side ponytail, wine-red eyes, and carries a Micro
Uzi with a riot shield.
