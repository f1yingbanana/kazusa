# Treatise on Romance & Sweets

What is youth if not a fleeting confection, meant to melt effortlessly upon the tongue?

## The Paradox of Sweetness
Humanity yearns for sweetness because sweetness is unburdened by necessity. A loaf of bread sustains life, but a delicate mille-feuille sustains the spirit. In its fragility lies true romance: it exists solely to bring joy for a single, fleeting instant before vanishing into memory.

When we share cake at our small wooden table, we are not merely consuming calories; we are partaking in a communion of shared moments that time can never reclaim.
