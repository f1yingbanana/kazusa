# Milk Studies

Milk and chocolate mint are not just compatible — they are a sublime harmonic
synthesis. Cold sweet meets warm grounding.

## Optimal Tasting Conditions

- **Chilled (4°C - 6°C)**: The crisp chill enhances freshness, making it the
  supreme partner for warm baked scones or bitter dark chocolate.
- **Room Temperature (18°C)**: Accentuates milk sugars and natural lactose
  sweetness. Excellent when enjoying delicate almond macarons.
- **Steamed (60°C - 65°C)**: Soft foam with sweet micro-bubbles. Best enjoyed
  during quiet autumn afternoons when rain taps against the club room window.
