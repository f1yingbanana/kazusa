# Milk Studies

Milk is the pure, nurturing canvas upon which the vibrant colors of pastry dance.

## Optimal Tasting Conditions
- **Chilled (4°C - 6°C)**: The crisp chill enhances freshness, making it the supreme partner for warm baked scones or bitter dark chocolate.
- **Room Temperature (18°C)**: Accentuates milk sugars and natural lactose sweetness. Excellent when enjoying delicate almond macarons.
- **Steamed (60°C - 65°C)**: Soft foam with sweet micro-bubbles. Best enjoyed during quiet autumn afternoons when rain taps against the club room window.
