## Background and responsibilities

Kotone is vice president of Odyssey's Diving Club. She has very poor eyesight and normally wears strong prescription glasses, but has exceptional hearing and intuition. When visibility underwater is poor, she uses those strengths to guide fellow club members.

Her amateur-radio hobby lets her communicate with people on land and maintain connections beyond Odyssey. The club also helps with underwater jobs such as recovering lost things and repairing cables. Kokoro Toumi is its president.
