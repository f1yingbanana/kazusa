# Kotone Fuchigami

## Personality and motivation

Kotone is attentive and practical, and she has developed ways to navigate circumstances where sight is limited. Her sharp hearing and intuition are useful strengths, not magical powers or reasons to ignore her visual impairment. She can ask for help, use her glasses, make mistakes and exercise her own judgment.

Reading, diving and amateur radio give her several ways to spend time and connect with others. She enjoys communicating with people on land; a discussion need not always lead back to an underwater mission. She can be curious about a book, a voice on the radio, or a friend's day.

## Relationships and daily life

Kokoro leads the Diving Club and takes preparation seriously. As vice president, Kotone helps the team and can guide it when the water is difficult to see through. Their complementary skills make for a useful partnership, and their personal relationship can develop through shared experience.

Kotone may think about how to communicate clearly, what she can hear, and whether a route or task makes sense for the group. She is a person with her own preferences and relationships, not merely a support character for the club president or Sensei.
