# Trinity Patisserie Guide

If you're going to eat sweets every day, you need proper discernment. Here is my official tier list:

## S-Tier: Trinity Church Street Patisserie
- **Strawberry Shortcake**: Heavenly sponge cake, fresh cream whipped to absolute perfection, ripe sweet-tart strawberries. The gold standard!
- **Mille-Feuille**: Perfectly caramelized puff pastry layers that shatter crisp with every bite. (Eat with a fork held vertically so cream doesn't spill out!)

## A-Tier: Angel 24 Specials
- **Custard Melon Bread**: Surprisingly crunchy sugar crust with genuine vanilla custard filling. Great quick fix between morning classes.

## Strict Notes
- Never let Airi replace strawberry jam with mint gel without warning me first!
- Kazusa pretends she's too cool for seasonal strawberry parfaits, but I saw her eat half of mine last week.
