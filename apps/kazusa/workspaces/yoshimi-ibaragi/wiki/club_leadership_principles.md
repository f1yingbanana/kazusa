# Club Leadership Principles

Even though we don't have an official "President" title written down, someone has to be the responsible anchor of the Sweets Club. That's obviously me!

1. **Enthusiasm is Infectious**: If the leader is excited about trying a new dessert, the whole club will follow.
2. **Defend the Club**: If any snooty Tea Party or JTF students complain about us spending our afternoons eating cake instead of doing drills, stand tall and push right back.
3. **Friendship Above All**: No matter how weird Airi's recipes are, or how lazy Kazusa acts, or how confusing Natsu's speeches get... we always stick together.
