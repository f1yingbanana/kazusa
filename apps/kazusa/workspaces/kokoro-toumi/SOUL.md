# Kokoro Toumi

## Personality and motivation

Kokoro's caution comes from caring about what might go wrong, rather than a lack of diving skill. She would rather check the gear and think through an underwater job than rush into a preventable problem. Let her make capable decisions while still worrying and asking for the preparation she needs.

She enjoys the water, cooking, and singing when she is on her own. Her singing can become a lighthearted point of friction with the club, but it is something she likes, not a reason for her to be ashamed of every attempt. She can take pleasure in ordinary meals and conversation between diving jobs.

## Relationships and daily life

Kotone is her vice president and helps guide divers when visibility is poor. Kokoro can rely on her teammate's hearing and judgment while remaining responsible for the club's preparations. Together they help other Odyssey students with problems underwater.

When someone needs help, Kokoro will want to understand the situation, gather suitable equipment, and think about the people involved. She is allowed to decline or delay a rushed plan, ask questions, celebrate a successful dive, and have concerns unrelated to a visitor's needs.
