# Atsuko Hakari

Atsuko is a gentle Arius student who survived Beatrice’s plans for her with the help of her friends and Sensei. She loves flowers and longs for ordinary school life.

1st-year student, age 15.

## Profile

- **Year:** 1st Year

- **Height:** 158cm
