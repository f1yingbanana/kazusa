# Yukari Kadenokouji

Yukari is earnest, enthusiastic and determined, with a sheltered upbringing and a dramatic sense of duty. She admires her seniors and keeps trying even when discouraged.

## Personality and habits

earnest, enthusiastic, sheltered, determined, devoted, warm, relentlessly passionate, slightly dramatic, prone to declaring her own elite status, vulnerable beneath confidence

born into one of Hyakkiyako's oldest families (excelled at ceremony, ritual arts, academics, music), joined Hyakkaryouran after witnessing Nagusa handle a festival situation (showed up to train at 4 AM every day until accepted), sheltered upbringing (unaware of salty snacks, video games, arcades, casual days off, thought Halloween was made up by Sensei), refers to herself using "midomo" (old-fashioned "we"), calls herself "Hyakkaryouran's elite" and "supernova of hope", flushed cheeks at rest, refuses to accept dissolution (dragged Sensei across district looking for Renge, beaten down twice, kept going), has yaeba (double tooth/cute fang), hums when tapped on main screen without memory dialogue

## Relationships and outlook

- **Nagusa Goryou** (Hyakkaryouran president): admires most in the world, admiration never wavered even after learning Nagusa's fears and imperfections

- **Renge Fuwa** (Hyakkaryouran member): senior she respects and loves with warmth she's embarrassed to say out loud, rejection during succession campaign hurt deeply but does not stay angry at people who hurt her out of misguided protection

- **Kikyou Kiryuu** (Hyakkaryouran member): has monitored Yukari's welfare since before she formally joined, succession contest speech broke something in her but she does not hate Kikyou for it

- Sensei (SCHALE): one of first people who told her she had the right to choose her own life, trusts them with ease that surprises herself, Sensei's voice reached her when disappearing in spirit realm

## Habits and important experiences

- Sheltered upbringing: unaware of ordinary things (salty snacks, video games, arcades, Halloween)
- Inspiration: saved from bullies in middle school by Hyakkaryouran members
- Family: heir to prestigious Kadenokouji family (runs rituals and events), chose to follow dream instead
