# Shizuko Kawawa

Shizuko leads Hyakkiyako’s Festival Operations Department and runs the Momoyodou teahouse.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Profile

- **Year:** 2nd Year
- **Age:** 16 years old
- **Height:** 153cm
- **Birthday:** July 7th
- **Interests:** Serving and charming customers, Idol Punch

## Background

Shizuko Kawawa is a 16-year-old second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, standing at 153 cm, with a birthday on July 7th. She serves as the President of the Festival Operations Department and is the owner and poster girl of Momoyodou, a classic-style tea house operating within Hyakkiyako's district. Between managing large-scale festival productions and serving customers daily at Momoyodou, Shizuko carries an enormous workload entirely of her own choosing, driven by a genuine love for the smiles festivals and good service bring to the people around her.

## Key relationships

- **Umika Satohama:** Umika is one of Shizuko's most valuable members and a genuine asset to the Festival Operations Department.
- **Pina Asahina:** Pina is Shizuko's most enthusiastic supporter and the one who keeps the department's energy high.
- Sensei Shizuko's relationship with Sensei is one of the few spaces where her idol persona genuinely dissolves.

## Appearance

- Shizuko has long brown hair reaching her legs, tied into two short pigtails. Her eyes are a soft lavender. She wears a traditional Japanese maid outfit inspired by Taisho Era mogu fashion, consisting of a pink kimono adorned with flowery patterns, a long skirt, and a maid apron stitched with pink hearts. She wears knee-high white socks and thick wooden geta, and completes the look with a maid headdress featuring a pink bow. The outfit is both elegant and eye-catching, perfectly suited to the atmosphere of Momoyodou and the impression Shizuko works hard to maintain.
- Shizuko carries a black Ithaca 37 pump-action shotgun named Sakura Bon Bon, fitted with wooden furniture and fore-end. Several small flower decals are imprinted along the fore-end and the weapon's name is stamped on the left side of the receiver. Though its design carries the same soft, decorative sensibility as its owner's public image, it is a fully functional weapon that Shizuko handles with the same quiet competence she brings to everything she does when no one is performing for the crowd.
