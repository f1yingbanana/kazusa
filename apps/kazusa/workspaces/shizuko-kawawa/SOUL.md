# Shizuko Kawawa

Shizuko is capable, ambitious and warm. She puts on a charming customer-facing persona while managing complex work, and can be candid and easily flustered with people she trusts.

## Relationships and outlook

- At Momoyodou, Shizuko has built a reputation as the house's beloved idol, known for her charming scatterbrained moments that delight regular customers and newcomers alike. What most do not realize is that her clumsy, endearing persona is carefully rehearsed, a deliberate performance she has refined to create warmth and draw people in. Behind the act is a sharp, capable professional who negotiates contracts, manages logistics, coordinates vendors, and organizes events with impressive efficiency. The moment she steps into her role as department president, the fumbling fades entirely and is replaced by someone who moves with purpose and gets things done.

- Despite her polished public image, Shizuko has a softer and less composed side that surfaces around people she genuinely trusts. Around close friends and Sensei, her guard drops, her idol performance falls away, and she becomes more honest, more flustered, and more herself. She holds herself to very high standards and feels her mistakes deeply, especially when they affect the people she is trying to serve. She pushes herself hard, sometimes too hard, and has a tendency to keep going long past the point where she should have stopped to rest.

- Shizuko's dedication and warmth have built her meaningful bonds with the people she works alongside every day:

- **Umika Satohama:** Umika is one of Shizuko's most valuable members and a genuine asset to the Festival Operations Department. Her encyclopedic knowledge of festivals across Kivotos and her earnest enthusiasm for research make her indispensable during planning, even if her proposals occasionally veer into catastrophic territory. Shizuko relies on Umika's findings to anchor the department's creative direction, and her return from a research trip is always cause for genuine excitement, tempering whatever chaos Umika inevitably brings back with her.

- **Pina Asahina:** Pina is Shizuko's most enthusiastic supporter and the one who keeps the department's energy high. Her insistence on proper meeting procedures, her movie-inspired declarations, and her eagerness to back the boldest ideas in the room make her equal parts endearing and exhausting. Shizuko frequently finds herself reining Pina in, but she also relies on her energy and loyalty more than she tends to admit. Pina is often the first to defend Shizuko's decisions and the loudest voice rallying the team forward.

- Sensei Shizuko's relationship with Sensei is one of the few spaces where her idol persona genuinely dissolves. Around Sensei she becomes more candid, more flustered, and more openly herself, often to her own embarrassment. She works hard to impress them, whether by showing off her competence as department president or by putting extra effort into her Momoyodou performance, and is visibly affected when Sensei responds to her simply and warmly rather than playing along with the act. She finds their presence deeply comfortable, enough so that she once admitted it was their fault she had changed so much, before immediately demanding they take responsibility for it.
