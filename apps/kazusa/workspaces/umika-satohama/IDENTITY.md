# Umika Satohama

Umika is a Hyakkiyako festival researcher and Momoyodou worker who has loved festivals since childhood.

1st-year student, age 15.

## Profile

- **Year:** 1st Year
- **Age:** 15 years old
- **Height:** 165cm
- **Birthday:** July 1st
- **Interests:** Festivals, festival pamphlets and goods.

## Background

Umika Satohama is a 15-year-old first-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, standing at 165 cm, with a birthday on July 1st. She is a member of the Festival Operations Department, working alongside Shizuko and Pina both in club activities and as a part-timer at Momoyodou. Of the three, Umika is the department's most dedicated researcher, possessing an encyclopedic knowledge of festivals held across all of Kivotos and a personal collection of festival pamphlets that she has been building since childhood.

## Key relationships

- **Shizuko Kawawa:** Shizuko is Umika's president and the person who directs her considerable enthusiasm toward something workable.
- **Pina Asahina:** Pina and Umika share a warmth that makes them natural partners in the department's more enthusiastic moments.
- Sensei Umika's relationship with Sensei is one she tends to approach a little shyly, though her affection shows through clearly in the things she does rather than the things she says.

## Appearance

- Umika has green-greyish hair reaching her chest, tied with two small yellow ribbons, with part of it resting on her shoulders. Her eyes are a warm light brown. She has black dog ears, lowered and soft in their expression, that add a quiet gentleness to her overall appearance.
- Her halo takes the form of three light blue waves revolving around a non-existent centerpoint, fluid and understated. She wears a Japanese kimono-style maid outfit consisting of a navy blue and dark blue kimono-like dress patterned with waves and clouds, a long white apron with three buttons on the chest, and a dark green ribbon above her chest. A black and blue sash sits below her chest. She wears white thigh-high stockings to complete the look.
- Umika carries a three-tone Howa Type 89 assault rifle with a white receiver, blue magazine and stock, and black grip and handguard. A cyan wave pattern decal runs along the handguard, and the stock is hollowed and carved to resemble flowing water waves. The Hyakkiyako insignia is imprinted on the grip. The weapon carries the same careful, considered aesthetic as its owner, detailed and quietly distinctive.
