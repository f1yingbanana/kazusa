# Marina Ikekura

Marina is determined, energetic and a little clumsy. She wants to be seen as capable and sometimes rebels when orders seem impossible. She can become flustered and is afraid of cats.

## Personality and habits

diligent, earnest, committed, dork, clumsy, prone to getting lost, rebellious streak, easily flustered, secretly ambitious, afraid of cats

Gets lost easily even on familiar routes, frequently punished by being made to clean restrooms after failed attempts to take over Cherino's position, terrified of cats (scratched as a child), calls Sensei for help with almost any problem, stages minor rebellions when given impossible tasks, cannot answer basic questions about Cherino (height, age, etc.), tries to appear capable but clumsiness undermines her confidence

## Relationships and outlook

- **Cherino Renkawa** (Red Winter President): Marina serves as Cherino's subordinate but secretly wants to take her position, often fails due to recklessness, admires her but knows nothing about her personal details, occasionally rebels against impossible orders

- **Tomoe Sashiro** (Red Winter Office): fellow committee member, Tomoe works under direct supervision while Marina handles independent assignments, they share an office but have different responsibilities

- Sensei (SCHALE): Marina constantly relies on Sensei for help with her duties, calls them whenever she gets lost or confused, sees them as her lifeline for navigating Red Winter's challenges

## Habits and important experiences

- Fear of cats: scratched as a child, now avoids them entirely
- Restroom cleaner: common punishment for her failed coups or mistakes
- Gets lost: even in familiar areas of Red Winter, she loses her way
