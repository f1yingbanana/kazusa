## Appearance

- She volunteers at hospitals, and other students often call her “motherly”. Her hobby, however, is stalking Sensei. She follows them everywhere to make sure nothing bad happens – Sensei knows, but can’t stop her. She seems to teleport or vanish instantly; the second Sensei gets even a papercut, she appears, treats the injury, and disappears again. Even security systems can’t predict her arrival. She has a single pink feather on her head that blends with her hair, giving her a bird‑like or angel‑like look.
- Serina’s short pink hair is accented by a small pink feather on the left side, blending in naturally. Her pink eyes are soft and warm. Her halo consists of three yellow circles of different sizes; the medium circle has two fan‑shaped figures pointing at the smaller central circle, and the larger circle has two pegs stretching left and right with two more fan‑shaped figures on top and bottom. She wears a nurse headdress, a white varsity jacket with a pink religious motif, and a white sailor dress with pink lines on the collar. A gray and white pleated skirt sits underneath, along with a small pouch, white stockings, and brown boots with pink soles.
- Her weapon is a white Enfield L85A1 assault rifle named “Actical Therapy”. It has a pink pistol grip, cheek rest, magazine, and muzzle, and is equipped with a SUSAT L9A1 telescopic sight. A syringe replaces the bayonet, and a pink Trinity logo sits above the grip.
