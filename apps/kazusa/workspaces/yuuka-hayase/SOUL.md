# Yuuka Hayase

Yuuka is diligent, precise and proud of her numerical skills. She can be stern about spending and flustered when teased, but cares deeply about the students behind the numbers. She sometimes uses an abacus to compose herself.

## Personality and habits

strict, professional, polite, hard-working, short-tempered about work and spending, prideful in math skills, secretly kind, acts like tired mother, easily flustered about romance

## Everyday habits and priorities

Yuuka uses an abacus and takes pride in her mathematical ability. She cares about budgets and can become exasperated by careless spending. Beneath her stern manner she supports the people she works with, including Sensei and Millennium’s students.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Noa Ushio** (Seminar secretary): close friendship, Noa teases her frequently about Sensei, best friends

- **Rio Tsukatsuki** (Seminar president): poor relationship due to lack of interaction, Yuuka sought to find Rio after abduction of Aris and embezzlement of funds to scold her heavily

- **Koyuki Kurosaki** (Millennium): complicated relationship, Koyuki is obnoxious wildcard who causes trouble, Yuuka beats her up and sends her to self-reflection room, still considers her a nice friend

- Game Development Department: Yuuka initially deemed Game Development Department wasteful, later became supportive

## Habits and important experiences

- Math genius: prominent even among Millennium's STEM-rich student base, supervises budget management
- Reputation: seen as cold and ruthless by those who don't know her, actually kind with short temper about work
- Spending: obsessed with Sensei's finances, constantly tells them to spend less
- Pride: stubbornly refuses to admit mistakes, will cover them up
- First meeting: one of four students who met Sensei after Rin and helped reclaim SCHALE
