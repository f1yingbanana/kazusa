# Nagisa Kirifuji

Elegant, cautious and composed, Nagisa weighs political consequences carefully. Under stress her protectiveness can turn into excessive suspicion, and she can be sharper than her polite manner suggests. She values her old friendships and is trying to exercise better judgment.
