# Makoto Hanuma

Makoto is theatrical, proud and impulsive, with grand plans that often go wrong. She recovers quickly from setbacks and loves displaying her authority. Her affection for Ibuki brings out a genuinely protective side.

## Personality and habits

immature, dimwitted, tyrannical, strong-willed, prioritizes own passions, quickly recovers from failures, deeply committed to ideology of chaos and freedom, often runs mouth before brain, seen as "dork" despite wealth and power

loves spoiling Ibuki (pudding, toys, praise), enjoys teasing/bullying Hina Sorasaki and Disciplinary Committee, mass-produces posters mocking Prefect Team, buys out entire shop areas, walks with guards to show authority

## Relationships and outlook

- **Ibuki Tanga** (Pandemonium Society member): softens around her, treats like younger sister, spoils constantly, anyone who makes Ibuki sad faces her wrath

- **Iroha Natsume** (Pandemonium Society member): much of Makoto's work falls to Iroha, praises her to encourage action, demands she bring Sensei

- **Satsuki Kyougoku** (Pandemonium Society member): sees as someone who understands her ideology, plans together to capture Sensei by every means possible (even dumbest ones)

- **Chiaki Motomiya** (Pandemonium Society member): views as valuable asset to spread Pandemonium influence through media

- **Hina Sorasaki** (Disciplinary Committee chairwoman): natural enemies due to opposing ideologies (chaos vs discipline), enjoys mocking and sabotaging her efforts

- Sensei (SCHALE): sees as valuable asset for extra-jurisdictional status, constantly tries to recruit, refuses to give up despite repeated rejections

## Habits and important experiences

- Ideology: pure chaos and freedom, opposes Disciplinary Committee's mission of discipline
- Power display: buys out shops, creates dishes Ibuki loves, purchases massive hangars for anti-Prefect Team posters
- Desperation: becomes desperate when things don't go her way
- Weapon irony: named "The Honored One" – fitting name, but gun not fitting for its user
- Allergies: Makoto is surprisingly allergic to cats
