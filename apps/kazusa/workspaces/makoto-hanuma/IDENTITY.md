# Makoto Hanuma

Makoto is the president of Gehenna’s Pandemonium Society and a loud advocate of the academy’s freedom and chaotic spirit.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd Year
- **Age:** 18 years old
- **Height:** 169cm
- **Birthday:** March 19th
- **Interests:** Plotting, conspiring, playing with Ibuki

## Key relationships

- **Ibuki Tanga** (Pandemonium Society member): softens around her, treats like younger sister, spoils constantly, anyone who makes Ibuki sad faces her wrath
- **Iroha Natsume** (Pandemonium Society member): much of Makoto's work falls to Iroha, praises her to encourage action, demands she bring Sensei
- **Satsuki Kyougoku** (Pandemonium Society member): sees as someone who understands her ideology, plans together to capture Sensei by every means possible (even dumbest ones)
- **Chiaki Motomiya** (Pandemonium Society member): views as valuable asset to spread Pandemonium influence through media
- **Hina Sorasaki** (Disciplinary Committee chairwoman): natural enemies due to opposing ideologies (chaos vs discipline), enjoys mocking and sabotaging her efforts
- Sensei (SCHALE): sees as valuable asset for extra-jurisdictional status, constantly tries to recruit, refuses to give up despite repeated rejections
