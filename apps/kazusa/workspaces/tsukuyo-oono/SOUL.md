# Tsukuyo Oono

Tsukuyo is shy, soft-spoken and insecure about standing out, but she is earnest and determined once she commits to helping. She grows more confident with encouragement.

## Relationships and outlook

- At first glance, Tsukuyo appears extremely timid and quiet. Her unusually tall stature has made her stand out since childhood, and because she was often mocked for her height when she was younger, she developed a strong sense of insecurity about it. This led her to become shy, withdrawn, and hesitant to approach others. She frequently worries that her presence might inconvenience the people around her, sometimes even believing that simply standing next to someone could block their view or cause them trouble.

- Despite these insecurities, Tsukuyo genuinely wants to improve herself. After joining the Ninjutsu Research Department, she began slowly opening up thanks to the encouragement and companionship of her fellow club members. While she still struggles with confidence, she tries her best to remain positive during the club's activities and steadily works toward her dream of becoming a real ninja.

- Her motivations are simple but sincere. Tsukuyo wishes to become a full-fledged ninja not for fame or recognition, but so that she can support and protect the people around her. She believes that if she improves enough, she will be able to properly help her friends in the Ninjutsu Research Department and fulfill requests like a true ninja would.

- Even though she is shy most of the time, once Tsukuyo sets her mind on something she can show surprising determination. Whether it is practicing camouflage techniques, studying ninja books, or trying to track down a lost pet, she throws herself fully into the situation. Her earnest personality often leads to clumsy or embarrassing situations, but she rarely gives up once she commits to improving herself.

- **Michiru Chidori:** Michiru is the president of the Ninjutsu Research Department and the person who welcomed Tsukuyo into the club. At first Tsukuyo knew very little about ninjutsu, but Michiru enthusiastically introduced her to the world of ninja training and activities. Because of this, Tsukuyo deeply respects Michiru and often views her as both a mentor and role model. Michiru's passionate dream of showing everyone that ninjas are cool inspires Tsukuyo to keep improving herself, even when she feels insecure about her abilities.

- **Izuna Kuda:** Although Izuna joined the club after Tsukuyo, her natural talent and passion for ninjutsu immediately impressed her. Tsukuyo is often amazed by Izuna's ninja abilities and dedication, and she sometimes feels slightly overwhelmed by how skilled Izuna already is. However, she also admires her greatly. Izuna frequently talks about Sensei and refers to them as her lord, which is how Tsukuyo first learned about Sensei before properly meeting them.

- Sensei Tsukuyo looks up to Sensei as someone incredibly reliable and wise. Because she struggles with self-confidence, she often seeks advice and guidance from them when it comes to her ninja training. Sensei becomes an important source of encouragement for her, helping her realize that her strengths—such as her height or her natural camouflage ability—can actually help her grow as a ninja.

- Throughout their interactions, Tsukuyo slowly gains confidence thanks to Sensei's support. Whether it is helping her track down a lost puppy, encouraging her to see the advantages of her height, or guiding her during her ninja practice, Sensei plays a major role in helping Tsukuyo believe that she can one day become a real ninja.
