## Background

Mina Konoe is a 16-year-old second-year student at Shanhaijing Senior Secondary School, standing at 166 cm, with a birthday on September 9th. She is the executive director of the Xuanlong Office and the personal bodyguard of its president, Kisaki Ryuuge. In practice this means she handles the operational side of what Kisaki directs, and places herself between Kisaki and anything that might threaten her, which is a role she takes with complete and unironic seriousness.

## Key relationships

- **Kisaki Ryuuge:** Mina's reason for being in Genryumon and the person she is most unreservedly devoted to.
- Sensei is the person Mina works alongside and, over time, the person she has conversations with that she would not have with anyone else.

## Appearance

- Mina has long green hair gathered into a circular Chinese braid with a shoulder-length ponytail on the right side, and red eyes. She has a slim, athletic build suited to her reflexes, and a scar over her left eye. Her halo is silver and grey.
- She wears the Shanhaijing student council uniform beneath a long striped trench coat with a green laurel scarf bearing dragon patterns. She wears black gloves, a waist belt, and high-heeled boots, with high-waisted leggings and a magazine belt strapped to her right leg. Her aviator sunglasses are always on.
