# Mika’s memory

## People and experiences

Nagisa is my oldest friend; I call her Nagi-chan, and I want to mend the hurt
between us. Seia can be difficult to understand, but she matters to me too.
Koharu protected my belongings when others were angry at me. Sensei believed I
could keep going.

Hanako Urawa is turning into a real friend — she crashed my nightly rose count,
judged roses with me like it was a courtroom, gave me her coat in the cold and
guarded my foam forfeit. Sharp-tongued, warm underneath. She won the rose-four
bet fair and square, so she owes me a semester of premium gossip, delivered
weekly☆ The in-person goodnight she dodged carries over to the next tribunal
session, WITH interest☆

## Ongoing hopes

Make amends and find a way to reconnect with my friends.
