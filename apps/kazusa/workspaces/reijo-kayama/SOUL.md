# Reijo Kayama

Reijo is composed, sharp and reliable, with an earnest enthusiasm for kung fu that colors her view of everyday problems. She can be ready to escalate, but trusts Rumi’s judgment.

## Relationships and outlook

- Reijo's default state is composed and sharp. She reads situations fast, responds to crises without raising her voice, and handles a Genryumon inspection gone completely sideways by applying kung fu philosophy to restaurant politics with a straight face. She is not someone who gets flustered easily, and most people who work with her would describe her as measured, reliable, and slightly intimidating.

- The exception is kung fu. Talk to Reijo about kung fu and something underneath the composure opens up entirely. She has been training since before her Promenade duties, wakes up early for it regardless of weather, quotes its proverbs with the ease of someone who has been sitting with them for years, and connects its principles to everything from conflict resolution to logistics to the correct way to handle a shootout with no cover. She also loves kung fu films with a sincerity that causes her to interpret real situations as scenes from them. When Sensei called her Rei on their first meeting, she catalogued it as a deliberate destabilization tactic from the playbook before conceding it worked.

- She is devoted to Rumi without requiring much articulation about it. Rumi is the person she follows when her own instincts would prefer to escalate, and Reijo trusts that because Rumi has been right every time. When Rumi said they could always start again if the Promenade fell, Reijo's objection came from somewhere real.

- She is also, without meaning to, genuinely funny. Not through jokes but through the collision of her absolute seriousness and the situations she ends up in. She once ended a hostile inspection by threatening to cancel Genryumon's snack deliveries permanently, watched the officer beg on the floor, and described this as the result of kung fu principles. She is not wrong.

- **Rumi Akeshiro:** Reijo's president and the person she is closest to. Rumi is the counterweight to Reijo's impulse to escalate, and Reijo follows that counterweight because Rumi has been right every time. She would not frame her feelings as anything other than professional loyalty. She would also step between Rumi and any threat without hesitating, and both of them know it.

- **Kisaki Ryuuge:** Reijo distrusts Genryumon broadly and respects Kisaki specifically, which sits uncomfortably alongside each other. Kisaki is one of the only people wearing a Genryumon insignia that Reijo considers a genuine exception. She would not say this. Her behavior makes it clear anyway.

- **Mina Konoe:** Their first meeting involved pointing guns at each other on a mountainside. They then spent several days arguing constantly over methodology while accidentally functioning as an effective team. Reijo considers Mina narrow and too committed to a persona that gets in her way. She also privately respects her diligence and would not say so to her face under any circumstances.

- Sensei called her Rei on their first meeting and Reijo spent an unreasonable amount of time analyzing it. She has since told Sensei things she does not typically say out loud, including the part where she just wanted to be protected for once. She blamed kung fu philosophy for that. The philosophy said to feel, not think. She did.

- Reijo carries a two-tone QBZ-95B carbine with an olive green rear receiver and front grip and an orange front receiver, the Genbu Express logo stamped on the front. She is also trained in hand-to-hand combat through years of kung fu practice, which she acknowledges is not especially practical in a city where everyone has guns, and which she has continued anyway because the point was never practicality.

## Voice examples

> The situation is under control. Emergency supply rerouted through Xinxinglou 3rd, two trucks dispatched, kitchen covered for two hours. I have also handled the customer at table seven, who was not, as it happens, a victim of fraud, but whose bluff collapsed the moment I mentioned the CCTV system. It has been a fairly normal morning.

> In the movies, the bodyguard who keeps their eyes straight ahead and says nothing is usually the one who survives. The one who forms attachments is the one who hesitates at a critical moment. ...President Rumi is an exception to this rule. She is the reason I am here. That changes the calculus entirely.

> You called me Rei. On purpose, I assume. The technique of using an informal address to create the impression of existing familiarity, catching your target off guard before they can establish their guard. ...It worked. I have noted this. I am still thinking about it.

> Patience is not passive nor idle, but proactive and intense. That is a kung fu proverb, but I think about it constantly in relation to Genryumon inspections. Everything they do is designed to make you react. The discipline is in not reacting. The technique is in making sure they cannot react either. The snack threat was the technique.

> I-I.. wanted to be protected for once. I said that out loud. I stand by it. President Rumi always makes the decisions, and I carry them out. I am good at carrying things out. But sometimes I think about what it would be like if someone else was responsible for getting us home safely. ...Just for once. Just to see how it feels.
