# Niya Amachi

Niya leads Hyakkiyako’s Yin-Yang Club, an influential organization in the alliance’s decentralized politics.

## Profile

- **Year:** 3rd Year
- **Age:** 17 years old
- **Height:** 166cm
- **Birthday:** January 1st
- **Interests:** Doting on underclassmen, scheming

## Background

Niya Amachi is the president of the Yin-Yang Club at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy. On paper she holds one of the most significant positions in the academy, responsible for mediating conflicts and maintaining harmony across a district built on the uneasy coexistence of dozens of clubs and factions. In practice, she delegates most of it to Kaho and spends the rest of her time engineering situations she finds entertaining.

## Key relationships

- **Kaho Kuwakami:** Kaho is Niya's vice president and the person who ensures the club functions while Niya is otherwise occupied.
- **Chise Waraku:** Chise is a fellow Yin-Yang Club member whose detached, unhurried presence creates an atmosphere that even Niya finds comfortable.
- Niya often calls on Sensei, even when her requests are mostly an excuse to spend time together.

## Appearance

- Niya has short, scruffy dark hair that fades into a bluish-gray tone, with straight-cut bangs. She wears a subtle red makeup accent around her eyes. Two horns sit on her head, orange at the base and fading to red at the tips, with her left horn broken halfway. She has gold O-shaped earrings and a mole beneath her left eye.
- Her halo is a light white consisting of a central circle surrounded by four concentric circles with symmetrical cross-like extensions and four spikes emanating from the gaps between them, all set close together.
- She wears a white side-less kimono top with detached sleeves decorated with Japanese-style line patterns, small circles, and loose yellow paint-like markings. The right side of the kimono ends at the waist while the left covers half of her black pleated skirt, which has a yellow line running along its edges. She wears a white high thigh-high on her right leg and a matching white sock on her left, along with traditional Japanese okobo.
- Niya carries a Nambu Type 14 pistol named Forked Tongue, finished in two-tone metallic white with black accents. The rear post sight and trigger are painted dark pink. The Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy insignia is imprinted on the black polymer grip. Only a handful of people have ever seen her actually fire it. She also carries a fan that she deploys at moments of particular composure, which is most moments.
