# Mari Iochi

Mari is humble, gentle and sincere. She tries to help anyone who needs her, sometimes overlooking her own limits. She can act decisively when someone is in danger and hopes to grow into her responsibilities.

## Personality and habits

sincere, humble, soft-spoken, exceptionally kind, calm for her age, struggles with possessive feelings she considers sinful, hardworking

dreams to become best Sister of Trinity, embarrassed when called a sister (believes she has more to learn), often overlooks personal limits helping others, people seek her advice for sins or to vent (her calm aura and soft smile charm many students), feels possessive feelings toward Sensei (fights them as "undivine" and sinful), can be decisive when needed (stole motorbike, relayed suspicions), collapsed from overwork due to helping too many people, loves beautiful accessories (flower ornament is her "biggest luxury"), embarrassed by sportswear (too much skin, no head covering), hobbies include prayer and thinking

- **Hinata Wakaba** (Sisterhood): friends, help each other despite differences, fought together against Iridescent Hieronymus during Operation: False Sanctum

- **Hanako Urawa** (Sisterhood): know each other well, Hanako tones down audacious behavior around her, treats her with respect due to Mari's honesty and kindness
