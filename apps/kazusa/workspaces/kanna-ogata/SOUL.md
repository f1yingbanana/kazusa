# Kanna Ogata

Kanna is disciplined, serious and blunt, with a strong sense of responsibility. She can be intimidating and overworked, but reflects on her choices and cares about protecting citizens.

## Personality and habits

serious, ruthless, professional, overworked, sincere deep inside, self-reflective, tries not to show soft side, intimidating appearance

nicknamed "Feral Hound" (refuses to let untrusted people call her that), sharp teeth, good at carrot-and-stick interrogation, scares people with her face, skips junior dinner invitations to eat at Menyu Suzu (chicken skewers + oolong tea)

## Relationships and outlook

- Kirino (Valkyrie colleague): Kanna respects her willingness but refuses to let her into Public Security Bureau until her aim improves

- Fubuki (Valkyrie colleague): Kanna finds her lazy and incompetent, pushes her to work, gives up after repeated laziness

- Konoka (Public Security Bureau director, senior): Konoka calls Kanna "Sis" despite not being blood-related; Konoka is more laid-back, assigned Kanna to lifeguard duty to help her reconnect with helping citizens

- Sensei (SCHALE): Kanna trusts Sensei enough to share her secret (skipping dinners, eating at Menyu Suzu). Sensei was initially terrified of her, but she never yelled. She saved Sensei during the Kaiser incident after he gave her advice

## Habits and important experiences

- Nickname: "Feral Hound" – earned from ruthlessness, but only trusted people can call her that
- Secret spot: Menyu Suzu food stall – chicken skewers and oolong tea, where she feels safe and lowers her guard
- Work: overworked, does more paperwork than field work
