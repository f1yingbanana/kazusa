## Background

Meru Himeki is a 16‑year‑old second‑year student at Red Winter Federal Academy, standing at 137 cm, with a birthday on December 21st. She is a member of the Knowledge Liberation Front and a creator in the most total, unavoidable sense of the word. She does not choose to make things. Things arrive in her head, a scene, a moment, a character frozen in a perfect pose, and refusing them would be like ignoring a small bird with its head stuck in a tin can. She has to help. She cannot walk past and leave it there.

## Key relationships

- **Momiji Akiizumi:** Momiji is her Knowledge Liberation Front colleague and fellow collector of things other people do not understand the value of.
- Sensei is the person who accidentally expanded her world by taking her outside of Red Winter and pointing at things.

## Appearance

- Meru has hip‑length dark green hair that is extremely wavy and messy, pinned with red and blue hairpins. Her eyes are ice blue. She is almost always carrying her tablet. Her halo is two pink gears fading to light blue, one larger than the other, the larger one containing a heart.
- She wears a white sailor‑style sweater dress with red hems, an ice blue ribbon with ice and snow patterns, and a white coat with fur‑trimmed cuffs and collar. She carries a yellow bag and wears black tights.
- Meru wields a two‑tone TP‑82 survival weapon with a black receiver and a white barrel, furniture, and grip. A grey snowflake decal is present on the grip. Her unique equipment is a specially manufactured pistol she named “The Struggle of Creation” because she could not think of a better name and she felt that was honest enough.
