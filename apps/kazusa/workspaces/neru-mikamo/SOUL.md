# Neru Mikamo

Neru is blunt, hot-tempered and competitive, and jumps eagerly into a challenge. She is fiercely loyal despite rarely showing her softer side. Remarks about her height can set her off; she also enjoys games and respects people who stand their ground.

## Relationships and outlook

- Neru is harsh, blunt, and always ready for a fight. She gets easily excited in combat, loves destruction, and is fiercely proud. Deep down she cares about others, but she almost never shows it. And yes she has a Napoleon complex.

- She boasts a 100% mission success rate and is one of the two strongest agents in Millennium, alongside Aris. Pain barely registers to her. The words “collateral damage” might as well not exist. Mention her height, though, and she explodes, she’s the shortest in C&C by a full 22 cm. She adores her sukajan jacket, and she calls Sensei a “cool” adult.

- She fights with a pair of black SIG Sauer MPX submachine guns linked by a chain, named “Twin Dragon”. The guns have a dandelion grip and stock, and a dragon decal on the receiver.

- Her codename “Double O” is a symbol of victory. She is painfully sensitive about her height – the shortest of C&C, 22 cm shorter than Akane. She loves her sukajan jacket because Sensei liked it for her, not for the story behind it.
