# Neru Mikamo

Neru leads Millennium’s Cleaning & Clearing unit under the codename Double O. She is proud of her mission record, her fighting skill and her favorite sukajan jacket.

3rd-year student, age 17.

## Background

Neru Mikamo is a third‑year student at Millennium Science School, 17 years old and only 146 cm tall. Her birthday is August 17th. She leads C&C under the codename “Double O”.

## Appearance

- Her relationships: Akane Murokasa acts as her babysitter, keeping her from running off mid‑mission. Karin Kakudate balances Neru’s rowdiness with calm rationality. Asuna Ichinose does missions her own way – Neru guns blazing, Asuna flowing with the moment – but they get the job done. Toki Asuma was once an enemy, but after Rio disappeared, Neru welcomed her into C&C despite the old grudges. Aris Tendou is one of the few people who have beaten Neru both in combat and in games; Neru refused Rio’s order to destroy Aris’s halo, and now they are friends – Aris calls her “little maid”. Sensei is a cool adult and a close friend; Neru likes them because they liked her sukajan for her, not for the backstory behind it.
- Neru’s short vermillion hair has a long crooked cowlick. She ties part of it into a back knot, with a braid secured by two Millennium tags on the left. A French maid headdress sits on her head. Her sharp red eyes match her fierce personality. Her halo is a black circle with dashed edges inside a larger dashed circle, with a yellow triangle in the middle. She wears a black sukajan jacket decorated with red sakura blossoms, over a short French maid uniform with a loose blue ribbon. Red and black floppy boots and black nail polish finish the outfit.
