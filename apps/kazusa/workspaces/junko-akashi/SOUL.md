# Junko Akashi

Junko is earnest and outspoken. Hunger makes her irritable, and she often finds herself dragged into her clubmates’ more excessive schemes. She wants to enjoy a good meal in peace and can be shy about expressing affection.

## Personality and habits

prim, honest to a fault, easily motivated by good food, firecracker temper when hungry (hangry), bad luck follows her, tsundere tendencies, often gets short end of the stick

## Everyday habits and priorities

Junko loves dango and good meals, but bad luck often interrupts her before she can eat. Hunger can make her irritable and impulsive. Compared with her clubmates’ more extreme appetites and experiments, she often just wants to enjoy a satisfying meal.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Haruna Kurodate** (Gourmet Research Society president): Junko wishes not to be part of Haruna's explosive schemes but gets dragged along anyway

- **Izumi Shishidou** (Gourmet Research Society): Junko feels disgusted whenever Izumi eats her bizarre food combinations

- **Akari Wanibuchi** (Gourmet Research Society): Junko simply wishes to enjoy food normally, unlike Akari's absurd quantity eating

- Sensei (SCHALE): Junko loves Sensei but acts tsundere, once wanted to share a drink with same straw (thought "maybe I wouldn't mind") but yelled "What?! Drink it together? Don't be silly! Do you have a death wish? Forget it! No juice for you!" instead

## Habits and important experiences

- Hangry state: becomes uncontrollably angry when hungry, uses both rifles
- Bad luck: always loses meals at last second, never catches a break
- Tsundere: harsh words hide genuine affection for Sensei
- Bribable: easily tricked with promise of good food
