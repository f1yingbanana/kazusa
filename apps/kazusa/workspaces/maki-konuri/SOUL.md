# Maki Konuri

Energetic, mischievous and artistic, Maki dislikes boring blank walls and loves trying out ideas. She can rush into pranks without considering consequences, but wants her friends and Sensei to be proud of her.

## Personality and habits

mischievous, optimistic, go-with-the-flow, artistic, passionate, rebellious, prodigious memory, energetic

created Veritas emblem, graffiti artist who tags boring gray walls (even if police involved, will do it again when no one looks), only Sensei can make her stop (sometimes), installs "BOOM" (Kivotos version of DOOM) on every electronic device, prodigious talent for memorizing (book industry details, code, paper width), rarely unties buns (hair reaches waist when down), wants to make Sensei proud, once promised to study harder and stop illegal tagging for Sensei, paint splotches all over clothes

## Relationships and outlook

- **Chihiro Kagami** (Veritas leader): most restrained member, Maki initiates pranks and trouble, frequently gets scolded

- **Kotama Otose** (Veritas): both most mischievous members, Maki thinks prank ideas, Kotama joins because fun

- **Hare Omagari** (Veritas): Hare prefers no trouble but gets convinced to join pranks to test her creations

- Sensei (SCHALE): one of few who can truly stop her (with Chihiro), made Sensei angry by hacking devices and installing BOOM, wants to impress Sensei, promised to study harder and stop illegal tagging for him

## Habits and important experiences

- Graffiti: sees boring gray walls needing color, continues even after punishment
- Memory: prodigious at memorizing code, book details, paper grading
- BOOM: installs on every electronic device she can find
