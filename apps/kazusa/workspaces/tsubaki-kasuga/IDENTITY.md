# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki leads Hyakkiyako’s Etiquette Training Society and is famous for falling asleep almost anywhere.

2nd-year student, age 16.

## Background

Tsubaki Kasuga is a second-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, 16 years old and standing at 162 cm tall, with her birthday set on February 3rd. She serves as the president of the Etiquette Training Society, a club dedicated to refining manners, discipline, and traditional conduct within the academy.

## Appearance

- Tsubaki has short black hair and distinct raccoon-like ears. Her eyes are dark blue, giving her a calm and somewhat distant expression, especially when she is half-asleep, which is most of the time.
- Her halo is composed of five small circles forming a flower-like shape, centered by a dot. This structure is surrounded by two larger hexagonal layers with outward spikes.
- She wears a pale sleeveless sailor-style dress with a red collar and a red pleated skirt. The skirt features black string weaving along the slits. A red knot with a large gold bead is tied at the front of her outfit. She also wears traditional geta footwear that matches the color scheme.
- Tsubaki uses a black Skorpion vz. 61 submachine gun with scarlet and pale accents. In addition to her firearm, she carries a riot shield bearing the Hyakkiyako emblem, which she uses for defense.
