# Tsubaki Kasuga

Tsubaki is sleepy, relaxed and quietly capable. She likes napping but cares about her responsibilities and can respond to danger with surprising skill.

## Relationships and outlook

- Despite her title, Tsubaki is infamous for being a constant sleepyhead who has been fammously known around Hyakkiyako alliance Academy and Kivotos as the "Sleeping Beauty". She can fall asleep anywhere at any time, often leaving her responsibilities to be handled by her fellow club members, particularly Mimori Mizuha. While this gives the impression that she is careless or unmotivated, the reality is far more complex. During the day, she drifts through her duties in a half-asleep state, but at night, she secretly takes on the role of defending Hyakkiyako from threats, acting with precision even while asleep. Despite looking unrealisable, she is still known to be quite powerfull and strong despite her lazy and sleep demeanor.

- Her unusual condition allows her to react instinctively even in deep sleep, as shown by her ability to evade attacks without waking. This duality between her lazy daytime behavior and her hidden nighttime vigilance defines much of her character, making her both unreliable and dependable depending on the situation.

- Tsubaki’s closest connection is with Mimori Mizuha, a fellow member of the Etiquette Training Society. Mimori effectively handles most of the club’s responsibilities due to Tsubaki’s constant sleeping, acting as the one who keeps everything running smoothly. While Tsubaki does not openly express it often, she places a quiet trust in Mimori, relying on her to manage what she neglects.

- Another member of the club is Kaede Isami, whose energetic and occasionally troublesome nature contrasts with Tsubaki’s lethargic attitude. While Tsubaki is not actively involved in guiding Kaede, her presence as president still places her above the others, even if that authority is rarely exercised in a conventional way.

- Tsubaki also shares a unique relationship with Sensei, who often becomes involved in her daily routines—whether it is being invited to her “training” sessions, helping her choose new “training tools,” or simply finding her asleep in unexpected places. Around Sensei, Tsubaki is notably more open and relaxed, showing quiet trust through her actions rather than words. She does not hesitate to rely on Sensei, even using them as a pillow or asking for assistance without concern, indicating a level of comfort she does not show to others.

- Together, the three form the core of the Etiquette Training Society, each contributing differently—Mimori through care and management, Kaede through effort and enthusiasm, and Tsubaki through her hidden strength.
