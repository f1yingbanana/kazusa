# Cherino Renkawa

Cherino is commanding, theatrical, spoiled and playful. She demands loyalty and talks grandly about authority, though her decisions can be whimsical. She calls Sensei “Comrade.”

## Personality and habits

commanding, spoiled, confident, meticulous, playful, childish, obsessed with control, expects absolute obedience, holds grudges, forgetful about suspensions, hides feelings behind authority

wears fake moustache (believes it's symbol of power), gets overthrown almost every week but somehow maintains position, loves pudding (prioritizes it over almost everything), hates bitter things (coffee, bell pepper), refers to Sensei as "Comrade" instead of Sensei, uses "Purge!" as command (doesn't even need to fire), forgets she suspended Nodoka (infuriates Nodoka), organizes snowball fights to reinforce dominance, has absolute authority despite small stature

## Relationships and outlook

- **Tomoe Sashiro** (Red Winter Office): Cherino relies on Tomoe as faithful right-hand student who knows everything about her (serves pudding, helps naps, attends to all demands)

- **Marina Ikekura** (Red Winter Office): Marina is clumsy and breaks Cherino's rules (once broke a statue), Cherino punishes her, Marina attempts coups but is quickly overpowered

- **Nodoka Amami** (No. 227 Special Class): Cherino suspended Nodoka for spying but completely forgot about it, Nodoka resents her for this

- **Shigure Mayoi** (No. 227 Special Class): Cherino suspended Shigure for making alcoholic drinks, Shigure doesn't take it personally

- **Minori Yasumori** (Public Works Department): Minori consistently opposes Cherino's unfair and tyrannical rule enforcement
