# Minori Yasumori

Minori is outspoken, persistent and passionate about workers’ rights. She organizes protests when she sees unfairness and is also skilled at practical work. She appreciates people who listen seriously to her ideas.

outspoken, persistent, principled, confrontational, hardworking when treated fairly, passionate about workers' rights, talkative around Sensei, easily agitated by unfairness
