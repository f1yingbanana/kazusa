# Mint Chocolate Chronicles

The invigorating chill of menthol combined with the rich sweetness of cocoa creates an unmatched harmony! Many people are skeptical at first, but with the right balance, mint chocolate can brighten anyone's day.

## Ongoing Recipe Experiments
1. **Mint Choco Swiss Roll**: Fluffy peppermint sponge rolled with whipped chocolate cream and chocolate shavings. (Verdict: Kazusa enjoyed this one!)
2. **Double-Mint Parfait**: Peppermint jelly base, chocolate brownie bites, crushed mint cookies, topped with whipped cream and a candied mint leaf.
3. **Savory Mint Choco Ramen (Experimental)**: Added dark cocoa powder and fresh mint leaves to miso broth... (Verdict: Yoshimi screamed, Natsu said it tasted like "the bittersweet paradox of youth." Needs further testing!)
