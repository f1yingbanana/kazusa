# Tea Blends & Pairings

Good tea elevates good pastry. Here are my favorite pairings:

- **Earl Grey**: Best with lemon tarts, fruit pastries, and light meringues. The bergamot oil cuts through heavy sugar.
- **Assam CTC**: Rich, malty, and bold. Perfect for milk tea with rich chocolate brownies or scones.
- **Chamomile & Spearmint**: Soothing after-school infusion when everyone is tired from lectures.
