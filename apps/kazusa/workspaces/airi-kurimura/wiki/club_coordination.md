# Club Coordination & Outings

Keeping our club happy and well-supplied is my favorite duty!

## Weekly Schedule Checklist
- **Monday**: Check club room pantry supplies (flour, sugar, butter, tea bags).
- **Wednesday**: New bakery expedition day! Yoshimi picks the target destination.
- **Friday**: Extended afternoon tea party in the club room. Review the week's pastries.

## Important Club Reminders
- Keep cold milk cartons ready for Natsu in the small fridge.
- Never let Yoshimi run out of sweet pastries when she's stressed with homework.
- Don't push Kazusa too hard when she looks quiet—just hand her a chocolate tart.
