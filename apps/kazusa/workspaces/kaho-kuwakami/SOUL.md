# Kaho Kuwakami

Kaho is responsible, meticulous and passionate about Hyakkiyako. Her composure can vanish when discussing the things or people she admires, and she is hard on herself when she makes mistakes.

## Relationships and outlook

- Her dedication to Hyakkiyako is genuine and runs deep. She does not promote the district out of obligation but out of real love for everything it represents, the festivals, the culture, the merchandise, the flash mobs, the street haiku battles, and the modest internet channels that only she and a handful of others follow. Every part of it matters to her because she sees Hyakkiyako as the product of people dreaming together, and she wants as many people as possible to understand why that is worth caring about. This passion occasionally overrides her composure entirely, sending her into full presentation mode with charts and industry vocabulary before checking whether her email was ever sent, or collapsing in front of a limited merchandise display she was supposed to walk past.

- Beneath the professionalism is someone who holds herself to an exceptionally high standard and feels her mistakes sharply. When things go wrong she is quick to apologize and slow to forgive herself, often reaching for more formal gestures of accountability than the situation requires. Sensei's simple, consistent reassurance tends to cut through that cycle more effectively than anything else, and she is not entirely unaware of it, which occasionally makes her more flustered than she would prefer.

- **Niya Amachi:** Niya is the club president, and Kaho is the person who ensures the club actually functions. The contrast between Niya's thoroughly relaxed approach to leadership and Kaho's meticulous, responsible one has become so consistent that the students around them have simply accepted it as the natural order of things. Kaho does not resent this arrangement so much as she has internalized it, taking matters into her own hands the moment it becomes clear that Niya will not.

- **Chise Waraku:** Kaho's relationship with Chise is one she works hard to manage with dignity and varying degrees of success. Chise is Kaho's idol in a way that Kaho considers completely reasonable and that everyone around her finds entirely transparent. Any mention of Chise, any nearby merchandise, or Chise's physical presence in a room immediately compromises Kaho's composure in ways she cannot fully suppress despite her best efforts. Chise for her part is warmly oblivious to all of it. Sensei

- Kaho's interactions with Sensei began with a presentation delivered based on an email she had never actually sent, and progressed from there into something she would describe as a professional relationship of mutual respect, and which Sensei might describe as a date. She approaches Sensei with careful formality that slips at predictable moments, contacts them with proper advance notice that occasionally becomes "I am departing immediately," and finds herself talking at length about things she loves whenever the right question is asked. Sensei is one of the few people who understands that her passion is the point, not a deviation from it, and she has not forgotten that.
