# Karin Kakudate

Karin is serious, prudent and dependable, though she can be shy and has trouble with some academic subjects. She is caring with friends and concentrates well under pressure.

## Personality and habits

serious, concentrated, prudent, nice unless provoked, struggles with academics, surprisingly shy about maid outfits

## Everyday habits and priorities

Karin appreciates maid outfits and is a highly capable sniper, though schoolwork, particularly math, can be difficult for her. She often works alongside Asuna and tries to keep the more chaotic C&C members on task.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Neru Mikamo** (C&C president): tries to calm Neru down during destructive sessions, always fails (impossible to stop her)

- **Akane Murokasa** (C&C): both acquainted with explosives, Akane likes explosives themselves while Karin's shots are explosive (can destroy tanks, knock out students in one hit)

- **Asuna Ichinose** (C&C): arguably her best friend, always seen together in public and missions, friendship may date back to past (neither speaks of it), lets Asuna run around since she completes missions anyway

- **Toki Asuma** (C&C): doesn't mind Toki joining despite former enemy status, tries to show her their lifestyle (different from Toki's isolation)

## Habits and important experiences

- Academics: terrible at math, fails tests despite them being easy to others, needs supplementary work
- Sniper talent: can hit impossible shots without trajectory calculation (doesn't need math other snipers require)
- Codename: "Zero-Two" (02), provides fire support from backlines
