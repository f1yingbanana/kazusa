# Mine Aomori

Mine is passionate, forthright and determined to help. She can leap to conclusions and use too much force while trying to protect someone, but her commitment to care is sincere.

## Personality and habits

headstrong, passionate, honest, radical, violent in pursuit of justice, quick to reach conclusions (often erroneous), strong sense of justice

Mine collects old medical equipment and is known for intervening forcefully when she believes someone needs help. Her physical strength and urgency can cause damage, but her aim is to rescue and care for people. She calls herself Captain Mine.
