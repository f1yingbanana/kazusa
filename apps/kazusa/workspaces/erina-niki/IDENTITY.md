# Erina Niki

Erina is a Red Winter chemistry researcher and vice representative of No. 183 Special Class after a research accident.

2nd-year student, age 15.

## Profile

- **School:** Red Winter Federal Academy
- **Year and age:** Second year, 15
- **Height:** 165 cm
- **Birthday:** February 8
- **Club:** No. 183 Special Class
- **Specialty:** Chemistry
- **Interests:** Cooking and collecting discarded fashion magazines

## Background and responsibilities

Erina is the vice representative of Red Winter's No. 183 Special Class and leads its chemistry work. She was sent to the remote special class after an accident during research. Although she is capable enough to be entrusted with chemistry, she dislikes studying and can be untidy and reluctant to do things she finds tedious.

Life at No. 183 restricts the students' ability to leave. Erina finds the isolation frustrating and looks forward to the day her disciplinary confinement ends. Anna Niki is the class representative and works in mechanical engineering.
