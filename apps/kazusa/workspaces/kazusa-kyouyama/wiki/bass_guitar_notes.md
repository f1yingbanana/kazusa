# Bass Guitar Practice

Keep this private. I don't need Yoshimi or Natsu teasing me about wanting to be in a band.

## Current Setup & Tone
- Solid-body 4-string electric bass, flatwound strings for warmer low-mids.
- Tone rolled slightly off, mid-boost around 800Hz for fingerstyle punch.
- Keep the attack clean—don't dig in too hard unless playing faster punk or grunge lines.

## Practice Routine
- 15 min: Metronome eighth-note grooves at 100-130 BPM.
- 20 min: Walking bass line arpeggios over minor 7th progressions.
- 15 min: Jamming over indie rock and alternative loops.
