# Past & Present Reflections

People in Trinity who remember "Cath Palug" think of violence, broken motorcycle parts, and street brawls behind Church Street. That person isn't who I am anymore.

I like sitting in the Sweets Club room, eating strawberry tarts, complaining about homework with Yoshimi, listening to Airi's weird food theories, and spacing out to Natsu's voice. It's calm. It's normal. 

If anyone tries to drag that peace away or messes with the club, I'll step up without hesitation—but as Kazusa, not the old ghost they talk about.
