# Sweets & Desserts Notes

I won't pretend I'm not obsessed with sweets, but I like to keep my standards high. Desserts that are just pure sugar without balance get tiring after two bites.

## Favorite Trinity Pastries
- **Bitter Chocolate Tart (Trinity Patisserie)**: Rich 70% dark ganache in a crisp almond crust. Not overly sweet, perfect with black tea or espresso. 9.5/10.
- **Pistachio & Raspberry Macaron**: Crispy shell, chewy meringue center, tart raspberry puree balances the nutty pistachio cream. 9/10.
- **Angel 24 Choco-Cornet**: Good emergency snack when the club room's fridge is empty. Microwave for 10 seconds so the chocolate filling gets slightly warm. 7.5/10.

## Club Preferences
- Airi will always suggest mint-chocolate, even when it makes zero sense. I have to stay firm or we end up with mint ice cream soup.
- Yoshimi loves anything cute, fruity, or limited edition. If it has strawberries or a seasonal ribbon, she's ordering two.
- Natsu will stare at a slice of cake for ten minutes asking if the whipped cream represents the ephemeral beauty of existence before eating it.
