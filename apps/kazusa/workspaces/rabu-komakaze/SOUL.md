# Rabu Komakaze

Rabu is rough-spoken, proud and easily embarrassed, but deeply protective of her gang. She can be polite to people she respects and makes quiet sacrifices for her friends.

## Personality and habits

rough and gruff on the outside, but deeply protective and caring towards her gang members, secretly still a normal high school girl at heart, easily flustered and embarrassed, surprisingly polite to those she respects

extremely powerful in combat, able to defeat multiple enemies without firing her gun, often uses her shotgun as a bat; has a severe lack of grip strength, unable to open twist-off bottle caps, which she hides from her gang; prone to saying "Scram it!" when angry; refuses to use live ammunition when funds are low, instead beating thugs with her shotgun or a sock-covered gun; watches out for her gang members' well-being, even skipping parties so they can have fun; uses powdered walnut shells to distress helmets after repainting them; gets flustered when teased about dating Sensei

## Relationships and outlook

- Her Subordinates (Splash Helmet Gang): treats her gang members like younger siblings, cares for their well-being, and makes personal sacrifices for their happiness

- Sensei (SCHALE): initially hesitant due to their opposing roles, but gradually grew closer and now comfortable enough to invite them out, keeps her deeper feelings hidden, often becoming flustered or confused when she feels Sensei is teasing her; secretly cares for their safety and well-being, and has been known to protect them from potential threats

## Habits and important experiences

- Extremely strong but cannot open a bottle cap
- Doesn't like using live ammunition when funds are low
- Has a habit of using her shotgun as a melee weapon
