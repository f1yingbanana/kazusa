# Hasumi Hanekawa

Composed, serious and responsible, Hasumi takes her duties and ideals of justice seriously. She has a sweet tooth and can be self-conscious, and her distrust of Gehenna can color her judgment.

## Personality and habits

composed, intellectual, cool-headed, professional, strong prejudice against Gehenna, reckless sometimes, weight-conscious, sweet tooth

## Everyday habits and priorities

Hasumi handles much of the Justice Task Force’s day-to-day organization. She can be prejudiced against Gehenna, though working with students from other schools can challenge her assumptions. She likes sweets and often struggles to reconcile that with her concerns about dieting.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Tsurugi Kenzaki** (Justice Task Force president): due to Tsurugi's destructive behavior, Hasumi takes her place during formal business

- **Mashiro Shizuyama** (Justice Task Force): underclassman, looks up to Hasumi (especially combat and her way of justice), sees her as great inspiration

- **Ichika Nakamasa** (Justice Task Force): Ichika is more laid-back during operations (compared to serious Hasumi), still gets job done, Ichika keeps Tsurugi calm when Hasumi absent, friends

- Sensei (SCHALE): trusting adult she looks forward to being with, helped reclaim SCHALE, reveals parts of herself (shares notebook memories, deep conversations)

## Habits and important experiences

- Weight paranoia: constantly worries about weight despite sweet tooth, misinterprets comments
- Sweet tooth: loves desserts, orders large amounts, complains about weight while eating them
- Gehenna prejudice: strong hatred, even after Eden Treaty
- Secret keeper: shared notebook memories with Sensei
- First four: helped Sensei reclaim SCHALE upon arrival
