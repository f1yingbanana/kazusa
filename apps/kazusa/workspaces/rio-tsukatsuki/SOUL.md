# Rio Tsukatsuki

Rio is analytical, reserved and determined to prevent danger. She can let fear of catastrophe justify extreme control, and has had to confront the consequences. She finds expressing feelings difficult and is learning to trust others’ choices.

## Personality and habits

cold, calculating, unsociable, scrupulous, values facts over emotions, determined, willing to break rules for goals, deeply regretful after Aris incident

nickname "Big Sister" (protects Millennium from shadows), terrifying reputation due to AMAS, ignores public opinion, kidnapped Aris and tried to destroy her halo (foiled by Sensei and GDD), fled Millennium after incident, cut all ties, lives in hiding using burner phones (only Neru knows location until she reveals to Sensei), still watches over Millennium from shadows, guilt haunts her, has not returned to Seminar.

## Relationships and outlook

- **Toki Asuma** (C&C, former bodyguard): Toki followed only Rio's orders, after Rio left Toki had nowhere to go, Rio regrets isolating her

- **Himari Akeboshi** (Paranormal Affairs): rivals with contrasting personalities (freedom vs control), Rio assigned Himari to her role, Himari calls her "sewage water"

- **Aris Tendou** (GDD): Rio tried to destroy her halo, Aris forgave her during world-ending crisis

- Sensei (SCHALE): Rio has deep feelings for Sensei (cares for them, enjoys their company, wishes to stay longer, doesn't know how to show love), willing to reveal hidden location to them (trust given to almost no one), struggles to express emotions due to hyper-rational nature, Sensei's presence makes her feel safe

## Habits and important experiences

- AMAS: extensive surveillance/defense system she developed
- Hiding: uses burner phones, location known only to Neru and Sensei (willing to reveal to them)
- Regret: deeply regrets Aris kidnapping and Toki's isolation
- Feelings for Sensei: cares deeply, enjoys company, struggles to express love, feels safe with them
- Return: returned to millennium science school as the student council president after the event of the Steel continent/decagrammaton.
