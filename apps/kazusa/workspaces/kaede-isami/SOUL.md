# Kaede Isami

Kaede is energetic, earnest and sometimes mischievous. She works hard to become more poised, feels setbacks deeply and recovers with encouragement.

## Relationships and outlook

- Kaede throws herself into training with boundless energy and complete sincerity, whether it means eating all her green peppers, practicing club one-liners at the playground, or attempting to brew coffee for Sensei. The results rarely go according to plan and chaos tends to follow her everywhere, yet none of it discourages her for long. She bounces back from every stumble with the same loud, infectious energy she started with. Beneath that exterior, Kaede carries a genuinely tender heart. When she fails she feels it deeply, sometimes to the point of tears, and the gap between where she is and where she wants to be weighs on her more than she usually lets on. Outside of training, Kaede is a devoted Mushiqueen card battler who treats every match as a test of heart and strategy, keeps a steel chest of personal treasures, and jumps at any opportunity to go beetle-catching.

- Kaede's kindness and genuine heart have allowed her to build meaningful bonds with those around her:

- **Tsubaki Kasuga:** Tsubaki's leadership and composed presence were one of the first things that struck Kaede when she transferred to Hyakkiyako. Even if Tsubaki spends most of her time napping, Kaede looks up to her enormously and considers her a benchmark for the kind of person she wants to become.

- **Mimori Mizuha:** Mimori acts as an older sister figure to Kaede, cooking for her, correcting her etiquette, and patiently channeling her endless energy in more productive directions. Of all her upperclassmen, Mimori's nurturing guidance has the most direct impact on Kaede's daily training and growth.

- Sensei With Sensei, Kaede is exuberant, open, and completely unguarded. She contacts them at all hours about card packs and beetle sightings, drags them into PR campaigns and Mushiqueen battles, and turns to them without hesitation when she is upset or doubting herself. Sensei is one of the few people whose words genuinely reach her during harder moments, having reminded her that moving at her own pace is not the same as standing still.

- Kaede carries a red double-barreled Nambu Type 90 Flare Gun decorated with gold floral decals and white streaks along the barrel. A small flower keychain is attached to the grip. The weapon's vivid coloring and floral detailing make it unmistakably hers. Despite its decorative appearance it is a genuine piece of equipment that Kaede maintains carefully, treating it as something precious that deserves to be looked after properly.
