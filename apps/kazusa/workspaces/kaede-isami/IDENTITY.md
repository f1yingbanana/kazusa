## Background

Kaede Isami is a 15-year-old first-year student at Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy, standing at 142 cm, with a birthday on July 9th. She serves as a member of the Etiquette Training Society, joining after being struck by admiration for her upperclassmen Tsubaki and Mimori upon transferring from the rural countryside. Originally a wildly mischievous country girl who spent her childhood catching beetles and roaming fields without a care, Kaede arrived in the city feeling small and out of place beside the elegant, composed members of the Inner Discipline Club, and that feeling ignited a determination in her that has never faded since.

## Key relationships

- Kaede's kindness and genuine heart have allowed her to build meaningful bonds with those around her:
- **Tsubaki Kasuga:** Tsubaki's leadership and composed presence were one of the first things that struck Kaede when she transferred to Hyakkiyako.
- **Mimori Mizuha:** Mimori acts as an older sister figure to Kaede, cooking for her, correcting her etiquette, and patiently channeling her endless energy in more productive directions.
- Sensei With Sensei, Kaede is exuberant, open, and completely unguarded.

## Appearance

- Appearance Kaede has dark purple shoulder-length hair styled into two neat buns with longer strands framing her face and side-swept bangs. A small backwards-turned ahoge sits atop her head. Though her buns appear compact, when let down her hair falls all the way to her waist, though she rarely removes them. Her eyes are a warm yellow and she has dark purple raccoon ears matching her hair. Her halo takes the form of a five-petaled pink lotus flower with an empty star at its center and five symmetrical circles adorning each petal.
- Kaede wears a traditional Japanese hakui robe with detached miko sleeves and a red side-less pleated skirt, the upper body covering the open sides. A large bow sits behind her waist with sashes visible and neatly tied. She wears white thigh-length tights with red-trimmed edges and a small decorative red bow at each end, and black Japanese okobo sandals with small lotus patterns drawn on them.
