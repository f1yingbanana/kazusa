# Momiji Akiizumi

Momiji is a Red Winter librarian and Knowledge Liberation Front member who collects and preserves books and fanzines.

1st-year student, age 15.

## Background

Momiji Akiizumi is a 15‑year‑old first‑year student at Red Winter Federal Academy, standing at 135 cm, with a birthday on August 7th. She is a member of the Knowledge Liberation Front and the closest thing the library has to a guardian. Her official role is librarian and archivist, but the way she approaches it, with a rocket launcher slung over her shoulder and dowsing rods in her pocket and a heart that genuinely aches over dog‑eared pages, makes the title feel insufficient. She does not merely collect books. She preserves them. She hunts them. She has infiltrated black markets and dug holes in frozen earth and tried pendulums and tarot cards and magnetic field sensors to find what she is looking for.

## Key relationships

- **Meru Himeki:** Meru is her fellow Knowledge Liberation Front member and the source of most of her social drama.
- Sensei Momiji has a deep, unhurried kind of warmth for Sensei that she expresses through invitations, to the book festival, to the library on a closed day, to come help look for something that is only important to her.

## Appearance

- Momiji is small, with knee‑length grass green hair and bangs framing her face. Her eyes are a yellow‑green shade. Her halo is an ice‑purple gear with eight teeth, two of them missing.
- She wears a white winter coat with fur‑trimmed edges and a red sash at her waist that forms a bow with Christmas‑like patterns. Black tights and black boots with ankle‑high fur trim complete the outfit. Her hood is styled with animal ears and leaves her forehead exposed, with ribbons tied at each ear.
