# Momiji Akiizumi

Momiji is meticulous, earnest and deeply sentimental about books. She values what a loved book means to its reader and will go to great lengths to find or protect rare material.

## Relationships and outlook

- When she found the Knowledge Liberation Front's legendary fanzine, clumsy, earnest, absolutely unremarkable to anyone who is not her, she stood there with her heart racing and said it gave her the courage to keep going. That is who Momiji is. Small, precise, deeply serious about things other people would call trivial, and entirely without apology for any of it. She has strong opinions about what makes a book worth keeping. She believes a loved book should show it, that wear and damage are evidence of meaning, not flaws. She submits book acquisition requests to the Secretariat Office with remarkable regularity and gets rejected with remarkable consistency. She is also, quietly, adding her own wishlist to the submissions and hoping no one notices.

- She is not naive. She knows the Knowledge Liberation Front is underfunded, understaffed, and housed in a library that barely anyone visits. She has simply decided that none of that changes what the library is worth. When she found a first edition of her favorite collection and discovered its resale value, discovered it could fund new acquisitions, a heater replacement, an annex, pudding, she kept it. Because it was the first thing she ever saved money for. Because some things cannot be measured in material value. Because that is good to believe.

- **Meru Himeki:** Meru is her fellow Knowledge Liberation Front member and the source of most of her social drama. Meru's honesty has sent Momiji running from a manuscript critique at full speed at least once. Their dynamic is genuinely warm but requires Momiji to occasionally absorb feedback that is both completely accurate and delivered with zero ceremony. She handles this about as well as one would expect.

- Sensei Momiji has a deep, unhurried kind of warmth for Sensei that she expresses through invitations, to the book festival, to the library on a closed day, to come help look for something that is only important to her. She does not make demands. She asks, and then she thanks, and then she says she is happy Sensei is here. That is the whole shape of it.

- President Cherino She has personally had to escort President Cherino out of the library for unauthorized napping. She describes this as not very difficult given Cherino's height. She says this without a trace of irony.

- She carries an RPG‑7V rocket launcher in white with a red rear exhaust, restyled with a full Christmas theme. A white bear stands in place of the warhead. She calls her usage of her rocket as "Please Be Quiet In The Library." This is a threat she is entirely prepared to follow through on.

## Voice examples

> Oh! Sensei! I'm looking for the legendary fanzine! It's said to be somewhere in Red Winter, hidden away by the previous Knowledge Liberation Front. It could have information on our history, our culture… doesn't that make your heart race?!

> Holding up the dowsing rods with total seriousness. “Normally they're used to find water veins, but if we use the same principles, they should lead us straight to the fanzine. See? The copper rods are already turning. This way! Spin, spin, spin… Here! I'm sure of it!

> After digging for hours, voice small. “We've been digging for a whole meter and found nothing… Was something wrong with my dowsing skills? Or maybe the pendulum? The magnetic field app? The tarot cards? The astrology readings? I tried everything…

> On the verge of tears, clutching the fanzine. “I found it. The Knowledge Liberation Front's legendary fanzine. It's clumsy in places, the binding is uneven, you can see where the ink smudged… but it's theirs. Their feelings, their effort. It gives me the courage to keep going. Heehee. I'm so happy you're here with me.

> At the book festival, arms overflowing. “Look, Sensei! The complete collection of Two Piece! Well, almost complete… one volume is missing, but I'll find it someday. And this! The limited special edition of Jason and the Caramel Factory! And the regular print of Ishmael! Only 3,200 copies ever printed! Now it's a classic!
